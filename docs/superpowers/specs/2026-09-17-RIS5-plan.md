# RIS5 — Ideas engine + expectations-based valuation (plan v1, 2026-09-17)

Refer to this as **RIS5**. Builds on RIS4 slices 2–3 (portal live at https://claude.ai/artifact/BRc8rhxBgBDjtwGpzGS4N1, v5).

## Context

The Ideas tab shows machine events (theme newly said, screen cleared). The operator wants investment ideas with a direction: names where conviction is improving or inflecting (New / Increase) and names to reduce or sell (losing share, eroding moat, stretched valuation), with durability of growth, innovation velocity, and competition weighted heaviest, valuation as a first-class driver, flows as context only, and a "Discuss" path into Ask. Ideas must be persistent items with a lifecycle so outcomes can be measured.

Inventory (2026-09-17) established the binding constraints: evidence log has 10 days of history, assumption changes 7 days, ranking/consensus/insiders one snapshot each, peer-ETF trades a 14-day window never archived; the only deep series (guidance/consensus/actual, 21 quarters, 56 tickers) is stale since 2026-06-12; the reviewer's quarterly innovation/competition/AI reads (48 tickers, up to 4 quarters) are prose; the score-proposal regex misses "hold at" and "Populate as" so 5/88 theses carry proposals; valuation is absent everywhere; every existing path is additive (no reduce). Therefore: **Part A foundations first, Part B engine second.**

## Weights (config, editable; re-fit from ledger outcomes later)

Fundamental momentum (change over the last 2–4 quarters, 90-day decay on dated evidence): innovation velocity 30 · competitive position 30 · growth durability 25 · AI positioning 15.
Priced-in: valuation gap 60 · consensus revision breadth 25 · street attention (theme diffusion stage at the name vs peers) 15. Peer-ETF trades and insiders: context chips only, never scored.
Idea strength = momentum change × (1 − priced-in). Two independent evidence families required to open an idea (management narrative; street; other managers; insiders; price).

**Innovation velocity, defined:** change in shipping cadence (new products/platforms per year, accelerating or not), first-to-disclose behaviour on themes analysts later ask peers about, the reviewer's §6 innovation reading per quarter, R&D intensity later. Measured as change, not level.

## Valuation design — expectations gap (the thoughtful part)

Per ticker, per build, from a daily FactSet snapshot (mcp-lean `claude_p.run_mcp`, `.pvt` guard, batched ids):
- **Inputs (all shown on the card):** price, market cap, shares (GlobalPrices `prices` + `market_value`), net debt / cash and gross margin, operating margin, FCF margin (Fundamentals), consensus SALES/EPS FY1–FY3 and long-term growth (EstimatesConsensus `consensus_fixed`/`consensus_rolling`), consensus history (daily snapshots accrued from day one → revision breadth over 4/13 weeks).
- **Layer 1 — what is priced:** reverse-DCF-lite: given EV, a stated discount rate (config, default 10%), a terminal growth (config, 3%), and a margin path that converges from today's FCF margin to a stated terminal margin over 5 years, solve for the constant 5-year revenue growth that reproduces EV. Output `implied_growth_5y` and `implied_terminal_margin`. Sensitivity: ±1pt discount rate and ±3pt terminal margin shown as a range.
- **Layer 2 — what the evidence supports:** `supported_growth` = base path from consensus FY1–FY3 extended by the durability score (theme lifecycle stage of the name's themes, credibility/guidance track record, structured reads trend) with an explicit downside path where the core assumption is `challenged`. Stored as a range `{base, downside}` with the assumption ids that drive it.
- **Layer 3 — the gap:** `expectations_gap = implied_growth_5y − supported_growth.base` (percentage points). Also `gap_downside`. Positive = price needs more than the evidence gives.
- **Lenses:** forward EV/Sales and P/E relative to growth (PEG-like) vs the name's own 3-year history (z-score from daily snapshots; until history accrues, vs FactSet historical multiples if available) and vs its theme peers (median of tickers sharing ≥2 themes); revision breadth (up minus down revisions, 4 and 13 weeks).
- **Scoring:** priced-in valuation component = f(gap z-score, PEG-vs-history z, PEG-vs-peers z), clipped; `valuation_extreme` flag when gap > +8pts or PEG-vs-history z > +2 → can open a **Reduce** on its own for tier 1; part of the **Sell** trigger with a challenged core assumption + competitive position turning down.
- **Never a price target.** Cards show implied vs supported growth, the gap, the multiple vs history/peers, and the revision trend, each with date and inputs.

## Amendment v1.2 (2026-09-17 19:00 ET, operator guidance on valuation)

Operator: "Valuation should be forward looking. We use P/E adjusted for growth for some companies, EV/EBITDA if they have no earnings, and sometimes EV/FCF. We look at estimates 1–3 years out, sometimes as far as 5, never beyond. Companies like Tesla and SpaceX are obviously tougher."

Binding changes:
1. **Forward multiple ladder with a selection rule (per ticker, per card).** Primary lens is chosen in this order and the choice is printed: (a) **PEG** = forward P/E (FY1, FY2) ÷ EPS CAGR FY1→FY3, when FY1 EPS > 0 and EPS growth > 0; (b) **EV/EBITDA-to-growth** = forward EV/EBITDA (FY1, FY2) ÷ EBITDA CAGR FY1→FY3, when EPS ≤ 0 but consensus EBITDA > 0 and growing; (c) **EV/FCF-to-growth** = forward EV/FCF (FY1, FY2) ÷ FCF CAGR FY1→FY3, when consensus FCF > 0 and growing (also shown alongside PEG for cash-rich names); (d) **EV/Sales-to-growth** only as a last resort, flagged `no_profit_lens`. **Every rung is adjusted for growth** (operator clarification 19:05 ET): multiple ÷ the CAGR of its own denominator, FY1→FY3. The raw multiples are printed next to the adjusted ones. Every lens is computed on consensus estimates, never trailing figures; a rung whose growth ≤ 0 is null with reason.
2. **Horizon.** Consensus FY1–FY3 is the working horizon; FY4–FY5 are used only where consensus exists (count ≥ 3 analysts) and are shown with their counts; nothing is estimated beyond year 5. The reverse-DCF explicit path stays 5 years; the terminal value is not a forecast, so every card prints `terminal_share_of_ev` (share of EV explained by value beyond year 5) so the reader sees how much of the price rests past the horizon.
3. **Hard names (long-duration / pre-profit / thin consensus, e.g. TSLA, SPCX).** When `terminal_share_of_ev > 0.75`, or consensus covers < 3 forward years, or the primary lens falls to (d), the card is marked `long_duration` and the module shows only what is priced (implied growth, terminal share, the available multiples) — no supported-growth leg, no gap, no `valuation_extreme`. These names are handled by the operator, not the engine.
4. **A3 inputs.** Daily consensus pull adds EBITDA and FCF (FactSet EstimatesConsensus metrics, FY1–FY3, same batching); FY4–FY5 for SALES/EPS/EBITDA/FCF pulled weekly (Sunday) with counts. `latest.json` carries them under the same nested shape.
5. **PEG vs history/peers** stays the z-scored lens for the priced-in component; EV/EBITDA and EV/FCF get the same own-history and theme-peer comparisons once history accrues.
