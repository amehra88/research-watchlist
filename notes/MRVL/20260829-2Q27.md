## 1. Headline read

Confirm-with-acceleration on the numbers, but the call's defining event is the Google agreement — and the widely-circulated "$120B" attached to it is a **warrant vesting ceiling, not backlog**. The 8-K (2026-08-19, Item 1.01) discloses 240 equal warrant tranches vesting "for each $500 million in Custom Products revenue" from Q3 FY27 through end of FY2033; $120B is the arithmetic product of that schedule (240 × $500M), i.e. the cumulative revenue at which Google's warrant fully vests, not a contracted purchase commitment. Against the 1Q27 read (six straight Confirms, "no theme drifting or breaking"), five themes stay Confirm and **hyperscaler_revenue_concentration moves to Drift** — the prior quarter's mechanism was roster-broadening; this quarter one customer anchors the FY29+ custom story and holds a warrant on up to 6.7% of the company. ## 4. Thesis read by theme

### silicon_architecture_competition

- **Status:** Confirm (strengthened, with an unresolved alignment tension)
- **Evidence:** The 8-K (2026-08-19, Item 1.01) states verbatim that the expanded Google partnership "spans a comprehensive range of custom silicon programs that attach to the TPU ecosystem, including AI inference accelerators, storage controllers, network interface controllers, memory interface controllers, and near-memory compute." Custom to more than double y/y in FY28; management indicated the prior $8–10B custom target could be exceeded (Sur, Reitzes exchanges — paraphrase).
- **Implication:** MRVL's #2-behind-AVGO custom position is now anchored to the single largest non-NVDA accelerator program in existence. But this creates a genuine, unresolved alignment tension the PM should hold explicitly: MRVL is now warrant-aligned to the leading anti-NVDA silicon program (TPU) while NVDA holds $2B of MRVL equity and MRVL sits inside NVLink Fusion — and Google is AVGO's flagship custom customer, so MRVL is taking attach share adjacent to AVGO's crown jewel. Murphy's Computex "Switzerland of the industry / we work with everybody" framing is precisely what makes this coherent rather than contradictory, but it is now load-bearing rather than rhetorical.

### ai_infrastructure_capex

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** FY27 data center growth raised to ~60% y/y from ~50%; FY28 data center >60% y/y; total FY28 raised to ~$18B (+~50%) from $16.5B (+45%) — an *acceleration* in the growth rate, not just the dollar level. Q3 data center guided +~75% y/y.
- **Implication:** Last quarter's key nuance was that MRVL sustained +45% FY28 growth *despite* cloud capex growth moderating to ~30% — the "MRVL outgrows capex" decoupling. This quarter strengthens that: the FY28 growth rate went up, not down. Content-per-box and share gain, not just capex beta, remain the driver. Confirms and reinforces.

### networking_competitive_landscape

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** All three major connectivity businesses (optical DSPs, broadband analog, scale-out switching / DCI modules) described as on or ahead of $1B annualized run-rate trajectories. Scale-out switching on track to more than double in FY27. Strong 800G and 1.6T optical DSP demand. Management cited a **larger-than-expected scale-up optics revenue forecast for FY28** and adoption of both NPO and CPO packaging approaches.
- **Implication:** The connectivity franchise is executing ahead of the already-raised 1Q27 plan, and the scale-up optics upgrade is the single innovation-adjacent datapoint of the call. Read-through pressure sustained on AVGO (optics share), CRDO (PAM4/DSP), COHR/LITE (coherent). MRVL supporting *both* NPO and CPO rather than betting one is a hedge worth noting — it preserves optionality but implies the packaging winner is not yet settled.

### hyperscaler_revenue_concentration

- **Status:** **Drift**
- **Evidence:** The 8-K discloses a warrant to Google for up to **58,970,907 shares at $206.58**, of which only 1,360,867 are time-based; the remaining ~57.6M vest in 240 tranches, one per $500M of Custom Products revenue, through FY2033. Three of the nine catalogued Q&A exchanges (O'Malley, Sur, and Reitzes — the call's one flagged *challenging* exchange) were Google-focused. Data center rose to 79% of total revenue from 76% in 1Q27.
- **Implication:** This is the quarter's one genuine thesis movement. The 1Q27 read was "Confirm with slight constructive drift toward diversification" — roster broadening, >50 opportunities lowering single-customer tail risk. The mechanism has inverted: FY29+ custom upside now rests materially on one named customer, who is simultaneously an equity claimant on up to 6.7% of the company. The thesis is not broken — the economics are excellent and the vesting structure aligns rather than extracts — but magnitude and mechanism both moved, and concentration is now the correct frame rather than diversification. Watch: whether Investor Day discloses non-Google FY29 custom composition.

### ai_compute_topology

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** Management positioned MRVL for the copper-to-optical transition in **scale-up** networking specifically, with customers described as planning deployments starting in 2027. Moore (Morgan Stanley) asked about the copper-to-optical transition; Murphy framed MRVL's path as continuous with its established switching credibility and stressed the end-to-end portfolio (paraphrase). Rakers (Wells Fargo) asked on CXL and scale-up optics including Celestial AI; Murphy called CXL a successful investment and described a growing scale-up optics opportunity (paraphrase).
- **Implication:** Directly confirms the Computex "200G per lane is the last copper generation" thesis with a dated commercial hook — customer deployments starting 2027 puts the electrical-to-optical scale-up transition inside the FY28 guidance window rather than in outyear narrative. The Celestial AI Photonic Fabric remains the highest-leverage unproven asset; a "growing opportunity" characterization and a raised FY28 scale-up optics forecast are directionally supportive but still short of the $500M Q4 FY28 run-rate milestone the scoring notes flag as the score-moving event.

### inference_compute_economics

- **Status:** Confirm (with a margin-mechanism caveat)
- **Evidence:** The Google agreement is explicitly an *inference* franchise — the 8-K names "AI inference accelerators… memory interface controllers, and near-memory compute" as covered Custom Products. Against that, Q3 gross margin guided down to 57.5–58.5% from 58.9%, attributed to custom mix; Bolton (Needham) pressed Murphy and Durn on exactly this, and management acknowledged the mix effect while maintaining the 38–40% operating margin structure (paraphrase).
- **Implication:** The demand-side thesis — that inference economics reward memory bandwidth and interconnect over raw FLOPS — is confirmed about as directly as a filing can confirm it; "near-memory compute" as a contracted product category is the memory-wall thesis made commercial. The caveat is on conversion: custom silicon scales revenue at structurally lower gross margin, so inference-driven volume translates to profit through operating leverage rather than through margin. That is a workable model while revenue compounds at 45–50%, but it makes the operating-expense discipline the load-bearing variable, and it means gross margin will keep drifting down as the thesis works.

  - Recommendation: **revise to 4+**
- **Overall** (current: 4)
  - Recommendation: **hold at 4**
  - Reasoning: The prior note's formulation was "innovation creates distribution, but distribution does not yet protect innovation" — the moat compounds in design wins but is re-won every cycle. The Google warrant is the first evidence that MRVL's distribution is becoming *contractually durable* rather than cycle-renewed: a seven-year, 240-tranche instrument gives the customer a direct equity stake in MRVL's own revenue outcome, which is a materially stickier bond than a design win. That justifies distribution 4 → 4+. But the same instrument concentrates: the durability MRVL gains is durability *with one counterparty*, and the innovation axis produced no new capability this quarter to widen the base. The two dimensions therefore partly offset rather than compound — distribution strengthened in quality while narrowing in breadth, and innovation held flat — so overall stays at 4. Overall moves to 4+ if either (a) Investor Day shows FY29 custom growth with meaningful non-Google composition, or (b) the Celestial AI Photonic Fabric hits its Q4 FY28 $500M run-rate milestone.

4. **O'Malley (Barclays) — declined to break out Google contributions.** O'Malley asked for revenue contribution detail on the Google relationship split between inference accelerator and XPU attach; Murphy gave a broad product overview "without specifying individual contributions" (summary's characterization). 5. **Bolton (Needham) — custom mix and gross margin.** Bolton pressed Murphy and Durn on data center guidance and margin impact; management acknowledged custom growth is influencing margins while maintaining confidence in the operating margin structure (paraphrase). ## 9. Vocabulary candidates

- **near-memory compute** — named as a contracted product category in the 8-K, distinct from CXL/memory-attach. If this becomes a discrete revenue line it is the commercial expression of the memory-wall thesis; watch for AVGO/MRVL/NVDA convergence on the term.
- **customer warrant alignment / revenue-vesting warrant** — the structural pattern of issuing a customer equity that vests against purchase volume (cf. the AMD–OpenAI warrant). This is becoming a recognizable financing category in AI silicon and deserves operator consideration as a cross-ticker theme, not just an MRVL fact.
- **Custom Products revenue** — a *contractually defined* metric in the Google warrant (each $500M tranche). If MRVL begins reporting against it, it becomes an externally auditable disclosure line, which would be unusual and useful.
- **NPO (near-package optics)** vs **CPO (co-packaged optics)** — MRVL is supporting both. NPO is not currently in the themes vocabulary; the NPO/CPO distinction is the live packaging debate across MRVL/AVGO/COHR/LITE.
- **scale-up optics** — carried forward from the 1Q27 note as a candidate; this call gives it a revenue forecast and a 2027 deployment date, strengthening the case for promoting it to a tracked theme.
- **XPU attach** — carried forward from 1Q27; the 8-K now enumerates the attach categories concretely (storage controllers, NICs, memory interface controllers), which sharpens the definition.
