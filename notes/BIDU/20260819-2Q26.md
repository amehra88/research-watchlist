# BIDU — 2Q26 earnings read

_Prepared by earnings-reviewer, 2026-08-19. Tier: T2._

## 1. Headline read

The Q2'26 print **breaks the core mechanism of the Q1'26 read**. The Q1 note's thesis was that AI Cloud + AI applications would scale faster than legacy decline; this quarter they did not. Baidu General Business fell to RMB 25.2B (**-4% YoY, -3% QoQ**), erasing the "+2% YoY, first positive growth in five quarters" that headlined the Q1 note one quarter ago. The AI-powered business — the Q1 note's flagship inflection metric — **declined sequentially** to RMB 12.5B (-8% QoQ, +25% YoY, decelerating from +49%) and slipped from 52% to 50% of BGB. Decomposition is the real finding: essentially **all** AI-powered YoY growth is AI Cloud Infra (+50%); AI Applications grew +3% YoY and AI-native Marketing Services 0% YoY. The "re-architected incumbent" is narrower than the Q1 note asserted — it is a cloud-infrastructure story, not an application-layer transformation. Offsetting: GPU Cloud accelerated to +283% YoY, capex tripled (+201% YoY), and the international robotaxi footprint genuinely advanced. Sentiment fell to Neutral from Slightly Positive, with Advertising scored outright Negative.

## 3. Forward guidance

Management again gave **no quantitative point-estimate guidance** — consistent with standing BIDU practice. Qualitative signals, and note the mix of upgrades and an explicit negative guide:

- **AI Cloud Infra:** "confident in maintaining strong growth in the second half of 2026, with potential for further acceleration." Margin expansion expected from GPU mix, scale, product optimization and self-developed chips. Management framed the segment **exclusively on the +50% YoY** figure and did not address the -17% QoQ decline (RMB 8.8B → 7.3B) — see §8.
- **Advertising — explicit negative guide:** Luo (Chong Q&A) "acknowledged competitive pressures and the deliberate approach to monetization, predicting **continued pressure on advertising business in the near term**," with weakness expected to persist into H2'26. This is unusually direct forward negativity from BIDU management, and it applies to 52% of BGB.
- **Capital allocation / capex:** commitment to "invest decisively in technologies and capabilities" while maintaining "a disciplined focus on ROIC." The 4Q25 note's flag on "alternate financing methods" has now materialised in the financing lines: capex tripled YoY against **net financing inflows of +RMB 17.4B in Q2'26**, driving FCF to -RMB 7.95B. (Total debt rose ~RMB 14.3B over H1'26 on a net basis — the gross new borrowing is larger but partly refinances maturing debt.)
- **Hong Kong dual-primary listing — a dated, mechanical catalyst:** Board approved July 2026; application acknowledged by HKEX; **EGM 2026-08-26**; conversion expected effective within 2026 subject to shareholder and HKEX approval. Management explicitly cites **Stock Connect inclusion** as the follow-on prize (southbound flow access). This is the single hardest-dated catalyst in the name.
- **Kunlunxin:** roadmap cited (optimised M100, upcoming M300); domestic market "supply-constrained." Listing **"still in process, with no new milestones announced"** — third consecutive call without progress.

Tone: InsiderScore sentiment **Neutral, a decrease from Slightly Positive**, with Advertising scored **Negative** and Financials Slightly Negative. Zero challenging exchanges flagged. Confident but measured; management is explicitly framing a multi-year investment cycle rather than near-term delivery.

**Street positioning (FactSet, 2026-08-19).** Ratings **OVERWEIGHT 1.263** (n=38: 28 buy, 4 overweight, 4 hold, 0 underweight, 2 sell) — marginally better than the 1.29 at the Q1 print, so the sell-side has not capitulated on rating. But revision *breadth* is decisively negative: Q3'26 SALES mean 4,615.78 (n=17) with **1 up / 11 down**, and Q4'26 mean 4,978.46 (n=16) with **1 up / 11 down**. Note Q3'26 consensus (4,615.78) sits **below** the Q2'26 actual (4,645.80) — the Street is now modelling a sequential decline, a direct reversal of the Q1 note's observation that "sell-side is modelling continued sequential acceleration."

## 4. Thesis read by theme

_Themes sourced from the `tier_3_watchlist` BIDU entry (line ~1411); the `tier_2_active_candidates` entry carries `themes: []` — see §10._

### search_disruption

- **Status:** **Break (of the Q1 substitution-offset corollary)** — read the one-word status carefully: the *disruption* itself is not in question, it is confirmed and intensifying. What breaks is the corollary the 1Q26 note upgraded on, namely that BIDU's AI revenue would offset the search decline.
- **Evidence:** Online Marketing Services RMB 13.1B, **-19% YoY**; Legacy Business -23% YoY (6-K Ex-99.1 segment tables). Against that, the offsetting engines are flat: **AI-native Marketing Services RMB 2.6B, 0% YoY**; AI Applications RMB 2.5B, **+3% YoY**. Management confirmed weakness will persist into H2 and that it is "deliberately delaying monetization of AI search to focus on user experience" (Chong/Luo Q&A; Advertising sentiment paragraph, scored Negative). Baidu App MAU 644M — reach is not the constraint.
- **Implication:** The Q1 note concluded the substitution path was "delivering" and reframed search disruption from "imminent risk" to "in-progress substitution." Q2 contradicts that specific claim. With 644M MAU producing 0% YoY growth in AI-native marketing, the bottleneck is demonstrably **monetization/pricing power, not distribution**. The cleanest single test of the thesis — does AI growth outrun legacy decline? — failed this quarter: AI-powered +RMB 2.5B YoY versus legacy/other -RMB 3.6B YoY, netting BGB to -4%. Until AI-native marketing or AI Applications inflects, ad decline sets the top line. Watch AI-native Marketing Services YoY as the single highest-signal line item next quarter.

### china_ai_infrastructure_demand

- **Status:** **Drift** (demand intact; the reported growth path decelerated and the mechanism narrowed)
- **Evidence:** AI Cloud Infra RMB 7.3B, **+50% YoY but -17% QoQ** (from RMB 8.8B). GPU Cloud **+283% YoY, accelerating from +184%**, a fourth consecutive triple-digit quarter. Demand described as broad-based across Internet, gaming, embodied AI and autonomous driving, with existing key clients increasing spend and the customer base expanding; domestic market characterised as supply-constrained (Yap/Shen and Kong/Shen Q&A). **Capex RMB 11.37B, +201% YoY** — the company is committing capital into the demand. Note the disclosure relabel: "GPU Cloud" was previously "subscription-based revenue from AI accelerator infrastructure" (6-K footnote 1).
- **Implication:** The underlying China AI-compute demand thesis is intact and the capex tripling is the strongest corroboration available — companies do not triple capex into softening demand. But this is a Drift, not a Confirm, on two counts. First, headline growth decelerated 29 points (+79% → +50%). Second, and unaddressed by management, the segment **declined 17% sequentially in absolute RMB**. With GPU Cloud simultaneously accelerating to +283%, the arithmetic implies non-GPU cloud infra is contracting sharply — the aggregate is being held up by a fast-growing minority while the base shrinks. That could be benign (deliberate mix shift out of low-margin CPU cloud, consistent with the margin-expansion narrative) or a lumpy-deal artifact, but it is not "acceleration." Do not read the +283% sub-metric as the segment's growth rate.

### autonomous_vehicle_competition

- **Status:** **Drift** (China volume broke; international footprint genuinely advanced — the growth mechanism has shifted)
- **Evidence:** Apollo Go delivered **~1M fully driverless rides in Q2 vs 3.2M in Q1'26 — a ~69% sequential collapse**, attributed to "regulatory adjustments in certain Chinese cities"; operations have since resumed and management expressed confidence in regaining momentum (Robotaxi sentiment paragraph; Jiang/Li Q&A). The ride count is internally consistent with cumulative disclosure (22M+ through April → 23M+ now). Against that, international progress is unambiguous and verifiable: **28 cities** (from 26); **Hong Kong's first fully driverless testing permits** — the first robotaxi operator globally to test fully driverless in a right-hand-drive, left-hand-traffic market; **London open-road testing with both Uber and Lyft**; **Dubai fully driverless commercial operations, now the largest robotaxi service there**, bookable via Uber; Switzerland open-road testing with PostBus; MOU with Kazakhstan's Turlov Private Holding. Cumulative 350M+ autonomous km incl. 240M+ fully driverless, with very low airbag-deployment rates.
- **Implication:** Both prior notes classified this Confirm on a rising ride count; carrying that forward would be a trajectory error. The discriminating question is whether the regulatory pause is a one-quarter event or a structural China ceiling — this quarter cannot resolve it, and that ambiguity is precisely why this is Drift rather than Confirm or Break. What has changed is the *mechanism*: quarterly China ride volume is no longer a clean progress indicator, and the investable case now rests on the international footprint, where management continues to argue unit economics are structurally better on higher fares. The HK right-hand-drive milestone is a genuine technical moat marker (it implies platform portability, not just geographic licensing). Read-through unchanged for TSLA/Waymo: BIDU remains the credible non-US global scaler. Track Q3 China ride recovery as the single item that resolves Confirm-vs-Break.

### ai_re_architected_incumbent

- **Status:** **Drift (materially negative)** — the transformation is real and holding at half of BGB, but it has stalled and is narrower than the Q1 note claimed
- **Evidence:** Baidu Core AI-powered Business RMB 12.5B, **+25% YoY (down from +49%), -8% QoQ**, and **50% of BGB, slipping from 52%**. CFO He framed it as having "continued to account for half of Baidu General Business revenue" — a hold, not a compounding. Composition is the key evidence: of the RMB 2.5B YoY increase, AI Cloud Infra contributed RMB 2.4B; **AI Applications +3% YoY and AI-native Marketing Services 0% YoY**. Product cadence remains high (DuMate enterprise edition launched June 2026, GenFlow upgrades in Wenku/Drive, Miaoda MAU +67% Mar→Jun, AI DAU penetration +27.4% YoY) but is not converting to segment revenue. Li: transition "from an internet-centric company to an AI-first company."
- **Implication:** The Q1 note called the >50% crossing "the formal inflection point where the incumbent transformation thesis completes" and projected ~60% of BGB by exit-2026 on a +49% YoY compounding assumption. That projection is now unsupportable: at +25% YoY and -8% QoQ, share went backwards. More importantly, the Q1 note's open question — "are these revenues structurally recurring or one-off project bookings?" — is partially answered, and unfavourably: the application layer (the recurring, high-multiple part) is flat at RMB 2.5B for three straight quarters, while the growth is concentrated in capital-intensive cloud infra carrying RMB 11.4B/quarter of capex. This is not the asset-light incumbent re-architecting the Q1 note described; it is a pivot into an infrastructure business. That distinction matters for valuation and for ROIC. Not a Break because 50% of BGB genuinely is AI-powered and cloud infra still grew 50% YoY — the transformation is real, just plateaued, narrower, and far more capital-hungry than believed.
