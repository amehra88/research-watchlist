## 1. Headline read

**Confirm — three of the four verification items the June note left open resolve favorably, with the AI-cost question relocating rather than closing: it has moved off subscription gross margin and onto services.** Q4 revenue of $411.1M beat consensus by 2.1% and cleared the top of management's own $396M–$406M guide; non-GAAP EPS of $0.99 beat by 6.1%; ending ARR of $1,242M came in $5M *above* the high end of the $1,229M–$1,237M guide — the ARR beat the June note named as the specific test for moving potential investor interest off 3, though it clears the bar thinly (+0.4%). The Q3→Q4 deal slip that management called "timing, not macro" appears vindicated (26 core cloud deals in Q4 of 62 for the year, a tier-1 Nationwide cloud migration, zero challenging analyst exchanges this call versus the Q3 slip probe). Against the prior quarter's "Drift (positive)" on the AI theme, AI has now moved from adjacent product line to the CEO's lead framing. The two genuine soft spots: Q4 services gross profit went **negative** on a GAAP basis, and UnderwritingCenter — the one product the June note flagged as the next leg to watch — did not visibly advance.

## 3. Forward guidance

**FY27 guidance is the headline forward item and it is confident.** From the 8-K Ex-99.1 Business Outlook:

*FY27 (year ending 7/31/27):* Ending ARR **$1,450M–$1,460M**; subscription & support revenue $1,240M–$1,246M; total revenue **$1,707M–$1,727M**; GAAP operating income $197M–$217M; non-GAAP operating income **$403M–$423M**; operating cash flow **$445M–$465M**.

*Q1 FY27:* Ending ARR $1,253M–$1,259M; subscription & support revenue $279M–$283M; total revenue $372M–$378M; GAAP operating income $19M–$25M; non-GAAP operating income $64M–$70M.

**Magnitude and base discipline.** The FY27 ARR midpoint of $1,455M is +17.1% on the $1,242M constant-currency base and +17.6% on the $1,237M revalued base, which is the figure that actually carries into FY27; management characterized the guide as ~18% at the midpoint (InsiderScore AI summary). Either way this is a *mild deceleration* from FY26's 19%, not an acceleration. Total revenue at the midpoint implies +16.3%; non-GAAP operating income +21.5%; operating cash flow +16.8% — profit and cash growing faster than revenue, so the margin-inflection leg of the thesis is guided to continue. Subscription revenue is guided to +31% with license revenue guided *down $46M* YoY, the deliberate license-to-subscription substitution.

**Guidance philosophy unchanged** — ranged, ARR-anchored, with the same acknowledgment that a small number of large discrete deals drives quarterly volatility. Two notes of embedded conservatism worth tracking: (1) management guided FY27 gross ARR attrition to **normalize** from FY26's record-low <1.5%, an explicit self-imposed headwind; (2) the **Q1 FY27 ARR guide is below consensus**, which management attributed to FX and deal timing (AI summary). That is the second consecutive quarter in which "timing" is the explanation for a soft near-term ARR datapoint — though the Q3 version did resolve favorably. FX also cost $5M of reported ARR in the year-end revaluation, the same factor cited for the Q1 softness. Against that, the Q4 exit-rate S&S non-GAAP gross margin of ~75.7% is already at/above the FY27 75–76% guide, suggesting the margin guide is set conservatively.

## 4. Thesis read by theme

### ai_re_architected_incumbent

- **Status:** Confirm (upgraded from Q3's "Drift (positive)")
- **Evidence:** CEO Rosenbaum's lead quote in the press release (8-K Ex-99.1): *"AI is driving our momentum, as more of our insurance customers choose to align their AI transformation with Guidewire."* Per the InsiderScore AI summary: the **Qusar release** introduced developer assistants and an **agentic platform** enabling customers to build standalone AI agents; ProNavigator's AI capabilities are being embedded directly into core products, with management forecasting it becomes standard in all future implementations; **Nationwide** — a long-standing tier-1 partner — signed an all-encompassing cloud migration and adopted both PricingCenter and ProNavigator.
- **Implication:** The June note called this Drift because AI was becoming a discrete demand driver faster than the watchlist's "AI-as-feature" framing implied. A quarter later it is no longer drift — AI is the organizing frame for the platform (agentic development in the core release) and for management's own narrative. The re-architecture is being executed, not merely announced. Thesis confirmed and strengthening.

### enterprise_ai_adoption

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** Cloud ARR grew 35% YoY and now represents **84% of total ARR**; **62 core cloud deals** closed in FY26 with **26 in Q4** alone; named wins include Nationwide, AF Group, MAPFRE US and a top Canadian insurer (InsiderScore AI summary). Gross ARR attrition finished at **<1.5% overall and <1% for core-systems customers** — the lowest since GWRE began measuring ARR (CFO Cooper, 8-K Ex-99.1). Management also cited traction among **smaller carriers and MGAs**, a widening of the addressable base.
- **Implication:** Adoption is broad-based across carrier size and geography, and the sub-1% core attrition is the strongest available evidence that the installed base is not merely retained but deepening. The Q3 rebuttal to AI-budget-crowding (modern core systems as an AI prerequisite) is now supported by the numbers rather than argued. Confirms the thesis as written.

### vertical_ai_applications

- **Status:** Confirm, with one unresolved sub-item
- **Evidence:** **ProNavigator: 14 wins in Q4, 28 for the year. PricingCenter: 8 deals in Q4, 12 for the year**, with **Nationwide as its first US tier-1 customer** (InsiderScore AI summary). Both products were described as exceeding initial expectations. PricingCenter's integration with PolicyCenter was positioned against a fragmented field of established pricing tools. Cross-sell into the installed base dominates today; management expects these to drive new-logo wins over time. **UnderwritingCenter**, however, drew only qualitative commentary — Rosenbaum expressed excitement about early customer feedback and AI's potential in underwriting (Q&A, Fitzsimmons / Piper Sandler) with **no disclosed win count, GA date, or ARR contribution**.
- **Implication:** Two of three vertical AI applications are now demonstrably commercialized with disclosed deal counts, monetizing through the core-system install base exactly as the thesis predicts. The third — UnderwritingCenter, which the June note named as "the next leg to watch," expected to gain traction in FY27 — has not visibly advanced. The theme is confirmed on the strength of ProNav/PricingCenter; UnderwritingCenter remains an open FY27 verification item rather than a break.

- **Current score:** **3** per `config/watchlist.yaml` (scoring_date 2026-06-02, notes: *"Vertical SaaS; AI-as-feature, not AI-core"*). **Pending, unapplied:** the June Q3 note recommended a drift to **3+** (under review for 4); the operator has not applied it, so the YAML still reads 3.
- **Evidence from this call:** ProNavigator 28 FY wins / PricingCenter 12 FY wins with Nationwide as first US tier-1 for PricingCenter; Qusar release shipping an agentic development platform and AI assistants; ProNavigator being embedded into core products as a forecast standard in all future implementations; CEO framing AI as the driver of company momentum in the press release headline quote; AI-powered implementation tooling (Project Harness) reducing project complexity and duration.
- **Recommendation:** **drift to 3+** (i.e. apply the June recommendation, now on materially stronger evidence; keep under review for 4)
- **Reasoning:** The YAML's "AI-as-feature, not AI-core" characterization is now clearly stale and should be rewritten. But the June note set an explicit bar for a 4 — an *AI-driven re-rating of ARR* — and this print does not clear it: AI product ARR is still not separately disclosed, revenue remains cross-sell dominated rather than new-logo driven, and the FY27 ARR guide of ~18% is a mild deceleration from 19%, not a re-rating. Re-upping 3+ with much stronger evidence is the honest read, and it gives the operator one unambiguous action rather than a two-step jump built on an unapplied prior recommendation. **Condition for 4:** disclosed AI-product ARR contribution, or AI-led new-logo wins outside the existing core-system base.

## 6. Competitive advantage signal

- **Innovation rate** (current: **3**; June note recommended **3+**, not applied by operator)
  - Evidence: Qusar release with agentic development platform and developer assistants; ProNavigator and PricingCenter both scaling commercially within a year of launch; Project Harness AI implementation tooling cutting project duration; UnderwritingCenter in early customer hands. This is a materially faster cadence than the YAML's "steady cloud-platform innovation with GenAI add-ons."
  - Recommendation: **revise to 3+** — apply the June recommendation. Holding short of 4 is deliberate and consistent with §4: UnderwritingCenter, the newest product, showed no disclosed progress this quarter, so the cadence claim is not yet uniform across the portfolio.
- **Distribution** (current: **4**)
  - Evidence: 570+ insurers across **44 countries** (up from 43 at Q3); 62 core cloud deals in FY26; Nationwide tier-1 all-encompassing cloud migration; PricingCenter attaching to PolicyCenter; gross ARR attrition <1.5% overall and <1% for core customers; 1,700+ implementations and the largest P&C partner ecosystem; new push into smaller carriers and MGAs.
  - Recommendation: **hold at 4.** The sub-1% core attrition and the MGA/smaller-carrier extension are what would eventually support 4+; one quarter of commentary on the MGA channel is not yet enough.
- **Overall** (current: **4**)
  - Recommendation: **revise to 4+**
  - Reasoning: This is not the average of 3+ and 4. What changed this quarter is the *interaction*: GWRE is now monetizing genuinely new vertical AI products (ProNav, PricingCenter) almost entirely through cross-sell into a base whose core-system attrition is below 1%. That combination — a rising innovation clock feeding a distribution channel with near-zero leakage and near-zero incremental customer-acquisition cost — is worth more than either dimension standing alone, and it is why non-GAAP operating income grew 63% on 23% revenue growth. The moat is deepening, not merely holding. A **5** still requires the AI products to win materially *outside* the installed core-system base, which management explicitly frames as a future rather than present dynamic.

## 7. Potential investor interest signal

- **Current score:** **3** (notes: *"3+ trajectory: cloud-transition margin-inflection story, steady rather than AI-hot; premium valuation"*)
- **Evidence from this call:** Clean double beat (revenue +2.1%, non-GAAP EPS +6.1%) plus the ARR beat the June note specified; FY27 guide implying +21.5% non-GAAP operating income and +16.8% operating cash flow on +16.3% revenue — margin inflection guided to continue; subscription revenue guided +31%; FY26 operating cash flow +30% at a 26% CFO margin; $606.3M repurchased in FY26 reducing diluted share count 3.1% YoY. **Tempered by:** only **$31.9M remains on the buyback authorization** (a renewal is needed for the repurchase pace to continue — a potential catalyst but currently an open question); the **Q1 FY27 ARR guide is below consensus** on FX and deal timing; attrition is guided to normalize off a record low, an acknowledged FY27 headwind; FY27 ARR growth of ~18% is a mild deceleration; and Q4 services gross profit was negative on a GAAP basis. Sector heat is improving as the AI framing lands, but this remains vertical-insurance software, not front-line AI.
- **Recommendation:** **drift to 3+**
- **Reasoning (adjudicating the June test explicitly):** The June note set "a Q4 ARR beat" as the condition for moving PII from 3 to 4. The beat happened — but by $5M / +0.4% above the high end, on the constant-currency basis, and it is offset by a Q1 ARR guide *below* consensus and a decelerating FY27 ARR guide. A marginal beat paired with a soft next-quarter guide does not support a full 4. 3+ records the genuine improvement in earnings-revision direction and capital returns while leaving 4 available if the buyback is re-authorized and Q1 ARR lands above the guided range.

Explicitly not the result of any disagreement. Minor, but a CFO doubling as PAO is a modest internal-controls concentration worth noting.
