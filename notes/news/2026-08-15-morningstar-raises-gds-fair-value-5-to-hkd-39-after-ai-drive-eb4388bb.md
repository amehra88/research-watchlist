## Morningstar Raises GDS Fair Value 5% to HKD 39 After AI-Driven Guidance Upgrade

- The revision is explicitly attributed to AI-driven demand strength — not a standalone rating change.

- Suggests the post-earnings rally has fundamental support at current Morningstar estimates.
