## Samsung Narrows HBM Gap with SK Hynix as HBM4 Shipments Accelerate

A narrowing SK Hynix HBM share lead is a direct read on the premium and preferred-supplier pricing power that underpins the SK Hynix bull case — as Samsung qualifies HBM4 at scale, the moat compresses. For AI compute buyers (NVDA and hyperscalers), a competitive two-supplier HBM market is structurally favorable on cost; for SK Hynix holders, the pace of Samsung's qualification ramp is the key watch.

- Five outlets across multiple geographies confirm Samsung is closing the revenue gap with SK Hynix in the HBM market, with HBM4 shipments identified as the acceleration driver.
- SK Hynix retains the market leadership position; Samsung is the challenger gaining share.
- One outlet cites Samsung at approximately 33% of the broader memory chip market, though the precise HBM-specific share figure and the total versus HBM-only basis vary across the coverage — outlets differ on the exact scope.
- The competitive dynamic is tightening, suggesting Samsung has meaningfully recovered from earlier HBM yield and qualification setbacks.
