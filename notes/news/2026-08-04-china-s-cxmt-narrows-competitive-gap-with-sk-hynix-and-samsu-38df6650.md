## China's CXMT narrows competitive gap with SK Hynix and Samsung in smartphone DRAM segment

Chinese DRAM progress in smartphone-tier products is the historical leading indicator of eventual pressure on higher-margin segments; if CXMT achieves parity in mobile, SK Hynix and Samsung face margin compression in commodity DRAM and the story sets up a case for further US export-control escalation.

- CXMT, a Chinese memory manufacturer, is narrowing its performance and capability gap with SK Hynix and Samsung in smartphone memory.
- The segment in focus is smartphone DRAM — a lower-specification tier relative to HBM or server DRAM.
- Coverage frames this as an accelerating Chinese memory competitive threat to incumbent Korean suppliers.
- Both SK Hynix (000660.KS) and Samsung (005930.KS) are named as the benchmarked incumbents.
