## MSFT jumps 3% after hours on lower-than-expected capex and strong Azure sales

Lower-than-expected capex alongside strong Azure growth is the outcome the market most wanted from a hyperscaler this cycle — evidence that AI spend is inflecting to revenue without runaway cost growth; this is a direct positive read-through for AI infrastructure suppliers and a potential re-rating anchor for MSFT entering tomorrow's session.

- Microsoft shares rose 3% in after-hours trading following its quarterly results.
- Q4 capital expenditure came in below market expectations, directly addressing investor concern about AI spend discipline.
- Azure sales were characterized as strong, providing revenue-side validation for the AI buildout investment.
- The combination of capex below expectations and Azure growth was cited as the driver of the sentiment boost.
