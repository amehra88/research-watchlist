## Apple Launches First Foldable iPhone Duo at $1,999; Stock Reaction Muted as Wall Street Remains Underwhelmed

Apple's entry into foldables at a $1,999 price point expands its ASP ceiling and puts pressure on Samsung's premium foldable franchise, which is a read-through for Samsung's high-end revenue mix; the muted AAPL stock reaction signals the market needs sell-through evidence before re-rating on the new form factor.

- Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo, its first foldable smartphone, priced at $1,999, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro lineup.
- Stock reaction was mixed and largely flat—multiple outlets report a slight decline or no change; at least one notes a brief rise; FactSet sentiment reads Neutral.
- The analyst and investor community reaction is characterized as underwhelming relative to the launch's product significance.
- Counterpoint Research flagged that Samsung faces 'visible share pressure' from Apple's foldable entry, confirming competitive read-through.
- The iPhone Duo is described as passport-sized in form factor, differentiating it from existing Android foldables.
