## Nvidia reportedly near $14B acquisition of Hugging Face with $1B employee retention package

If confirmed, the acquisition would reposition Nvidia as a vertically integrated AI platform company — owning both the compute substrate and the dominant model-distribution/fine-tuning layer — compressing the addressable stack for pure-play AI software intermediaries. The $1B retention package signals how central human capital is to the thesis.

- Single-outlet (Seeking Alpha) report: Nvidia in advanced talks to acquire Hugging Face at a ~$14B valuation
- Deal structure reportedly includes a $1B employee retention package, signaling concern about talent flight post-close
- Would represent NVDA's deepest move into the model-hosting and ML-tooling layer, well above the silicon stack
- No official confirmation; treat as unverified until corroborated by additional outlets
