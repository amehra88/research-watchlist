## Chinese optical transceiver maker Zhongji InnoLight pursues $7 billion Hong Kong IPO targeting AI data centers

A $7B InnoLight IPO would inject a heavily capitalized Chinese rival into the AI optics market, directly threatening the margin and share trajectory of Western transceiver players; this structural competitive risk cuts against the bullish read on COHR's momentum and should be weighted accordingly.

- Zhongji InnoLight is seeking a $7 billion listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, per Indiatimes.
- The company is explicitly focused on AI data center optical connectivity — the same market served by COHR, LITE, CIEN, and CRDO.
- A listing at this scale would provide InnoLight significant capital to fund manufacturing capacity and R&D.
- The IPO represents both a China AI infrastructure demand signal and a direct competitive threat to Western optical names.
