## EU revises battery replacement rules, exempting smartphones and wearables while maintaining pressure on Kindle and Nintendo

The carve-out removes a potential forced redesign burden from Apple's core device lines, reducing near-term regulatory risk to iPhone and Watch margins. Kindle's continued exposure is a minor headwind for Amazon's hardware segment but unlikely to be material at the portfolio level.

- EU softened its battery replacement mandate, carving out smartphones and wearables from the most burdensome compliance requirements.
- Amazon's Kindle is explicitly named as facing continued compliance pressure under the revised rules.
- Nintendo is also cited as a device category still subject to the requirements.
- The exemption is a regulatory tailwind for Apple, which has faced design constraint concerns around battery replaceability in iPhones and Apple Watch.
