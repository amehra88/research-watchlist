## Unitree Robotics surges 629% on Shanghai debut, briefly hitting $66B valuation

A $66B debut valuation for a Chinese humanoid robotics company raises the competitive bar for Tesla Optimus and validates the market's pricing of the category — though the primary listing is non-investable for most Western portfolios, the competitive signal is real.

- Chinese humanoid robotics firm Unitree Robotics soared 629% on its Shanghai stock market debut.
- The company briefly reached a $66 billion valuation on listing day.
- Unitree is a direct competitor to Tesla's Optimus humanoid robot program.
- The debut signals intensifying, well-capitalized competition in the humanoid robotics category from Chinese players.
