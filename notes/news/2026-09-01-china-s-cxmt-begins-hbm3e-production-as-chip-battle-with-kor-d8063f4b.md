## China's CXMT Begins Small-Scale HBM3E Production, Widening Chip Battle With Korean Memory Leaders

Even at small scale, CXMT entering HBM3E production is a structural signal that China is closing the advanced-memory gap faster than the market has priced; if CXMT scales, the pricing power and margin premium that SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron currently hold in HBM faces longer-term erosion.

- China's CXMT has begun HBM3E production, with both outlets characterizing it as small-scale.
- The development is framed as a widening competitive battle with SK Hynix, Samsung, and implicitly Micron.
- CXMT clearing the HBM3E proof-of-concept barrier is the milestone; volume capability is not claimed.
- No yield, capacity, or performance specifications are cited in the headlines.
