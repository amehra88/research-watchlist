## TSM Record Sales Confirm AI Chip Boom as Nvidia-AMD Competition Shifts to Margin

TSM record sales validate continued AI capex acceleration, but the margin-competition framing signals the industry may be entering a phase where pricing power — not just demand — determines winners; critical input for relative-value positioning across NVDA, AMD, and TSM.

- TSMC reported record sales, providing a foundry-level confirmation that AI chip demand remains robust.
- The competitive narrative between Nvidia and AMD is shifting from volume share to margin retention.
- As the shared leading-edge foundry, TSM benefits from both NVDA and AMD ramps, but downstream margin pressure could affect customer pricing dynamics.
- Coverage implies the next leg of the AI chip cycle is less about unit growth and more about who captures the economics.
