## AWS Acquires DuckLabs and the DuckDB Analytics Engine

DuckDB is widely used for embedded and serverless analytics workloads; bringing it under AWS tightens the data-to-AI pipeline and positions Amazon more directly against Snowflake and Databricks on the analytics layer. This is a platform lock-in move that extends the AWS infrastructure software thesis.

- Amazon Web Services acquired DuckLabs, the company behind the DuckDB open-source analytics database.
- AWS framed the deal as 'building the future of analytics together,' signaling strategic intent to deepen its data stack.
- DuckDB brings an embedded, in-process analytics engine with broad developer community adoption into the AWS platform.
- Financial terms of the acquisition are not specified in the headlines.
