## Temasek Reportedly Plans First-Ever Korean Market Investment in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix

A Temasek entry into Korean memory names for the first time is a high-credibility institutional signal on HBM cycle durability; if confirmed, it adds a sovereign anchor to the Samsung/SK Hynix thesis and narrows the 'cycle-peak' discount the market has been applying to both names.

- Singapore sovereign wealth fund Temasek is reportedly planning to invest in both Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, which would mark its first-ever investment in the Korean equity market.
- The report drove a rally in both stocks and lifted the broader KOSPI index.
- Coverage originated as an exclusive from Asia Business Daily; other outlets framed it as Temasek 'considering' or 'planning' the investment — unconfirmed as of coverage time.
- FactSet sentiment is Neutral, consistent with a market-moving but unverified institutional report.
- The sovereign-fund framing signals long-duration conviction on the HBM and broader memory cycle.
