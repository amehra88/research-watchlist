## SK Hynix and SanDisk Co-Launch High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) Standard Targeting AI Inference Bottlenecks at Up to 3TB/s

A new flash-based high-bandwidth standard from SK Hynix and SanDisk could diversify the AI memory supply chain away from HBM DRAM dominance, with implications for SK Hynix's competitive positioning versus Samsung and Micron and for the cost structure of inference-at-scale deployments.

- SK Hynix and SanDisk have jointly unveiled a new High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) industry standard designed to relieve memory bottlenecks in AI inference workloads.
- The standard targets bandwidth of up to 3TB/s.
- HBF is positioned as a flash-based alternative or complement to DRAM-based HBM, addressing a different point in the memory-compute hierarchy.
- The joint standardization effort signals an industry-level push to broaden high-bandwidth memory options beyond current HBM architectures.
