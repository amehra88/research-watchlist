## Marvell Issues $12 Billion Stock Warrant to Google for Custom AI Silicon

A $12B warrant to a single hyperscaler crystallizes MRVL's concentration risk and signals how deeply Google is committing to custom silicon as AI infrastructure strategy; the equity-linked structure makes this a governance and dilution watch item for MRVL shareholders, not just a revenue line.

- Marvell granted Google a $12 billion stock warrant, a landmark commercial arrangement in the custom AI silicon space.
- The warrant structure ties MRVL equity directly to a single hyperscaler relationship, making concentration risk explicit at the balance-sheet level.
- For Google, the deal represents a material commitment to custom silicon as a strategic alternative to merchant GPU supply.
- Coverage is from a single outlet; the $12B figure and stock-warrant mechanism are both explicitly stated in the headline.
