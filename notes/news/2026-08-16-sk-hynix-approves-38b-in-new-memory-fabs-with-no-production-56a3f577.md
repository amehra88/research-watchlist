## SK Hynix approves $38B in new memory fabs with no production output before December 2028

A $38B approved fab commitment with no output before December 2028 structurally anchors memory supply tightness — and thus pricing power — for at least the next two-plus years; this is the single most bullish supply-side data point for the memory cycle thesis in today's digest, and the board approval removes execution uncertainty on the capex commitment itself.

- SK Hynix's board has approved approximately $38B in new fab construction — a board-level commitment, not a preliminary proposal.
- No production output from the approved fabs is expected before December 2028, per the headline.
- The more-than-two-year gestation period means current NAND and HBM supply tightness cannot be alleviated by this capex in the near term.
- Coverage confirmed across two outlets; figures and timeline are consistent across headlines.
