## Kioxia CEO Rules Out SK Hynix Merger and Pledges to Moderate Chip Prices

A major NAND player committing to price moderation while rejecting consolidation is a structural headwind for memory ASP recovery expectations — relevant for any thesis that bakes in a sustained memory supercycle.

- Kioxia's CEO publicly ruled out any merger with SK Hynix, removing a consolidation scenario that had been a potential overhang.
- The CEO also pledged to moderate chip prices, signaling a competitive rather than cooperative pricing posture.
- This combination — no consolidation plus price moderation commitment — is a direct negative signal for memory ASP trajectory.
