## Lutnick Warns Samsung and SK Hynix on US Semiconductor Tariffs

Tariff pressure on the two dominant HBM makers is a direct risk to AI infrastructure cost structures — hyperscalers and Nvidia's supply chain both depend on Korean memory; if tariffs materialize, sourcing could shift toward Micron or disrupt lead times across AI compute builds, with knock-on effects on data center capex timelines.

- Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has directly warned Samsung and SK Hynix regarding US semiconductor tariffs.
- The warning targets the two dominant HBM memory producers, elevating trade-policy risk to Korean memory supply.
- No specific tariff rates, timelines, or escalation mechanisms are detailed in the single-outlet headline.
