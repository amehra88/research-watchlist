## Apple Proposes 15% Fee on App Purchases Completed via External Links

Apple's attempt to apply a 15% charge to external-link transactions is a direct App Store monetization signal — whether regulators or courts accept this rate will materially affect App Store take-rate models and the antitrust risk embedded in AAPL's services revenue.

- Apple has proposed a 15% levy on purchases made through external links within apps, per Briefs Finance.
- The proposal targets a specific transaction type — sales routed outside the App Store's native payment system.
- Whether the proposal is voluntary, court-ordered, or regulatory in origin is not specified in the headline.
- The 15% rate implies Apple is attempting to preserve a significant take rate on off-platform transactions.
