## Amazon strikes up-to-$8B generator supply deal with Generac, receives warrants; GNRC surges

An $8B generator commitment signals that Amazon is locking in backup power at scale — a clear confirmation that on-site power infrastructure, not just land or permits, is the binding constraint on data center deployment timelines; positive read for any power-infrastructure or standby-generation exposure in the portfolio.

- Amazon entered a deal with Generac worth up to $8B to supply generators powering its data centers.
- Amazon also secured warrants in Generac as part of the agreement.
- GNRC stock soared on the news; one outlet cited a gain of nearly 20%.
- Deal is framed across outlets as repricing or resetting Generac's growth story.
- Transaction is direct evidence that power supply constraints remain a binding bottleneck on hyperscaler buildout pace.
