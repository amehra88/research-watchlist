## China's CXMT reports 82% EBIT margin in Q2, reportedly surpassing global memory chip leaders

State-backed Chinese DRAM at reported 82% EBIT margins raises the possibility of aggressive export pricing that could compress global memory ASPs; this is a developing structural risk for Micron's non-restricted business and for HBM pricing power across the sector, though source credibility warrants independent corroboration before acting.

- CXMT reported an 82% EBIT margin for Q2, which the headline asserts surpasses global memory chip giants — a striking profitability claim from a Chinese DRAM entrant.
- The report implies Chinese DRAM is maturing rapidly toward cost competitiveness, a direct long-term threat to Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung.
- The sole source is Global Times; independent verification of the margin figure is absent from this single-outlet cluster.
- If accurate, margins of this magnitude would imply either significant cost advantages or state subsidy support enabling aggressive future pricing room.
