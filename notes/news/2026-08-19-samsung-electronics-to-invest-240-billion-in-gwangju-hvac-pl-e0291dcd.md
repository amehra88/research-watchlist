## Samsung Electronics to Invest ₩240 Billion in Gwangju HVAC Plant Targeting AI Data Centers

Samsung entering HVAC production for AI data centers is a supply-side confirmation that thermal management has become a recognized capacity bottleneck in AI infrastructure scaling; it also expands Samsung's AI-infrastructure addressable market beyond chips, relevant to how the market assigns AI premium to the conglomerate.

- Samsung Electronics will invest ₩240 billion to build an HVAC production line in Gwangju, South Korea, explicitly targeting global AI data center customers.
- One headline identifies the line as operating under FläktGroup branding; Samsung frames it as part of a strategy to 'cultivate a global production base.'
- At least one outlet reported Samsung shares fell on the announcement day; the headline does not specify magnitude or establish causation.
- The facility is Samsung's first HVAC plant in Korea, per one headline, marking a new manufacturing category for the company.
