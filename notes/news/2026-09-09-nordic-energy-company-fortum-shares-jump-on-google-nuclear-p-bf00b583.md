## Google Signs Nuclear Power Agreement with Nordic Energy Company Fortum; Fortum Shares Jump

Each named nuclear PPA by a hyperscaler narrows the addressable pool of committed nuclear capacity and raises long-term power procurement costs for competitors — relevant both for Google's own AI infrastructure cost structure and for the broader energy procurement race among hyperscalers.

- Google entered into a nuclear power purchase agreement with Fortum, a Nordic energy company, to support AI infrastructure power needs.
- Fortum's shares jumped on the announcement, per WSJ coverage.
- The deal is part of Google's broader strategy of sourcing power via nuclear for AI data center demand.
