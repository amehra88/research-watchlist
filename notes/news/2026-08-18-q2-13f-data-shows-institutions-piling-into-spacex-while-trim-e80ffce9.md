## Q2 13F Data Shows Institutions Piling Into SpaceX While Trimming Nvidia and Broadcom

Institutional rotation out of NVDA and AVGO — the core AI capex beneficiaries — into SpaceX is a directional signal worth monitoring; if the trim trend persists into Q3 filings, it could pressure multiples on dominant AI infrastructure names even if underlying earnings remain strong.

- Q2 13F filings show SpaceX emerging as a consensus institutional favorite with zero net selling reported.
- Nvidia and Broadcom both saw heavy trims from large institutional players over the same period.
- The pattern suggests rotation out of established AI infrastructure winners and into space/next-generation assets.
- SpaceX is pre-IPO; institutional positions would be through private market vehicles or secondary shares, not public markets.
