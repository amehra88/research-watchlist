## SK Hynix Explores Exit or Stake Sale in $3 Billion Chongqing Packaging Plant

Exiting or diluting the Chongqing plant would reduce SK Hynix's China footprint and likely accelerate supply repatriation toward Korea and US-aligned geographies — a structural de-risking move that could improve the company's regulatory standing with US customers but requires navigating complex cross-border asset disposition.

- Three outlets (Korea JoongAng Daily, Korea Herald, The Standard HK) corroborate a Bloomberg-sourced report.
- Options under consideration include a full exit or partial stake sale in the Chongqing packaging facility.
- Plant is valued at approximately $3B — a material asset if disposed of or restructured.
- Framed as a China supply chain restructuring response to US-China tech tension pressure.
