## KOSPI Triggers Circuit-Breaker Halt in Asian Chip Rout; MU, SNDK, Samsung, SK Hynix, and SoftBank Retreat

A KOSPI circuit-breaker event in a tech-driven selloff is a rare, high-severity signal; portfolios with memory, HBM, or DRAM supply-chain exposure should assess whether this reflects a forward demand revision or a sentiment-driven dislocation, as the two have very different recovery timelines and portfolio implications.

- A coordinated Asian technology chip selloff triggered a circuit-breaker halt on the KOSPI.
- US-listed memory names MU and SNDK dipped overnight in sympathy.
- Samsung, SK Hynix, and SoftBank were explicitly named as declining in the same session.
- The breadth of the move — spanning US ADRs, Korean blue-chips, and a Japanese conglomerate — signals macro-level cross-asset stress, not a single-name event.
