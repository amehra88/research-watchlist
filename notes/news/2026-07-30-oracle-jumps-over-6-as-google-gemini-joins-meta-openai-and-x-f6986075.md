## Oracle Jumps Over 6% as Google Gemini Joins Meta, OpenAI, and xAI in Its Cloud AI Model Lineup

Oracle's multi-model cloud strategy positions OCI as a neutral infrastructure layer for frontier AI — a direct competitive response to Azure and AWS. For GOOG, OCI distribution extends Gemini's enterprise reach without cannibalizing Google Cloud's own customer relationships.

- Oracle stock rose over 6% after announcing an expanded Google Gemini partnership embedded across its cloud applications.
- Gemini joins Meta, OpenAI, and xAI models already on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, broadening OCI's frontier-model roster.
- Strong Microsoft cloud results were cited as an additional sector tailwind reinforcing the Oracle move.
- Headlines frame this as both a partnership and a competitive positioning win for OCI against AWS and Azure.
