## Nvidia Stock Flat as OpenAI's $105 Billion Purchase Guarantee Is Framed as Two Quarters of Cash Flow

A $105B single-customer guarantee validates NVDA's near-term backlog at an extraordinary scale, but the flat stock reaction signals the market is now interrogating whether this pace of AI capex commitment is sustainable — concentration and durability of demand are the second-order risks to monitor.

- OpenAI has reportedly committed a $105 billion purchase guarantee to Nvidia — coverage frames this as roughly equivalent to two quarters of NVDA cash flow.
- Despite the headline magnitude, NVDA shares held flat, suggesting meaningful AI demand strength was already priced in.
- The commitment is the largest single-customer AI capex signal in the coverage universe to date.
- The story raises a dual read: extraordinary near-term revenue visibility paired with elevated customer concentration risk.
