## Nvidia Guarantees Up to $105B of OpenAI Leases After Huang Signaled $30B Investment Might Be the Last

Guaranteeing $105B in OpenAI leases is a balance-sheet event, not merely a partnership signal — it raises legitimate questions about Nvidia's capital allocation discipline and concentration risk to a single counterparty, while simultaneously cementing OpenAI as structurally dependent on NVDA infrastructure for the foreseeable future.

- Nvidia has guaranteed up to $105 billion of OpenAI's lease obligations — a structurally different and larger commitment than the direct equity investment.
- Jensen Huang previously characterized the $30 billion OpenAI investment as potentially the final direct investment of its kind.
- The lease guarantee deepens Nvidia's financial entanglement with OpenAI well beyond a vendor relationship.
- The scale of commitment reinforces Nvidia's role as the foundational infrastructure layer for frontier AI development.
