## Nvidia Rolls Out Samsung-Manufactured Groq AI Chip, Boosting Samsung Foundry Path to Profit

A confirmed Nvidia foundry win gives Samsung evidence that its leading-edge process can attract AI chip production beyond captive Exynos business; for the portfolio, this is relevant to Samsung's foundry recovery thesis and signals Nvidia is actively diversifying its manufacturing supply chain.

- Nvidia is deploying an AI chip manufactured by Samsung, referred to in Korean financial press coverage as the 'Groq AI chip.'
- Samsung's foundry business is expected to move closer to profitability as this ramp proceeds.
- Two Korean outlets corroborate the production milestone, lending credibility to the report.
- The arrangement represents a meaningful foundry win for Samsung's ambitions in AI chip manufacturing.
