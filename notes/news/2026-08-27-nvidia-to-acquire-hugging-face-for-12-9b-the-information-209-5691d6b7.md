## Nvidia Reported in Advanced Talks to Acquire Hugging Face at Valuation Above $13B

If consummated, an NVDA-Hugging Face combination would extend NVDA's competitive moat from silicon into model distribution and developer tooling, raising both software margin potential and antitrust scrutiny risk — both of which are material to the long thesis.

- Multiple outlets report Nvidia is in 'serious' talks to acquire Hugging Face.
- Outlets differ slightly on the cited valuation: one reports 'more than $13B,' another cites approximately $12.9B; no deal is confirmed.
- A completed acquisition would represent a major vertical integration move into the open-source model-hosting and developer ecosystem layer.
- The talks signal NVDA's strategic intent to expand beyond hardware into the AI software stack.
- FactSet sentiment is neutral; no confirmation of a signed agreement in available headlines.
