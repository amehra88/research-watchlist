## South Korean brokerages slash Samsung and SK Hynix price targets on storage capacity peak fears

Coordinated target cuts exceeding 30% from local brokers on both leading Korean memory names is a high-conviction cycle-peak signal for NAND storage; negative read-through for any portfolio names with significant NAND revenue exposure and a potential headwind for the broader semiconductor cycle recovery thesis.

- Multiple South Korean brokerages cut price targets on both Samsung Electronics (005930.KS) and SK Hynix (000660.KS).
- The steepest cut among the brokerages exceeded 30%.
- The rationale is concern that storage capacity is peaking, implying a potential turn in the NAND demand cycle.
- Both Samsung and SK Hynix are directly named, making this a coordinated local broker downgrade sweep.
