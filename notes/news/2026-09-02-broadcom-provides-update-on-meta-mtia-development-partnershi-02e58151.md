## Broadcom Confirms Meta MTIA Partnership on Track: Three Generations by YE27, 3GW Deployment Through FY28

Explicit 3GW deployment guidance through FY28 is unusual specificity for a multi-generation chip roadmap — it anchors the Meta-Broadcom custom ASIC revenue trajectory well into the next planning cycle and validates durable hyperscaler demand for custom silicon as distinct from merchant GPU spend.

- Broadcom provided a Q3 earnings-call update confirming the Meta MTIA custom chip partnership remains on schedule.
- Three MTIA chip generations are targeted for delivery by year-end 2027.
- Broadcom stated it has line of sight to deploy 3GW of Meta AI infrastructure through FY28.
- FactSet sentiment is Neutral, consistent with confirmation of an existing roadmap rather than an upside surprise.
- This is an explicit, dated anchor for sustained multi-year custom silicon revenue from a single hyperscaler.
