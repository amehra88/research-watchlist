## WSJ higher-rates report triggers memory sector selloff; Micron, SanDisk, and Western Digital all fall sharply

A rate-sensitivity signal this sharp in memory names highlights how much of the MU bull case is a levered macro bet; if rates stay elevated, the financing assumptions underlying the memory demand cycle may be re-rated, posing downside risk to MU and the HBM thesis beyond today's move.

- A Wall Street Journal report on higher rates catalyzed a sharp decline across memory names.
- Outlets differ on exact magnitudes: one headline cites Micron -5%, SanDisk -6%, Western Digital -7%; another cites SanDisk -9%, Micron -7%, Western Digital -5% — the figures are not reconcilable from the headlines alone.
- All three companies are affected; the selloff is macro-rate-driven rather than company-specific.
- The move tests the 'memory boom' narrative, which depends in part on a favorable financing environment for AI data center buildout.
