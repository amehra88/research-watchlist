## SK Hynix Races to Restart Dalian Fab Before U.S. License Expires Amid Domestic-Buildout Pressure

The expiring U.S. license for Dalian is a hard deadline constraining SK Hynix's China capacity utilization; combined with domestic-buildout pressure from Washington, this is a capital allocation and geopolitical risk that could affect HBM supply timelines and cost structure, both directly relevant to the portfolio's HBM thesis.

- Washington is simultaneously pressuring Korean chipmakers to invest in domestic U.S. manufacturing.

- Export-control timeline pressure adds hard-deadline urgency to SK Hynix's capital allocation decisions.
