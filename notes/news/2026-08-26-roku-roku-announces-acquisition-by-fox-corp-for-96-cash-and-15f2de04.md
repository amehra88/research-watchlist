## Fox Corp to Acquire Roku at $96 Per Share in Cash and Stock

A Fox acquisition would consolidate streaming distribution and ad inventory under a single media owner, removing Roku as an independent platform and reshaping the connected-TV ad market; for any ROKU position the question shifts immediately to deal certainty, regulatory clearance, and whether $96 fully values the ad platform.

- Fox Corp is acquiring Roku (ROKU) at $96 per share in a combination of cash and shares, per Yahoo Finance.
- The deal would bring a leading streaming platform under traditional media ownership.
- Roku's connected-TV ad inventory and platform infrastructure would become Fox assets.
- At the stated price, the transaction represents a defined premium realization event for ROKU shareholders.
