## Spot Memory Prices at Four Times Contract Levels — Analysts See Mid-Cycle, Not Peak

A four-times spot-contract spread is a supply-tightness signal bulls will use to argue the upcycle has room to run; the key follow-on question is whether contract prices reprice upward to close the gap — that inflection is what would drive the next round of earnings estimate revisions for MU.

- Spot memory prices are currently running at four times contract prices, a historically wide spread.
- Coverage argues this spread is characteristic of mid-cycle tightness, not cycle exhaustion.
- A wide spot-to-contract premium typically reflects near-term supply constraints relative to longer-dated commitments.
- MU, SK Hynix, and Samsung are the primary public beneficiaries of the pricing dynamic.
