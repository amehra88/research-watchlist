## Chinese Memory Maker CXMT Posts 10-Fold Revenue Surge Amid AI-Driven Memory Shortage

If the CXMT revenue ramp reflects genuine capacity and yield maturation rather than a low-base effect, it marks an inflection in China's memory self-sufficiency that pressures the long-run pricing power of MU, SK Hynix, and Samsung and begins to complicate the HBM supply-scarcity narrative underpinning several semi bull cases.

- CXMT, a Chinese domestic memory manufacturer, reported a roughly 10-fold revenue increase, per a Korean-language source.
- The surge is attributed to AI-driven demand during a period of global memory supply tightness.
- A revenue ramp of this magnitude signals meaningful scaling of Chinese domestic memory capacity.
- Direct competitive read-through to Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung across DRAM and potentially HBM markets.
- Single Korean-language outlet (Chosun Ilbo); Western confirmation pending, but the revenue magnitude is significant if accurate.
