## Deutsche Bank Previews WMT Comp-Sales Miss, Sees TGT Poised to Beat

The simultaneous TGT beat call muddies the macro message — suggesting idiosyncratic execution factors rather than outright demand weakness, which limits the read-through.

- The same call sees Target positioned to beat comparable-sales estimates.
