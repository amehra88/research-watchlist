## Trump Administration Reportedly Considering Sweeping New Tariffs on Semiconductor Chips

Sweeping chip tariffs would be a broad-based sector headwind, raising input costs for domestic OEMs and disrupting global supply chains across nearly every semiconductor holding; the policy-risk overhang alone may weigh on valuations until the scope is clarified.

- Politico reports the Trump administration is actively considering broad new chip tariffs.
- No final policy has been announced; coverage is at the 'considering' stage.
- The potential scope is described as sweeping, implicating the broad semiconductor supply chain.
- Implicated names span chip designers, foundries, and equipment makers across the sector.
- Immediate supply-chain disruption and pricing effects would follow if policy is enacted.
