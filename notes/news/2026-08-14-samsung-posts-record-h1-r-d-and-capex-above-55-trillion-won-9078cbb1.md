## Samsung posts record H1 R&D and capex above 55 trillion won in AI chip push

Samsung's record H1 spend signals the HBM competitive race is escalating; for SK Hynix and Micron this means rising competitive intensity in AI memory, while for AI infrastructure customers it is a positive demand-confirmation signal for continued memory supply expansion.

- Samsung achieved a record combined R&D and capital expenditure level in the first half, exceeding 55 trillion won.
- The spending is directed toward AI chip development, including advanced memory.
- This signals Samsung is intensifying its commitment to compete in HBM and next-generation AI semiconductor markets.
- No breakdown between R&D and capex, or between memory and logic spend, is provided in the headline.
