## SK Hynix and Intel in talks to manufacture AI memory chips at Ohio facility

US onshoring of AI memory production would reshape HBM supply-chain geography and could give Intel's foundry ambitions a high-profile anchor customer; if consummated, it directly affects SK Hynix capacity allocation and the competitive position of Micron as the primary US-based memory incumbent.

- SK Hynix and Intel are in active discussions — not a signed deal — to co-produce AI memory chips at a US facility in Ohio.
- The talks carry a reshoring dimension, manufacturing AI memory domestically rather than in Korea or Taiwan.
- Both stocks reportedly moved sharply higher on the news, suggesting the market is pricing material upside if talks close.
- Headlines do not specify which memory type (HBM vs. standard DRAM) would be produced, nor deal economics.
