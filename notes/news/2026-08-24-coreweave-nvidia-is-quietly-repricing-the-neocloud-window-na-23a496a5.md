## Seeking Alpha Thesis: Nvidia Quietly Repricing GPU Access Is Structurally Narrowing CoreWeave's Margin Window

If Nvidia is repricing GPU supply upward as neoclouds scale, the entire neocloud margin model is under structural pressure — a direct risk to CRWV's unit economics and a second-order margin benefit to NVDA that the market may not have fully priced into either name.

- A Seeking Alpha analysis argues Nvidia is 'quietly repricing' GPU access in a manner that structurally compresses the margin window for neocloud operators, with CoreWeave named as the primary subject.
- The 'quietly' framing suggests the repricing is not being announced prominently but is visible in contract terms or cost structures — an asymmetric information risk for neocloud investors.
- The thesis frames NVDA's supplier pricing power as being exercised at the direct expense of GPU-as-a-service operators' unit economics.
