## Temasek Reportedly Plans First-Ever Investment in Korean Stock Market via Samsung and SK Hynix

Temasek's reported entry provides sovereign-fund validation of the HBM/memory cycle thesis at a moment when peak-cycle fears are also circulating; if confirmed, it signals that a sophisticated long-horizon allocator sees value in Korean memory at current levels, which materially improves near-term sentiment and could attract further institutional interest.

- Multiple outlets across Korea, Singapore, and global financial press report Singapore sovereign wealth fund Temasek is considering or initiating positions in both Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.
- One outlet frames this as Temasek's first investment in the Korean stock market — a notable framing if accurate, though it is sourced to a single exclusive.
- Both Korean memory stocks rallied materially on the report, lifting the broader KOSPI.
- FactSet sentiment is Positive, consistent with the market's reaction to large institutional validation of the Korean memory complex.
