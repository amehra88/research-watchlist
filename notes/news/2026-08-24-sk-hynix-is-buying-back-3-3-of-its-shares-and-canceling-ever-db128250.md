## SK Hynix Announces Buyback and Full Cancellation of 3.3% of Shares Outstanding

Outright share cancellation at this scale from the HBM cycle's dominant name signals management conviction that the current demand environment is structurally durable — a direct read-through for HBM pricing sustainability and a positive data point for the broader memory sector thesis.

- SK Hynix announced a share repurchase covering 3.3% of shares outstanding, with all repurchased shares to be cancelled rather than held as treasury stock.
- Full cancellation — as opposed to a standard buyback — is an unusually strong capital-return signal, reducing the share count on a permanent basis.
- The announcement comes from the leading HBM supplier, amplifying its read as a management confidence statement on the durability of the memory demand cycle.
- Three outlets cover the story with consistent detail on the 3.3% cancellation structure, providing high confidence on the fact.
