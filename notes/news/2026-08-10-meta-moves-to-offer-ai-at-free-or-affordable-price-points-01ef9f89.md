## Meta Moves to Offer AI at Free or Affordable Price Points

Meta pricing AI at or near zero compresses the margin outlook for paid AI services across the ecosystem; this is a direct competitive challenge to OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft Copilot-style offerings — a PM with AI software or foundation-model exposure should assess whether this reprices the winners set.

- Meta is pursuing a strategy of making its AI available at free or low-cost pricing.
