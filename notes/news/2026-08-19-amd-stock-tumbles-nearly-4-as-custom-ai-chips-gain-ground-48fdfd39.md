## AMD Shares Fall ~4% as Custom AI Chips Cited as Gaining Competitive Ground

Each episode of the market pricing custom-ASIC share shift into AMD is a test of how durable AMD's AI revenue thesis is; a PM long AMD should track whether these moves reflect new customer decisions or are sentiment-driven rotations with no fundamental data behind them.

- AMD stock fell approximately 4% in the reported session.
- The decline is attributed to custom AI chips gaining ground — pointing to share-shift risk toward merchant ASICs or hyperscaler-designed silicon.
- Coverage frames this as a competitive architecture event, not a macro or cyclical driver.
- The move reinforces the recurring overhang that AMD's AI GPU share is contestable by the custom silicon build-out.
