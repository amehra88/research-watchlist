## AMD Shares Rise ~5% After Server-CPU TAM Outlook Updated to $201 Billion

A $201B server-CPU TAM with AMD positioned as the primary share-gainer is a direct earnings growth catalyst; the ~5% single-day move suggests the market is pricing in accelerating Epyc penetration and reinforces AMD as a multi-leg AI/datacenter story beyond GPU accelerators.

- AMD shares rose approximately 4.9% following a server-CPU market update.
- The total addressable market forecast for server CPUs grew by roughly $37B to reach $201B, per the headlines.
- The updated TAM signals continued Intel market-share displacement in the datacenter server CPU segment.
- AMD's Epyc processor franchise is the primary driver behind the expanded estimate.
