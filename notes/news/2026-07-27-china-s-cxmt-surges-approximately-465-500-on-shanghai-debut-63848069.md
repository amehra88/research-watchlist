## China's CXMT surges approximately 465-500% on Shanghai debut, eclipsing Intel in market value

A debut market cap above Intel signals strong domestic investor conviction in CXMT as a credible memory challenger, with medium-term implications for MU and SK Hynix pricing power in DRAM and eventually HBM. This is simultaneously a competitive and geopolitical signal — accelerating China's memory independence thesis and a watch item for future export-control escalation.

- CXMT, a Chinese DRAM maker, surged approximately 465% on its Shanghai debut per one outlet and 'almost 500%' per another — outlets differ slightly on the exact figure.
- The listing propelled CXMT's market capitalization above Intel's, per both headlines.
- The debut marks a significant milestone for Chinese domestic memory chip capacity and reinforces chip-sovereignty ambitions.
- CXMT is a direct competitive threat to MU, SK Hynix, and Samsung in DRAM and potentially HBM over the medium term.
