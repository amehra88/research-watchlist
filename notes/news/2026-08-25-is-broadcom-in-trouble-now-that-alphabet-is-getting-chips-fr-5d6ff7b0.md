## Alphabet Adds Marvell as Custom Silicon Supplier Alongside Broadcom

Alphabet multi-sourcing custom silicon is a structural check on Broadcom's pricing power with its most important customer; for MRVL holders it confirms a second major hyperscaler ramp and validates the custom silicon diversification thesis at the portfolio level.

- Alphabet is sourcing custom chips from Marvell in addition to its existing Broadcom relationship, introducing a second hyperscaler silicon vendor.
- Coverage frames this as a potential threat to Broadcom's hyperscaler revenue concentration with its key customer.
- No volume, contract value, program timeline, or chip generation details are specified.
- The development is incrementally positive for MRVL's hyperscaler exposure and raises vendor concentration risk for AVGO.
