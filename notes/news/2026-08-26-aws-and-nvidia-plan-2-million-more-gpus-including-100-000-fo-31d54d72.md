## AWS and NVIDIA Plan Joint Deployment of 2 Million GPUs, Including 100,000 for U.S. Government AI

A 2M GPU joint plan with a discrete government tranche is the clearest public validation yet that sovereign AI demand is structurally additive, directly extending NVDA's revenue visibility beyond commercial hyperscalers. For a PM holding either name, this reduces the concentration risk of the thesis being dependent on a single demand channel.

- AWS and NVIDIA announced plans for a joint deployment of 2 million additional GPUs.
- 100,000 of those GPUs are specifically earmarked for U.S. government AI workloads, confirming sovereign AI as an incremental demand source.
- The headline establishes sovereign AI as additive to — not cannibalizing — commercial hyperscaler GPU demand.
- The partnership signals deep AWS-NVIDIA infrastructure integration at the compute layer.
