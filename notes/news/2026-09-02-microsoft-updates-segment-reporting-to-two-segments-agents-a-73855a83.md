## Microsoft Restructures Into Two Segments — Agents and Infra/Devices and Consumer — and Reaffirms Outlook

Creating a discrete 'Agents' reporting segment is more than a branding move — it will make AI agent monetization a measurable, reportable line item for the first time, increasing Microsoft's value as a benchmark for the enterprise agentic AI adoption rate and creating direct comparability pressure on peers.

- Microsoft filed an 8-K announcing a reorganization into two new reporting segments: 'Agents' and 'Infra/Devices and Consumer.'
- The company reaffirmed its prior financial outlook alongside the structural change.
- The renaming formalizes an agent-centric organizational framework at the reporting level.
- FactSet sentiment is Neutral, consistent with a structural disclosure rather than an earnings event.
