## NVIDIA Preparing Nemotron 4, a Large Open-Weight Model to Expand Its AI Software Lineup

NVDA entering the open-weight model space reinforces its ambition to own the full AI stack — hardware, software, and models; if developers anchor to NVDA's model ecosystem, it deepens the compute pull-through moat and raises switching costs well beyond the GPU layer.

- NVIDIA is readying Nemotron 4, described in coverage as a 'giant' open-weight AI model.
- Move extends NVDA's competitive presence beyond silicon into the AI software and model layer.
- Open-weight positioning parallels Meta's strategy and signals NVDA is competing for developer ecosystem share at the model level.
- Contributes to the open-source model commoditization dynamic already pressuring closed-model vendors.
