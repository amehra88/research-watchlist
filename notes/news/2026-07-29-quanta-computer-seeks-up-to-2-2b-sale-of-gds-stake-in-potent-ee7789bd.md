## Quanta Computer Seeks Up to $2.2B Sale of GDS Stake in Potential Record Taiwan Transaction

A major shareholder exiting China's largest independent data center operator at record scale raises pointed questions about the risk-return profile of China AI infrastructure—whether this reflects Quanta's capital reallocation strategy or a deteriorating view on GDS's terminal value is the key question for the theme.

- Quanta Computer is seeking to sell its stake in GDS Holdings for up to $2.2 billion.
- Both outlets confirm the $2.2 billion figure; one describes it as a potential record-sized Taiwan stake sale.
- GDS is a major Chinese data center operator; Quanta's exit signals a strategic or risk-management decision.
- The scale and framing of coverage suggests this is a deliberate, not distressed, exit.
