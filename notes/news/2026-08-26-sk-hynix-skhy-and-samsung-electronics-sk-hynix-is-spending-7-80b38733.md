## SK Hynix Commits $720 Billion to Memory Chip Expansion Despite 21% Stock Decline

SK Hynix committing massive capital against a 21% stock decline is a high-conviction signal that management views AI memory demand as structural — if they are right, this capex secures HBM supply that AI compute demand will eventually absorb. For a PM with HBM read-through exposure (NVDA, AMD), this de-risks the supply side of the memory equation even as equity markets price skepticism.

- SK Hynix is spending $720 billion on memory chip capacity, a multi-year capital commitment.
- The stock has already fallen 21% despite — or alongside — this commitment, creating a divergence between management conviction and market pricing.
- The scale of investment signals high management conviction on HBM and AI memory demand as structural, not cyclical.
- The headline pairs the capex announcement with the stock decline as a tension rather than a resolved narrative.
