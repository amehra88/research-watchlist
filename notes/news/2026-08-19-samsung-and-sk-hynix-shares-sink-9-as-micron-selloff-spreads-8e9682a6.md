## Samsung and SK Hynix shares sink ~9% as Micron selloff spreads to South Korean memory sector

Contagion from MU to the Korean memory majors at this magnitude signals the market is repricing the memory cycle broadly; any long thesis anchored to HBM durability — directly or via NVDA's supply chain — faces a sector-level air pocket that warrants reassessment of near-term timing.

- Samsung (005930.KS) and SK Hynix (000660.KS) each fell approximately 9%.
- Decline is attributed to contagion from a Micron selloff rather than company-specific news.
- Cross-market spread suggests the move reflects broader memory-cycle concerns, not idiosyncratic MU risk.
- The sell-off spans the full HBM competitive landscape: MU, Samsung, and SK Hynix all affected.
