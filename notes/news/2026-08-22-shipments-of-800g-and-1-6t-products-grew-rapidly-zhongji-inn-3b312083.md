## Innolight Posts 241% Profit Growth in First Half on Surging 800G and 1.6T Optical Module Demand

Innolight's 241% first-half profit surge on 800G/1.6T volumes is a direct positive volume signal for COHR and LITE as competing Western suppliers — but the same data point also confirms that Chinese manufacturers are capturing significant share of the demand surge, a pricing-power risk the headline leaves unresolved.

- Zhongji Innolight (300308.SZ), a major Chinese optical transceiver manufacturer, reported a 241.7% year-on-year increase in first-half net profit attributable to shareholders — figures are RMB-denominated for a Chinese-listed company.
- The profit surge is driven by rapid growth in 800G and 1.6T optical product shipments, confirming explosive volume demand at the next-generation transceiver tier.
- Innolight is a T4 ecosystem name tracked specifically because its results read through to COHR and LITE, which compete in the same 800G/1.6T segment.
- Triple-digit profit growth at a major volume supplier confirms that next-gen transceiver demand is real, not deferred.
- Chinese manufacturers' scale and cost position in this segment also raise the question of whether Western vendors' pricing power is under concurrent pressure.
