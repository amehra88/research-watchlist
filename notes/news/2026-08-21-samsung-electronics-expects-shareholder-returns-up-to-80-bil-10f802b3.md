## Samsung Electronics Plans Record Shareholder Return of Up to ~110 Trillion Won (~$80B) in 2026, Driven by AI Chip Profits

A ~$80B capital return from Samsung — the world's largest memory maker — is a definitive signal that AI-driven HBM profitability has reached a scale where the company is comfortable committing extraordinary cash to shareholders; for tech PMs, this validates the AI memory supercycle thesis and raises the bar on capital discipline for the semiconductor sector as a whole.

- Samsung announced a shareholder return plan for 2026 that the overwhelming majority of 22 outlets describe as 'up to 110 trillion won' — equivalent to roughly $79-80B USD at current rates; a small number of outlets cite slightly different ranges, but the upper bound is consistently 110T won.
- The record return is explicitly attributed to profits from AI chip demand, pointing to HBM and advanced DRAM as the primary profit drivers.
- The plan is described as roughly seven times Samsung's 10-year historical average for shareholder returns, per one outlet.
- Samsung shares rallied on the announcement in Korean trading.
