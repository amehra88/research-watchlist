## Memory Crunch Drives Nvidia AI Server Price Hikes, Lifting Samsung and SK Hynix Pricing Power

If confirmed, Nvidia passing memory-driven cost increases to customers compresses hyperscaler and neocloud unit economics while handing durable pricing power to HBM-capable suppliers — a key variable for any thesis that is simultaneously long memory and long Nvidia.

- A memory supply crunch is forcing Nvidia to raise AI server prices, per two Korean financial outlets.
- Samsung and SK Hynix are cited as direct beneficiaries, gaining pricing leverage as HBM suppliers into the constrained supply chain.
- The dynamic links memory scarcity to downstream AI server cost inflation — a second-order read for hyperscaler capex budgets.
- Coverage originates from Korean financial press only; the Nvidia pricing action is not yet confirmed by Western outlets, which is worth flagging before treating as established fact.
