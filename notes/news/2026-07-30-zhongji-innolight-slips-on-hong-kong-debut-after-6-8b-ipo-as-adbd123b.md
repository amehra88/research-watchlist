## Zhongji Innolight Slips on Hong Kong Debut After $6.8B IPO as AI Sentiment Cools

A stumbling debut for the HK market's largest 2026 IPO — a pure-play China AI datacenter supplier — is a sentiment barometer for the entire China AI infrastructure build thesis; if the IPO market is reluctant to price AI optical aggressively, it raises questions about valuation support across the broader China hyperscaler supply chain.

- Zhongji Innolight raised $6.8 billion in what headlines call Hong Kong's largest share sale this year — and the biggest in the city in roughly seven years.
- Shares fell on debut; outlets cite varying declines ranging from roughly -1% to -2.86%, with no single confirmed figure across coverage.
- Coverage frames the weak open against a broader 'AI selloff,' suggesting macro sentiment rather than company-specific news drove the stumble.
- The company announced a share buyback ahead of the listing, which headlines say eased pre-debut jitters but did not prevent the opening-day decline.
