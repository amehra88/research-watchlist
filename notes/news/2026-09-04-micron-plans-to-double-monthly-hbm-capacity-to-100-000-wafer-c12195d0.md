## Micron plans to double HBM wafer output to 100,000 per month to close gap with Samsung and SK Hynix

A doubling of MU's HBM output increases competitive pressure on SK Hynix's near-monopoly positioning and complicates Samsung's recovery narrative. For MU longs it signals execution commitment; for the sector, a supply ramp of this scale could pressure HBM ASPs in 2027 if demand growth moderates — a risk to monitor as capacity comes online.

- Micron is targeting 100,000 HBM wafers per month — a doubling of current output — explicitly framed as a catch-up move against Samsung and SK Hynix.
- The capacity expansion signals aggressive investment in HBM, the highest-ASP memory segment tied to AI compute.
- No timeline for the ramp or associated capital expenditure figures are given in the headline.
- A supply-side expansion of this scale has medium-term pricing implications for the HBM market.
