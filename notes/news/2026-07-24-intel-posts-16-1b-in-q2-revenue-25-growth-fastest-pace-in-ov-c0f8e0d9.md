## Intel Posts $16.1B in Q2 Revenue, 25% Growth — Fastest Pace in Over 15 Years — on AI Data-Center CPU Demand

Intel's first meaningful AI-driven revenue reacceleration in over a decade validates that data-center compute spend is broadening beyond GPUs into the CPU layer — a positive read-through for the wider semiconductor capex cycle and a direct fundamental catalyst for INTC re-rating.

- Intel reported Q2 revenue of $16.1B, up 25% year-over-year, the fastest growth rate in more than 15 years (one outlet dates the comparison to 2011).
- AI data-center CPU demand is cited across all outlets as the primary driver; at least one headline characterizes the demand environment as 'unprecedented.'
- Results exceeded consensus expectations; Intel's stock jumped on the print.
- The beat is broadly attributed to structural AI infrastructure spending rather than a one-time effect.
