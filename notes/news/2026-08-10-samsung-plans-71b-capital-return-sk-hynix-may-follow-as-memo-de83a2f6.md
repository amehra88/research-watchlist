## Samsung Plans $71B Capital Return; SK Hynix May Follow as Memory Chipmakers Signal Cycle Confidence

Capital return expansion at Samsung and SK Hynix signals memory-cycle conviction rather than defensive capex cuts — a second-order positive for HBM-exposed names in the watchlist (MU, NVDA demand chain) and validates the upcycle thesis.

- Samsung Electronics announced a target of $71B in capital returns, signaling confidence in the demand outlook.
- SK Hynix is reportedly considering a similar move, suggesting sector-wide balance sheet conviction.
- Capital return expansion at the two leading memory suppliers reinforces a positive semiconductor cycle read.
- Both Samsung and SK Hynix are primary HBM suppliers, making their capital allocation posture a meaningful cycle signal.
