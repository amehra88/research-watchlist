## Chinese Optical Transceiver Maker Zhongji Innolight to Acquire ~10-11% Stake in Power Filter Maker Jones Tech for ~¥1.75 Billion

Vertical integration moves within China's AI networking supply chain — optical transceivers acquiring into power components — indicate intensifying domestic AI infrastructure investment; this could affect competitive pricing and supply dynamics for non-Chinese networking and photonics suppliers.

- Zhongji Innolight, a Chinese optical transceiver manufacturer, is proposing to acquire approximately 10-11% of Jones Tech, a power filter maker, for approximately 1.75 billion yuan (outlets report slightly differing stake percentages: 10.47% vs. 11%)
- Jones Tech shares hit limit-up on the announcement
- The deal signals vertical integration and consolidation within China's AI photonics and power supply chain
- The acquirer's optical networking positioning links this to China's domestic AI infrastructure buildout
