## GE Vernova and Korea's LS Electric Form Joint Venture Targeting HVDC Transmission Market

Power delivery — not compute or real estate — is increasingly the binding constraint on AI datacenter expansion; a GEV-LS Electric HVDC JV is a direct response to that bottleneck and signals GEV is positioning itself as a critical infrastructure enabler. For a PM tracking the datacenter build theme, this expands GEV's addressable market and thesis surface area.

- GE Vernova (GEV) and South Korea's LS Electric announced a joint venture targeting the high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission market.
- HVDC is a critical enabler for long-distance, high-efficiency power delivery, including to large-scale AI data centers.
- The JV pairs GEV's grid technology with LS Electric's manufacturing capabilities and regional footprint.
- The move is a concrete strategic step in addressing power delivery constraints that are pacing the datacenter buildout.
