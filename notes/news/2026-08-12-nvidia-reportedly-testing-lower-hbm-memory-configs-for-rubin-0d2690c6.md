## Nvidia reportedly testing lower HBM memory configs for Rubin Ultra as HBM shortage constrains design

HBM supply constraints forcing flagship GPU configuration compromises signal a potential bottleneck in the AI compute supply chain; reduced memory per chip could dampen AI training throughput and create demand-side disappointment even against a backdrop of strong AI capex commitments.

- NVDA is reportedly exploring reduced HBM memory configurations for its Rubin Ultra GPU
- Configurations tested reportedly include as little as 192 GB and represent a step back to HBM4
- Memory shortage is cited as the driver forcing design flexibility
- SK Hynix and Micron are implicated as HBM suppliers with read-through supply implications
- Headline was subsequently updated, suggesting the situation is evolving
