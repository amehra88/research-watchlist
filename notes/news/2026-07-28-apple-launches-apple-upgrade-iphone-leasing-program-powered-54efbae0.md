## Apple launches 'Apple Upgrade' iPhone leasing program powered by Klarna, starting at $17.99/month

A lease/subscription wrapper on hardware is a take-rate expansion play: Apple monetizes upgrade cadence and customer lock-in beyond App Store commissions. The neutral FactSet read suggests the market treats this as modest near-term, but directionally it extends Apple's shift toward services-like recurring economics on device revenue.

- Apple has introduced a new product leasing program called 'Apple Upgrade,' powered by Klarna, starting at $17.99 per month — a price point confirmed across multiple outlet headlines.
- The program covers iPhone and at least one additional Apple product line, per one headline.
- FactSet sentiment is neutral, indicating the market views this as an incremental monetization extension rather than a major catalyst.
- The structure uses a BNPL/lease model to lower upfront device cost, with Klarna bearing credit risk and Apple capturing recurring revenue.
