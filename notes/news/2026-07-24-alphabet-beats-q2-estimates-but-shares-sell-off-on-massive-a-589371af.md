## Alphabet Beats Q2 Estimates but Shares Sell Off on Massive AI Capital Spending Disclosure

Alphabet's willingness to accelerate AI capex even at the cost of an immediate stock selloff signals that hyperscaler spending commitments are intensifying — constructive for AI infrastructure demand but a near-term multiple headwind for mega-cap tech broadly.

- Alphabet's Q2 results topped analyst estimates on the top and bottom lines.
- Despite the earnings beat, the stock fell sharply as investors reacted to disclosed AI capital spending plans described as 'massive.'
- Headlines frame the sell-off as a reaction to the scale of forward AI infrastructure investment rather than operational underperformance.
- The capex overhang narrative dominated coverage, overshadowing the beat entirely.
