## Coatue and Laffont Report Over 7% Beneficial Stake in Cerebras Systems

Coatue's dual same-day positioning in both an AI chip company (CBRS) and an AI power company (FPS) implies they are expressing a view that AI infrastructure scarcity is compute- and power-constrained simultaneously — a thesis frame worth internalizing when building out infrastructure coverage.

- Coatue Management and Philippe Laffont reported beneficial ownership exceeding 7% in CBRS.
- This is the second major institutional CBRS filing on the same day — Altimeter separately disclosed 6.2%.
- Combined, two high-profile AI-focused funds hold substantial, named positions in Cerebras.
- Coatue's simultaneous filing in FPS (power infrastructure) suggests a coordinated AI-infrastructure portfolio thesis.
