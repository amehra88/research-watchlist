## South Korea to establish dedicated fund for domestic semiconductor materials, components, and equipment

Industrial policy of this type is a secular tailwind for Korean semiconductor self-sufficiency — it reduces cost exposure to geopolitical supply disruptions and can lower input cost volatility for Samsung and SK Hynix over time. The practical impact depends on fund scale and deployment speed, which are not yet disclosed.

- South Korean government is setting up a fund targeting domestic semiconductor materials, parts, and equipment supply chains.
- Policy is directed at reducing reliance on foreign sourcing and strengthening the domestic supply ecosystem.
- Primary beneficiaries would include Korean chipmakers (Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix) and their domestic vendor base.
- No fund size or timeline is specified in the Reuters headline.
