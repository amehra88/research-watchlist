- South Korean workers at SK Hynix formed a new, consolidated union.
- The formation follows a breakdown or stall in wage talks.
- Labor action risk is the key concern, with potential implications for production continuity.
- HBM supply chain is the most relevant downstream risk given SK Hynix's leading market position.
