## TSMC Developing 'EMIB-Like' Advanced Packaging to Counter Intel; Shares Surge ~7.6%

If TSMC successfully replicates EMIB-like packaging at scale, it further erodes Intel's foundry differentiation while cementing TSMC as the full-stack advanced manufacturing leader — structurally negative for INTC's moat and positive for TSM's pricing power and AI chip supply chain dominance.

- TSMC is developing advanced packaging technology described as 'EMIB-like,' directly targeting a packaging capability area where Intel has been differentiated.
- Shares surged approximately 7.6% on the news, a notable single-day move for a large-cap foundry.
- The development signals TSMC is competing with Intel not just on process nodes but on advanced multi-die interconnect packaging, which is increasingly important for AI chip density.
- Intel's packaging roadmap is directly implicated: TSMC entering this space increases competitive pressure on one of Intel's remaining differentiation levers.
