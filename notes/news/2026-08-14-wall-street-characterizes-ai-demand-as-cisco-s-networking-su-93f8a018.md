## Wall Street Characterizes AI Demand as Cisco's 'Networking Supercycle,' Sees Further Upside

Sell-side consensus coalescing around a networking supercycle narrative signals that AI capex is increasingly being recognized as flowing to networking, not just compute silicon — relevant for PMs underweight on infrastructure plays relative to semiconductor AI exposure.

- Wall Street analysts are framing AI-driven networking demand as a 'supercycle' opportunity for Cisco.
- Sell-side coverage indicates expectations of further upside in CSCO stock.
- AI infrastructure capex is positioned as a durable rather than transient demand driver for networking.
- This frames Cisco as a beneficiary of AI buildout investment beyond its traditional enterprise base.
