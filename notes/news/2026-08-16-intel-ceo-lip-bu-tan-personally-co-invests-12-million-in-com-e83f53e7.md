## Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan personally co-invests $12 million in company equity raise

CEO personal co-investment directly addresses the shareholder dilution concern inherent in any equity raise; skin-in-the-game alignment reduces one key bear argument, though execution on the AI strategy remains unresolved.

- CEO Lip-Bu Tan is investing $12 million of personal capital into the same offering Intel is conducting for external investors.
- Analyst Daniel Newman publicly describes the move as a 'strong sign of confidence' in Intel's future.
- Insider co-investment alongside a dilutive raise is a materially different signal than a standard secondary offering.
- Note: a second outlet (see cluster 87d800b42e0163e1) reports the figure as $10 million — exact amount differs across sources.
