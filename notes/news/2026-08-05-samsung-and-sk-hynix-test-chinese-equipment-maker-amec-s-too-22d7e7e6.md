## Samsung and SK Hynix Test Chinese Equipment Maker AMEC's Tools in China Fabs as Hedge Against US Export Risks

If Samsung and SK Hynix qualify Chinese AMEC tools for production use, it validates domestic Chinese equipment as a credible partial substitute for US-export-controlled gear — a structural negative for Western equipment makers and a signal that the export-controls moat is being gradually eroded at the operational level.

- Both Samsung and SK Hynix are reportedly evaluating AMEC chip-making equipment specifically for use in their China-based fabrication facilities.
- The testing is characterized as a deliberate hedge against potential tightening of US export controls on semiconductor equipment.
- The story is a Reuters exclusive, lending it elevated credibility relative to typical secondary coverage.
- The move signals Korean memory leaders are actively building contingency domestic-equipment supply chains for their China operations.
