## Chinese Optical Supplier InnoLight Seeks $7 Billion Hong Kong IPO Targeting AI Data Centers

A $7 billion Chinese IPO explicitly targeting AI data center optics intensifies competitive pressure on COHR and LITE in their highest-growth end market; if InnoLight captures hyperscaler spend in China and eventually pushes into global markets, it compresses both the addressable market and pricing power of Western optical incumbents — a material risk to the AI optical networking re-rating thesis.

- Zhongji InnoLight is pursuing a listing on the Hong Kong stock exchange targeting a $7 billion valuation, per the headline.
- The company's stated strategic focus is AI data center optical supply.
- The IPO scale signals a well-funded Chinese competitor entering — or expanding in — the AI optical networking market.
