## CoreWeave CEO confirms 2020-era NVIDIA GPU bookings extend through 2029 at full price

Locked GPU demand at full price through 2029 is among the strongest forward revenue signals NVDA could receive from an operator; it implies pricing power will persist well past the current Blackwell cycle. Second-order read: if operators are absorbing legacy SKUs for years out, next-gen GPU supply relief may not compress margins as quickly as bears assume.

- CoreWeave CEO stated the company is booking older-generation NVIDIA GPUs through 2029 at full, unreduced pricing.
- The disclosure is a locked, multi-year demand signal for NVDA hardware well beyond the current product generation.
- Suggests AI infrastructure buildout remains supply-constrained enough that operators are not waiting for next-gen silicon.
- Provides unusual revenue-visibility data for NVDA and validates CRWV's long-dated capacity expansion thesis.
