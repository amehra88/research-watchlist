## SK Hynix Explores Bringing in Outside Investor for $3 Billion Chongqing Plant

- SK Hynix is considering roping in an external investor for its Chongqing manufacturing plant, with the plant valued at approximately $3 billion per the headline.
- Consistent with a broader pattern of Korean chipmakers restructuring China exposure under export-control pressure.
- High-confidence signal; strategic asset management with explicit geopolitical overtones.
- An outside investor could provide capital relief while reducing SK Hynix's direct China operational footprint.
