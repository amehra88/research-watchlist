## Marvell Integrates Across Google's Full TPU Stack With Warrant Tied to $120B Milestone

Broad TPU stack integration with warrant economics signals MRVL is graduating from component vendor to strategic partner — a qualitative shift that changes the duration and defensibility of the Google revenue stream. If the blueprint scales to future TPU generations, it structurally displaces competing silicon across one of the largest AI training environments on the planet.

- Marvell has attached itself across Google's full TPU stack — a broad custom silicon integration spanning the entire accelerator architecture, per single-outlet reporting (The Futurum Group).
- A warrant vesting toward $120B underscores the financial depth and long-term structural commitment of the partnership.
- The 'full stack' framing suggests MRVL's role extends beyond a single component to architecture-level engagement across Google's AI chip program.
- Single-outlet coverage; the $120B warrant figure should be treated as reported, not independently corroborated.
- Competitive read-across is negative for merchant silicon alternatives competing for the same TPU design real estate at Google.
