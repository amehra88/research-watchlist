## Judge Rejects Forced Sale of Google Ad Tech, Preserving Alphabet's Vertical Integration

The ruling eliminates the tail risk of a forced structural breakup that would have materially impaired Alphabet's ad monetization engine; for a tech PM, this largely resolves the regulatory structural overhang on the ad business, though behavioral remedies may still follow.

- A federal judge ruled against requiring Alphabet to divest its ad tech business, per two separate outlets.
- The decision removes what had been a major structural overhang on Alphabet's advertising segment.
- Alphabet's integrated stack spanning buy-side, sell-side, and exchange remains intact.
- FactSet sentiment is Neutral, consistent with relief rather than a new positive catalyst.
