## SK Hynix Announces ₩40 Trillion (~$28.6–29B) Share Buyback and Cancellation

A buyback-and-cancel of this scale from the leading HBM supplier is a direct management statement on FCF visibility at this point in the memory upcycle; it mechanically reduces share count and can lift per-share earnings, with second-order read-through to HBM-adjacent BCTK names such as NVDA, AMAT, and LRCX.

- SK Hynix will buy back and retire approximately 40 trillion won of common shares — headlines cite a range of ~$28.6B to $29B across outlets, with minor variance in FX translation.
- The buyback is to be completed within a three-month window before shares are cancelled, per at least one outlet.
- Ten outlets across Korean and international wire services covered the announcement simultaneously, consistent with a coordinated corporate disclosure.
- The size and capital-destruction structure (buyback + cancellation, not redistribution) signals management conviction in sustained free cash flow through the current HBM cycle.
