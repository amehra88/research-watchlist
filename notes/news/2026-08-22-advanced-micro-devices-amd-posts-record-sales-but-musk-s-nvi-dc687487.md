## AMD Posts Record Sales but Musk's Public NVIDIA Commitment Weighs on the Stock

Musk's stated NVDA preference at SpaceX's scale is a concrete competitive-moat data point for NVDA holders; for AMD's AI-GPU thesis, it raises the question of whether marquee customer wins can offset hyperscaler loyalty to NVDA before AMD's next architecture step-up.

- AMD reported record sales in its latest quarter — a strong fundamental outcome.
- Despite the beat, the stock sold off, with coverage attributing the pressure to Elon Musk's public pledge to NVIDIA as preferred compute supplier.
- Musk's commitment is being read as a competitive displacement signal — a marquee customer publicly staying with NVDA at scale.
- SpaceX (SPCX) is the implied anchor given Musk's role; SPCX's AI/compute footprint amplifies the signal beyond a retail-level endorsement.
- Record AMD sales and high-profile NVDA loyalty can coexist in a large market, but the near-term narrative creates a headwind for AMD GPU share-gain arguments.
