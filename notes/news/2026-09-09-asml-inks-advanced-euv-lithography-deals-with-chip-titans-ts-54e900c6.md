## ASML Inks High-NA EUV Supply Deals with TSMC and Samsung for Leading-Edge AI-Era Chip Production

Simultaneous High-NA EUV commitments from the world's two leading foundries de-risks ASML's demand outlook for its most advanced (and highest-ASP) tool and signals that leading-edge capacity investment will continue accelerating — a structural positive for the AI chip supply chain.

- ASML has signed High-NA EUV lithography supply agreements with both TSMC and Samsung, per coverage across two outlets.
- The deals are framed as a milestone for leading-edge foundry capacity ramp tied to AI chip demand.
- Both foundry customers locking in next-generation lithography supply signals confidence in sustained advanced-node demand.
