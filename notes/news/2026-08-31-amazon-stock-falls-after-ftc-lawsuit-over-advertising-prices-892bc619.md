## FTC Takes Aim at Amazon Ad-Pricing Practices; Stock Declines

An FTC lawsuit targeting Amazon's ad-pricing practices threatens one of its highest-margin business lines; even absent a near-term financial penalty, litigation overhang and potential remedies could constrain the advertising segment's pricing power and competitive positioning.

- The FTC is taking legal action against Amazon over its advertising pricing practices — outlets differ on whether the suit has been formally filed or is still being prepared.
- Amazon stock fell on the news, indicating the market views this as material legal exposure rather than noise.
- The action targets Amazon's conduct in ad pricing specifically, framing this as an antitrust challenge to a high-margin revenue segment.
- No specific financial penalties or remedies are cited in the coverage.
