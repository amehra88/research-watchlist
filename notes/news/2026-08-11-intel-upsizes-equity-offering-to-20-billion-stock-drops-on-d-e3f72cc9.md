## Intel Upsizes Equity Offering to $20 Billion, Stock Drops on Dilution Concerns

A $20B equity raise is a major balance-sheet event that signals Intel is funding its foundry buildout via dilution rather than debt or government subsidy alone — the scale suggests urgency and limited alternative financing. For the broader semiconductor landscape, this confirms Intel is still competing aggressively for advanced-node capacity, keeping competitive pressure on TSMC's fab-services pricing and on ASML's equipment order backlog.

- Intel launched and then upsized a stock offering — initial coverage cited a $15B deal before the company confirmed a $20B raise.
- One outlet reported pricing at $95 per share; other outlets did not specify a price, so treat that figure as single-source.
- The offering is framed in the context of intensifying chip-war investment cycles and Intel's need for foundry capital.
- The stock fell on the announcement, consistent with near-term dilution concerns on a large secondary offering.
