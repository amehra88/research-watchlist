## China's CXMT reaches 8% global DRAM share, sharpening competitive threat to Samsung and SK Hynix

China reaching 8% DRAM share via CXMT is a structural competitive development with direct pricing implications for Samsung and SK Hynix, the dominant HBM suppliers. For a portfolio with AI infrastructure exposure, this is an early-stage supply-side risk that could erode the margin premium Korean memory makers currently command in HBM.

- State-backed Chinese DRAM maker CXMT has reached 8% global market share, per headline data.
- CXMT's own stock surged 466% per the headline, reflecting domestic market enthusiasm for the competitive milestone.
- Samsung (005930.KS) and SK Hynix (000660.KS) are named as the primary competitive targets.
- Geopolitical dimension: this is a state-capacity-driven market share gain, not purely organic competitive dynamics.
