## SK Hynix Board Commits Korean HBM Supply Through 2033; US Has No Domestic HBM Wafer Fabs

Zero domestic US HBM production capacity is a structural national security and supply chain risk; this will likely intensify US policy pressure (CHIPS Act extensions, allied-fab incentives) and elevates the geopolitical premium on SK Hynix's long-term supply commitments — directly relevant to any sovereign AI infrastructure thesis and to the durability of Korean memory supplier pricing power.

- Tech Times reports SK Hynix's board has committed Korean AI memory production capacity through 2033.
- Headline explicitly states the US has zero HBM wafer fabrication capacity — a supply dependency fact.
- A commitment horizon extending to 2033 signals demand visibility from customers backing the investment.
- Supply concentration in South Korea creates a structural dependency for US AI infrastructure on allied-nation fabs.
