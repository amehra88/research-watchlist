## Amazon reportedly in talks for up to $60B Qualcomm chip procurement as Apple moves modems in-house

If Amazon's Qualcomm procurement materializes at the reported scale, it would represent a significant diversification of AI chip sourcing away from NVDA and internal custom silicon, with major competitive implications; simultaneously, Apple's modem in-housing is a long-anticipated negative for Qualcomm's handset revenue — creating asymmetric risk/reward for QCOM across two fronts.

- Amazon is reportedly considering sourcing up to $60 billion in chips from Qualcomm, a potentially large-scale procurement shift away from current AI chip suppliers.
- Simultaneously, Apple is moving to bring modem chip design in-house, reducing Qualcomm's share of a major existing revenue stream.
- Coverage frames both developments as part of a broader AI silicon-strategy pivot while questioning whether the AI angle is genuine.
- Both the Amazon procurement figure and Apple's modem plans are attributed to reports; no confirmed deal or product announcement is stated.
