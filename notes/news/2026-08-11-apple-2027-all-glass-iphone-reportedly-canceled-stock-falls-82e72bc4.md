## Apple 2027 All-Glass iPhone Reportedly Canceled; Stock Falls on New CEO Hardware Roadmap Concerns

Cancellation of a flagship form-factor innovation raises questions about Apple's ability to sustain premium pricing power and the hardware upgrade cycle under its new CEO — a direct risk to any AAPL thesis premised on a 2027 iPhone super-cycle.

- Reports indicate Apple has canceled plans for an all-glass iPhone originally targeted for 2027.
- The cancellation is framed as a setback for new CEO John Ternus's premium hardware ambitions.
- AAPL shares declined in response to the news.
- A second outlet (Mac Observer) corroborates the cancellation, raising confidence.
