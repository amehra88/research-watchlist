## Intel raises roughly $15B via stock sale to fund AI build-out; ~3% dilution triggers ~5% premarket decline

A $15B equity raise with a stated AI build-out purpose and a 5% premarket hit reflects the market's discomfort with near-term dilution despite the strategic rationale; the key question for the thesis is whether Intel can deploy this capital into returns before competitive windows close.

- Intel is selling approximately $15 billion of common stock, explicitly to fund its AI infrastructure build-out.
- The offering represents roughly 3% dilution to existing shareholders per the headline.
- Shares declined approximately 5% in premarket trading on the announcement.
- At least one analyst outlet frames the equity raise as a buying opportunity, providing a contrarian data point to the premarket selloff.
