## SK Hynix Expands Memory Roadmap with High-NA EUV Tools and Phase-Shift Masking

High-NA EUV adoption by SK Hynix is both a demand confirmation for ASML's next tool cycle and a signal that HBM roadmaps are pulling advanced lithography forward — reinforcing the thesis that memory capex intensity rises structurally with AI demand. A positive read-through to ASML backlog visibility.

- SK Hynix is expanding its EUV lithography roadmap to include High-NA tools from ASML and phase-shift masking (PSM).
- The combination targets leading-edge memory production with clear relevance to next-generation HBM output.
- ASML is the sole supplier of High-NA EUV tools, making this a direct demand signal for ASML's leading-edge equipment cycle.
- Simultaneous adoption of High-NA and PSM signals an accelerated process node roadmap, not an incremental step.
