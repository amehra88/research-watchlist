## Samsung Unveils Processing DRAM as Competitive Alternative to HBM

Samsung's Processing DRAM is a strategic pivot that could reshape the high-bandwidth memory market if hyperscalers and chip designers qualify it; for the portfolio, the critical question is whether it competes with or complements HBM, which directly affects SK Hynix's pricing power and market-share durability.

- Samsung has announced a 'Processing DRAM' technology it is positioning as an HBM alternative.
- The product is a direct competitive response to SK Hynix's dominance in the high-bandwidth memory segment.
- Samsung appears to be pursuing a differentiated memory architecture rather than simply closing the HBM yield gap.
