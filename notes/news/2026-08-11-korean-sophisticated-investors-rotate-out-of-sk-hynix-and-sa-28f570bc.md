## Korean sophisticated investors rotate out of SK Hynix and Samsung into PCB maker Daeduck Electronics

Korean sophisticated-investor rotation away from HBM and DRAM leaders into a PCB supplier could be an early-warning signal on the memory cycle; worth monitoring as a potential leading indicator ahead of MU and Hynix results.

- South Korea's 'top 1%' investors reported selling SK Hynix (000660.KS) and Samsung Electronics (005930.KS).
- Rotation target is Daeduck Electronics, a printed circuit board maker — a downstream component supplier.
