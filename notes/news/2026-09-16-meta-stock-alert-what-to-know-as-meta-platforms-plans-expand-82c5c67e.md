## Meta Platforms Plans Expanded Use of Proprietary In-House Chips

Continued in-house chip expansion by Meta is a structural headwind for external AI accelerator vendors, as each GPU equivalent Meta self-supplies is one fewer purchased; the medium-term thesis risk is that hyperscaler silicon self-sufficiency could durably moderate the external TAM for AI chip suppliers.

- Meta is reported to be expanding its reliance on proprietary, internally designed chips.
- The move is framed as reducing Meta's dependence on third-party silicon suppliers.
- The expansion reshapes Meta's AI infrastructure capex mix toward internal silicon at the margin.
