## Jensen Huang Reveals Nvidia Will Guarantee Chip Residual Values to Unlock $500 Billion AI Financing

Nvidia guaranteeing its own chips' residual values is a novel and significant risk-transfer — it means Nvidia's exposure does not end at the point of sale, and a demand shock or technology transition (next-gen GPU architecture, competing ASICs) that collapses used-GPU values could create balance-sheet stress. This detail materially changes the risk profile of the $500B plan and is likely the proximate cause of the stock's negative reaction covered in cluster 4fd7b24f85c5d6ae.
