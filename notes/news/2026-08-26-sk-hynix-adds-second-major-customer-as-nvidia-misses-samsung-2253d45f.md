## SK Hynix Adds Second Major HBM Customer as Nvidia Reportedly Exits Samsung Top Five

SK Hynix's concurrent share gain and Samsung's apparent customer loss is the sharpest near-term read on the HBM competitive divide; portfolio exposure to either Korean memory name needs a view on whether Samsung can close the gap before the next HBM generation qualifies.

- SK Hynix has secured a second major HBM customer, per Digitimes.
- Nvidia has reportedly dropped out of Samsung's top-five customers, suggesting a shift in memory sourcing.
- Taken together, the headlines point to a meaningful HBM market-share transfer from Samsung to SK Hynix.
- Samsung's AI-related revenue concentration appears to be weakening on both the foundry and memory sides.
