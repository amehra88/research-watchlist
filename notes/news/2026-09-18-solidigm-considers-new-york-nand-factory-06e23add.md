## SK Hynix subsidiary Solidigm weighs first US NAND factory on East Coast, cites AI demand and pre-IPO context

US-based NAND capacity from Solidigm would diversify AI storage supply away from Asia, potentially affecting pricing dynamics and competitive positioning for Samsung and Kioxia; the pre-IPO framing suggests Solidigm may be using the reshoring narrative to support a standalone valuation.

- No final site selection, investment size, or timeline has been announced; headlines reflect an evaluation stage.
- The move aligns with broader US semiconductor reshoring momentum.
