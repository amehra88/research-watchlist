## Cisco Beats on All Lines but Stock Falls as AI Infrastructure Shipping Costs Weigh on Outlook

CSCO's beat-and-fall is a warning sign that AI infrastructure costs are beginning to impair networking margins even for companies executing well on revenues — a key risk for any portfolio exposure to the networking layer of the AI stack.

- Cisco (CSCO) beat across all reported financial metrics in its earnings release.
- The stock declined despite the beat, with AI infrastructure shipping costs cited as the headwind explanation.
- The beat-and-fall pattern signals the market is focused on margin trajectory rather than top-line execution.
- AI infrastructure exposure is now appearing to compress networking margins rather than purely enhancing them.
