## SK Hynix becomes Kioxia's second-largest shareholder via bond conversion, triggering Japanese government alert

SK Hynix acquiring a strategic stake in rival Kioxia materially reshapes NAND competitive dynamics and introduces Japanese regulatory risk; if blocked or conditioned, it could disrupt the cross-ownership structure and affect NAND pricing dynamics that underpin the memory cycle thesis.

- SK Hynix has become de facto Kioxia's second-largest shareholder through a bond conversion, per two Korean outlets.
- Headlines differ slightly on finality: one treats it as pending conversion, the other as an accomplished fact — status unconfirmed.
- The Japanese government is described as 'on alert,' indicating potential regulatory or geopolitical scrutiny of the arrangement.
- This deepens cross-ownership between two of the world's largest NAND flash producers.
- No specific stake percentage is stated in the available headlines.
