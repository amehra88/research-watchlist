## TSMC August Revenue Surges 53% YoY to Record NT$514.8 Billion on AI Chip Demand

A record monthly revenue print with 53% YoY growth is the hardest near-term data point confirming the AI-driven capex super-cycle is intact; the read-through is broadly positive for AI semiconductor supply-chain names across the portfolio.

- TSMC reported August monthly revenue of NT$514.8 billion, a 53% year-over-year increase and a record high.
- The acceleration is attributed to surging AI chip demand, with NVDA, AMD, and AAPL among key customers.
- The result affirms continued high foundry utilization and strong order momentum into the back half of the year.
- Coverage frames the next catalyst as what TSM stock needs to sustain the move, signaling Wall Street wants forward-looking confirmation.
