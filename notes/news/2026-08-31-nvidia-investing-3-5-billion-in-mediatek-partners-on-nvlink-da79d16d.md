## Nvidia Invests $3.5 Billion in MediaTek, Partnering on NVLink Fusion Interconnect Platform

A $3.5 billion Nvidia commitment to MediaTek and the NVLink Fusion platform is a strategic move to make NVDA's interconnect fabric the default across a wider universe of silicon — extending the ecosystem moat into edge and mobile AI territories and raising the switching cost for any alternative interconnect standard.

- Nvidia is investing $3.5 billion in MediaTek and the two companies are jointly developing the NVLink Fusion platform, per the headline.
- NVLink Fusion would extend Nvidia's proprietary interconnect ecosystem into MediaTek's silicon, potentially covering AI edge and mobile compute.
- The investment signals Nvidia's intent to establish NVLink as a broad-market standard beyond its own data-center GPU stack.
- MediaTek gains capital and a deep technology partnership with the leading AI silicon vendor, strengthening its competitive position against Qualcomm and Arm-based rivals.
