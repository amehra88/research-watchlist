## Apple Beats Q3 Headline Estimates but Stock Falls ~2% as Services, China, and iPad Miss

Services is the linchpin of Apple's re-rating narrative; a miss there directly challenges the premise that the installed base is monetizing at the rate bulls assumed. China weakness adds a geopolitical demand-risk overlay that hardware strength alone cannot offset.

- Apple's Q3 2026 headline EPS and revenue beat consensus estimates.
- Services revenue, iPad revenue, and China revenue all came in below expectations.
- AAPL shares fell approximately 2% on the print, with the reaction concentrated in the miss on high-multiple services.
- The miss pattern — hardware beats, services/China misses — has been a recurring AAPL narrative and limits re-rating potential.
