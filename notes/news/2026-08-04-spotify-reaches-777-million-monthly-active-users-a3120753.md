## Spotify Reaches 777 Million Monthly Active Users

777M MAU versus 300M premium subs implies a substantial free-to-paid conversion runway; for a PM modeling SPOT's take-rate expansion, the MAU-to-premium funnel is the key metric to track as the company leans into pricing power and audiobook or podcast monetization.

- Spotify's total monthly active user base reached 777 million, a milestone highlighted in regional and trade outlets.
- This figure reflects total audience reach and is distinct from the 300M premium subscriber count reported in Q2 results.
- The gap between MAU and premium subscribers underscores a large free-tier monetization opportunity.
