## Apple partners with Alibaba to power AI features on iPhones in China

This is the clearest signal to date that Apple has resolved its China AI-feature gap through domestic LLM partnership — a prerequisite for iPhone AI parity in the world's largest smartphone market and a meaningful overhang removal for AAPL bulls. For BABA, a marquee enterprise AI win validates LLM commercial traction and strengthens the case for Alibaba Cloud's positioning in the domestic AI stack.

- Apple is shifting its China AI strategy to a partnership with Alibaba, using Alibaba's LLM to enable iPhone AI capabilities in the Chinese market.
- Coverage frames this as a meaningful strategic adaptation to China's regulatory and competitive AI environment, not an incremental move.
- The arrangement positions Alibaba's model as the key enabler of Apple Intelligence localization in China.
- No financial terms or feature-launch timeline are disclosed.
