## SK Hynix and Sandisk Unveil First High-Bandwidth Flash Standard, Supporting Up to 512GB for AI Inference

HBF as a new flash tier for AI inference is potentially additive to NAND demand from AI infrastructure — broadening the AI memory opportunity beyond HBM — and could improve SK Hynix's revenue mix from its NAND assets (Solidigm) if the standard gains traction with inference platform vendors.

- SK Hynix and Sandisk jointly introduced the first industry standard for high-bandwidth flash (HBF), targeting AI inference memory bottlenecks.
- The HBF standard supports configurations up to 512GB, positioning flash as a large-capacity complement to HBM for AI workloads.
- The partnership defines a new memory tier between DRAM and conventional NAND that could address capacity constraints in inference deployments.
- This is a standard launch, not a product shipment announcement; customer adoption timelines are not specified in the headlines.
