## Google to Receive Marvell Stock Warrants as Part of Custom Chip Purchase Agreement

The warrant-as-payment structure at MRVL-Google reveals the strategic depth — and concentration risk — of this custom-silicon relationship; if Google's chip volumes are large enough to move MRVL's equity, any Google design pivot or demand slowdown becomes a direct MRVL earnings event.

- Google (GOOGL/GOOG) is receiving Marvell stock warrants as a form of payment for purchasing Marvell's chips — an atypical capital arrangement.
- The warrant structure reveals unusual depth in the MRVL-Google relationship: hyperscaler compensating supplier partly in equity upside rather than pure cash.
- For MRVL, the arrangement signals Google as a major, committed chip customer while also introducing hyperscaler revenue concentration and equity dilution considerations.
- For GOOGL, holding MRVL warrants creates alignment with its own custom-silicon supplier's upside — but deepens supply chain counterparty entanglement.
- Coverage spans two outlets, both characterizing the structure as notable enough to require explanation.
