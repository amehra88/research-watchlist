## China's CXMT Overtakes SK Hynix and Micron on Memory Profit Margins

If CXMT is already leading on profit margins, China's semiconductor self-sufficiency is advancing faster than consensus expected, which could structurally pressure DRAM pricing for MU and SK Hynix and complicate the near-term memory cycle thesis.

- Chinese DRAM maker CXMT has reportedly surpassed both SK Hynix and Micron on memory profit margins, per Nikkei Asia
- The development marks an escalation of China's competitive threat in the global memory market
- Geopolitical backdrop: a China-backed supplier achieving margin leadership over established Korean and U.S. players
- Near-term risk for MU and SK Hynix on DRAM pricing power and market share
