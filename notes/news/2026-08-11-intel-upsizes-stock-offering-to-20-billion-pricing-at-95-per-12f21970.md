## Intel Upsizes Stock Offering to $20 Billion, Pricing at $95 per Share

A $20B equity raise is a transformational capital event for Intel — it funds the IFS build-out but confirms the balance-sheet stress that necessitates it. For a tech PM, the critical question is whether the dilution is now priced in or whether the commitment to advanced foundry capacity creates a multi-year capex overhang narrative competing with TSMC for design wins.

- Intel priced an upsized equity offering at $20 billion; one outlet specifies the share price at $95.
- The offering was increased from an initially smaller size, indicating a deliberate decision to maximize capital raised.
- Shares fell in premarket trading, reflecting near-term dilution concern.
- Multiple outlets frame this as one of Intel's largest capital raises in recent years, linked to its foundry investment program.
