## Zhongji InnoLight Raises HK$7.94B in Hong Kong's Largest IPO of 2026

A well-capitalized InnoLight is a more formidable competitor for COHR and LITE in optical transceiver markets; the IPO scale signals that Chinese players intend to fight aggressively for AI datacenter photonics market share, raising the competitive risk for Western names in that segment.

- Zhongji InnoLight, a Chinese optical transceiver manufacturer, raised HK$7.94B via a Hong Kong IPO.
- The overallotment option was fully exercised, indicating strong institutional demand.
- The IPO was the largest in Hong Kong for 2026.
- InnoLight is a direct competitor to COHR and LITE in optical transceivers, particularly for China AI infrastructure.
