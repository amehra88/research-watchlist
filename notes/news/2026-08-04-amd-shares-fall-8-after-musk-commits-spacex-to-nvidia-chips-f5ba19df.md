## AMD Shares Fall 8% After Musk Commits SpaceX to Nvidia Chips

Musk's explicit NVDA commitment for SpaceX is among the clearest demand-validation signals for Nvidia's AI compute stack this cycle; for AMD, it sharpens the question of whether the company can win marquee AI workloads beyond data center diversification plays.

- AMD shares dropped 8% following news that Elon Musk explicitly committed SpaceX to Nvidia chips
- The commitment is a direct competitive win for NVDA and a demand loss for AMD in high-profile AI infrastructure
- SpaceX represents a significant AI compute customer, making this a material demand signal for NVDA
- The move reinforces Nvidia's position as the dominant chip stack for frontier AI applications
