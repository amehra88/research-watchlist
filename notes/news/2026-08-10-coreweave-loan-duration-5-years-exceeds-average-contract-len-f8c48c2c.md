## CoreWeave Loan Duration (~5 Years) Exceeds Average Contract Length (~3 Years), Creating Structural Refinancing Risk

A loan-to-contract duration mismatch is the latent bear case for CRWV's balance sheet: the business model depends on contract renewal rates that are not yet proven at scale. This is the key asymmetric downside risk heading into Q2 and should be evaluated alongside the margin compression narrative.

- CRWV loans run approximately 5 years on average while customer contracts average approximately 3 years.
- The duration mismatch means debt obligations extend materially beyond the revenue commitments backing them.
- If key customers reduce or do not renew contracts at maturity, CRWV must refinance against a potentially smaller revenue base.
- This structural issue is distinct from near-term margin pressure and represents a fundamental credit risk entering Q2.
