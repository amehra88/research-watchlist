## AMD Q2 Data Center Revenue Doubles to $6.7B; Stock Falls ~9% Despite Beat and Broad Analyst PT Raises

AMD delivering doubled data center revenue while still trading down is the defining PM signal of the session: the market is applying a higher AI monetization hurdle than reported results clear, and the wide PT dispersion ($465–$650) across analysts signals genuine fundamental disagreement about AMD's AI revenue durability.

- AMD reported Q2 data center revenue of $6.7B, more than doubling year-over-year, with overall revenue growing approximately 50%.
- The stock fell roughly 9% despite the beat, with market concern attributed to rising capital expenditure rather than the revenue line.
- Multiple sell-side analysts raised price targets following the print: Bernstein to $650, Morgan Stanley to $465, Truist to $594; Jefferies and Roth/MKM also constructive.
- The gap between strong reported growth and a negative price reaction reflects market skepticism about whether AI compute spend is translating into durable AMD-specific economics.
