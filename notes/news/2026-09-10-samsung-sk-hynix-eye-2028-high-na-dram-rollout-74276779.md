## Samsung and SK Hynix Both Target 2028 for High-NA EUV DRAM Production

Dual commitment from the two largest DRAM makers to adopt High-NA EUV by 2028 provides long-dated equipment demand visibility for ASML and confirms the continued capital intensity of the memory roadmap; ASML's monopoly position in High-NA makes it a structural beneficiary of this transition.

- Samsung and SK Hynix have each announced plans to begin producing DRAM using High-NA EUV lithography by 2028
- The dual commitment provides multi-year roadmap visibility for next-generation memory
- ASML, as the sole supplier of High-NA EUV systems, stands as the primary equipment beneficiary
- Timeline aligns with expected growth in HBM and advanced DRAM demand from AI workloads
