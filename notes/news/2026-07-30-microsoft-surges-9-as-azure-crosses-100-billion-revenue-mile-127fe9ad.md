## Microsoft Surges 9% as Azure Crosses $100 Billion Revenue Milestone and Q4 Beat Drives Street-Wide Price Target Upgrades

Azure crossing $100B is a landmark that confirms MSFT as the largest enterprise AI platform at scale, and the 9% move with broad PT upgrades indicates consensus is revising structural estimates upward. For a tech PM, this is the strongest near-term validation of the enterprise AI monetization thesis and sets a high bar for competitive cloud platforms.

- MSFT shares rose approximately 9% following Q4 results.
- Azure surpassed the $100 billion annualized revenue milestone, a first for the segment.
- Q4 results beat expectations broadly, prompting price target upgrades across most of the Street.
- The combination of a scale milestone and widespread PT upgrades signals the Street is raising long-run estimates, not just marking to market.
