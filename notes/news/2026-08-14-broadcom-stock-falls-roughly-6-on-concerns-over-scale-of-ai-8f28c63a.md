## Broadcom Stock Falls Roughly 6% on Concerns Over Scale of AI Financing Commitment

Markets are beginning to reprice the debt-sustainability risk embedded in the hyperscaler AI capex buildout — a second-order risk for AVGO as a key beneficiary; the thesis holds if the spend lands profitably, but the tail risk is now being priced more explicitly.

- Broadcom (AVGO) shares fell approximately 6% on concerns tied to a reported $370B AI financing commitment.
- The market reaction frames the financing scale as a risk rather than a demand signal — debt trajectory is the concern.
- This narrative is distinct from the concurrent VMware security headline also pressuring AVGO.
- The selloff appears driven by anxiety about the sustainability of hyperscaler AI capex financing at this scale.
