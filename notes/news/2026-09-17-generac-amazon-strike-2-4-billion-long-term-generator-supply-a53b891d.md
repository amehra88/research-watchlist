## Amazon Signs Long-Term Generator Supply Deal with Generac for AI Data Center Buildout

A large-scale, multi-year generator supply commitment from a hyperscaler confirms that power procurement — a binding constraint on data center deployment — is being locked in with urgency, signaling Amazon is not decelerating its AI buildout. For the broader AI capex thesis, this validates the supply-chain winners theme in power infrastructure and adds credibility to extended hyperscaler spending duration.

- Amazon and Generac struck a long-term generator supply deal to power AI data center infrastructure; the deal size is reported as $2.4B by multiple outlets but one outlet cites $8B — figures conflict and should be treated as unresolved pending official disclosure.
- Generac's stock surged sharply on the announcement; the reported magnitude of the move varies across outlets (35%, 40%, and 45% cited in different headlines) and should be read as a large but unconfirmed precise figure.
- Barron's characterized the deal as a 'game-changer' for Generac, implying meaningful multi-year revenue visibility for the company.
- The deal is framed across outlets as directly linked to Amazon's AI infrastructure expansion, not a routine procurement.
