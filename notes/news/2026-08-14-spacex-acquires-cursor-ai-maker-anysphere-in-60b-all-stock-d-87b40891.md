## SpaceX acquires Cursor AI maker Anysphere in $60B all-stock deal

At $60B, this is a landmark AI software acquisition that repositions SpaceX as a direct player in AI developer tools and the agent framework layer; for the portfolio, it raises questions about SPCX's post-IPO strategic identity and how the market will value AI software optionality relative to the space and launch core business.

- SpaceX has agreed to acquire Anysphere, the company behind the Cursor AI coding assistant, for $60 billion in an all-stock transaction.
- The all-stock structure means no cash outlay but carries meaningful equity dilution at the SPCX level.
- Cursor/Anysphere is a prominent AI-native developer tool operating in the agent and coding-assistant space.
- The deal represents a significant capital allocation move by SpaceX into AI software and the agent framework layer, well beyond its core space and launch business.
