## Baidu shares slide 14% as core ad revenue collapses while AI investment hits record levels

- Baidu shares fell 14% following results that showed a sharply declining core advertising business, characterized in the headline as 'free-falling.'
- The company simultaneously reached a record level of AI investment spending.
- The divergence between collapsing legacy revenue and accelerating AI capex is placing monetization credibility under investor scrutiny.
