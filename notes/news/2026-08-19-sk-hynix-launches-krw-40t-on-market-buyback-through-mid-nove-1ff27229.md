## SK Hynix Launches KRW 40T On-Market Buyback Through Mid-November

A buyback of this scale from SK Hynix signals management confidence in cycle durability and free cash flow; it is also a major capital return event that may influence institutional positioning in the Korean memory complex, particularly set against the simultaneous broad selloff in Korean chips.

- SK Hynix has announced an on-market share repurchase program totaling KRW 40.004 trillion.
- The program runs from tomorrow through November 19.
- The reference share price in the announcement is KRW 1,500,000 per share.
- FactSet sentiment is Neutral; the scale of the buyback is a significant capital return signal from the HBM memory cycle leader.
