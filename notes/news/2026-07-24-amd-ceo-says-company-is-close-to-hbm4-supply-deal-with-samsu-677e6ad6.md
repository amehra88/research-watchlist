## AMD CEO Says Company Is Close to HBM4 Supply Deal with Samsung

An AMD-Samsung HBM4 deal would meaningfully erode SK Hynix's near-monopoly on leading-edge HBM, with downstream implications for HBM pricing power, Samsung memory ASPs, and the competitive structure of the AI accelerator supply chain.

- AMD's CEO indicated the company is nearing an HBM4 supply agreement with Samsung, per Seoul Economic Daily.
- If completed, the deal would diversify AMD's HBM sourcing beyond its current primary supplier, SK Hynix.
