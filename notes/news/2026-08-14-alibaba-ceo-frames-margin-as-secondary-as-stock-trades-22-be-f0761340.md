## Alibaba CEO frames margin as 'secondary' as stock trades 22% below January levels

A CEO publicly deprioritizing margin against a backdrop of a 22% drawdown reframes BABA as a deliberate AI reinvestment story, not a mean-reversion trade; for a PM with China tech exposure this changes both the holding thesis and the patience required, and raises questions about capital discipline if AI returns are slow to materialize.

- BABA shares are approximately 22% lower since January, per the TIKR headline.
- The CEO has publicly characterized margin as 'secondary,' signaling a deliberate strategic trade-off in favor of growth and AI investment.
- The statement implies near-term earnings power is being subordinated to longer-term AI infrastructure and market-share objectives.
- This framing is consistent with broader China AI infrastructure spending themes and places BABA in a growth-reinvestment posture rather than a value-recovery narrative.
