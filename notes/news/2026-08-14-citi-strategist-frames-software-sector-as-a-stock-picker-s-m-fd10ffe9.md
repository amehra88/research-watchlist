## Citi Strategist Frames Software Sector as a Stock-Picker's Market on AI Differentiation

A sell-side framework shift toward selection in software argues against riding sector beta and supports concentration in AI-differentiated names; tech PMs should sharpen single-name conviction rather than relying on the group to re-rate together.

- Citi's Tyler Radke characterizes enterprise software as a stock-picker's market rather than a sector-wide trade.
- The implied driver is widening AI differentiation separating software winners from laggards.
- Broad-based or ETF-style software exposure is framed as less rewarding than concentrated single-name selection.
