## SpaceX Reports First-Ever Earnings; Stock Falls on Higher-Than-Expected AI Capex Despite Strong Overall Growth

SpaceX's inaugural earnings introduced AI capex as a meaningful cost variable, signaling the market is pricing SPCX partly as an AI infrastructure consumer — a new risk dimension that reframes its valuation beyond a pure space play.

- SpaceX reported earnings publicly for the first time as a listed company
- Overall growth was characterized as strong in the headline
- The stock fell following the report, attributed to a higher-than-expected AI capex disclosure
- AI capex overage — not weak core operations — was the stated driver of the negative market reaction
