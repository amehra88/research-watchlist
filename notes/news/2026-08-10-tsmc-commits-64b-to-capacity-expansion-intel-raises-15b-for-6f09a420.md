## TSMC commits $64B to capacity expansion; Intel raises $15B for competing buildout

TSMC's $64B expansion directly supports AI compute demand and benefits customers across the advanced-node roadmap — positive read-through for NVDA, AAPL, and others. Intel's $15B raise signals continued foundry commitment but imposes dilution on existing shareholders, making execution credibility the central watch item for INTC bulls.

- Intel separately announced a $15 billion capital raise framed as funding for its own buildout.
- Both moves are direct capital-allocation events for the two largest players in advanced logic manufacturing.
- The simultaneous announcements underscore an industry-wide race to add leading-edge and AI-oriented capacity.
