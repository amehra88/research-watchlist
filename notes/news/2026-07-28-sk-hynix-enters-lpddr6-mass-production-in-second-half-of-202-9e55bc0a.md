## SK Hynix Enters LPDDR6 Mass Production in Second Half of 2026, Xiaomi Named First Customer

LPDDR6 production ramp with Xiaomi as the anchor customer signals mobile AI memory demand is materializing at scale; this is a near-term volume and competitive-positioning milestone for SK Hynix at a moment when its shares are under pressure from other headlines.

- SK Hynix has entered mass production of LPDDR6 mobile memory in the second half of 2026 per two specialist outlets.
- Xiaomi is identified as the first customer, establishing an anchor relationship with a major Android OEM.
- The move signals mobile AI memory is transitioning from development roadmap to commercial volume.
- Two outlets (TechPowerUp, Digitimes) independently align on both the production timing and the Xiaomi customer detail.
