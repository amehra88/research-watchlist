## Fervo Energy surges 28% on 396-megawatt geothermal power deal with Google

Hyperscalers are moving beyond the spot power market into large structured power procurement to de-risk data center expansion; this transaction validates energy supply as a genuine constraint on AI infrastructure pacing and creates an investable theme across power generation, transmission, and energy-adjacent infrastructure names.

- Fervo Energy (FRVO) shares rose 28% after announcing a 396 MW geothermal power deal with Google (GOOGL)
- Deal represents direct hyperscaler procurement of dedicated, firm power for data center needs
- Confirms that energy supply constraints are real and material enough to drive large, structured off-take agreements
- Geothermal's always-on profile differentiates it from intermittent renewables as a data center power solution
