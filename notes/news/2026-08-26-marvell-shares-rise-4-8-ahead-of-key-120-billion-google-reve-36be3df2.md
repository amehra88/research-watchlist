## Marvell Shares Rise ~5% Ahead of Google Earnings as Custom Silicon Hyperscaler Thesis in Focus

Marvell's tight pre-earnings correlation to Google is a concentration risk as much as a positive signal; a strong Google result and capex guide could re-rate MRVL further, but the same linkage means any Google capex guidance disappointment would hit MRVL disproportionately, which is the core hyperscaler-revenue-concentration risk to monitor.

- Marvell (MRVL) shares rose approximately 4.8% in advance of Google's earnings report.
- The rally is linked to investor optimism around the Google revenue print, with the headline citing a $120B revenue earnings report as the key event.
- Marvell's custom silicon exposure to Google is the identified driver of the sympathetic move.
- The price action highlights the extent to which MRVL's near-term trajectory is tightly correlated to a single hyperscaler.
