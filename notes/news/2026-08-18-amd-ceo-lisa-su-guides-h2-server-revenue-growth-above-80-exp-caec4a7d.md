## AMD CEO Lisa Su guides H2 server revenue growth above 80%, explicitly excluding AI accelerators

Greater than 80% server revenue growth ex-AI-accelerator is a strong signal that enterprise compute demand is recovering broadly across CPU server workloads; this directly pressures Intel's server franchise and validates AMD's non-AI server share gains.

- Lisa Su guided AMD server revenue growth of more than 80% for the second half of the year.
- She explicitly flagged that this growth projection excludes the AI accelerator business — traditional and CPU-based server compute is the driver.
- The distinction frames AMD's H2 strength as broad-based across server architectures, not solely GPU/accelerator-dependent.
- Competitive implications for Intel in the CPU server market are a direct second-order read from the silicon architecture competition theme.
