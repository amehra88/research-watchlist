## Samsung Electronics Posts Record ₩55 Trillion in H1 R&D and Capital Expenditure as Mobile Memory Prices Triple

Tripling mobile memory prices confirm a memory upcycle is underway; Samsung's record capex and R&D commit signals it is leaning aggressively into HBM competition with SK Hynix, with positive read-through for the broader memory and semiconductor supply chain.

- Samsung spent a record ₩55 trillion on combined R&D and facilities in the first half of the year
- R&D alone reached a record ₩27 trillion in H1, per Korean-language outlet coverage
- Mobile memory prices have tripled, signaling a demand-driven recovery in the memory cycle
- The record investment level signals Samsung's intensified commitment to HBM and memory technology competition
