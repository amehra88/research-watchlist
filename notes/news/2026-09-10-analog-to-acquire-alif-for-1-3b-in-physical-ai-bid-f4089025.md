## Analog Devices to Acquire Alif Semiconductor for $1.3 Billion in Physical AI Push

At $1.3 billion, this is a meaningful strategic bet that broadens ADI's AI exposure from industrial/analog into edge inference — a segment with strong secular tailwinds from robotics and physical AI; execution risk and integration timeline are key variables to monitor.

- Analog Devices (ADI) is acquiring Alif Semiconductor in a transaction valued at $1.3 billion
- The deal is explicitly positioned as ADI's entry into the physical AI and edge inference market
- Represents a material strategic pivot for ADI beyond its traditional analog and mixed-signal business
- Signals ADI's intent to compete directly in the growing embedded AI inference chip space
