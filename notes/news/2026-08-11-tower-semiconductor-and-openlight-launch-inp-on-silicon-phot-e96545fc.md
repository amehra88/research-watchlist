## Tower Semiconductor and OpenLight Launch InP-on-Silicon Photonics PDK on Cadence Design Platform

Silicon photonics is a critical enabling technology for AI data center interconnects at scale; anchoring TSEM's InP-on-silicon process in Cadence's EDA ecosystem lowers the design barrier and broadens the potential customer base. For a tech PM with AI infrastructure exposure, this is a supply-chain watch item — TSEM's photonics design-tool integration could surface as a revenue line in future quarters as co-packaged optics adoption accelerates.

- Tower Semiconductor (TSEM) and OpenLight have jointly launched the OpenLight PDK on Cadence EDA tools, targeting TSEM's PH18DA InP-on-silicon photonics process.
- The integration allows chip designers to develop photonic integrated circuits using TSEM's photonics process within industry-standard Cadence design infrastructure.
- The move positions TSEM as a photonics-capable foundry with a reduced design-barrier for optical transceiver and co-packaged optics customers.
