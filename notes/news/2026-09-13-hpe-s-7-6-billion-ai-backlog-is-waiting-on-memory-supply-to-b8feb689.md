- HPE has a $7.6 billion AI backlog that cannot be converted to revenue until memory supply catches up, confirmed by two outlets.
- The bottleneck is supply-side, not demand-side — end-market AI server appetite is clearly robust.

- HPE faces revenue timing risk if the memory supply gap persists, with costs potentially locked in while shipments are deferred.
