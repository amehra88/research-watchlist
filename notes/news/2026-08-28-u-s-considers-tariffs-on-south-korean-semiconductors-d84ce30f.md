## U.S. Considers Imposing Tariffs on South Korean Semiconductors

Tariffs on Korean chips would raise input costs for U.S. system builders and data center operators, affect global memory pricing, and introduce a new risk premium into SK Hynix and Samsung — the dominant HBM suppliers to Nvidia and the AI stack — at a time when that supply chain is already under scrutiny.
