## SK Hynix establishes first High Bandwidth Foundation (HBF) standard for next-generation AI memory

Authoring a memory interface standard is a classic moat-building move; if HBF gains adoption among chip designers, SK Hynix could lock in preferential design-in status at the architecture layer, compounding its existing HBM supply advantage.

- SK Hynix announced it has set the first HBF (High Bandwidth Foundation) standard for AI memory architecture.
- The move is positioned as a bid for ecosystem and architecture leadership in next-generation high-bandwidth memory.
- Standard-setting at this layer could entrench supplier alignment for future AI accelerator designs.
