## U.S. Threatens 100% Tariffs on Samsung and SK Hynix Memory Chips

A 100% tariff on Korean memory would be an existential competitive shock to Samsung and SK Hynix's U.S. business and would structurally advantage Micron across both commodity and HBM segments; with AI infrastructure demand concentrated in HBM, this is a first-order signal for the memory competitive thesis.

- Washington is reportedly threatening 100% tariffs on Korean memory producers Samsung and SK Hynix.
- Tariff level cited (100%) would be among the most severe trade actions in the semiconductor sector.
- Competitive beneficiary read points to Micron (MU) as the domestic alternative.
