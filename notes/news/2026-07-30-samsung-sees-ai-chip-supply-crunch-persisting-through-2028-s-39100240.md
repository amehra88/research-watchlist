## Samsung Sees AI Chip Supply Crunch Persisting Through 2028, Signs Long-Term Deals as Profit Hits Record

Samsung's explicit 2028 shortage call — from the supply side — is a structural bull signal for HBM pricing and utilization; for a tech PM holding MU or SK Hynix, this is incremental confirmation that the memory upcycle has multi-year runway rather than a near-term mean-reversion risk.

- Samsung publicly guided that the AI chip supply shortage will extend through 2028 — a multi-year constraint call from the largest memory producer.
- The company reported record profit alongside this guidance, suggesting current pricing power is intact even as it signals sustained tightness.
- Samsung is signing long-term supply deals, indicating hyperscaler and OEM customers are locking in capacity rather than waiting for spot relief.
- The supply-crunch call has direct read-through for the HBM competitive set: SK Hynix (000660.KS) and Micron (MU) stand to benefit from the same demand signal.
