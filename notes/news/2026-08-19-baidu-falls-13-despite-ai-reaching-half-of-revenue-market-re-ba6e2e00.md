## Baidu Falls 13% Despite AI Reaching Half of Revenue; Market Rejects Monetization Pivot Narrative

BIDU's reaction illustrates the 'show-me' dynamic in AI monetization: revenue-mix milestones alone do not satisfy the market if the legacy business appears to be deteriorating; this is a calibration data point for how investors price AI-transition narratives at other large-cap platform names.

- Baidu (BIDU) fell 13% in a single session.
- AI now reportedly constitutes roughly half of Baidu's revenue — a significant compositional shift.
- Despite the AI revenue milestone, investors appear unconvinced by the monetization transition narrative.
- The market reaction implies concern about the pace or sustainability of the core business alongside the AI pivot.
