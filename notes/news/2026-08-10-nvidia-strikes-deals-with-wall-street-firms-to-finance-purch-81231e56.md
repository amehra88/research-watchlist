## Nvidia Strikes Deals With Wall Street Firms to Finance Purchases by Its Own Customers

If Nvidia is routing Wall Street capital to backstop customer purchases, it raises the question of whether organic demand is constrained by buyer balance sheets rather than appetite — a nuanced risk beneath the bullish demand narrative, and one that introduces financial intermediary exposure into the AI capex chain.

- Nvidia has struck deals with Wall Street financial firms specifically to fund its own customers' AI purchases.
- The structure is designed to sustain demand by reducing upfront capital requirements on the buyer side.
- Coverage frames this as a demand-sustaining financing mechanism rather than a conventional product or revenue story.
- The deal involves Nvidia acting, in effect, as an indirect demand engineer through third-party capital providers.
