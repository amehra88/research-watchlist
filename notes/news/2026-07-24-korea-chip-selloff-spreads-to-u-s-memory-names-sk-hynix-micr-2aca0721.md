## Korea Chip Selloff Spreads to U.S. Memory Names; SK Hynix, Micron, and SanDisk All Decline

A Korea-sourced memory selloff hitting SK Hynix and MU simultaneously is a semiconductor cycle warning signal; if the driver is demand-side deterioration rather than FX or policy, it would pressure near-term HBM pricing assumptions and MU earnings revision paths.

- Selloff originated in Korea and transmitted to U.S.-listed memory names.
- Coverage characterizes the weakness as broad-based across memory names, not issuer-specific.
- HBM and NAND names are both implicated in the move.
