## Apple Eliminates 200+ Roles Across Siri, AI, and Vision Pro Organizations

Cutting 200+ roles specifically in Siri, AI, and Vision Pro—the three product lines most central to Apple's next-cycle growth narrative—raises the question of whether this is focused efficiency or a signal of strategic reprioritization; the ambiguity in direction is the key risk given Apple's AI monetization thesis remains unproven.

- Apple cut more than 200 positions spanning its Siri team, Intelligent Systems Experience AI group, and Vision Pro organization, per Bloomberg.
- Shares traded at $308.52, down $2.78 on the session, per the headline.
- FactSet sentiment on the story is rated Neutral.
- No stated rationale or restructuring plan details appear in the headline beyond the team scope of the cuts.
