## Meta Agrees to Pay Up to $18 Billion and Accept Younger-User Restrictions in Legal Settlement

A potential $18 billion cash outflow is significant for capital allocation priorities, but restrictions on younger users could cap long-term engagement growth — a pillar of Meta's ad revenue thesis and AI monetization narrative.

- Meta has agreed to pay up to $18 billion as part of a legal settlement
- The settlement also includes agreed limits on how younger users interact with the platform
- Coverage poses the question of whether META stock is a buy despite the settlement
- Represents a material legal and regulatory overhang on capital allocation and engagement growth
