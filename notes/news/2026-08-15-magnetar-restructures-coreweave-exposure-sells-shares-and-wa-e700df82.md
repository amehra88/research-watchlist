## Magnetar restructures CoreWeave exposure — sells shares and warrants while simultaneously buying call options

The derivative overlay materially complicates the bearish read from the large share sale: Magnetar appears to be reshaping — not eliminating — its CRWV exposure. PM should not treat the headline share sales as outright distribution without netting against the call option positions.

- Magnetar-managed funds sold 307,131 CRWV Class A shares in August trades; separately, entities report holding 1.89M shares via a master fund structure.
- Concurrently, Magnetar funds entered call options on 1,000,000 shares and separately report call options on 2,000,000 Class A shares in filings.
- Magnetar funds also sold warrants on 100,000 underlying shares and 200,000 additional CoreWeave-linked warrants.
- The simultaneous sell-shares / buy-calls / sell-warrants activity indicates a position restructuring, not a clean directional exit; net economic exposure cannot be determined from headlines alone.
