## Apple confirms Qualcomm modem exit; Amazon reportedly offers $60 billion deal as potential offset

Apple's confirmed modem departure validates AAPL's silicon self-sufficiency trajectory and removes a key Qualcomm revenue anchor; the Amazon $60B offset, if real, signals potential for Qualcomm to pivot toward data-center or edge workloads, creating second-order competitive implications for both AAPL's supply chain strategy and AMZN's chip partnerships.

- Apple has confirmed it is dropping Qualcomm's modem business, realizing the long-telegraphed design-out.
- Amazon is reported to have offered Qualcomm a $60 billion deal, framed as a potential replacement revenue source.
- The AAPL loss removes a significant customer from Qualcomm's modem revenue base.
- If the AMZN deal materializes, it would represent a substantial pivot in Qualcomm's business mix toward cloud or edge-compute applications.
