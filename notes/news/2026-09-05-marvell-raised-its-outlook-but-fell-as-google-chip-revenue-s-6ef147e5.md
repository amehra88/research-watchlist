## Marvell Raises Outlook but Sells Off as Google Custom-Chip Revenue Timing Disappoints

A stock falling on a raised guide is a textbook valuation-versus-timing signal — the market had priced in Google ASIC revenue earlier than management is guiding; this is the most direct data point yet on hyperscaler revenue concentration risk for ASIC-dependent chip designers, and it applies to AVGO's custom silicon segment as well.

- MRVL raised its forward outlook, a positive fundamental development.
- The stock fell despite the raise, indicating the market was disappointed on a separate dimension.
- Google custom-chip revenue was characterized as 'distant,' suggesting a timing miss vs. buy-side expectations.
- Coverage questions whether the AI custom-silicon payoff has already been fully priced into the shares.
- The headline explicitly surfaces GOOGL customer concentration as the source of uncertainty.
