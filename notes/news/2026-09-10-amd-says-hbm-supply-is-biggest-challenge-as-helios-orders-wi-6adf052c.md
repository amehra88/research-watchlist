## AMD Identifies HBM Supply as Biggest Constraint; Helios GPU Orders Paired with Samsung HBM4 Exceed Expectations

HBM supply as AMD's binding constraint validates the HBM scarcity thesis and is a direct read-through for SK Hynix and Samsung's HBM pricing power; if AMD cannot source sufficient HBM4 it risks ceding near-term AI GPU share to NVDA even as demand for its product accelerates.

- AMD named HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) supply as its single largest operational constraint to meeting demand.
- Orders for AMD's Helios GPU, configured with Samsung HBM4, are reported to be exceeding expectations.
- Strong demand against a tight HBM supply backdrop signals near-term shipment risk even if end-market demand is robust.
- Samsung (005930.KS) is identified as a key HBM4 supply partner for AMD's next-generation GPU ramp.
