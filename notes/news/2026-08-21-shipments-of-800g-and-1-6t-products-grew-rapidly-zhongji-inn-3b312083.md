## Chinese Rival Innolight Reports 241.7% Net Profit Surge on Rapid 800G/1.6T Shipment Growth

Innolight's near-tripling of profits on 800G/1.6T shipments confirms explosive demand for high-speed optical interconnects in AI data centers, but also validates that Chinese competitors are capturing meaningful share at scale — a key risk to monitor for COHR and LITE margin and volume assumptions.

- Zhongji Innolight (300308.SZ) reported a 241.7% year-over-year increase in net profit attributable to shareholders for the first half, reaching RMB 13.651B.
- The growth was driven by rapid shipment growth in 800G and 1.6T optical transceiver products.
- This is a direct competitive read-through for US optical networking peers COHR and LITE, which compete in the same high-speed transceiver market.
- No market share data or customer-level detail is provided in the headline.
