## China's CXMT eyes NAND flash entry, posing a new competitive challenge to Samsung and YMTC

CXMT's NAND ambitions follow a pattern similar to its DRAM expansion, where Chinese entrants have historically pressured incumbent pricing and market share; Samsung is the primary incumbent at risk, and a NAND supply glut scenario would weigh on 005930.KS margins.

- Chinese memory maker CXMT is reportedly targeting entry into NAND flash production, per a single-outlet report (finance.biggo.com).
- The move is framed as a direct challenge to Samsung (005930.KS) and existing Chinese NAND producer YMTC.
- No timeline or production volumes are specified in the headline.
- Geopolitical context: domestic Chinese NAND capacity expansion reduces China's reliance on imported flash memory.
