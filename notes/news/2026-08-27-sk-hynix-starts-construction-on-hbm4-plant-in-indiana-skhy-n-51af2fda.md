## SK Hynix Breaks Ground on ~$4B Indiana HBM Packaging Plant, First US-Domestic HBM Production Hub

US-domestic HBM packaging capacity directly addresses supply-chain concentration risk for AI accelerator memory and strengthens SK Hynix's positioning under the CHIPS Act framework; for NVDA and hyperscaler customers, a domestic HBM4 source reduces geopolitical single-point-of-failure risk, though production remains several years out.

- SK Hynix held a formal groundbreaking at Purdue Research Park in Indiana for its first US HBM advanced packaging facility, with Indiana Governor Braun present.
- Outlets report the investment at approximately $4B; one outlet cites $3.87B specifically — exact figure differs slightly across sources.
- Cleanroom completion is targeted around October 2028, with volume production referenced for 2029 in some outlets and 'by 2030' in others.
- The plant is described as an HBM4-capable packaging facility — the first US-based site for domestic HBM chip packaging.
- FactSet sentiment registers as Neutral, consistent with a large-capex announcement whose financial impact is multi-year.
