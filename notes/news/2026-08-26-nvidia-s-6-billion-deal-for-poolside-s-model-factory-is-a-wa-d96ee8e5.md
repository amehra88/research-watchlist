## Nvidia Acquires AI Model Factory Poolside for $6 Billion, Betting Open-Source Models Drive Chip Demand

The acquisition signals Nvidia believes owning open-source model tooling creates a demand flywheel for its silicon; for the portfolio, this deepens the NVDA AI-platform thesis and raises the question of whether Nvidia is becoming a full-stack AI company, not just a chip supplier.

- Nvidia is acquiring Poolside, an AI model factory, for $6B, per Motley Fool.
- The strategic rationale is that open-source model proliferation expands aggregate GPU compute demand, benefiting Nvidia.
- The deal extends Nvidia's stack further up the AI value chain — from silicon into model infrastructure.
- Signals Nvidia views open-source model growth as a demand multiplier rather than a commoditization threat.
