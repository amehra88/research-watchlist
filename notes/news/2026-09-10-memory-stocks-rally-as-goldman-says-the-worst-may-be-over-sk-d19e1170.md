## Memory Stocks Rally Broadly After Goldman Signals Potential End to Downcycle; SK Hynix and Micron Lead

A Goldman inflection call on memory is a potential leading indicator for a broader semiconductor cycle recovery; with memory serving as a critical input to AI infrastructure, a floor in pricing and an improving supply/demand balance would support margins across the AI stack.

- Goldman Sachs indicated the worst of the memory downcycle may be over, triggering a broad-based sector rally.
- SK Hynix rose approximately 5% and Micron approximately 2%; the headline also cites SanDisk advancing approximately 3%.
- The breadth of the move across multiple memory names suggests a sector re-rating signal rather than a company-specific catalyst.
- Goldman's call is framed as directional, not a definitive inflection confirmation.
