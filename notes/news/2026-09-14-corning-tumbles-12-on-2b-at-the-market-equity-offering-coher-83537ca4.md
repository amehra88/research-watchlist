## Corning Tumbles 12% on $2 Billion At-the-Market Equity Offering; Coherent, Lumentum, and Fabrinet Fall in Sympathy

A large ATM dilution at an optical sector bellwether during a period of AI-driven multiple expansion invites re-examination of valuations across the entire optical supply chain — the contagion pattern to COHR and LITE suggests the market fears the offering signals a softer demand outlook than consensus, which would be material to the 1.6T transition and co-packaged optics ramp timelines.

- Corning announced a $2 billion at-the-market equity offering, triggering an approximately 12% selloff in GLW shares per the headline.
- Sector contagion was immediate and sharp: Coherent fell approximately 11%, Lumentum dropped approximately 9%, and Fabrinet slid approximately 6%, per headline figures.
- The ATM structure — which allows dilution at market prices rather than a negotiated block — raises questions about the urgency of the capital need or management's willingness to dilute at current valuation levels.
- The breadth of the decline across optical names suggests the market is treating this as a sector-level valuation reset, not an isolated GLW balance sheet event.
