## Analyst Target Price Dispersion Reaches 3x on SK Hynix and 2x on Samsung Amid HBM Demand Uncertainty

Wide analyst dispersion is a direct risk signal: when the smartest buyers and sellers of a name are 2-3x apart on intrinsic value, position sizing should reflect that uncertainty, and options structures may be preferable to outright until demand visibility improves.

- Brokerage targets on SK Hynix span a 3x range from low to high — an unusually wide dispersion for a large-cap semiconductor.
- Samsung Electronics targets show a 2x spread, narrower but still reflecting significant analyst disagreement.
- Coverage explicitly acknowledges that predictions are 'difficult,' an unusually candid admission of low conviction.
- The dispersion is concentrated on HBM demand trajectory and memory cycle timing uncertainty.
