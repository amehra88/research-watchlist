## Samsung and SK Hynix leveraged ETFs drawing retail inflows amid memory boom-bust framing

Leveraged ETF inflows into memory names are a sentiment indicator, not a fundamental signal; elevated retail crowding late in a momentum cycle typically sharpens the eventual drawdown and can amplify contagion to HBM-adjacent watchlist names when sentiment reverses.

- Investors are channeling capital into leveraged ETFs tracking Samsung (005930.KS) and SK Hynix (000660.KS), per Bloomberg.
- Bloomberg explicitly frames the dynamic as retail momentum chasing a boom-and-bust cycle in memory and HBM.
- Leveraged structures amplify both upside and drawdown, elevating the risk profile of the positioning.
- The 'boom-and-bust' framing signals media concern about cycle timing rather than endorsement of the trade.
