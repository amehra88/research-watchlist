## Meta and State AGs Discussing Possible Settlement in Social-Media Addiction Trial

- Meta is in active settlement discussions with state attorneys general over the social-media addiction trial, per Bloomberg citing people familiar with the talks.
- This is a discussion, not a confirmed agreement; terms and outcome remain unspecified.
