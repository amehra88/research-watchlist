## Lumentum's 1.6T and 200G Optical Ramp Faces Direct Competition from Cisco and Nvidia

LITE's optical ramp thesis is directly complicated by competitive pressure from CSCO and NVDA — two companies with significantly greater scale and bundling power; this is a margin-compression risk that PMs holding LITE for the AI networking cycle should actively monitor against the growth opportunity.

- Lumentum is ramping 1.6 Terabit and 200G optical networking products.
- Cisco and Nvidia are identified as direct competitors in this segment.
- The competitive dynamic raises margin risk for Lumentum's optical business.
- The story highlights crowding in AI networking as large incumbents and platform players enter.
