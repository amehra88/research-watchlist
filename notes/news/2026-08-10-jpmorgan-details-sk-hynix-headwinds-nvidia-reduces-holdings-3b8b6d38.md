## JPMorgan Details SK Hynix Headwinds: Nvidia Reduces Holdings, ~50% Price Discounts, Buybacks—Stock Drops ~15% in One Week

This is the sharpest near-term bearish signal in the Korean memory cluster today: Nvidia reducing exposure alongside roughly 50% price discounts directly threatens HBM margin assumptions. If confirmed, this pressures the entire HBM bull case and has read-through implications for NVDA's supply-chain positioning.

- JPMorgan published a detailed note on SK Hynix citing Nvidia reducing its holdings, price discounts of approximately 50%, and buyback activity as key dynamics.
- SK Hynix shares reportedly fell approximately 15% in a single week, per the headline framing.
- The combination of demand-side pullback and steep pricing pressure represents a significant deterioration signal for near-term HBM economics.
- Buybacks suggest management is responding to share weakness but do not address the underlying demand deterioration.
- The JPMorgan note adds institutional weight to what had been emerging as a multi-source negative read on SK Hynix.
