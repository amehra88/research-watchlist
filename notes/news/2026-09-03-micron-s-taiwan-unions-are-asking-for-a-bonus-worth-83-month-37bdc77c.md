## Micron Taiwan Unions Demand 83-Month Bonus; Strike Vote Could Come This Month

Micron's Taiwan fabs are central to its memory output and HBM ramp; a labor disruption at this stage of the AI-driven memory cycle would tighten DRAM and HBM supply at the worst possible moment — a potential short-term supply shock that would benefit Samsung and SK Hynix and pressure MU's ability to execute its competitive catch-up.

- Unions at Micron's Taiwan operations are seeking a bonus equivalent to 83 months of pay.
- A strike vote is described as potentially imminent — could come within the current month.
- A work stoppage at Micron's Taiwan fabs would directly threaten DRAM and NAND output.
