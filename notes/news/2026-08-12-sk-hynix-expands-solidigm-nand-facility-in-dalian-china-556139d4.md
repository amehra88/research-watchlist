## SK Hynix Expands Solidigm NAND Facility in Dalian, China

SK Hynix committing to China-based NAND capacity expansion signals conviction in the NAND cycle recovery — positive for memory demand broadly — but the China siting concentrates supply-chain risk at a facility that could face export-control disruption, creating a two-sided read for memory-adjacent names in the portfolio.

- SK Hynix is expanding Solidigm's NAND manufacturing plant located in Dalian, China.
- The capacity expansion signals supply-side confidence in the NAND demand recovery cycle.
- Solidigm is SK Hynix's NAND brand (acquired from Intel), making this an SK Hynix operational story.
- The Dalian location in China creates latent geopolitical and export-control risk that could disrupt the facility under future regulatory action.
