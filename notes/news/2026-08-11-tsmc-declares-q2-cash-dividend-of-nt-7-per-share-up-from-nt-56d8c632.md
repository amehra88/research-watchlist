## TSMC Declares Q2 Cash Dividend of NT$7 per Share, Up from NT$5 a Year Ago

A meaningful dividend step-up from TSMC during a major capex expansion cycle is a strong capital-discipline signal; it implies management views the earnings trajectory as durable enough to return more cash while simultaneously investing in capacity — a constructive read for the foundry thesis.
