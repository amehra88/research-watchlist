## South Korean Retail Poured $4.5 Billion Into U.S. Stocks in July, Paying a 10% Premium for SK Hynix ADRs

Retail crowding into SK Hynix ADRs at a 10% premium alongside leveraged ETF demand creates a fragile positioning dynamic; a sentiment reversal or fundamental disappointment could drive outsized selling pressure in the HBM and memory complex beyond what fundamentals alone would justify.

- South Korean retail investors deployed $4.5 billion into U.S.-listed equities in July per the reporting.
- SK Hynix ADRs were purchased at a 10% premium to the underlying, indicating elevated speculative demand for the HBM memory name.
- Triple-leveraged ETFs topped the retail buying list — a sign of high-conviction, high-risk positioning in the cohort.
- The pattern signals retail-driven momentum rather than fundamental institutional conviction.
