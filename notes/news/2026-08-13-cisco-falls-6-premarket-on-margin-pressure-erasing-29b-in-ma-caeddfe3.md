## Cisco Falls ~6% Premarket on Margin Pressure, Erasing ~$29B in Market Cap

CSCO's beat-and-gap-down is a clear signal that the market is scrutinizing margin conversion in AI networking, not just demand; this is a negative read-through for networking equipment margin assumptions across the supply chain and raises the question of whether AI-driven revenue mix is dilutive to blended margins.

- Cisco (CSCO) dropped approximately 6% in premarket trading following its earnings report.
- Margin pressure is cited as the driver of the selloff, despite underlying AI networking demand remaining intact.
- The move erased approximately $29 billion in market capitalization.
- The premarket selloff contrasts with a headline EPS beat ($1.22 vs. $1.17 consensus), illustrating that the beat alone was insufficient to offset margin disappointment.
