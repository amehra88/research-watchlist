## Nvidia Guarantees Chip Residual Values to Unlock $500 Billion in AI Infrastructure Financing

If Nvidia is absorbing depreciation risk, it removes a major barrier to AI data center financing — effectively adding a financial-services layer to its hardware franchise. For NVDA holders, this deepens customer lock-in; for the broader AI capex complex (power, cooling, networking), it could accelerate deployment timelines by unlocking capital that was previously constrained by collateral concerns.

- Nvidia is backstopping the residual value of its own chips as a capital-markets mechanism to mobilize AI infrastructure financing.
- The headline-cited target is $500 billion — a landmark scale for a hardware-vendor-backed financing structure.
- The mechanism addresses a core friction: lenders' reluctance to extend credit against rapidly depreciating silicon assets.
