## SK Hynix in Talks with Intel on First-Ever US Memory Chip Production, Ohio Fab Cited

If consummated, this would mark the first US-soil memory production by SK Hynix, materially reshaping domestic semiconductor supply chain resilience and potentially giving Intel's Ohio fab a near-term utilization anchor. For the portfolio, it is a read-through on Washington's accelerating push to onshore advanced memory alongside logic — watch for CHIPS Act incentive angle and any HBM supply implications.

- SK Hynix is in active discussions with Intel about producing memory chips in the United States for the first time, with Intel's Ohio fab referenced as the potential site.
- A joint-venture structure is being weighed, per Reuters sourcing; the talks are described as driven at least partly by Washington pressure.
- Both companies' shares rose on the report; headlines note the deal would also help ease a memory supply crunch.
- No terms, investment figures, or timeline have been confirmed in the headlines — deal structure and close remain speculative.
