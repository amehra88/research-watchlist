## SK Hynix designates $4 billion US factory as key AI memory production base

Designating the US facility as an AI memory hub de-risks SK Hynix's HBM exposure to export control or geopolitical disruption and strengthens its position with US hyperscalers under political pressure to source domestically. This is a positive long-duration strategic signal for SK Hynix's AI memory franchise.

- SK Hynix has designated its $4B US factory as a strategic production base for AI memory.
- The designation confirms the facility's role in HBM supply for the US market.
- Onshoring AI memory production aligns with US semiconductor policy objectives and reduces geopolitical supply risk.
- Positions SK Hynix as a domestic-supply AI memory vendor for US hyperscaler customers facing domestic sourcing pressure.
