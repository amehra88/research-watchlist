## Fervo Energy Signs 396-MW, 15-Year Geothermal Deal with Google; Stock Surges ~24%

Google committing to a 15-year, 396 MW geothermal block is one of the clearest signals yet that AI data center power constraints are structural and durable, not transitory; for the portfolio, it validates the thesis that power availability — not compute silicon — is the binding constraint on AI buildout pacing through at least 2028.

- Fervo Energy (FRVO) signed a 396-megawatt, 15-year geothermal power agreement with Google — described across outlets as the largest geothermal deal yet
- FRVO stock surged approximately 24% on the announcement
- The Utah project is expected to come online in 2028 per coverage
- Deal confirms hyperscaler urgency to lock in firm, always-on power amid grid constraints
