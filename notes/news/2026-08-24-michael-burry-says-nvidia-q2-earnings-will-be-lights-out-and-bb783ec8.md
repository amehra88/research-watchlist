## Michael Burry Predicts 'Lights Out' Nvidia Q2 Earnings While Flagging 'Circular' Revenue Structure

Burry's circular-revenue framing directly challenges the quality, not just the size, of Nvidia's reported growth — even a Q2 beat does not resolve the structural question of whether AI capex reflects terminal demand or a temporary feedback loop, and this narrative will intensify scrutiny on hyperscaler forward guidance through earnings season.

- Michael Burry publicly states Nvidia's Q2 earnings will be 'lights out' — characterizing the expected result as a strong beat.
- He simultaneously claims '100% of announced revenue is circular,' framing hyperscaler AI capex spend as a self-reinforcing loop that may not reflect genuine end demand.
- The dual claim — strong near-term result, structurally suspect revenue quality — is the most concise public articulation of the AI capex sustainability bear case to date.
- Coverage is from a single outlet; Burry's track record of attention-generating but sometimes prescient calls warrants PM-level awareness regardless of conviction level.
