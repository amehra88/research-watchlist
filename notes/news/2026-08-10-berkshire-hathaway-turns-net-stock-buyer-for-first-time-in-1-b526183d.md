## Berkshire Hathaway Turns Net Stock Buyer for First Time in 14 Quarters Under Greg Abel

Berkshire's reversal after 14 quarters of net selling is a rare sentiment anchor for institutional investors; for a tech-focused PM it signals a more constructive macro backdrop and may reduce the discount the market has been applying to longer-duration growth equities.

- Greg Abel-led Berkshire Hathaway has ended a 14-quarter consecutive run as a net seller of equities, turning net buyer.
- The reversal represents roughly 3.5 years of sustained selling giving way to a risk-on posture at one of the most closely watched capital allocators.
- Coverage frames the shift as a potential macro risk-appetite inflection signal across equities broadly.
- The headline marks Abel's tenure as the visible decision-making context, distinct from Buffett-era positioning.
