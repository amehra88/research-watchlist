## Generac surges on large Amazon data center generator supply agreement

A multi-billion-dollar generator commitment from Amazon is hard evidence that power infrastructure — not just chips — is a binding constraint on AI data center expansion; energy and power-generation names become a read-through sector, and the deal underscores the physical-infrastructure intensity of the AI capex cycle.

- Generac announced a significant generator supply deal with Amazon to power data centers, sending GNRC shares sharply higher — outlets report the stock move in a 16–18% range.
- Deal size is disputed across outlets: the majority of headlines cite $2.4 billion while one outlet reports $8 billion; the figure should be treated as unconfirmed pending primary source.
- Broader AI power infrastructure stocks also rallied on the news, suggesting the market read this as a sector-wide demand signal.
- The deal directly confirms hyperscaler power-infrastructure buildout continues at pace, with Amazon as a confirmed large buyer of backup generation capacity.
