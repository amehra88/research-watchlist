## ASML Navigates U.S. China EUV Export Restrictions Against a Fully Booked EUV Pipeline

For ASML holders, the key risk is whether China restrictions become a structural revenue ceiling as non-China fabs absorb available tool slots — the fully booked pipeline suggests demand is not the risk, but geopolitical containment may durably shrink the addressable market versus pre-restriction baselines.

- ASML faces dual pressure: U.S.-imposed restrictions on EUV shipments to China, and a fully booked non-China EUV pipeline.
- The restrictions cap China-addressable revenue even as overall demand is robust.
- A fully booked pipeline means the binding constraint is geopolitically imposed, not demand-driven.
- Coverage frames this as a two-front challenge with no near-term resolution.
