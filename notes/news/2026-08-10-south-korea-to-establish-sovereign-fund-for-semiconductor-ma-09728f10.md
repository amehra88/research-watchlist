## South Korea to Establish Sovereign Fund for Semiconductor Materials, Parts, and Equipment

Sovereign capital directed at semiconductor input resilience is a structural positive for Korean chipmakers; it signals Seoul's intent to reduce exposure to upstream supply disruptions, directly de-risking the foundry and memory thesis for both Samsung and SK Hynix.

- A South Korean official confirmed plans to establish a government fund dedicated to the semiconductor materials, parts, and equipment supply chain.
- The move carries explicit geopolitical framing — domestic supply-chain resilience is the stated rationale.
- Primary implied beneficiaries are South Korean chipmakers, including Samsung (005930.KS) and SK Hynix (000660.KS).
- No fund size, launch timeline, or specific scope is provided in the headline.
