## Zhongji Innolight Acquires Stake in Power Filter Maker Jones Tech for ~1.75 Billion Yuan

Zhongji Innolight integrating into power components signals that China's optical players are building vertical cost and supply-chain advantages at scale; this is an indirect competitive pressure on Western optical interconnect suppliers — including COHR — competing in AI networking infrastructure.

- Chinese optical transceiver maker Zhongji Innolight is acquiring a stake in Jones Tech, a power filter manufacturer, for approximately 1.747–1.75 billion yuan (outlets differ slightly on stake size: 10.47% vs. approximately 11%).
- Jones Tech shares hit the daily limit-up following the announcement.
- The deal represents vertical integration by a leading Chinese optical transceiver supplier into power components.
- China's optical supply chain is consolidating, with indirect competitive implications for Western optical interconnect peers.
