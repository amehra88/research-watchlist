## DOJ Opens Probe into Nvidia's ~$20 Billion Groq Acquisition

If the DOJ challenges the deal, NVDA's vertical integration roadmap stalls at a pivotal moment in the AI inference cycle; the probe also signals regulators are willing to scrutinize AI-compute M&A, which may chill NVDA's future acquisition optionality.

- The Department of Justice has opened an antitrust probe into Nvidia's approximately $20 billion acquisition of Groq
- NVDA shares fell on the news
- The probe introduces material regulatory uncertainty to the deal's completion timeline
- A block or extended review would constrain NVDA's strategy to consolidate AI compute across training and inference layers
