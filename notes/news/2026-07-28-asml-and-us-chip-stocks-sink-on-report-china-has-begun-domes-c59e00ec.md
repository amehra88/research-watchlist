## ASML and US chip stocks sink on report China has begun domestic DUV lithography tool production

Chinese DUV self-sufficiency is the core bear case for ASML's export-control premium; if the report is accurate, it accelerates the timeline for China to reduce dependence on ASML tools and undermines the regulatory moat embedded in the stock's multiple — a thesis-level risk event, not routine sector rotation.

- Reports attributed to The Information indicated China has begun domestic production of DUV chip-making equipment.
- ASML shares fell sharply on the news; FactSet sentiment is flagged as Negative and US chip stocks declined in sympathy.
- DUV tools are the segment of ASML's portfolio most exposed to China demand and most directly constrained by export controls.
- Domestic Chinese DUV capability, if confirmed, would directly erode the export-control moat underpinning ASML's pricing power in that segment.
- The report is attributed to a single outlet; no official confirmation from Chinese authorities or ASML is noted in the headlines.
