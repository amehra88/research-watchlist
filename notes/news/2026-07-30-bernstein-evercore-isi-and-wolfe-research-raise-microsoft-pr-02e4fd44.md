## Bernstein, Evercore ISI, and Wolfe Research Raise Microsoft Price Targets on Azure Growth; Stock Up After Earnings

Broad sell-side target upgrades on the same day as a double-digit stock pop confirm that MSFT's Azure beat reset estimates materially upward; the 'steady capex' read is meaningful because it defuses the hyperscaler overspend narrative that has pressured the group.

- At least three sell-side firms — Bernstein, Evercore ISI, and Wolfe Research — raised Microsoft price targets citing Azure growth following Q4 results.
- One headline cites an outperform rating with a $550 target on strong Azure growth.
- Microsoft shares rose roughly 9% on the day, per one headline; another cites a ~10% surge — outlets differ slightly on the intraday figure.
- Steady capex spending alongside revenue growth was cited positively, suggesting the market viewed the spend-to-return ratio favorably.
