## Samsung, SK Hynix, and Hyundai Accelerate $100 Billion U.S. Investment Push

A $100B Korean-conglomerate U.S. commitment reshapes the competitive map for domestic foundry players and alters HBM supply geography; PM should assess second-order effects on TSM's U.S. positioning and on existing HBM incumbents.

- Samsung, SK Hynix, and Hyundai are jointly accelerating a $100 billion U.S. investment commitment.
- The move is a direct response to CHIPS Act incentives and geopolitical pressures reshaping semiconductor supply chains.
- Expansion covers U.S. foundry capacity and, given SK Hynix's profile, likely HBM-related manufacturing.
- The scale and multi-conglomerate coordination signal durable, not opportunistic, onshoring intent.
