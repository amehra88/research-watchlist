## Marvell Falls 7% as Google Custom AI Chip Revenue Is Deferred to Fiscal 2029; Intel Slips, Nvidia Holds

A deferral to FY2029 calls into question the ASIC revenue ramp assumptions underpinning MRVL's and peer networking-silicon multiples; it simultaneously delays the displacement threat to Nvidia's GPU position — a read that explains NVDA's relative resilience and is the cleanest framework for understanding today's cross-name divergence.

- Marvell declined roughly 7% after headlines indicated Google's custom AI chip revenue payoff is now expected in fiscal year 2029.
- Intel also slipped on the news while Nvidia moved little, suggesting the market reads this as a MRVL-specific timeline issue.
- A fiscal 2029 payoff represents a multi-year push-out from near-term expectations that had been embedded in MRVL's multiple.
- Two outlets (24/7 Wall St., Yahoo Finance) report consistent directional moves across all three names.
- Story is a concrete custom-silicon timeline data point: hyperscaler ASIC ramp is later than previously priced.
