## Walmart to Accept Apple Pay and Google Pay Starting August 24 After Years of Holdout

Walmart's acceptance is a step-change in US contactless payment reach given its position as the largest US retailer by transaction volume — incremental take-rate economics flow into Apple's and Google's services segments, and the credibility effect may accelerate adoption among other major merchant holdouts.

- Walmart will begin accepting Apple Pay and Google Pay on August 24, per the single-outlet headline.
- The move ends years of Walmart's documented resistance to the two dominant mobile payment platforms.
- The change directly adds Walmart's transaction volume to Apple's and Google's payment ecosystems.
- No take-rate terms, volume estimates, or exclusivity conditions are confirmed in the headline.
