## Broadcom Expands Aggressively into Vendor Financing for Hyperscaler Chip Deployments

Vendor financing can accelerate hyperscaler chip adoption but shifts credit risk onto Broadcom's balance sheet — a capital allocation tradeoff that could constrain AVGO's buyback capacity and introduce a new form of customer concentration risk worth monitoring as the program scales.

- Broadcom is moving into vendor financing to fund customer chip deployments, per a single Seeking Alpha report.
- The strategy ties hyperscaler customers more tightly to AVGO by reducing their upfront capital requirements.
