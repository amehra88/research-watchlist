## Cisco Shares Gain Intraday on Strong Earnings Before After-Hours Services Miss Reversal

The intraday-to-AH reversal on CSCO illustrates how rapidly the market re-prices on revenue mix details when enterprise AI adoption is the stated growth catalyst; services growth is the measurable KPI on that thesis, and a miss — even against a record headline quarter — is treated as a thesis test.

- CSCO shares rose approximately 2.9% intraday on the day of its earnings release.
- Initial market reaction characterized the results as strong, consistent with the intraday gain.
- After-hours trading subsequently reversed the gain as a services revenue miss emerged — the full picture is a beat on headline, reversal on mix.
- Together with the AH coverage, this cluster confirms the earnings session followed a positive-to-negative arc driven by services weakness.
