## Oracle AI chips ran at 97.9% utilization last quarter, illustrating GPU scarcity for Nvidia

97.9% utilization at Oracle is hard empirical evidence that GPU supply constraints are real and ongoing, reinforcing NVDA's pricing power thesis and validating continued AI infrastructure capex; it also highlights Oracle's own capacity risk if GPU supply does not expand to meet its demand.

- Oracle reported AI chip utilization of 97.9% last quarter, leaving virtually no idle capacity buffer.
- The Motley Fool explicitly frames this as evidence of what a GPU shortage looks like for Nvidia.
- Near-full utilization at a large cloud operator confirms that demand is absorbing all available GPU supply with no slack.
- This is corroborating, real-world evidence for the thesis that NVDA supply-demand tightness is durable.
