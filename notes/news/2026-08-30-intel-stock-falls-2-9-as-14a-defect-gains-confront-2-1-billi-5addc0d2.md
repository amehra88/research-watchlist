## Intel stock falls 2.9% as 14A process defects emerge alongside $2.1 billion foundry loss

This is a thesis-level risk event, not a routine miss.

- A $2.1B foundry loss underscores the structural cost burden of Intel's IFS buildout.
- The dual negative — yield/defect headwind plus large financial loss — compounds thesis risk simultaneously.
