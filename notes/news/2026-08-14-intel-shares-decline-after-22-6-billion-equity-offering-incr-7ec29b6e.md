## Intel Shares Decline After $22.6 Billion Equity Offering Increases Share Count by 4.8%

A $22.6B equity raise confirms Intel's foundry ambitions require sustained external capital at scale; the dilution drag makes the path to foundry profitability longer and more uncertain, with competitive implications for TSMC's market position and for the broader foundry capacity thesis.

- Intel announced a $22.6B equity offering that increases share count by 4.8%, per the headline.
- The stock declined in response, a typical reaction to dilutive equity issuance.
- The scale of the raise signals continued heavy capital requirements for Intel's foundry build-out program.
- 4.8% dilution is material and creates direct EPS headwind on a per-share basis.
