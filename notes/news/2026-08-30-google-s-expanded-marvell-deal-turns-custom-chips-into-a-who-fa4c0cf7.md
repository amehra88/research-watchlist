## Google-Marvell deal expands from custom silicon to full data center build

A full data center build engagement is a step-change from chip supply — it expands MRVL's revenue opportunity per customer significantly and validates the hyperscaler-driven custom silicon supercycle thesis. Concentration risk also rises: MRVL becomes more strategically tied to a single hyperscaler.

- Google has expanded its existing custom chip engagement with Marvell to encompass a full data center build, per reporting.
- The move represents a material TAM expansion for MRVL beyond discrete chip design into broader infrastructure delivery.
- Deepens hyperscaler dependency: MRVL becomes more embedded in GOOGL's end-to-end AI infrastructure stack.
- Signals hyperscalers are moving custom silicon partnerships upstream into system-level architecture decisions.
