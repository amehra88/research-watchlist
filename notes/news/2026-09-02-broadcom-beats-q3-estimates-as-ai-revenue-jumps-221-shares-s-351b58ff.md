## Broadcom Q3 Beats Estimates as AI Revenue Surges 221%; Shares Slide on Soft Guidance

AVGO is the primary read-through for custom silicon demand from hyperscalers; a blowout AI revenue print paired with a guidance miss suggests hyperscaler ASIC deployment schedules may be stretching, which has direct implications for the broader custom-chip thesis and for timing expectations across the space.

- Broadcom reported Q3 results above consensus, with AI revenue up 221% year-over-year.
- Despite the top-line beat, shares declined on guidance that came in below expectations.
- The soft guidance introduces uncertainty around the pace of hyperscaler custom ASIC deployment timing.
- AI revenue growth in absolute terms remains exceptional; the market is pricing the forward, not the quarter.
