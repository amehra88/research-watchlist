## Nvidia is financing customer GPU purchases, acting as a credit facility for its own buyers

Vendor financing can mask underlying demand softness and introduces credit risk concentration at Nvidia; if neocloud or smaller hyperscaler customers struggle to service GPU-related debt, NVDA faces both potential credit losses and a demand air-pocket — a material risk to monitor alongside the prevailing scarcity narrative.

- Nvidia has taken on a role financing its customers' GPU purchases, effectively functioning as a lender to its own buyer base.
- The 'credit line' framing suggests the arrangement is meaningful in scale, not incidental.
- Vendor financing may be supporting demand that would otherwise be constrained by customers' own capital limitations.
- This concentrates credit risk on NVDA's balance sheet if GPU-financed customers face financial stress.
