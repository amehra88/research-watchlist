## Samsung Stock Rebounds 1.75% After Unveiling Shareholder Return Program of Up to KRW110 Trillion

A large capital return commitment from Samsung functions as a management-set floor under the stock, signaling that leadership views cycle recovery as more likely than sustained deterioration. For a PM tracking the memory recovery thesis, Samsung's decision to return capital rather than build reserves is a positive directional signal on cycle conviction.

- Samsung Electronics announced a shareholder return program of up to KRW110 trillion.
- The stock rebounded 1.75% following the announcement.
- The scale of the capital return signals management confidence in the semiconductor cycle recovery.
- Returning capital at this magnitude rather than hoarding it is a constructive cycle-timing signal from management.
