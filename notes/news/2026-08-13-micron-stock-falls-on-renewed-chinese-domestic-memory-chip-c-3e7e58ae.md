## Micron Stock Falls on Renewed Chinese Domestic Memory-Chip Competition Threat

Chinese domestic memory competition is a structural thesis risk for MU's HBM and DRAM share premium — if Chinese suppliers close the technology gap faster than the market expects, MU's AI memory pricing power and share assumptions compress meaningfully; this warrants a conviction review on HBM durability.

- Micron (MU) shares declined on coverage of a fresh competitive threat from Chinese domestic memory chip makers.
- The concern centers on potential displacement of MU's market position — including in HBM — by Chinese suppliers.
- This is a geopolitical and competitive risk event, not a company-specific operational miss.
- Barron's treatment suggests the threat is viewed as material rather than background noise.
