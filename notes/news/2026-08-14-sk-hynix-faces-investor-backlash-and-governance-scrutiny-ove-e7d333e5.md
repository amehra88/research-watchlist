## SK Hynix Faces Investor Backlash and Governance Scrutiny Over Potential Solidigm Nasdaq Listing

A Solidigm IPO in a recovering NAND cycle could be seen as monetizing a cyclical peak at the parent's expense; governance friction and capital-allocation questions are a risk to SK Hynix's valuation multiple, and the outcome may signal how management prioritizes NAND vs. HBM investment.

- SK Hynix is reportedly considering a Nasdaq listing for Solidigm, its NAND storage subsidiary.
- Investors are pushing back on the plan, raising governance concerns around capital allocation in the NAND segment.
- The potential IPO would partially separate Solidigm from the SK Hynix parent.
- Backlash signals investor skepticism about whether a listing serves parent-company shareholders rather than unlocking value.
