## Druckenmiller Q2 2026 13F: Exits Micron and Intel, Adds Amazon

A simultaneous Druckenmiller exit from MU and INTC in Q2 2026 is a high-signal smart-money read against the memory and legacy-chip recovery trade; the paired AMZN add implies conviction that AI infrastructure value is compounding at the platform layer, not the component layer—a direct thesis test for any long MU or INTC positioning.

- Druckenmiller's Q2 2026 13F filing discloses exits from both Micron (MU) and Intel (INTC).
- Amazon (AMZN) was added to the portfolio in the same filing period.
- The move constitutes a rotation out of semiconductor cycle names toward a hyperscaler cloud platform.
- Three watchlist names are directly implicated: MU, INTC, and AMZN.
