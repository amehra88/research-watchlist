## OpenAI Selects Broadcom for Its First Custom AI Chip, Intensifying Custom Silicon Race

OpenAI's first custom silicon commitment going to Broadcom rather than NVDA signals a structural shift in how frontier labs will source compute at scale; AVGO gains a strategic design win while NVDA faces incremental share erosion from the custom ASIC vector.

- OpenAI has chosen Broadcom as its partner for its inaugural custom AI chip design
- Coverage frames this as dramatically escalating the race for the custom silicon position behind NVDA
- AVGO gains a marquee frontier-AI-lab customer, broadening its custom ASIC business
- Headlines characterize the competitive threat to NVDA as "dangerous" as hyperscalers and AI labs diversify away from merchant silicon
