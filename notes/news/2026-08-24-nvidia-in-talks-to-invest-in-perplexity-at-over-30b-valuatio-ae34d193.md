## Nvidia in Talks to Invest in AI Search Startup Perplexity at Over $30 Billion Valuation

Nvidia taking an equity stake in an AI search company at $30B+ valuation signals management conviction that inference-demand compounding is durable enough to warrant application-layer exposure — and suggests the company is building a strategic ecosystem moat beyond the hardware cycle.

- Nvidia is in discussions to invest in Perplexity at a valuation exceeding $30 billion, per The Information as cited by two outlets.
- The investment would extend Nvidia's strategic positioning beyond GPU hardware into the AI-native application layer.
- Perplexity operates in the AI search and inference-at-scale space, making this a signal of where Nvidia believes consumer AI monetization accrues.
- Both outlets confirm the $30B+ valuation and The Information sourcing; the deal is described as talks, not a closed transaction.
