## Microsoft Expands Maia Custom AI Chip Output at TSMC

Maia scaling at TSMC confirms Microsoft's commitment to custom AI silicon, which over time applies structural pressure to NVDA's GPU wallet share at one of its most significant accounts; for TSM, it adds a diversified AI-chip revenue stream and validates its position as the preferred advanced-node foundry beyond NVDA.

- Microsoft is scaling production of its Maia AI chip at TSMC.
- This confirms an active silicon diversification effort by a major hyperscaler away from reliance on third-party GPU suppliers.
- TSMC gains incremental AI-chip volume from a non-NVDA hyperscaler customer.
- The move reinforces the broader trend of large-cap technology companies developing proprietary AI silicon.
