## Nvidia acquires Hugging Face for ~$12.9–13 billion, extending from silicon into open-source model distribution

If Nvidia controls the leading open-source model hub, it gains a new lever to keep developers on Nvidia compute — compressing the moat of cloud-neutral AI platforms and positioning Nvidia as a vertical competitor to the AI software layer. For NVDA longs this is a potential software-margin unlock; for AI-native software names it signals a more aggressive Nvidia ecosystem encroachment.

- Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face; two outlets cite $12.9B, one rounds to $13B — figures are consistent and represent the same deal.
- The acquisition moves Nvidia beyond chip hardware into the dominant open-source model hub and distribution layer.
- Coverage across three outlets frames the deal as potentially reshaping AI ecosystem control.
- The move raises competitive stakes for cloud-neutral AI platform players by coupling hardware dominance with model distribution.
