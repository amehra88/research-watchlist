## Samsung and Broadcom ink $200B AI chip and memory pact through 2030

A multi-year $200B custom-silicon and memory commitment to Samsung foundry intensifies the Samsung-vs-TSMC competition for AI ASIC workloads and signals Broadcom is diversifying its fabrication relationships — a risk to TSMC's custom-AI share and a potential re-rating catalyst for Samsung foundry credibility.

- Samsung and Broadcom have signed a partnership covering AI chip fabrication and memory supply valued at $200 billion with a stated term extending through 2030.
- The deal spans both custom silicon foundry work and memory, positioning Samsung as a vertically integrated one-stop AI semiconductor supplier for Broadcom.
- Outlets characterize the agreement as potentially reshaping the AI chip market, particularly against a backdrop described as a memory crunch.
- A 2030 end-date provides substantial revenue visibility for Samsung's foundry business and multi-year certainty for Broadcom's custom ASIC roadmap.
- The announcement arrived alongside Samsung's broader US tech supply deals, suggesting a coordinated wave of AI partnership disclosures from Samsung this week.
