## Samsung Begins Tesla AI5 Chip Prototype Production, Narrowing Foundry Gap with TSMC

Samsung winning the Tesla AI5 prototype is a meaningful proof point for Samsung Foundry's competitive recovery — if prototype yields are satisfactory and it moves to volume, it would modestly erode TSMC's share of high-profile AI chip production and validate Samsung as a credible second-source option for hyperscalers and automakers.

- Samsung has started prototype production of Tesla's AI5 chip, per the headline.
- Coverage frames this win as Samsung narrowing its competitive gap with TSMC in advanced foundry.
- Tesla's AI5 engagement confirms continued investment in proprietary AI compute for its physical AI roadmap.
