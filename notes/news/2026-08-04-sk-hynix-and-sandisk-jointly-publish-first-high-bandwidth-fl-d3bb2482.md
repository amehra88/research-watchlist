## SK Hynix and SanDisk jointly publish first High Bandwidth Flash standard targeting AI memory workloads

An open HBF standard with SK Hynix as lead author diversifies the AI memory stack beyond HBM — a category SK Hynix already dominates; if hyperscalers adopt HBF for inference-tier storage, it expands SK Hynix's AI-memory TAM rather than cannibalizing it, which is an additive thesis read.

- SK Hynix and SanDisk co-released the first HBF (High Bandwidth Flash) specification, unveiled at FMS 2026.
- The spec targets up to 512 GB capacity and up to 3 TB/s bandwidth — figures appearing explicitly in headline coverage.
- HBF is positioned as a new flash-based AI memory standard, framed alongside and potentially in competition with HBM.
- Broad Korean and trade-press coverage signals wide industry attention to the announcement.
