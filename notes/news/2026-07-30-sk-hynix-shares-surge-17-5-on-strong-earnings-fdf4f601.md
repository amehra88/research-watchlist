## SK Hynix Shares Surge ~17.5% on Strong Earnings

SK Hynix's outsized earnings-day surge is the clearest confirmation this cycle that HBM demand remains robust; the positive read-through extends to Micron (MU) on the memory side and to NVIDIA and the broader AI chip supply chain.

- SK Hynix (NASDAQ ADR: SKHY) rose approximately 17.5% following a strong earnings report.
- The magnitude of the move is exceptional for a large-cap memory name and implies a material earnings beat rather than an in-line result.
- SK Hynix is the leading HBM supplier to AI chip customers; a strong print directly corroborates AI-driven memory demand.
- The result supports the thesis that the memory upcycle — led by HBM for AI compute — is intact and potentially accelerating.
