## Apple agrees to Samsung memory price hikes for 2027, with further iPhone price increases described as unavoidable

Memory cost inflation passing through to iPhone pricing creates a demand elasticity risk for Apple at a time when handset replacement cycles are already extended; simultaneously it confirms Samsung's pricing power in the DRAM/NAND supply tightness environment, a positive read for memory ASP forecasts.

- Apple has reportedly agreed to higher memory pricing from Samsung for 2027 supply contracts.
- The headline explicitly states further iPhone price increases are unavoidable as a result of the cost increase.
- The story is a direct intersection of Samsung's memory pricing power and Apple's component cost structure.
- No specific magnitude of price increase for memory or iPhones is cited in the headline.
