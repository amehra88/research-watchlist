## Nvidia Q2 results reveal non-hyperscaler buyers outpacing Microsoft, Google, and Amazon as a customer class

Demand broadening beyond hyperscalers is a structural positive for NVDA's durability — it reduces single-customer-class exposure and suggests enterprise and sovereign AI buildout is accelerating. For hyperscaler-heavy portfolio theses, this may signal capex leadership is beginning to rotate.

- NVDA's Q2 results surfaced a new buyer class — non-hyperscalers — growing faster than the big three cloud providers.
- Customer mix diversification reduces hyperscaler revenue concentration risk for NVDA.
- MSFT, GOOGL, and AMZN remain significant buyers but are no longer the sole growth driver.
- Two independent outlets (Globe and Mail, Motley Fool) flagged this customer mix shift as the primary earnings signal.
