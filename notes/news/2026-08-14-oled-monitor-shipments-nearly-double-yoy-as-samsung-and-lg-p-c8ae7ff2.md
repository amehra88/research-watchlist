## OLED Monitor Shipments Nearly Double YoY as Samsung and LG Push Ultra-High Resolution

Near-doubling of OLED monitor shipments is a concrete demand signal for the premium PC upgrade cycle; positive read-through for display suppliers and PC OEMs exposed to the high-end consumer and prosumer segments.

- OLED monitor shipments grew approximately 98% year-over-year per the reporting outlet
- Samsung and LG identified as primary market participants committing to the ultra-high-resolution segment
- Growth is concentrated in the premium display tier, signaling a demand mix-shift rather than broad-unit expansion
- Read-through to PC demand: willingness-to-pay for premium monitors suggests a durable upgrade cycle underway
