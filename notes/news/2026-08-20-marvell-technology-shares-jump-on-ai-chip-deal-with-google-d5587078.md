## Marvell Issues $12.2B Stock Warrant to Google in Expanded Custom AI Chip Partnership Through 2033

A warrant-linked partnership running to 2033 structurally upgrades MRVL's ASIC revenue line and signals Google is meaningfully diversifying custom-compute sourcing — the direct second-order read is incremental pressure on Broadcom as the prior perceived custom-ASIC incumbent, which the same day's tape confirmed (see cluster 15dd71ad2caeab26).

- Marvell has granted Google a $12.2B stock warrant tied to a custom AI chip collaboration running through 2033, representing an unusually long-dated ASIC supply commitment.
- The deal structure — a warrant rather than cash — aligns Marvell's equity with Google's success and deepens partnership lock-in beyond a typical design-win.
- MRVL shares rose approximately 9.8% on the announcement, reflecting market recognition of durable, hyperscaler-anchored revenue visibility.
- Coverage across six outlets converges on the $12.2B and 2033 figures, suggesting these are confirmed disclosures rather than estimates.
