## Nvidia Purchase Obligations Balloon from $95B to $279B in Six Months, Raising Supply Concentration Questions

Purchase obligations at this scale are among the strongest publicly available confirmations that the AI infrastructure capex supercycle is intact and accelerating; the flip side is that a sudden capex pause or supply disruption becomes proportionally more damaging as concentration grows.

- NVDA's disclosed purchase obligations grew from $95B to $279B over roughly six months, per SEC filings.
- Michael Burry had publicly called the prior $95B figure 'troubling' in February.
- The scale of commitments implies massive forward demand locking by hyperscalers and/or large AI labs.
- Coverage explicitly flags supply concentration as a risk alongside the demand signal.
