## Samsung Developing Custom 8-Layer HBM4E for Nvidia Targeting Up to 18 Gbps Transfer Speeds

Samsung winning a custom HBM4E socket for Nvidia would shift HBM competitive dynamics — reducing SK Hynix's near-monopoly on Nvidia's memory stack and improving Samsung's revenue mix quality; it also signals Nvidia is diversifying its HBM supply base, which has supply-chain resilience implications for AI infrastructure capacity.

- Samsung is building a custom 8-layer HBM4E variant specifically for Nvidia, per the headline.
- The targeted transfer speed is up to 18 Gbps, a specification cited in the headline.
- If validated, this represents a supply-chain win for Samsung in the HBM4E generation against SK Hynix's incumbency.
- No volume commitments, timeline, or qualification status are specified in the available headline.
