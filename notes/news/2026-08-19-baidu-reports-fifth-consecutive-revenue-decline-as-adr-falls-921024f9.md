## Baidu Reports Fifth Consecutive Revenue Decline as ADR Falls Nearly 13%

Five consecutive quarters of decline reframes BIDU as a business model transition story, not a cyclical trough play; for any thesis that positions Baidu as a China AI monetization beneficiary, the burden of proof on when AI revenue offsets structural search erosion has materially increased.

- Baidu's results show a fifth consecutive quarter of revenue decline, confirming a multi-period structural deterioration trend
- The ADR fell nearly 13% on the earnings release, reflecting a sharply negative market reaction
- AI investment is not offsetting ongoing deterioration in the core advertising and search revenue base
- Five consecutive declines distinguish this as a structural pattern rather than a single-quarter miss
