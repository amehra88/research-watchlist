## Samsung Reported to Have Locked Up 70% of HBM Capacity Through 2031

If Samsung has effectively pre-purchased the majority of HBM output for five-plus years, it creates a structural split in the AI memory supply chain — Samsung-aligned customers gain supply certainty while Micron and SK Hynix face a narrowed addressable market for non-Samsung design wins, with direct implications for MU's AI revenue trajectory.

- A single outlet reports Samsung has pre-committed approximately 70% of HBM supply capacity through 2031 — unconfirmed by multiple sources.
- If accurate, this would materially narrow available HBM allocation for SK Hynix and Micron and their respective customers.
- NVDA, as a primary HBM consumer, would face a structurally tighter multi-year supply environment.
- The headline frames the memory shortage as far from resolution, a sustained-tightness signal for the AI infrastructure capex cycle.
