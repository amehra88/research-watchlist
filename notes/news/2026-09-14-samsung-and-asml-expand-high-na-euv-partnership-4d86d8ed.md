## Samsung and ASML Expand High-NA EUV Partnership for Leading-Edge Node Development

An expanded Samsung–ASML High-NA EUV commitment anchors ASML's forward order visibility and confirms that leading-edge node investment is proceeding at the foundry level; it also signals that Samsung is maintaining its sub-2nm roadmap ambition, keeping competitive pressure on TSMC's process leadership.

- Samsung and ASML have formalized an expansion of their High-NA EUV partnership.
- The agreement constitutes a concrete commitment to the leading-edge lithography roadmap.
- The expansion has direct revenue implications for ASML as the sole High-NA EUV system supplier.
- For Samsung, the partnership signals continued investment in sub-2nm process capability and competitive positioning against TSMC.
