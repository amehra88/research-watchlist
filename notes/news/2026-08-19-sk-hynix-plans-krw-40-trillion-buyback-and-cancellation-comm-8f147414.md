## SK Hynix Plans KRW 40 Trillion Buyback and Cancellation, Commits to 50%-Plus Free Cash Flow Return

A standing 50%-plus FCF return policy is a structural shift in SK Hynix's capital allocation framework that aligns the company with capital-return-oriented investors; combined with the buyback scale, this signals management's conviction that the AI memory cycle will generate durable free cash flow, raising the floor on downside scenarios.

- SK Hynix announced plans to repurchase and cancel KRW 40 trillion worth of shares.
- The company committed to returning at least 50% of its free cash flow to shareholders on an ongoing basis.
- After-hours trading surged sharply following the announcement, per the headline.
- The 50%-plus FCF return commitment is framed as a standing capital allocation policy, not a one-time event.
