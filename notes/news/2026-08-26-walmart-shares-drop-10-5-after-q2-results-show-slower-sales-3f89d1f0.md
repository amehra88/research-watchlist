## Walmart Falls 10.5% After Q2 Results Show Slower Sales Growth and Rising Inventory

Walmart missing on sales growth with rising inventory is one of the clearest consumer-health warning signals in the market — it suggests end-demand may be softening in ways that carry implications for discretionary tech spend and SMB software renewal rates. A tech PM should treat this as a macro tail-risk flag on consumer and small-business demand assumptions embedded in portfolio models.

- Walmart (WMT) shares dropped 10.5% following Q2 results that showed both slower sales growth and rising inventory levels.
- As the largest U.S. retailer, Walmart is a primary consumer demand bellwether — a miss carries macro read-through.
- Rising inventory alongside slowing sales raises the prospect of forward margin pressure from markdowns.
- The combination of top-line deceleration and inventory build is a classic signal of softening consumer demand.
