## Micron Pursues Long-Term Supply Agreements in Strategic Bid to Dampen Earnings Cyclicality

If Micron successfully locks in long-term supply agreements, it could structurally de-risk memory earnings cyclicality and support a higher valuation multiple by reducing the boom-bust discount; this is a potential re-rating catalyst to track as the strategy matures.

- Micron is actively seeking long-term supply commitments (SCAs) with customers as a structural strategy.
- The initiative is framed as an effort to reduce the historically severe earnings cyclicality characteristic of DRAM and NAND markets.
- No specific deal terms, counterparties, or financial targets are mentioned in the headline.
- The strategy mirrors SCA models employed by other capital-intensive semiconductor suppliers.
