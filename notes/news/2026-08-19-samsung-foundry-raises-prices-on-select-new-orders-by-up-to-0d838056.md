## Samsung Foundry Raises Prices on Select New Orders by Up to 15%; Chinese Customers Accept Steepest Hikes

Selective foundry pricing power returning at Samsung is a positive read on demand tightness; Chinese customers absorbing the steepest hikes signals robust China-side foundry demand despite geopolitical headwinds — a nuanced datapoint for the broader semiconductor cycle thesis.

- Samsung Foundry is raising prices on some new orders by up to 15%, per two Korean financial outlets.
- Chinese customers are specifically reported to be accepting the steepest price increases among the customer base.
- The pricing action is limited to select new orders, not a broad-based ASP reset across all nodes.
- Headlines frame the move as a direct demand signal and a competitive pricing development in foundry services.
