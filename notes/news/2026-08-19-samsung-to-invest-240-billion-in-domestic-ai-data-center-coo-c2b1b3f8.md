## Samsung to Invest ₩240 Billion in Domestic AI Data Center Cooling Equipment Manufacturing

Samsung entering AI datacenter cooling manufacturing at scale simultaneously validates demand in a supply-constrained segment and introduces a significant new competitive entrant; existing thermal management names benefit from the demand signal but face a longer-term competitive overhang.

- Samsung Electronics announced a ₩240 billion investment for domestic production of AI data center cooling equipment
- The facility will be located in Gwangju, South Korea, signaling a committed domestic supply chain buildout
- The move positions Samsung as a new participant in the thermal management segment of the AI infrastructure supply chain
- Investment specificity and scale suggest a strategic entry rather than a pilot or exploratory initiative
