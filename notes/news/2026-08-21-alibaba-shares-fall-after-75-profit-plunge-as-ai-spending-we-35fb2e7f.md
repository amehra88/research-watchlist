## Alibaba Shares Fall After 75% Profit Plunge as AI Spending Weighs, Despite 45% Cloud Growth

Alibaba's result is a direct data point on the AI-investment-vs-profitability trade-off at Chinese hyperscalers; the 75% profit drop despite 45% cloud growth suggests the market's concern is not demand but the duration and magnitude of AI capex — a read-through to valuation multiples for any Chinese tech or cloud position.

- Alibaba's profit fell 75% year-over-year, attributed to the weight of AI infrastructure spending.
- Cloud revenue grew 45% year-over-year, but this growth was insufficient to offset the profit headwind from AI investment.
- Shares declined on the result.
- This pattern — strong cloud growth, weak profitability — mirrors the tension seen at other hyperscalers investing heavily in AI.
