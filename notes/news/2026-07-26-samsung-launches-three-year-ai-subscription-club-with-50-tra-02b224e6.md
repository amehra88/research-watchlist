## Samsung launches three-year 'AI Subscription Club' with 50% trade-in buyback guarantee on Galaxy Z Flip 8

Samsung's move to engineer a three-year device upgrade cycle with a guaranteed residual value directly targets the loyalty dynamic that has underpinned iPhone retention; if scaled, it could reduce Galaxy churn and provide more predictable hardware revenue visibility — the Apple playbook applied to Android.

- Samsung introduced a subscription program called the 'AI Subscription Club' tied to the Galaxy Z Flip 8 handset.
- The program guarantees a 50% buyback on trade-ins over a three-year term — both figures explicitly stated in headlines.
- This is framed as Samsung's first such program, per the coverage.
- The initiative functions as a consumer retention and device upgrade lock-in mechanism.
- Geographic availability and subscriber pricing details are not present in the headlines.
