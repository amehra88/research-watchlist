## Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan Commits $12M of Personal Capital to Company Capital Raise

CEO personal capital co-investment in a dilutive raise is among the strongest insider alignment signals available and reduces the principal-agent concern around turnaround credibility. For tech PMs watching INTC's multi-year restructuring, this raises the conviction bar on management commitment, though it does not itself resolve execution risk.

- Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan is reportedly investing $12 million of his own money into an Intel capital raise.
- Analyst Daniel Newman characterized the move as a 'strong sign,' highlighting insider alignment.
