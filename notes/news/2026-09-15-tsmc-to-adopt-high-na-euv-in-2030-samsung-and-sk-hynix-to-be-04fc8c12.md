## TSMC targets High NA EUV adoption in 2030; Samsung and SK Hynix aim for volume production in 2028

Concrete High NA EUV adoption timelines from the three largest semiconductor manufacturers underpin multi-year revenue visibility for ASML, while the earlier memory-side adoption could reshape process leadership dynamics between logic and memory fabs and affect the competitive positioning of Samsung's foundry business relative to TSMC.

- TSMC has communicated a 2030 timeline for adopting High NA EUV lithography in its production process.
- Samsung and SK Hynix are targeting volume production using High NA EUV starting in 2028, two years ahead of TSMC.
- The announced timelines provide concrete multi-year demand visibility for ASML's High NA EUV equipment roadmap.
- The 2028 vs. 2030 gap suggests memory producers may be earlier adopters of the next lithography node than leading-edge logic foundries.
