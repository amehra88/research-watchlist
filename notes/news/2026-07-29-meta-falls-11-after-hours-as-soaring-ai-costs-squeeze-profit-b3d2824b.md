## Meta Falls ~11% After Hours as Soaring AI Costs Squeeze Profits and Q3 Outlook Disappoints

Meta's after-hours reaction is the clearest market signal on investor tolerance for AI capex escalation — the selloff indicates the Street is no longer pricing spend unconditionally, making forward cost guidance a first-order variable for all hyperscaler positions.

- Meta shares fell approximately 11% after hours following its Q2 earnings release.
- Soaring AI costs are cited as the primary driver compressing profitability.
- Q3 guidance disappointed, adding a forward-looking overhang on top of the cost concern.
- The setup crystallizes the AI spend vs. near-term profitability tradeoff for hyperscalers.
