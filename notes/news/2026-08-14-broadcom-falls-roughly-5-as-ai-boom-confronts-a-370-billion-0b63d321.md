## Broadcom falls roughly 5% as AI boom confronts a $370 billion debt financing question

A multi-outlet, multi-percent selloff anchored to an AI debt sustainability narrative suggests the market is beginning to price in the financing risk of the AI capex super-cycle; as a major custom silicon and networking beneficiary, AVGO is a bellwether — pressure here has direct read-through to the broader AI infrastructure supply chain.

- AVGO dropped approximately 5% per Yahoo Finance, with the stated driver being uncertainty around $370 billion in AI-related debt financing.
- The headline frames the financing structure of the AI capex boom as a risk to AVGO's growth trajectory.
- The $370 billion figure appears directly in the headline and is the stated framing of the concern, not a verified market consensus figure.
- A second outlet (Benzinga, cluster 13affb170eb69f30) confirms the move at approximately 6% and names Apollo Global as a party to the financing question.
