## AMD Stock Rallies After CFO Raises AI Chip Addressable Market Forecast to $3 Trillion by 2030

A $3T TAM projection from AMD's CFO raises the competitive stakes for AI GPU and accelerator share; a larger implied market creates room for multiple winners but also sharpens the scrutiny on AMD's execution against NVDA and custom-silicon providers.

- AMD's CFO publicly raised the company's AI chip total addressable market estimate to $3 trillion by 2030.
- The stock moved higher on the announcement; outlets cite a range of roughly 3–6% (outlets differ on magnitude).
- The upward revision reflects AMD management's growing conviction in the long-term AI silicon demand cycle.
- The market-sizing statement is AMD's own guidance, not an independent third-party estimate.
