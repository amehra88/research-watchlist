## SK Hynix and SanDisk Jointly Publish High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) Specification via Open Compute Project

If HBF achieves OCP-backed adoption, it opens a NAND-based demand vector in AI infrastructure memory alongside DRAM/HBM — a structural positive for SK Hynix and SanDisk and a new competitive front worth monitoring as AI memory architecture evolves.

- SK Hynix and SanDisk co-released the HBF (High Bandwidth Flash) technical specification through the Open Compute Project.
- HBF is a flash-based memory standard for AI infrastructure, distinct from HBM (DRAM-based high-bandwidth memory).
- OCP publication signals a push for broad industry adoption beyond the two originators.
- This is a spec release, not a product or commercial availability announcement; timing is not specified in coverage.
