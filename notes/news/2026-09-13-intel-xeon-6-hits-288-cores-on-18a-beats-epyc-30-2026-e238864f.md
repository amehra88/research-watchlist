## Intel Xeon 6+ on 18A node claims 288 cores and 30% performance lead over AMD EPYC

- Intel claims a 30% performance advantage over AMD's competing EPYC server CPU.
- The 18A node is Intel's most advanced process, making this a dual validation of both product and process execution.
