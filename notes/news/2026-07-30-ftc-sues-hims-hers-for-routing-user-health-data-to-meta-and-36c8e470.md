## FTC Sues Hims & Hers for Routing User Health Data to Meta and Snap Ad Platforms

This is a second-order risk for Meta: the FTC is now pursuing third-party partners for feeding health data into Meta's ad system, which could force Meta to implement stricter inbound data filtering or expose it to co-regulatory scrutiny — a structural threat to the health and pharma ad vertical. Watch for whether follow-on filings name Meta directly as a respondent.

- The FTC filed suit against Hims & Hers Health alleging the company transmitted user health information to Meta and Snap's advertising infrastructure.
- Hims & Hers shares fell under selling pressure, with prices cited at $27.21 (down $2.11) and $26.06 (down $3.27) across two Reuters data points through the session.
- Hims & Hers issued a public response to the lawsuit; the substance of that response is not captured in the headlines.
- FactSet sentiment is negative; the suit directly names Meta's ad platform as a recipient of sensitive health data.
- The FTC action introduces regulatory and privacy risk to Meta's ad targeting system in the health and pharma vertical.
