## Netlist petitions US Customs to block Micron AI memory chip imports

A customs-block petition is a higher-urgency IP action than standard litigation — US Customs exclusion orders can move faster than court injunctions. Investors should track whether this escalates to an ITC exclusion order, which would directly impair MU's ability to ship AI memory into the US market.

- Netlist has filed a request with US Customs to block imports of Micron's AI memory chips at the border.
- The action is IP-driven, using customs channels rather than a court injunction as the primary mechanism.
- If pursued successfully, could create a supply disruption risk for Micron's HBM shipments.
- Likelihood and timeline of a customs exclusion order are not specified in the headline.
