## China Begins Domestic DUV Chipmaking Tool Production; ASML Shares Slide

If confirmed at scale, domestic Chinese DUV production structurally pressures ASML's China revenue and tests the durability of Western tech-control regimes—a significant risk to the lithography monopoly thesis underpinning ASML's valuation.

- China has started producing homegrown DUV lithography tools, according to a report from The Information.
- ASML shares fell on the news; FactSet sentiment is flagged Negative.
- The development signals a potential Chinese path around Western export controls on advanced semiconductor equipment.
- Bloomberg and Reuters amplified the report, lending high-credibility sourcing to a single originating story.
- The threat is specific to DUV tools, the segment most accessible to domestic Chinese replication.
