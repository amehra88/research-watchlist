## Apple to launch iPhone 18 in Pro-only configuration first, deferring the standard model past the holiday window

A Pro-first launch structurally skews iPhone 18's opening-quarter revenue mix toward higher-ASP units, which could support near-term gross margin and services attach; the flip side is execution risk if standard-model demand is larger than anticipated and the delayed availability costs units in the holiday window.

- Apple plans to release only Pro variants of the iPhone 18 ahead of the holiday sales period.
- The standard (non-Pro) model will follow at an unspecified later date.
- The strategy concentrates initial holiday-quarter unit volume in the premium tier.
- No launch dates, pricing figures, or unit volume estimates specified in coverage.
