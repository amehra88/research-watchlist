## Intel Plans $15 Billion Stock Sale Citing AI Demand

A $15 billion equity raise — one of Intel's largest capital markets events in recent memory — signals a major bet on AI capex tailwinds to justify significant near-term EPS dilution; it also validates Intel's foundry ambitions as a potential TSMC alternative in the AI era, which has implications for the foundry competitive landscape and TSMC's pricing power.

- Intel is selling $15 billion in equity, attributing the capital raise to increased AI-driven demand.
- An equity offering of this scale is materially dilutive to existing shareholders.
- Intel frames the raise as offensive — investing in AI positioning and foundry capacity — though market interpretation of motive is not addressed in the headline.
- The raise follows Intel's ongoing repositioning of its foundry and AI compute businesses.
