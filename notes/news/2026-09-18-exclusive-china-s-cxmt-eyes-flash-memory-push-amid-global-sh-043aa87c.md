## China's CXMT eyes entry into flash memory, targeting Samsung and YMTC amid global shortage

CXMT entering flash memory would add structurally low-cost Chinese supply into a tight market, posing a medium-term pricing risk for Samsung and Micron; it also raises the policy question of whether Western export controls will extend to NAND tooling, with direct implications for AMAT, LRCX, and KLAC exposure to China.

- CXMT, a Chinese memory firm, is reported to be planning a push into flash (NAND) memory, per an exclusive Reuters report.
- The company would directly compete with Samsung and domestic Chinese incumbent YMTC.
- The timing coincides with a described global NAND shortage, potentially disrupting supply-demand dynamics.
- The move carries geopolitical dimensions, advancing Chinese domestic semiconductor self-sufficiency in memory.
