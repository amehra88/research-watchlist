## OpenAI selects Broadcom for its first custom AI chip, intensifying the race for AI silicon alternatives

OpenAI adopting a custom AVGO chip signals that frontier model builders are beginning to diversify away from NVDA, a structural shift that could erode NVDA's pricing power over time while meaningfully expanding AVGO's addressable market in AI silicon.

- OpenAI has chosen Broadcom to design its first custom AI chip — a significant win that validates AVGO's custom silicon franchise at the world's highest-profile AI lab.
- The headline frames this as making 'the race for number two' dangerous, implying AVGO is now a credible platform challenger to NVDA in AI compute.
- The deal is a structural threat to NVDA's model of captive GPU dependency among frontier AI labs.
- Broadcom's ASIC-over-GPU approach gains a marquee reference customer, increasing its leverage with other potential hyperscaler and AI-lab clients.
