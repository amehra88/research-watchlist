## SK Hynix Evaluating Joint Venture Partner for China Plant as Export Control Compliance Pressures Mount

SK Hynix's China plant JV decision is a live test case for semiconductor companies managing US export control compliance — the chosen structure could become a template or cautionary precedent for peers with China fab exposure.

- SK Hynix is weighing bringing in a partner for its China manufacturing plant, per Finimize — a structural compliance adaptation to US export control restrictions on advanced semiconductor technology in China.
- A JV structure would allow SK Hynix to retain some China revenue exposure while distributing compliance and operational risk to a partner.
- The move has geopolitical dimensions: it signals how leading Korean chipmakers are actively navigating the US-China technology bifurcation rather than exiting China operations outright.
- Specific partner identity, ownership split, and timeline are not disclosed in the headline.
