## CSOP adopts flexible leverage on Samsung and SK Hynix ETF products amid chip-sector volatility

Structured-product managers treating current Korean memory volatility as acute enough to warrant leverage intervention can amplify near-term price swings in Samsung and SK Hynix if forced rebalancing occurs.

- ETF manager CSOP announced it is adjusting leverage flexibility on products tracking Samsung and SK Hynix amid elevated market volatility.
- The action is a risk-management response to sharp moves in Korean memory stocks driven by China competition fears.
- This is a product-level operational decision, not a fundamental statement about either company's outlook.
