## Chinese Tech Giants Shift Capital Allocation from Gaming to AI

Deliberate capex rotation from gaming to AI among China's largest platforms is a structural negative for NTES's gaming thesis and a positive for China AI infrastructure demand; the pace and sustainability of the shift will determine whether AI spend is additive or merely substitutive.

- Major Chinese technology companies are redirecting investment away from gaming and toward AI.
- BIDU, BABA, and NTES are named in coverage as part of the pivot.
- Structural capital reallocation rather than a tactical or quarter-specific move.
- Implies compression of gaming-segment investment and incremental AI infrastructure spend.
