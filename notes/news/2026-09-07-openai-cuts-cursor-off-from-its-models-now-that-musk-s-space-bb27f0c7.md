## OpenAI Cuts Cursor Off from Its Models After SpaceX Acquires the AI Coding Tool

OpenAI's willingness to cut off a paying customer to avoid enabling a Musk-controlled competitor signals that model access is now being wielded as a competitive weapon, not just a commercial product. For SPCX, it creates an urgent need to secure alternative model supply and raises the question of whether the Cursor acquisition thesis survives without OpenAI model access.

- OpenAI revoked Cursor's access to its models following SpaceX (SPCX) acquiring Cursor.
- The action is framed as OpenAI declining to supply a Musk-controlled entity with its models.
- This represents a platform and model-access risk for Cursor post-acquisition.
- Single-outlet (Yahoo Finance); terms of the SpaceX-Cursor acquisition are not disclosed in the headline.
