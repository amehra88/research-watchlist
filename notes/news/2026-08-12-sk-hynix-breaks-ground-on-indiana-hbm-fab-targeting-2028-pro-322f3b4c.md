## SK Hynix Breaks Ground on Indiana HBM Fab Targeting 2028 Production Start

US-based HBM capacity reduces concentration risk in the AI memory supply chain currently dominated by Korean fabs; the 2028 timeline sets expectations for when domestic supply could become material relative to NVDA and hyperscaler demand cycles.

- SK Hynix held a groundbreaking ceremony for its Indiana fabrication facility.
- The facility is targeted for HBM production beginning in 2028.
- Represents a US onshoring milestone for advanced memory manufacturing.
- Directly relevant to AI infrastructure supply chain security given HBM's centrality to GPU-adjacent compute.
