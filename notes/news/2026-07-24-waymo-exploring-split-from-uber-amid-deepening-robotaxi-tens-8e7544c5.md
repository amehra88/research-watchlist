## Waymo Exploring Split from Uber Amid Deepening Robotaxi Tensions

A Waymo-Uber split would force Alphabet to build or acquire its own ride-hail distribution layer, raising Waymo's capital requirements and strategic complexity; simultaneously, it strips Uber of its most credible autonomous vehicle partnership and increases competitive risk for the ride-hail incumbent.

- Waymo (Alphabet subsidiary) is exploring a separation from Uber, per Financial Times reporting cited in both FactSet feeds.
- Uber shares traded lower on the news.
- Coverage cites deepening tensions between the two parties as the driver.
- FactSet sentiment is neutral; no Alphabet share direction specified in the headlines.
