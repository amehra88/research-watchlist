## Intel Foundry grew 31% last quarter but posted a $2.1 billion operating loss

The 31%-growth / $2.1B-loss combination is the defining tension in the Intel foundry thesis: top-line traction is real but profitability is structurally distant, making the path to break-even and the adequacy of the capital raise the critical questions for underwriting the IFS bet.

- IFS (Intel Foundry Services) achieved 31% revenue growth in the most recent quarter.
- Despite top-line traction, the segment recorded a $2.1 billion loss in the same period.
- Unit economics are deeply negative: growth is not converting to profitability.
- The result sharpens the foundry viability debate — whether INTC can sustain this investment burn long enough to reach customer-win scale.
