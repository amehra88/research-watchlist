## SK Hynix in Talks to Lease Intel Ohio Fab for First U.S. Memory Production

If consummated, a SK Hynix lease of Ohio fab space would be the first U.S. memory manufacturing presence for a Korean major, with significant implications for domestic supply-chain policy and Intel's ability to generate revenue from underutilized capacity — a meaningful strategic pivot for both parties. For the semiconductor cycle thesis, incremental U.S. memory supply and shifting geography of production are data points worth tracking alongside any formal MOU announcement.

- SK Hynix is in exploratory discussions with Intel about leasing capacity at Intel's Ohio fabrication facility to establish a U.S.-based memory production footprint — a potential first for the company on American soil.
- No agreement has been confirmed; one outlet explicitly notes 'nothing confirmed,' characterizing the discussions as early-stage.
- Intel shares rose on the news, per headlines, reflecting market read of potential upside from monetizing idle Ohio capacity.
- A separate headline also references Intel signing an MOU with Egypt related to AI, though no details beyond the headline are available.
