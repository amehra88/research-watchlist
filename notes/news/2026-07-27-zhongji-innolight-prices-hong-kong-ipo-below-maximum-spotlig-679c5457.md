## Zhongji Innolight prices Hong Kong IPO below maximum, spotlighting Chinese AI optics competition

A publicly listed, better-capitalized Innolight intensifies pricing competition in AI data-center optics — a segment core to the COHR and LITE bull cases. Below-max pricing caps near-term sentiment upside but does not negate the structural competitive threat from a now-public Chinese rival.

- Chinese optical transceiver maker Zhongji Innolight priced its Hong Kong IPO below the maximum it had been marketing; one headline cites HK$980 per share as the pricing level.
- The listing is described in one outlet's headline as targeting a valuation around $6.8 billion.
- Pricing below the top of range signals tempered investor demand at peak valuation, though the deal still proceeded.
- Innolight is a direct competitor to COHR and LITE in AI data-center optical transceivers; its successful public listing raises the competitive profile and potential capital base of Chinese supply.
