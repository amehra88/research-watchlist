## SK Hynix exhausts SSD replacement inventory; drive prices have doubled, buyers receive face-value refunds

SSD prices doubling combined with SK Hynix exhausting replacement inventory is the sharpest concrete pricing signal for the NAND upcycle in today's digest; if spot prices at this level sustain, it foreshadows potential upward margin revisions for SK Hynix and supports the broader semiconductor cycle bull case.

- SSD drive prices have doubled since the original purchases, per the headline — meaning face-value refunds materially undercompensate buyers.
- This is the same warranty-shortage event as cluster 6d0a18af but with the price-doubling detail confirmed explicitly in the headline.
