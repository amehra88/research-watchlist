## Meta Stock Rises on Plans to Deploy Proprietary AI Chips for Cost Reduction

Meta's custom silicon push reduces merchant silicon spend — a marginal negative for NVDA exposure — and, if successful, sets a template for inference-cost compression that could materially improve Meta's AI unit economics and sustain higher capex intensity.

- Meta announced plans to deploy its own custom AI chips, with cost savings as the stated rationale.
- Shares rose on the announcement.
- The initiative signals a meaningful reduction in Meta's dependence on third-party merchant silicon for AI inference workloads.
