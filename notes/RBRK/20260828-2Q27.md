## 3. Forward guidance

**All guided metrics raised for FY27.** (8-K Ex-99.1, "Third Quarter and Fiscal Year 2027 Outlook")

- **Q3 FY27:** revenue $429–431M; non-GAAP subscription ARR contribution margin ~14%; non-GAAP EPS $0.07–0.09; ~230M weighted-average shares.
- **FY27:** subscription ARR $1,880–1,885M (~29% YoY); revenue $1,685–1,693M; non-GAAP ARR contribution margin ~15.5%; non-GAAP EPS $0.47–0.53; free cash flow $323–333M; ~228M weighted-average shares.

**Direction and tone.** Confident, and the raise is broad rather than selective. Management framed the half-year transition as entering "the second half of fiscal year 2027 from a position of strength" (CFO Choudary, 8-K Ex-99.1).

**Two things the guidance philosophy discloses.** First, the FY27 ARR guide of ~29% growth sits *below* the 33% just delivered, and analysts pressed on this (see §8). Second, and more revealing: H1 non-GAAP EPS came in at **$0.36**, yet the full-year guide is **$0.47–0.53** — implying only **$0.11–0.17** across H2, against a Q3 guide of $0.07–0.09. Management is guiding to roughly flat-to-down H2 profitability after an H1 that nearly doubled expectations. This is either genuine reinvestment (management flagged continued R&D and go-to-market spend, including scaling Identity Resilience and Agent Cloud) or a materially conservative guide. Given ten consecutive quarters of beating guidance (AI-summary paraphrase), the base case is conservatism — which is also precisely why the consensus-versus-actual percentages in §2 are inflated.

**Disclosed headwind:** material rights revenue declines ($4.7M this quarter vs. $14.2M a year ago). Normalized for material rights, revenue grew **43%** YoY, versus 38% reported — so the reported revenue line understates underlying momentum, and this headwind persists through FY27.

## 4. Thesis read by theme

### cybersecurity_competitive_landscape

- **Status:** **Confirm**
- **Evidence:** Named "a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Backup and Data Protection Platforms for the seventh consecutive year, positioned furthest in Vision" (8-K Ex-99.1, Recent Business Highlights). Customers with $100K+ subscription ARR reached **3,084, up 23% YoY**. Subscription ARR +33% to $1.66B with net new subscription ARR up 35% YoY. CEO Sinha, in the release: "Mythos and frontier AI models have fundamentally changed the cybersecurity landscape. This new reality demands not only machine speed cyber recovery but also autonomous runtime AI agent security." Management cited high competitive win rates and identity displacement wins (AI-summary paraphrase).
- **Implication:** Rubrik is taking share in a category that is itself being re-rated upward by AI-driven threat escalation, and doing so while accelerating rather than trading growth for margin. The competitive-landscape thesis is intact and if anything the category tailwind is stronger than the 2026-06-02 draft assumed.

### enterprise_ai_adoption

- **Status:** **Drift**
- **Evidence:** Rubrik launched "Rubrik AI, an agentic-first layer spanning Rubrik Security Cloud and Rubrik Agent Cloud" and "Rubrik Agent Cloud for Anthropic's Claude Code," alongside "Project Hourglass, a GSI alliance with Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA and Wipro to deploy it across enterprises" (8-K Ex-99.1). But on the call, in response to John DiFucci (Guggenheim) asking about Rubrik's AI-deployment readiness, Sinha emphasized that Rubrik is prepared to support customers **when they are ready** to deploy AI agents (AI-summary paraphrase, not a quotation), and management noted real-world agent adoption remains at an early stage.
- **Implication:** The thesis is not broken — Rubrik is credibly positioned and has built distribution ahead of demand — but the **mechanism has shifted**. Near-term revenue is being driven by AI-*threat*-induced demand for cyber resilience, not by customers' own enterprise AI deployments. The enterprise-AI-adoption leg is a FY28+ revenue story, not an FY27 one. Size the thesis on timing accordingly.

### ai_infrastructure_software

- **Status:** **Confirm**
- **Evidence:** Rubrik now describes itself as "the Security and AI Operations Company" (8-K Ex-99.1, About Rubrik) — a repositioning from pure data protection. Concretely: "Advanced Rubrik Annapurna, creating an AI-ready unstructured data layer for enterprise Data Intelligence platforms," and "Introduced Autonomous Business Recovery for cloud applications, powered by the Preemptive Recovery Engine," which "discovers application dependencies, validates clean recovery points, and rebuilds an organization's Minimum Viable Business across data, network, identity and configurations."
- **Implication:** Annapurna in particular is an infrastructure-layer claim, not a security-feature claim — Rubrik is arguing its backup estate is the natural AI-ready data substrate for the enterprise. That is the highest-optionality element of this print and the clearest support for raising ai_positioning. It is a claim, not yet revenue ("available today for qualified enterprise partners"), so it is confirming evidence for the thesis rather than proof of it.

### ai_agent_monetization

- **Status:** **Drift**
- **Evidence:** Rubrik Agent Cloud now has "four distinct pillars: Observability, Identity, Runtime Security, and Rewind" following the launch of Rubrik Agent Identity, which "operates alongside Rubrik's established SAGE governance framework" (8-K Ex-99.1). The call reported **over 15 paying RAC customers**. Against Keith Bachman's (BMO) challenging question on RAC competition and customer expectations, CFO Choudary noted the minimal RAC contribution to this year's ARR while expressing optimism for future growth (AI-summary paraphrase, not a quotation).
- **Implication:** The product architecture and the channel (Project Hourglass GSIs) are real and moving fast, but monetization is de minimis and **explicitly excluded from the FY27 guide**. This is the theme where the gap between narrative and P&L is widest. Drift, not Break: nothing contradicts the thesis, but anyone underwriting RAC revenue inside FY27 is underwriting something management is not. The tell to watch next quarter is whether Rubrik starts disclosing a RAC customer count or ARR contribution — voluntary disclosure would signal it has crossed materiality.

## 5. AI positioning signal

- **Current score:** 3 (analyst-draft, 2026-06-02, flagged pending this earnings cycle)
- **Evidence from this call:**
  - **Repositioning:** company now self-describes as "the Security and AI Operations Company" (8-K Ex-99.1).
  - **Product:** Rubrik AI (agentic-first autonomous layer with built-in guardrails); Rubrik Agent Identity (per-tool-call access control) added to SAGE governance framework; Agent Rewind (undo destructive agent actions); Annapurna AI-ready unstructured data layer; Preemptive Recovery Engine / Autonomous Business Recovery.
  - **Partnership:** Rubrik Agent Cloud for **Anthropic's Claude Code**; Project Hourglass GSI alliance (Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro); participation in Anthropic's Project Glasswing (AI-summary paraphrase); Lead Founding Partner of the Cloud Security Alliance's AI Resilience Center of Excellence.
  - **Capability gap:** monetization is the gap, not capability — >15 paying RAC customers, minimal FY27 ARR.
- **Recommendation:** **drift to 4**
- **Reasoning:** The 2026-06-02 note scored 3 on the basis of "an AI data-governance/security angle" — a feature-level characterization that this print has outrun. Rubrik has shipped a named agentic layer, an agent-identity control plane, an AI-ready data substrate, a frontier-lab integration and a five-GSI deployment channel in a single quarter. A 4 reflects AI as a core architectural commitment. It is not a 5 because none of it is yet in the numbers, and management guides FY27 as though it is not.

## 6. Competitive advantage signal

- **Innovation rate** (current: 4)
  - Evidence: In one quarter Rubrik shipped or announced Rubrik AI, Rubrik Agent Identity, Rubrik Annapurna, Autonomous Business Recovery, Identity Roll Forward and Identity Continuity — all 8-K-sourced — plus the Rubrik Flex unified contract vehicle (**AI-summary only; Flex does not appear in the 8-K**), and closed the **Strata.io** acquisition. R&D expense grew to $126.9M from $92.1M YoY (+38%, GAAP, 8-K income statement) — funding cadence, not just claiming it. Seventh consecutive year a Gartner MQ Leader, positioned **furthest in Vision**.
  - Recommendation: **hold 4** — but flag upward. This quarter's cadence is 5-like; I am holding because one quarter of launches without attached monetization is not yet a durable rate. A second consecutive quarter at this cadence *with* revenue attached would support 5.
- **Distribution** (current: 3)
  - Evidence — deliberately separating **new reach** from **deeper attach**, and scoring on reach: **Project Hourglass**, a five-firm GSI alliance (Cognizant, Deloitte, HCLTech, NTT DATA, Wipro), is a genuine new channel rather than an existing-account motion. A **£375 million ($500 million) UK investment with London named EMEA headquarters** is concrete geographic expansion. Customers with $100K+ ARR reached 3,084, **+23% YoY** — new logo and upsell breadth. Non-cloud ARR re-accelerated to roughly +10% after prior declines on sovereign, regulated and government demand (AI-summary paraphrase), reopening a segment previously in run-off. Largest international identity deal in company history (AI-summary paraphrase). _Not counted here: NRR >119% and Flex attach, which are deeper penetration of existing accounts and are credited under innovation/overall instead._
  - Recommendation: **revise to 4**
- **Overall** (current: 3)
  - Recommendation: **revise to 4**
  - Reasoning: Not an average of 4 and 4 — the two dimensions have started to compound in a way they previously did not. The 2026-06-02 draft's structural worry was correct at the time: innovation was outrunning the company's ability to carry it to market, so each new module needed its own sales cycle into a still-building installed base. **Rubrik Flex is the mechanism that changes this** — a single license and contract vehicle spanning the platform means the innovation cadence converts into attach without a proportional distribution cost. _Sourcing caveat: Flex is attested only by the InsiderScore AI summary and does not appear in the 8-K, so the single most load-bearing fact in this upgrade is also its weakest-sourced one._ The upgrade does not depend on it alone: **Project Hourglass** is 8-K-sourced and carries the same argument independently, outsourcing deployment capacity to five global SIs rather than requiring Rubrik to hire it. Innovation rate is now a *multiplier* on distribution rather than a parallel activity, which is what earns the 4 for a land-and-expand platform. **The named risk to this judgment is disclosed by the company itself:** the 8-K forward-looking-statements section flags "Rubrik's ability to effectively manage the leadership transition in its global revenue organization." A go-to-market leadership transition is precisely the thing that could stall the mechanism this upgrade rests on. Recommend the operator treat the 4 as conditional on that transition and revisit next quarter.

## 7. Potential investor interest signal

- **Current score:** 4
- **Evidence from this call:** Raised **all** guided FY27 metrics, with the press release leading on "Results exceeded all guided metrics" — the kind of clean beat-and-raise that drives upward estimate revisions. Tenth consecutive quarter of outperforming guidance (AI-summary paraphrase). Profitability inflection is the headline change: non-GAAP net income $44.7M versus a $(6.5)M loss a year ago, non-GAAP operating income $33.5M versus $(4.4)M, subscription ARR contribution margin 14.0% versus 9.4%. FY27 FCF guided to $323–333M. Sector heat is high and Rubrik is positioned at its centre. CEO commentary quality is strong and specific rather than promotional. Governance signal: appointment of Rakefet Russak-Aminoach to the board. Capital allocation is tuck-in M&A (Strata.io, $24.2M cash paid for business combinations H1) — **no buyback, no dividend**.
- **Recommendation:** **hold 4**
- Reasoning (offered though the template does not require it, because "hold" here is a judgment rather than deference): a 4 already contemplates a high-growth, high-revision, hot-sector name, and this print delivers exactly that. Three specific things cap it below 5. (i) The FY27 ARR guide implies deceleration to ~29% from 33% delivered, which was the single most-pressed analyst point on the call. (ii) GAAP economics remain loss-making — net loss $(61.8)M, stock-based compensation $101.0M in the quarter or ~24% of revenue, and a **total stockholders' deficit of $(499.4)M** — so the profitability inflection is a non-GAAP one and dilution is ongoing (diluted share count 226.8M non-GAAP versus 206.1M GAAP basic). (iii) No capital-return lever. Unlike the other two attributes, I do not think this call justifies moving the analyst draft.
