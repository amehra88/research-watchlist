## 1. Headline read

First observation for MPWR (no prior earnings notes exist; `notes/MPWR/` held only `_themes.md` and `_thesis.md`). The 2Q26 print (quarter ended 2026-06-30, call 2026-07-30) confirms the AI-server power thesis at a magnitude above what the 2026-06-02 scoring assumed: revenue $980.6M beat FactSet consensus by 8.6%, Enterprise Data grew 44.8% q/q to 38.8% of revenue, and the 3Q26 revenue guide midpoint of $1,150M sits ~15% above the consensus that existed at the time of the guide. ## 3. Forward guidance

3Q26 (quarter ending 2026-09-30), from 8-K Ex-99.1 "Q3 2026 Business Outlook": revenue $1,140M–$1,160M (midpoint $1,150M, +17.3% q/q on $980.6M at the mid); GAAP gross margin 55.2%–55.8%; non-GAAP gross margin 55.4%–56.0% (mid 55.7% vs 55.6% actual — the "slightly raised" GM guide the call summary references); GAAP opex $252.7M–$258.7M; non-GAAP opex $201.2M–$205.2M (mid $203.2M, +14.4% q/q — opex is growing fast in absolute terms but slower than revenue, so operating leverage continues); SBC $53.2M–$55.2M; interest and other income $7.8M–$8.2M; non-GAAP tax rate 15% for 2026; diluted shares 49.1M–49.5M.

The revenue guide midpoint is ~15.2% above the consensus-at-guide of ~$998.4M recorded in the Store B track record (`state/thesis/context/MPWR.md`). Guidance philosophy is unchanged: a $20M range around a point, same structure as prior quarters.

Beyond the quarter (InsiderScore AI summary of the call, paraphrase): management raised the 2026 growth "floor" for Enterprise Data from 85% to 130% (Tony Balow, Q&A); Automotive expected to ramp in 2H26 with mid-teens y/y growth for the end market; the capacity goal was extended "significantly beyond $6 billion" of annual revenue support, explicitly including systems and modules rather than only wafers; order visibility described as extending into early 2027; Communications momentum expected to continue into 3Q on optical modules, switches, DPUs and NIC cards. New categories (DDR5 high-speed interface components, RCD, 800V AC-DC) were framed as SAM expansion into 2027 with limited 2026 revenue. Capital return: an additional $500M buyback authorization (total $1B; $489.3M remained on the original program at 2026-06-30 per 10-Q) and a $2.00/share quarterly dividend (up from $1.56 a year ago, 10-Q Note 13); the 2026-09-10 8-K announces the 3Q26 dividend (exhibit title only consulted).

Tone: constructive and confident on data-center demand; explicitly cautious on Consumer and notebook-related Storage/Computing; explicit refusal to take opportunistic price increases.

## 4. Thesis read by theme

### ai_infrastructure_capex

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** Enterprise Data revenue $380.6M, +44.8% q/q and +164.3% y/y, 38.8% of total (32.7% in 1Q26, 21.7% in 2Q25), "driven by higher sales of our power management solutions for AI and server applications" (8-K Ex-99.1, "Q2 2026 Revenue Results"; 10-Q MD&A). Communications $131.5M, +78.3% y/y on "power solutions for optical modules and switches" (same). 3Q26 revenue guide midpoint $1,150M, +17.3% q/q. Q&A (paraphrase): Enterprise Data 2026 floor raised from 85% to 130% growth; Buchalter (Cowen) asked about inventory build and management pointed to low channel inventory and platform refreshes; the press release corroborates with DSO down 2 days and inventory days down 17.
- **Implication:** MPWR is converting AI-infrastructure capex into revenue faster than the June scoring contemplated; the near-term lever is the combined GPU-board, CPU-refresh and optical/switch power content, not any single platform. The thesis stands and its magnitude has moved up; the risk migrates to durability of the 2027 order book rather than share.

### automotive_semiconductor_demand

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** Automotive $157.1M, +3.1% q/q, +8.2% y/y, on "higher sales of Infotainment and ADAS power solutions" (8-K Ex-99.1); H1 auto revenue +6.7% y/y (10-Q MD&A). Management: products shipped for "over 1500 new sockets" year-to-date across ADAS and other in-vehicle applications (8-K Ex-99.1 highlights); Q&A (paraphrase, Quatrochi/Wells Fargo): confidence in a 2H ramp, mid-teens y/y growth for the end market in 2026, design wins diversified across zonal, 48V and LiDAR.
- **Implication:** Automotive is a live but secondary lever: H1 +6.7% y/y means the mid-teens full-year target requires roughly a 20%+ y/y second half, so delivery is back-end loaded and worth checking at 3Q. Share of revenue is falling (18.9% to 16.0%) purely because Enterprise Data is growing faster, not because auto is shrinking.

### data_center_deployment_constraints

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** Management "began sampling High Voltage AC to DC products for 800V data center architectures as we expand beyond our current AI and server core power solutions" and is developing in-house GaN and SiC devices for it (8-K Ex-99.1; Q&A paraphrase, Svanberg/Stifel: revenue timing depends on the market's transition to 800V). 48V vertical power modules cited with a broadening customer base (Q&A paraphrase, Schafer/Oppenheimer). On MPWR's own supply as a potential constraint: capacity goal extended "significantly beyond $6B" with back-end/module complexity flagged as the harder part (call summary); PP&E rose from $627.7M to $774.5M in six months, H1 capex up $64.8M y/y, a $40M refundable deposit was placed for a long-term assembly service agreement, and unconditional purchase commitments total $571.0M (10-Q Notes 4 and 7, MD&A cash flows). The 2026 executive PSU grant includes a goal tied to securing manufacturing capacity supporting a specified revenue level (10-Q Note 3).
- **Implication:** Power density and the 800V architecture shift are the deployment constraints MPWR monetizes, and the company is spending real capital (and tying executive pay) to avoid becoming a constraint itself. The theme is confirmed on both sides; the open question is when 800V AC-DC becomes revenue, which management would not time.

### humanoid_robotics_competition

- **Status:** Drift
- **Evidence:** Robotics appeared only as an emerging category bundled with building automation — management described a $40B–$50B combined opportunity, said building-controls software should be complete by year-end, and characterized the work as hardware plus software development with no near-term revenue (Q&A paraphrase, Stein/Truist). No humanoid customer, platform or timeline was named in the call summary or press release.
- **Implication:** For MPWR the theme is longer-dated and less specific than the scoring language implies: the mechanism is generic motor-control and power content for robotics/automation rather than a humanoid-specific position. Keep the theme, but do not underwrite any 2026–2027 revenue from it.

### semiconductor_cycle

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** "All end markets grew sequentially" (8-K Ex-99.1); DSO 32 days, inventory days down to 140 (121 on forward revenue) despite inventory dollars rising to $675.8M (8-K Ex-99.1); channel inventory described as low with order visibility into early 2027 (call summary); pricing discipline — management said growth was not price-driven and it avoids opportunistic increases in tight supply (Q&A paraphrase, Stein/Truist). Consumer remained the laggard (-4.8% y/y) and notebook-related Storage/Computing was called out as cautious. Channel structure: 88% of sales via distribution, Distributor A 28% of revenue (25% a year ago), Distributor D newly at 10% (10-Q Note 2).
- **Implication:** The print reads as a demand-led upcycle without channel stuffing — receivables and inventory days both improved while revenue grew 22% q/q. The cycle risk to watch is the rising distributor concentration and Taiwan ship-to (+148% y/y), which is where any AI-server order digestion would show first.

## 6. Competitive advantage signal

- **Innovation rate** (current: 4)
  - Evidence: Three new product vectors in one quarter — 800V AC-DC (with a strategic reversal to develop GaN in-house after previously dismissing it, per Q&A paraphrase, Svanberg/Stifel), DDR5 high-speed interface/RCD, and vertical power modules; R&D $118.6M, +23% y/y, 12.1% of revenue (10-Q MD&A). In optical transceivers management claims its high-power-density modules win sockets against PMICs and discrete DC-DC (Q&A paraphrase, Bolton/Needham).
  - Recommendation: hold (upward bias; revisit to 5 if DDR5/RCD or 800V convert to revenue by 2027)
- **Distribution** (current: 4)
  - Evidence: Channel — 88% of sales through distributors, Distributor A 28% of revenue and 33% of receivables, a value-added reseller newly at 20% of receivables (10-Q Note 2). Concentration — end-customer concentration remains undisclosed; management acknowledged significant data-center exposure without quantifying it. Geographic — 94% of revenue shipped to Asia; China 51%, Taiwan 27% and +148% y/y (10-Q Note 2, MD&A); capacity split between China and non-China described as balanced but not finalized (call summary). Customer base broadening in Communications and Enterprise Data (Q&A paraphrase).
  - Recommendation: hold
- **Overall** (current: 4)
  - Recommendation: hold
  - Reasoning: For MPWR the two dimensions compound rather than average — the module/solution form factor is simultaneously the innovation (density, integration) and the distribution wedge (it is why sockets are won against PMIC and discrete competitors), and the shift to modules raises switching costs at the platform level. What keeps overall at 4 rather than 5 is that reach is mediated by a distributor channel whose top account is growing as a share, and the end-customer mix is opaque, so the innovation advantage cannot yet be shown to have diversified the demand base.

EPS has no quantitative guide; consensus beat rate 1.0 over 28 periods, average beat +3.9%, so the 2Q26 +10.7% EPS beat is well above the record average.
2. **Inventory build in Enterprise Data (Buchalter, Cowen).** Asked for assurance that the 45% q/q Enterprise Data jump was not channel build. Management pointed to platform refreshes, CPU growth and low channel inventory. The press release supports the answer: DSO 32 days (down 2), inventory days 140 (down 17), forward-revenue inventory days 121 (down 7). This is the one question that maps directly to the open thesis assumptions and it went unanswered.
5. **800V timing and the GaN reversal (Svanberg, Stifel).** Management acknowledged it is now developing GaN in-house after earlier dismissing it and relies on SiC for the 800V solution; revenue timing was left dependent on the market's transition. Sandbag-neutral, but note the strategic reversal.
