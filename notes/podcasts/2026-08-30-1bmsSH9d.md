# $1,100,000,000. The Machine Age Fund | a16z

**AI Infrastructure Thesis: The "Shortage" Framework**

The core investment thesis is that the US faces fundamental shortages in three key areas critical for the AI buildout, creating a multi-year investment opportunity in companies that solve these bottlenecks:
1.  **Power:** Insufficient electrical grid capacity to power the massive data center expansion.
2.  **Compute:** A lack of data center space and networking infrastructure designed specifically for AI workloads.
3.  **Chips:** Continued high demand and constrained supply for advanced AI-enabling semiconductors.

This "shortage" in all three areas implies sustained pricing power and demand for the enabling infrastructure companies.

**Sector Trends & Capital Cycle**

-   **Massive Capex Supercycle:** The AI revolution is driving a need to "rebuild the entire infrastructure" of the US economy. This points to a historic capital expenditure cycle in physical assets, not just software.
-   **Data Center "Reboot":** The entire compute ecosystem is being "rebooted" for AI. Existing data centers, built and optimized for the SaaS era, are ill-suited for the demands of AI training and inference. This necessitates a complete re-tooling and new build-out, benefiting data center REITs, power management, cooling, and networking equipment providers.
-   **Supply Chain Strain:** The entire technology supply chain is under "immense pressure," indicating that bottlenecks will persist and companies providing key components or manufacturing capacity will be highly valued.

**Competitive Dynamics**

-   This thesis implicitly favors the picks-and-shovels plays over the end-application layer in the near term. The focus is on the foundational infrastructure (power, chips, data centers) where demand is clear and supply is constrained.
-   While not stated directly, the need to "retool" data centers suggests a major opportunity for both incumbent hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN) to invest heavily in new architectures and for specialized infrastructure players (e.g., networking like ANET, power management like ETN, chip designers like NVDA) to capture a significant portion of this capex wave.

**Specific Public Company Mentions**

-   None. The analysis is high-level and focused on the macro investment themes rather than specific company calls.

**Valuation & Market Calls**

-   No specific price targets or valuation metrics were mentioned. The commentary implies a long-term bullish view on the valuation of companies enabling the physical AI infrastructure buildout due to the described shortages.

**Regulatory & Macro Factors**

-   The commentary centers on a national-level challenge for the United States to build out its infrastructure. This aligns with and suggests continued support for initiatives like the CHIPS Act and other policies aimed at re-shoring and strengthening domestic tech and industrial supply chains. The power shortage is a significant macro bottleneck.
