# Hacking Is About to Get Out of Control - Ajeya Cotra

**AI/Tech Sector Trends & Risks**

The core investment thesis is the emergence of a massive new risk vector: the AI model training infrastructure of leading labs.

- **Escalating Cyber Threats:** The training and evaluation infrastructure for next-generation AI models is described as an "extremely attractive target." It will face an unprecedented level of attack from "superhuman" AI-powered hackers. The speaker speculates that the hacking effort aimed at this specific infrastructure could soon exceed the cumulative hacking effort in all of human history.
- **Investment Implication (Bullish for Cybersecurity):** This signals a massive, non-discretionary spending cycle on advanced cybersecurity to protect the core IP (the trained models) of AI leaders. Companies providing security for cloud infrastructure, data integrity, and AI-specific threat detection are poised to benefit.
- **Investment Implication (Bearish Risk for AI Leaders):** A successful attack could corrupt a multi-billion dollar training run, steal a foundational model, or subtly poison a model's output, creating catastrophic operational, financial, and reputational risk.

**Competitive Dynamics & Specific Company Implications**

- **Companies Mentioned:** The discussion directly centers on the next training runs at OpenAI (implying a direct risk to its primary partner, Microsoft - MSFT) and Anthropic (implying risk for its backers, Google - GOOGL and Amazon - AMZN).
- **AI-on-AI Warfare:** A novel competitive threat is the idea of rival AIs being used to "interfere with" or "manipulate" a competitor's model during its training process. This suggests the "AI race" includes a defensive, counter-intelligence component.
- **Investment Implication:** The ability to secure the model training supply chain will become a critical competitive differentiator. Investors should scrutinize the security posture and defensive investments of the hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN) who provide the infrastructure for these training runs. A security breach at one could shift market share to another.

**Regulatory & Macro Factors**

- **Nation-State Actors:** The threat is explicitly linked to geopolitical rivals like China. The theft or sabotage of a leading US-based foundational model is framed as a national security issue, not just a corporate one.
- **Investment Implication:** Expect increased government pressure and regulation on the security of leading AI labs. This could lead to mandated security standards, information sharing requirements, and potentially government contracts for companies that can secure this critical infrastructure.
