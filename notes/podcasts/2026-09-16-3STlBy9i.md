# The Agentic Economy Will Be Bigger Than Ours

### Key Investment Thesis: Specialized vs. General AI Models

The primary investment insight is the rising importance of smaller, specialized AI models fine-tuned on proprietary data. These models can outperform large, general-purpose "frontier models" (like those from OpenAI/Google) on specific enterprise tasks. This suggests a shift in where value will accrue in the AI stack.

The investment implication is that companies with unique, large-scale proprietary datasets are positioned to create significant competitive moats by building highly efficient, specialized AI agents. This challenges the narrative that value will consolidate solely with the builders of the largest foundation models.

### AI Sector & Competitive Dynamics

*   **Open-Source vs. Proprietary Models:** The discussion favors an "open-weight" or open-source model ecosystem. Enterprises can take these smaller models and fine-tune them in-house. This dynamic could commoditize the base model layer over time, putting long-term pricing pressure on the API-based services of proprietary model providers like OpenAI (via Microsoft, MSFT) and Google (GOOGL).

*   **Data as the Differentiator:** The ability to train models on proprietary data is the key advantage. Companies with vast, unique datasets (in finance, healthcare, software development, etc.) can create AI systems that are superior for their specific domain, which is a durable competitive advantage.

*   **The "Agentic Economy" Macro Trend:** The prediction is that the economy of AI agents will eventually be larger than the human economy. This is a major long-term bullish macro call for the entire AI ecosystem, from infrastructure providers (hyperscalers, semiconductors) to the application layer where these agents will be deployed. It points to a massive expansion of the total addressable market for software and computing.

### Specific Company Commentary

*   **Coinbase (COIN):** Mentioned as a specific example. The CEO notes that by training a smaller model on its proprietary compliance case data, Coinbase could create a compliance agent that is more effective than a general frontier model for that specific task. This is a signal of Coinbase's internal AI strategy and highlights how its proprietary data can be leveraged for operational efficiency and to create a defensible moat.
