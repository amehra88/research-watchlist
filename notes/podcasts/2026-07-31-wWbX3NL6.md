# Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak

Here are the key public market and AI investment insights from the podcast episode.

### Top Investable Insights

The largest opportunity for AI is applying it to "real-world" service industries (e.g., HVAC, plumbing, roofing, wellness) that are large, fragmented, and operationally complex. These industries are typically people-heavy in their back-office and customer service functions, making them prime for AI-driven automation to increase revenue and expand margins. This creates a significant market for vertical AI application companies that can automate customer intake, scheduling, dispatch, and even outbound sales.

Private equity firms are a major catalyst for AI adoption in these sectors. The traditional PE playbook of financial engineering is shifting towards driving tangible operational value. Since PE firms own many of these large service-based enterprises, they are highly motivated to deploy AI solutions that offer clear and rapid ROI through cost-cutting and revenue generation. This makes PE-owned portfolio companies a key, fast-moving customer segment for enterprise AI software providers.

### Competitive Dynamics & Sector Trends

-   **Vertical AI vs. Horizontal AI Labs:** Foundation model providers like OpenAI (partnered with Microsoft - MSFT), Anthropic (partnered with Google - GOOGL, and Amazon - AMZN), and Google itself are not seen as direct competitors to vertical application companies. The labs are focused on building generalized models, not the "last mile" productization, industry-specific data integration, and complex workflows required to solve specific enterprise problems. This suggests application-layer companies can build a defensible moat by focusing on deep vertical expertise.

-   **AI Agents Challenge Google's Search Dominance:** For local service businesses, a key customer acquisition channel is Google Search. AI-powered voice and text agents that can provide 24/7 availability and immediate booking challenge this model. If a customer calls and gets an instant, intelligent response, they are less likely to go back to Google to find the next provider, reducing customer churn and acquisition costs.

-   **Robotics Timeline is Long:** The deployment of general-purpose robotics into unstructured, real-world environments like homes is likely decades away. The dexterity required to handle the immense variability of physical tasks (e.g., different types of screws, walls, units) is a major unsolved challenge. Investors should temper expectations for rapid, large-scale adoption of humanoid or general-purpose service robots.

### Public Company Commentary

-   **Meta (META) & Hyperscaler AI Spend:** The guest's background at Scale AI included a major data-labeling deal with Meta. While specific details were not discussed, it highlights the immense and ongoing capital expenditure by hyperscalers on the data and infrastructure required to train and run large AI models.
