# Jason Calacanis: “Elon's Optimus will be the best-selling product of all time.”

### Key Investment Insights

**Company-Specific Insights: Tesla (TSLA)**

*   **Bullish Thesis on Optimus Robot:** Jason Calacanis makes a strong bullish call, stating, "Optimus will be the best-selling product of all time."
*   **Non-Public Information Signal:** This conviction is based on a private video of the Optimus robot's progress shared with him directly by Elon Musk. Calacanis claims the progress is "sick" and far more advanced than anything shown publicly.
*   **Valuation Implication:** The commentary suggests that the market is significantly undervaluing Tesla's robotics division. The progress is so advanced that Calacanis initially questioned if the video was CGI, implying a major technology leap that is not currently priced into the stock.

**AI/Tech Sector Trends**

*   **Pace of Humanoid Robotics Development:** The core insight is that the rate of progress in humanoid robotics is accelerating faster than public demonstrations suggest. Leading companies like Tesla may be intentionally withholding their most advanced capabilities from the public. This implies the total addressable market for automation and labor replacement could be realized sooner than anticipated.

**Competitive Dynamics**

*   **Tesla vs. The Field:** The discussion contrasts Tesla's privately-viewed progress against public footage of other robots failing (e.g., a competitor's robot falling during a race). This frames Tesla as having a potential hidden lead in the humanoid robotics space.
