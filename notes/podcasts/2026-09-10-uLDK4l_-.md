# Coinbase’s Everything Exchange: Agentic Finance, Stablecoins & Tokenization with CEO Brian Armstrong

**Key Investable Insights**

Coinbase (COIN) is strategically positioning itself as the financial infrastructure for the emerging "Agentic Economy," where AI agents transact with each other. The core thesis is that the massive volume of microtransactions generated by AIs will require crypto payment rails, as traditional systems like Visa (V) and Mastercard (MA) are economically unviable for this scale. This represents a potential decoupling of Coinbase's business fundamentals from crypto asset price speculation.

**Company Specific: Coinbase (COIN)**

*   **Bullish Thesis:** CEO Brian Armstrong outlines a three-pronged growth strategy:
    1.  **The Everything Exchange:** A vision to tokenize all asset classes (stocks, commodities, derivatives, prediction markets) and trade them on a single, global, 24/7 platform with superior capital efficiency (cross-margining).
    2.  **Stablecoin Payments:** This is a major growth driver, with transaction volume increasing "like crazy" independently of crypto market volatility. It offers a faster (<1 second) and cheaper (<1 cent) alternative to traditional payment rails.
    3.  **Agentic Finance (AIFi):** Building the financial plumbing for AI agents, which will need their own accounts to pay for compute, APIs, and other services. Coinbase aims to "bank the AIs."
*   **Valuation Commentary:** While COIN's stock price remains highly correlated with the price of Bitcoin, the underlying business is diversifying. 88% of revenue is already from non-Bitcoin trading. The strong, non-speculative growth in stablecoin payments suggests a potential future re-rating of the stock based on utility and transaction volume, rather than just asset prices.

**AI/Tech Sector Trends**

*   **The Rise of the Agentic Economy:** Armstrong predicts the economy of AI agents transacting with each other will eventually be larger than the human economy. This creates a massive new Total Addressable Market (TAM) for financial infrastructure.
*   **Microtransactions Invalidate TradFi Rails:** A key data point is that 76% of observed "agentic commerce" transactions are under 30 cents. The ~30-cent flat fee on typical debit/credit card transactions makes the existing financial system unsuitable for this high-volume, low-value economy.
*   **Crypto as the Native Currency of AI:** Stablecoins on efficient blockchains (Layer 2s) are presented as the only viable solution for AI-to-AI payments due to their low cost and near-instant settlement. This positions crypto infrastructure companies as essential utilities for the AI economy.

**Competitive Dynamics**

*   **Crypto vs. Payments Networks:** Stablecoin payment infrastructure is a direct long-term competitive threat to incumbent payment networks like Visa (V) and Mastercard (MA), particularly for the new market of AI-driven microtransactions and for improving the speed/cost of global cross-border payments.
*   **Banking the Unbankable (AIs):** Coinbase is enabling AI agents to have their own self-custodial wallets without traditional human-based KYC. This allows them to build a financial system for non-human actors, an area where traditional banking is structurally unable to compete.

**Regulatory Factors**

*   **Global Regulatory Clarity as a Catalyst:** Armstrong notes that increasing regulatory clarity outside the U.S. is a key enabler for the "Everything Exchange" vision, allowing Coinbase to expand its product offerings in more jurisdictions. The U.S. remains a comparative laggard.
