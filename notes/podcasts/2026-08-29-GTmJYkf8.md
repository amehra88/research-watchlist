# Two Labs Are About to Take Half the World's New Compute - Dylan Patel

**AI Investment Insights from Dwarkesh Patel & Dylan Patel**

**Core Thesis: Concentrated AI Compute Demand**

The most significant trend is the extreme and accelerating centralization of AI compute demand. Two private AI labs, OpenAI and Anthropic, are projected to consume 40-50% of all *new* incremental AI compute capacity over the next 12-18 months. This rapid consolidation of buying power is the single most important factor for the public companies in the AI supply chain.

**Specific Company Mentions & Competitive Dynamics**

*   **Google (GOOGL):** Bullish signal. Anthropic is explicitly purchasing and using Google's TPUs for its compute buildout. This validates Google's TPU architecture as a viable, large-scale alternative to NVIDIA's GPUs and positions Google Cloud as a key supplier to one of the two largest AI training customers.

*   **NVIDIA (NVDA):** Implicitly bullish in the short-to-medium term. The massive, accelerating, and price-insensitive demand for compute directly benefits the dominant market supplier. However, a long-term risk is noted as major customers seek to diversify and control their own hardware supply.

*   **Hyperscaler Risk/Shift:** The trend indicates that leading AI labs are moving beyond exclusively renting compute from hyperscalers (like AWS, Azure, GCP). They are aggressively pursuing a hybrid strategy of owning their own infrastructure (via custom chips or direct purchases) and leasing from new, non-traditional providers. This could alter the long-term role of hyperscalers from primary providers to one of several options for frontier model training.

**Capital Allocation & Market Signals**

*   **Massive Capex Acceleration:** The compute consumption from OpenAI and Anthropic is not just large, but growing exponentially. They accounted for ~30% of all incremental compute added this year and are on a path to consume nearly half of all new capacity next year based on contracts already signed. This signals a continued, massive capex cycle for AI infrastructure.

*   **Price-Insensitive Buyers:** The analysis highlights that OpenAI and Anthropic have the "marginal capability to pay the highest price" for compute. This indicates that for frontier model training, demand is highly inelastic. This supports continued strong pricing power for key suppliers of essential hardware like NVIDIA and advanced networking.

*   **Vertical Integration Trend:** OpenAI is developing its own custom chips. This is a clear signal of vertical integration, a long-term strategy to reduce dependency on merchant silicon providers like NVIDIA and control its own hardware roadmap and cost structure.
