# Intel CEO: They All Walked Out on Me

**Key Investment Insights from Lip-Bu Tan (Intel Board Member)**

**Semiconductor Sector Trend: Bullish Reversal & Valuation Rerating**

The most significant shift in technology investing over the past 15-20 years is the dramatic reversal in the semiconductor sector. Previously ignored by mainstream venture capital in favor of software, semiconductors are now considered "hot again" and "essential." This fundamental change in perception is the primary driver behind the massive public market valuations in the sector today. What was once a capital-intensive, out-of-favor industry is now the core foundation of the AI-driven market.

**Public Company Commentary & Valuations (Bullish Context)**

The speaker cites the market capitalization of several public semiconductor companies as direct evidence of this powerful trend. The commentary is broadly bullish on these leaders.

*   **NVIDIA (NVDA):** Mentioned first via CEO Jensen Huang as the leading example of the sector's massive valuation growth.
*   **TSMC (TSM):** Grouped with other multi-trillion-dollar market cap players, highlighting its scale and importance.
*   **Broadcom (AVGO):** Mentioned in the same group as TSMC, indicating its status as a top-tier semiconductor giant.
*   **AMD (AMD):** Mentioned via CEO Lisa Su, citing its impressive market cap (noted as "almost $800 billion") as another key winner in the current environment.
*   **Arm (ARM) & Samsung (SSNLF):** Mentioned as historically savvy corporate investors who backed semiconductor startups when financial VCs would not, signaling their long-term strategic view of the sector's importance.
