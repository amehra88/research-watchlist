# Kavak's Playbook for Rebuilding a Company Around AI

Here are the key public market investment insights from the podcast.

### Top Investment Thesis: The Rise of the "Agentic Enterprise"

The most significant shift in corporate strategy is rebuilding companies to be run *by* AI agents, not just assisted by them. This creates a new paradigm for evaluating companies.

-   **Investment Focus Shift**: Capital is shifting from "knowledge workers" (salaries, opex) to "tokens" (compute, API calls). The speaker states, "I'm investing more today in tokens than in knowledge workers." This signals a fundamental change in cost structure and capital allocation for AI-native companies, leading to massive operating leverage.
-   **Valuing AI Spend**: Not all AI spending is equal. A sophisticated approach is to tier the value of compute "tokens":
    -   **Tier 3 (Highest Value)**: Tokens used by autonomous agents with a directly measurable ROI tied to business outcomes (e.g., sales conversion, LTV). This is the most valuable use of AI.
    -   **Tier 2 (Medium Value)**: Tokens used for internal tools and processes that create indirect efficiencies.
    -   **Tier 1 (Lowest Value)**: General employee use of copilots and chatbots.
    -   **Investor takeaway**: Scrutinize a company's AI spend. Are they deploying high-value "Tier 3" agents that directly drive revenue and profit, or just providing low-value "Tier 1" tools?

### AI/Tech Sector Trends & Valuation

-   **Agent-per-Customer Model**: The future of customer interaction is assigning a dedicated, long-running AI agent to each customer. This agent has persistent memory and is tasked with a single long-term goal: maximizing that customer's lifetime value (LTV). This moves business models from transactional to relational, automated at scale.
-   **Superhuman Performance**: The goal is to build agents that "outperform the best human we had ever hired" on every key metric (conversion, LTV, customer satisfaction). Kavak's sales agents now convert at **2.1x the rate of human agents**, providing a concrete ROI on AI development.
-   **Compute at Scale**: This agent-centric model requires massive scale. Kavak instantiates 100,000 to 200,000 agents daily, each with its own virtual machine. This points to sustained, high-volume demand for cloud infrastructure and compute services from hyperscalers (e.g., AWS, GOOGL, MSFT) and semiconductor companies (e.g., NVDA).

### Competitive Dynamics & Strategy

-   **Creative Destruction 2.0**: The primary competitive advantage is not *adopting* AI, but *redesigning the entire organization* around it. Incumbent companies that simply layer AI onto legacy processes will be disrupted by new, AI-native companies built from scratch with an agent-centric architecture.
-   **What Winners Do**:
    1.  **Top-Down Mandate**: The AI transformation must be driven aggressively by the CEO. Bottom-up adoption via hackathons or individual use cases is insufficient and will fail.
    2.  **Rebuild, Don't Retrofit**: Be willing to destroy and rebuild existing processes and APIs so they can be operated by AI agents, not just humans.
    3.  **Obsess over Feedback Loops**: The key to creating "superhuman" agents is building robust evaluation ("evals") and feedback systems to generate proprietary data for fine-tuning models. Companies that master this create a powerful data moat.

### Human Capital and Organizational Structure

-   **The New Org Chart**: The future organization is a collaboration between humans and AI agents. In many cases, agents will manage humans, especially for physical-world tasks (e.g., an AI "CEO" for a city manages mechanics via messaging).
-   **Human Roles**: Humans will shift to three primary functions: 1) building the agents, 2) working *for* the agents (physical fulfillment), or 3) acting as "human-in-the-loop" exception handlers for tasks agents can't solve. This implies significant changes in headcount and skill requirements for all public companies.
