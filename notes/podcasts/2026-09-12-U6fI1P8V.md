# Will We Still Need Money in the Future?

**LEAD INSIGHTS**

- **Company View (Implied Bullish): Coinbase (COIN)**
Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong articulates a long-term bullish thesis for cryptocurrency's role in an AI-driven future. He argues that even with AI creating abundance, a "medium of exchange" will still be necessary. He positions crypto as the essential "rails" for an AI economy, suggesting it will be a "nice complement" and ultimately "essential for AI" to function and transact. This frames the crypto ecosystem's infrastructure as a key enabling layer for the AI revolution, providing a secular tailwind for companies like Coinbase.

**AI/TECH SECTOR TRENDS**

- **AI-Driven Deflation:** A major long-term trend will be significant price deflation in goods and services as AI and robotics drive down the cost of production for commodities, food, and housing. This could fundamentally alter the profitability of companies in these sectors and the broader economic landscape.
- **Intersection of AI and Crypto:** The core thesis is that the proliferation of AI agents will create a new, massive demand for a native digital medium of exchange. Crypto is positioned as the financial plumbing for this emerging machine-to-machine economy.

**MACRO & VALUATION COMMENTARY**

- **New Measures of Value:** In a future of AI-driven abundance, value will be measured by truly scarce resources like energy and land, rather than traditional currency. This suggests a long-term re-weighting of value towards companies that control or produce these scarce assets.
- **Competing Macro Forces:** Investors should monitor the dynamic between two opposing forces: deflationary pressure from AI/tech advancement versus inflationary pressure from government money printing. The outcome will have significant implications for asset valuations.
