# David Sacks: The Chip Stock Crash is Based on Momentum, NOT Fundamentals

**INVESTOR ANALYSIS: ALL-IN PODCAST ON CHIP STOCK CRASH**

**Core Thesis: Correction is Momentum-Driven, Not Fundamental**
The recent, sharp sell-off in AI and semiconductor stocks is the unwinding of a leveraged momentum trade, not a signal of deteriorating fundamentals. Speaker David Sacks views the pullback as "temporary market volatility amplified by leverage," suggesting the long-term investment case is unchanged. This implies the correction may be a buying opportunity for investors with a fundamental-driven thesis.

**Capital Allocation Signals (Bullish)**
- The massive CapEx investment cycle for the "AI boom" is fundamentally sound.
- Sacks believes this spending will eventually deliver a significant return on investment (ROI), countering the narrative that it is a speculative bubble. This is a long-term bullish signal for the infrastructure companies receiving the CapEx.

**Valuation & Price Commentary**
- AI "momentum trade" stocks have experienced a severe correction of 30-40%.
- Memory chip stocks saw a ~10x run-up in the past year, making them susceptible to a sharp pullback.
- The broader Nasdaq 100 index pulled back roughly 11% from its June 2024 peak.
- The South Korean KOSPI index was cited as an extreme example of the momentum unwind, collapsing 44% in 40 days.

**Specific Company & Sector Mentions**
- **Memory Chip Stocks:** This sector (e.g., Micron - MU) experienced a huge run-up and a subsequent sharp correction driven by momentum, not fundamentals.
- **Microsoft (MSFT) & Meta (META):** On-screen text notes that recent declines in these hyperscalers contributed to the negative sentiment and pullback in the AI sector ahead of their quarterly reports.
