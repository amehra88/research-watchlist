# Chamath: Your AI Data Is NOT SAFE

**Key Investment Thesis**

The primary investment opportunity identified is the emerging need for an "independent third-party layer" for AI security and governance. As enterprises adopt AI, their fear of proprietary data leakage creates a market for solutions that sit between their systems and the foundation models. This suggests a bullish outlook for companies that can provide verifiable data security, privacy, and exposure management, effectively acting as a trusted intermediary in the AI stack.

**Sector/Competitive Dynamics**

*   **Trust Deficit in Foundation Models:** Foundation model providers (like OpenAI, and by extension their hyperscaler partners) face a critical trust issue with enterprise customers. Their promises of "Zero Data Retention" (ZDR) are seen as unreliable and technically difficult to guarantee.
*   **Data Leakage is an Inherent Risk:** There are "non-obvious data leak vectors" in current AI models. Even with best efforts, model companies cannot fully prevent de-identified user data from being used to improve their models, creating a significant liability and risk for their enterprise clients.
*   **Emergence of a New AI Security Layer:** This privacy risk is creating a new competitive layer in the AI stack focused on security. Companies that can solve this trust issue for the enterprise will capture significant value, potentially more than the undifferentiated model providers over time.

**Public Company Implications**

*   **Bearish/Risk Factor for Hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN):** The data privacy concerns represent a major sales hurdle and potential liability for the large companies providing foundation models. Microsoft (through its deep partnership with OpenAI), Google, and Amazon (through its partnership with Anthropic) are all exposed. Slower enterprise adoption or the need to acquire/partner with third-party security firms could impact their AI revenue growth.
*   **Bullish for AI Security & Governance Platforms:** While no specific public companies were named, the analysis strongly implies a bullish case for any public company specializing in data security, governance, and observability that is pivoting to address AI-specific risks. These companies are positioned to become essential "toll booths" for enterprise AI adoption.
