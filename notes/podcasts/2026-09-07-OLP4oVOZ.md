# David Sacks: Trump’s Instincts on AI and Data Centers Are Right

Here are the investment insights from the podcast episode.

**Key Investment Thesis**

A potential Trump administration is viewed as a net positive for the AI infrastructure build-out due to a pro-innovation, anti-regulation stance. The core belief is this administration would facilitate, not hinder, the massive capital expenditure required for data centers, which are the backbone of the AI boom.

**Regulatory & Macro Factors for Tech/AI**

- **Pro-Innovation Stance:** A Trump administration is expected to be the "best friend of innovators" in Washington, prioritizing American leadership in technology and AI without what is described as "unnecessary regulation" or "doomer anxieties."
- **Local Control over Permitting:** A key policy point is that a Trump framework for AI would *not* use federal power to preempt state and local decisions on data center construction. The fight over permitting will remain at the local level.
- **Favorable Deal-Making Environment:** The overall approach would be to encourage local communities to make economically favorable deals with tech companies for data centers, rather than imposing top-down federal restrictions that could stall projects. This is a tailwind for companies undertaking massive capex.

**AI Sector Trends & Capital Expenditure (Capex)**

- **Data Center Build-out Headwinds:** A primary risk for the ongoing AI infrastructure investment cycle is local community opposition (NIMBYism) to new data centers, driven by concerns over power consumption, water usage, and aesthetics. This directly impacts the growth plans of hyperscalers (e.g., MSFT, GOOG, AMZN) and their suppliers.
- **Economic Model for Overcoming Opposition:** The podcast highlights a strong counter-argument: data centers can be a major financial boon for local communities.
    - **Case Study 1 (Loudoun County, VA):** Data center tax revenue led to a $6,000 property tax reduction for homeowners.
    - **Case Study 2 (Louisiana):** A county was able to fund teacher bonuses for the first time in years from data center revenue.
    - **Investment Signal:** The ability of tech companies to structure deals that provide direct, tangible financial benefits to local communities will be a critical determinant of the pace and success of the AI build-out.
- **Power & Grid Investment is Non-Negotiable:** Data center developers will be required to fund the construction of "net new power generation" and pay for necessary grid upgrades. This points to a significant, durable investment theme in utilities, power infrastructure, and energy companies that can support this demand.
- **ESG Risk Mitigation:** The argument that data centers use excessive amounts of water is directly refuted as "fake news," stating that modern cooling systems recirculate water. This is a key talking point for investors to counter a common ESG-related criticism of the sector.
