# The AI security market is not overreacting

**Market Call & Sector Trend: The AI Security Market is Not Overreacting**

The primary thesis is that the market is correctly pricing in a massive, fundamental shift in cybersecurity driven by enterprise AI adoption. This is not a hype cycle. The emergence of a new "AI attack surface" is creating an urgent, non-discretionary spending need for corporations. Security leaders at large enterprises recognize they must move "very quickly" to address these new vulnerabilities.

**Implications for Public Cybersecurity Companies (Bullish Signal)**

The adoption of AI does not eliminate the need for existing security infrastructure; it necessitates extending it. To secure the new AI stack, enterprises must first have "foundational pieces in place." This provides a strong tailwind for incumbent public cybersecurity leaders. Key categories mentioned and their public market implications:

*   **Identity:** Essential for controlling access to AI models and data. This is a bullish signal for leaders like Okta (OKTA) and CyberArk (CYBR).
*   **Firewall:** Needed to protect the network perimeter around AI systems. Bullish for platform vendors like Palo Alto Networks (PANW), Fortinet (FTNT), and cloud security specialists like Zscaler (ZS).
*   **Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR):** Required to secure the devices that access and interact with AI applications. Bullish for CrowdStrike (CRWD) and SentinelOne (S).

The core insight is that widespread AI deployment within an enterprise will force a corresponding expansion of spending on these foundational security platforms, creating a new wave of growth for the sector.
