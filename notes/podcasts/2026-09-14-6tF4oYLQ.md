# MUST SEE: Trump calls Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang live on stage!

Here are the key investment insights from the podcast episode.

**Most Investable Insights**

A potential Trump administration would aggressively deregulate and fast-track the permitting process for AI data centers in the United States. Trump explicitly labels the environmental opposition to data centers a "hoax" designed to slow US progress and benefit China. He expressed a strong desire to support the domestic buildout of this infrastructure, which he called "the oil of the next 20-25 years." This signals a major potential tailwind for the entire AI supply chain, particularly companies involved in data center construction, power, and hardware.

**AI/Tech Sector Trends & Valuation Commentary**

*   **Secular Bull Case for AI:** Trump stated that AI is "bigger than the internet" and that "whoever wins AI wins," framing the technology's development as a geopolitical and economic imperative. This suggests continued, massive-scale investment in the sector is a national priority.
*   **Data Centers as Critical Infrastructure:** Data centers are positioned as the central economic driver of the AI era. The argument is that they bring immense wealth to local and state economies, transforming them in the process. This provides a political and economic justification for overriding local opposition to their construction.

**Public Company Commentary**

*   **Nvidia (NVDA):** While not a direct valuation call, the segment is implicitly bullish for Nvidia. Trump highlighted the company's technological leadership, stating Jensen can develop "the most complex computer chip in the world that nobody can copy for 10 years." The pro-data center stance directly supports demand for Nvidia's core products.
*   **Google (GOOGL):** Trump called out Google for its plan to build a major data center in Finland, attributing the overseas capex to the inability to get permits in the US. He was explicitly "not happy about" this, signaling potential political pressure on hyperscalers to invest domestically.

**Regulatory & Macro Factors**

*   **Geopolitical Competition with China:** The primary macro theme is the AI race between the US and China. Trump argues that any regulations or permitting delays in the US directly benefit China's AI ambitions. This national security framing could be used to justify sweeping deregulation to accelerate the US AI buildout.
*   **Capital Allocation Signal:** Trump's comments strongly favor onshoring tech infrastructure investment. This could influence future capex decisions for major public tech companies if they anticipate a more favorable and faster regulatory environment in the US.
