# Rethinking Legacy Data Infrastructure with Eon Co-Founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein

Here are the top public market and AI investing insights from the podcast episode.

**M&A and Capital Allocation Signals**

The most significant signal is the emergence of "data-only" acquisitions, where a company's historical data is valued as a distinct, strategic asset for training AI models.

- **Google (GOOGL):** In a direct market call, the host and guests highlight that Google recently purchased the data of the bankrupt Spirit Airlines for $10 million. The key insight is that Google bought *only the data* (not the planes or other assets) specifically for training AI models. This demonstrates that large, proprietary datasets are becoming a new asset class that major AI players will acquire.

- **Merck (MRK):** A rumor was shared that pharmaceutical giant Merck was the other bidder for the Spirit Airlines data. This implies the value of large, unique datasets transcends industry lines. A non-tech company like Merck sees value in airline data for its own AI initiatives, broadening the potential market for data assets.

- **Amazon (AMZN):** The guests' previous company, CloudEndure, was acquired by AWS. This is cited as an example of the long-standing trend of hyperscalers acquiring key infrastructure technology to integrate into their platforms, a pattern likely to continue in the AI era.

**AI/Tech Sector Trends & Valuation Commentary**

The central theme is that the "AI era" is a more rapid and disruptive shift than the "Cloud era," creating urgency for enterprises to modernize their data infrastructure.

- **Data is the New Moat:** Proprietary historical data is becoming the most valuable asset an organization has. This is a direct driver of competitive advantage in the AI era, as high-quality, unique data is essential for fine-tuning models and creating differentiated AI products.

- **Legacy Data is "Locked":** The biggest challenge for established enterprises is that their decades of valuable data are siloed, unstructured, and inaccessible for modern AI workloads. This creates a massive market opportunity for companies that can help unlock, classify, and prepare this data.

- **The AI Transition is "On Steroids":** The shift to AI-native infrastructure is happening much faster than the decade-long cloud transition. Enterprises feel immense pressure to adopt AI or become irrelevant, which is unlocking significant budget for data infrastructure, security, and new AI applications.

**Competitive Dynamics & Security**

The widespread adoption of AI creates new, high-velocity threat vectors, opening opportunities for a new wave of security and governance companies.

- **The Rise of "Shadow AI":** A significant risk for enterprises is employees using unapproved public AI tools (like ChatGPT) and feeding them sensitive, proprietary company data. One-third of employees (38%) admit to doing this. This creates a critical need for new data governance, security, and compliance solutions to monitor and control data flows to external AI models.

- **Non-Human Threats:** AI agents themselves are becoming a new threat vector. Unlike human threats, AI agents with legitimate permissions can cause data loss, corruption, or exfiltration at extreme speed and scale. This will require a new paradigm in cybersecurity focused on detecting and responding to anomalous non-human behavior.
