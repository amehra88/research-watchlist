# Jason Calacanis on America’s AI Backlash: “They’re Sharpening the Guillotines”

Here are the public market investing insights from the podcast episode.

### Regulatory & Macro Risks

- **Primary Investment Risk:** A significant and growing political/regulatory backlash against the AI data center buildout is a major headwind for the sector. State governors (e.g., Greg Abbott in Texas, Josh Shapiro in Pennsylvania) are now taking direct executive action to halt or severely restrict new data center projects.
- **Specific Regulatory Actions:**
    - **Texas:** A directive has halted up to 1,800 data center projects, imposing new guidelines requiring them to not strain local water or power grids, lower electricity costs, and avoid disrupting communities.
    - **Pennsylvania:** An executive order is implementing the "strictest standards in the nation" for AI data centers, requiring strict environmental/transparency commitments, local community approval, and removing "Fast Track" permitting programs.
- **Root Cause:** The backlash is fueled by broad economic anxiety (inflation, wage stagnation) contrasted with record stock market performance and wealth creation for tech executives. This populist sentiment is translating directly into anti-tech, anti-growth policy at the state level.

### AI/Tech Sector Trends & Implications

- **Infrastructure Bottleneck:** The primary driver of the AI boom is the massive capital expenditure on physical infrastructure (data centers). The emerging regulatory hurdles create a tangible risk of a bottleneck, potentially slowing the pace of AI development and deployment.
- **Negative Sector Sentiment:** Public perception of AI is being negatively shaped by "doomsday" rhetoric from tech leaders (e.g., comments from Elon Musk and Anthropic's CEO about mass job loss). This fuels the public opposition that leads to regulatory action.
- **Investment Implication:** Companies directly involved in the data center supply chain face new, non-trivial project risk. This includes hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOG, AMZN) who need to build, data center REITs (EQIX, DLR), and utility companies that service these power-intensive facilities. The cost and timeline for AI infrastructure expansion may be higher and longer than currently priced in.

### Specific Public Company Mentions

- **Elon Musk (TSLA):** His public statements that "AI and robots will replace all jobs" are cited as a key contributor to public fear and the resulting political backlash against the AI industry. This highlights how executive communication can create macro risk for the entire sector.
