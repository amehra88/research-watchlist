## 1. Headline read

This is a **Confirm on the core lidar thesis plus a material Drift in what the company is becoming.** The lidar business did what the bull case requires: revenue RMB 681M (+30% YoY, eighth consecutive quarter of YoY growth), 471k+ units with ADAS and Robotics shipments both more than doubling YoY, GM held above 39%, and — the marquee item — Hesai named strategic lidar partner and confirmed supplier for **Mercedes-Benz L3 models** in Europe and China `[md_2_0]` `[md_2_5]`. Against the Q1'25 baseline note, every metric that mattered then (share, design wins, profitability, global OEM conversion) has improved, and the unnamed "top European OEM" has plausibly been unmasked (§8, item 2).

The Drift is that Hesai has reframed itself from a lidar scale player into a "Physical AI" enabler, with a new **Strategic Growth Initiatives (SGI)** segment and a deliberate RMB 51M quarterly operating loss to fund it `[md_3_0]` `[md_3_2]`. The core Lidar Business earned RMB 42M operating profit; SGI lost RMB 51M — so the group's GAAP profit of RMB 18M is *entirely* a function of how much the new venture is consuming. That is a genuine change in the shape of the investment case, not just a good quarter, and it is the single most important thing for the PM to hold in mind. Guidance is confident (Q2 RMB 850-900M; FY26 3.0-3.5M units reaffirmed vs 1.6M in 2025), but gross margin has compressed ~300bp YoY (42% in Q1'25 → 39.1%) and sell-side revisions ran 3-down/0-up into the print.

Attributes that are *not* corroborated: exclusivity is not restated for Mercedes, there is no reference to ICE/EV platform breadth or to the 2030s, and this agreement is framed L3-specific rather than platform-wide. **Confidence: probable but unconfirmed.** The "continuation of the existing collaboration" phrasing is the single strongest datapoint and it is doing most of the work. A 6-K or the FY26 20-F would settle it.

3. **SGI unit economics explicitly withheld.** Jessie Lo (BofA) asked for ASP, revenue and margin on Kosmo and the actuation modules `[qna_13_0]`. Andrew Fan declined on all three: reporting is "on a consolidated basis within SGI rather than splitting them out individually," and "current pricing is highly customized and driven by pilot programs... these early data points are not representative of long-term economics" `[qna_14_0]` `[qna_14_1]`. Defensible for a pre-commercial segment, but it means **the RMB 100M FY26 SGI guide is unmodellable** — no units, no price, no customer. Combined with the new segment reporting, the practical effect is that investors can now see SGI's *losses* precisely while its *revenue drivers* remain opaque.

4. **Jeff Chung (Citi) asked whether the guide was conservative and never got an answer.** Chung did the arithmetic live — noting that stripping SGI out leaves core Q2 growth of about 18% Q/Q — and asked "Would that be a little bit conservative?" `[qna_21_0]`. Fan's reply `[qna_22_0]` `[qna_22_1]` restated the guidance range, the unit figure and the demand drivers, and closed with "we believe Hesai is executing strongly across all fronts" — **the conservatism question itself was never addressed.** This is the cleanest sandbag flag on the call. (Chung also framed the RMB 100M SGI figure as a Q2 number; Fan's answer restates it correctly as "around RMB 100 million in **2026**," so this reads as a soft correction rather than an uncorrected error.)

5. **Robotic Actuation Modules: unfalsifiable and unsized.** Introduced in prepared remarks as "a first glimpse and Easter egg" `[md_2_9]`. Asked by Nora Min (UBS) about bottlenecks, customers, and whether the actuators are for body or hand `[qna_9_0]`, Li answered none of the three, offering instead: "we haven't yet disclosed detailed information, but early signals are very encouraging, and key performance metrics are already reaching what we believe is well among the industry's highest level" `[qna_10_1]`. No customer, no timeline, no spec, no revenue. Treat as narrative until a subsequent call puts numbers on it.

6. **Nobody asked about the gross margin decline.** GM fell from 42% in Q1'25 to 39.1%. Management's only framing was "stayed healthy at over 39%" `[md_3_1]` — technically accurate, and it avoids the YoY comparison entirely. **Not one of the seven analysts raised it.** Given that mix-driven margin compression is the direct counterweight to the volume story, this is a conspicuous gap in the Q&A.

7. **Light Q&A for a quarter with this much news.** Seven analyst questions, then "no further questions" `[qna_30_0]`, on a call that announced a Mercedes L3 win, a new reporting segment, a world-first SoC, a new device category and an actuation business. No follow-ups on Mercedes sizing, no question on margin, none on tariffs, delisting or export controls (all significant in Q1'25). Either the sell-side was pre-briefed or the strategic pivot outran their models.
