# SNOW — Q2 FY27 earnings read

_Prepared by earnings-reviewer, 2026-09-03. Tier: T2 (active candidate)._

## 1. Headline read

Print confirms and extends the thesis established last quarter: product revenue +37% YoY (third consecutive quarter of acceleration: 30% → 34% → 37%), FY27 product revenue guidance raised a second time from 31% to 36% growth, and non-GAAP operating margin guidance raised again from 13.5% to 14.5%. The one genuine drift is on the margin structure the Q1 note explicitly flagged for tracking — management has now put a number on the AI mix cost (FY27 non-GAAP product gross margin guided to 74%, against 75% delivered this quarter and 76% a year ago), converting last quarter's contested Q&A topic into a disclosed, quantified guide item.

## 3. Forward guidance

- **Q3 FY27 product revenue:** $1,588M–$1,593M, +37% to +38% YoY. Guiding to accelerate again off a much larger base.
- **FY27 product revenue:** raised to **$6,070M, +36% YoY**, up from prior guidance of $5,840M / +31%. This is the second consecutive raise (27% → 31% → 36%).
- **FY27 non-GAAP product gross margin:** **74%**, explicitly attributed by management to "a higher revenue mix from fast-growing AI workloads, which currently have a lower contribution margin." Against 75% delivered in Q2 and 76% in PY Q2, the guide embeds roughly a point of further mix compression across the back half.
- **FY27 non-GAAP operating margin:** raised to **14.5%** from 13.5%; Q3 guided to 15.5%.
- **FY27 non-GAAP adjusted FCF margin:** held at 23.0% (Q2 delivered 6% — seasonally low, consistent with SNOW's collections pattern).
- **GAAP profitability:** management reiterated a path to GAAP profitability in fiscal 2028.
- **Internal consistency (derived from sourced figures, labeled as such):** H1 non-GAAP operating margin was 14%, Q3 is guided to 15.5%, and the FY sits at 14.5% — implying roughly 15% in Q4. The guide is internally coherent rather than obviously sandbagged; management is spending the upside on AI cost of revenue rather than dropping it to the margin line.
- **Tone:** confident but grounded. The notable shift versus Q1 is that management pre-emptively quantified the AI gross-margin drag in the guide instead of defending it under questioning — a credibility-positive change in disclosure posture.

## 4. Thesis read by theme

### enterprise_ai_adoption

- **Status:** Confirm (strengthened)
- **Evidence:** CoCo surpassed **9,100 accounts** (from 7,100 at Q1, +2,000 in the quarter); CoWork — a product not present in the Q1 note — reached **5,800 accounts**, up ~11% QoQ. Customer use cases deployed +89% YoY; use cases won per account executive +43% YoY. 43% of customers now share data with at least one external party. Time-to-80%-consumption for new logos improved in recent cohorts. Management stated AI products contributed **approximately half of the acceleration** in the quarter and described AI as a "structural multiplier" for platform consumption (Ikeda exchange). (InsiderScore AI summary, AI/product and platform-adoption sections.)
- **Implication:** Adoption is now measurable in consumption, not just account counts — the half-of-acceleration attribution is the strongest quantitative link SNOW has yet drawn between AI product uptake and revenue. Faster time-to-value on new cohorts suggests the adoption curve is steepening rather than pulling forward a fixed pool of demand.

### ai_infrastructure_software

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** **Cortex AI Gateway** launched, routing AI tasks across models to optimize cost, performance and governance. Management positioned **model neutrality** as a core competitive advantage — supporting open-source and proprietary models, letting customers switch as needs evolve, and explicitly *not* pursuing large-scale model training while developing specialized **Arctic** models for narrow efficiency gains (Kleinerman, Knauff exchange). 330+ new product capabilities shipped in H1, +35% YoY. (InsiderScore AI summary, model-neutrality and product sections.)
- **Implication:** SNOW is deliberately positioning one layer above the model war rather than competing in it — capturing value from model commoditization instead of being exposed to it. The Gateway makes that architectural, not rhetorical. The trade-off is that this is precisely the layer hyperscalers also want, so the defensibility rests on data gravity and governance rather than on any model asset.

### observability_competitive_landscape

- **Status:** Drift (toward de-emphasis)
- **Evidence:** The only observability datapoint on the call is the Observe acquisition contributing **~1pp** to FY27 product revenue growth — the same figure carried in the Q1 note, unchanged. There is no Observe-specific narrative, no competitive commentary versus DDOG or SPLK, and no analyst question on observability across an eleven-analyst Q&A. (InsiderScore AI summary, guidance section; absence noted across the full Q&A roster.)
- **Implication:** Stated plainly: this is now the **least-evidenced of SNOW's five assigned themes**. Observe is being absorbed as a contributor rather than developed as a strategic pillar, and management's framing energy has moved entirely to the Agentic Enterprise. The thesis is not broken — the ~1pp is tracking as promised — but it is not being fed, and two consecutive quarters of no incremental evidence is worth an operator decision on whether the theme still belongs on SNOW. No evidence was manufactured to reach Confirm.

### ai_agent_monetization

- **Status:** Confirm (strengthened)
- **Evidence:** Management introduced the **"Agentic Enterprise"** framework — data foundation, AI models, application workflows and a governed control plane — as the organizing strategy, and positioned the governed control plane as SNOW's unique claim. CoCo and CoWork are both revenue-contributing at scale (9,100 and 5,800 accounts). Cortex AI Gateway extends the surface "from insight to action." Named deepening engagements: BlackRock, Block, Sayari, 1Password, Indeed. AI products credited with ~half the quarter's acceleration. (InsiderScore AI summary, AI/product and strategic-positioning sections; Zukin and Materne exchanges.)
- **Implication:** Q1 was the quarter agentic monetization first entered the print; Q2 is the quarter it became the stated corporate strategy with a named framework and a second scaled product. Materne's exchange put the new-versus-core contribution at roughly an even split — meaning agentic products are now approaching parity with the core platform as a growth driver, which is a faster ramp than the Q1 note anticipated.

### ai_re_architected_incumbent

- **Status:** Confirm
- **Evidence:** 330+ product capabilities delivered in H1, **+35% YoY**. Internal AI deployment cited for material marketing cost reduction and faster financial planning cycles. Management explicitly framed operational discipline as "strategic integration of AI to reduce reliance on headcount growth," with slowing expense growth despite rising cloud costs from AI workloads. Non-GAAP operating margin +400bps YoY to 15%; GAAP operating margin improved 1,247bps YoY. (InsiderScore AI summary, operational-efficiency section; margin figures from 8-K Ex-99.1 and FactSet Fundamentals.)
- **Implication:** The dogfooding story is holding into a second quarter and is now visible in the operating margin trajectory, not just anecdotes. The important nuance: SNOW is converting AI-driven opex leverage into headroom to absorb AI-driven *cost of revenue* pressure — the 400bps operating margin gain is being earned while product gross margin gives back a point. That internal trade is the actual mechanism behind the raised margin guide.

- **Overall** (current: none — not yet scored)
