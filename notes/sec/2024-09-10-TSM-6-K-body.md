TSMC August 2024 Revenue Report
HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Sep. 10, 2024 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for August 2024: On a consolidated basis, revenue for August 2024 was approximately NT$250.87 billion, a decrease of 2.4 percent from July 2024 and an increase of 33.0 percent from August 2023. Revenue for January through August 2024 totaled NT$1,773.97 billion, an increase of 30.8 percent compared to the same period in 2023.
