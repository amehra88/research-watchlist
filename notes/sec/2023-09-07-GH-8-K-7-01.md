Item 7.01
Regulation FD Disclosure.
Guardant Health, Inc. (the “Company”) will host an Investor Day on September 7, 2023 beginning at 8:00 a.m. Eastern time in New York City. The presentation will include remarks from Helmy Eltoukhy and AmirAli Talasaz, co-Chief Executive Officers, and other members of the executive management team, to highlight the Company’s operations and to provide updates to the Company’s key strategic priorities and financial outlook.
All interested parties may access the live and archived webcast of the presentation on the “Investors” section of the Company’s website at: www.guardanthealth.com .
