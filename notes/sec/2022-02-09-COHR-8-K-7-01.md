Item 7.01.
Regulation FD Disclosure.
On February 9, 2022, II-VI Incorporated (the “Company”) issued a press release to report its results for its fiscal 2022 second quarter ended December 31, 2021 (the “Release”). The Release was filed as an exhibit to a Current Report on Form 8-K filed by the Company on February 9, 2022. The Outlook section of the Release incorrectly stated the Company’s outlook for earnings per diluted share on a non-GAAP basis for the third fiscal quarter ending March 31, 2022. The purpose of this Current Report on Form 8-K is to correct that information.
The Company’s outlook for earnings per diluted share on a non-GAAP basis for the third fiscal quarter ending March 31, 2022 is $0.75 to $0.95, rather than $0.75 to $0.90 as reported in the Release. The remainder of the Company’s statements in the Release remain unchanged, including with respect to the highly volatile nature of non-GAAP adjustments and the Company’s low visibility as to the range that may be incurred in the future.
The information in this Current Report on Form 8-K shall not be deemed “filed” for the purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or otherwise subject to the liabilities under that Section. Furthermore, the information in this Current Report on Form 8-K shall not be deemed to be incorporated by reference into the filings of the Company under the Securities Act of 1933.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company has disclosed financial measurements in this Current Report on Form 8-K that are considered to be non-GAAP financial measures. These measurements are not a substitute for GAAP measurements, although the Company’s management uses these measurements as an aid in monitoring the Company’s on-going financial performance. Non-GAAP earnings per share excludes non-recurring or unusual items that are considered by management to be outside the Company’s standard operation and excludes certain non-cash items. There are limitations associated with the use of non-GAAP financial measures, including that such measures may not be entirely comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies, due to potential differences among calculation methodologies. Thus, there can be no assurance whether (i) items excluded from the non-GAAP financial measures will occur in the future or (ii) there will be cash costs associated with items excluded from the non-GAAP financial measures. The Company compensates for these limitations by using these non-GAAP financial measures as supplements to GAAP financial measures. Investors should consider adjusted measures in addition to, and not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial performance measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.
