Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders
On December 8, 2022, Fabrinet held its 2022 Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”). Present at the Annual Meeting in person or by proxy were holders of 34,851,171 ordinary shares of Fabrinet, or approximately 95.3% of the total shares entitled to vote. The voting results for each of the proposals considered at the Annual Meeting are provided below.
