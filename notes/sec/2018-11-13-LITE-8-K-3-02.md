Item 3.02. Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities.
Lumentum Holdings Inc. (the “ Company ”) issued 1,454,126 shares of its common stock to Amada Holdings Co., Ltd. (“Amada”) upon the conversion of 35,805 shares of the Series A Preferred Stock of Lumentum Inc., a subsidiary of the Company (the “ Series A Preferred ”), held by Amada. As previously disclosed in the Company’s periodic filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission, on July 31, 2015, 40,000 shares of the Series A Preferred were originally issued to Viavi Solutions Inc. (“Viavi”) in a private placement transaction. Pursuant to a securities purchase agreement between the Company, Viavi and Amada, 35,805 shares of Series A Preferred were sold by Viavi to Amada in August 2015. The conversion occurred pursuant to the terms of the Certificate of Incorporation of Lumentum Inc. and was exempt from registration under Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933. The conversion was deemed effective as of November 2, 2018 and the shares were issued on November 12, 2018.
