Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.
On March 26, 2020, following his interview earlier the same day with Yahoo Finance, Enrique Lores, the Chief Executive
Officer of HP Inc., made the following comment :
"Just to clarify, with reference to the question about Q2 and the rest of the year:
On earnings guidance we continue our policy not to comment on guidance mid-quarter. When I said we are not changing our guidance my intent was to say we are not
commenting on guidance. When I referred to the plan my reference was to the manufacturing plan in China we developed and discussed in February.”
The information in this Item 7.01 shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section and
shall not be deemed incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
