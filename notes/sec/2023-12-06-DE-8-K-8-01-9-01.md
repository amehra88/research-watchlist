Deere & Company Raises Quarterly Dividend
​
MOLINE, IL (December 6, 2023) –The Deere & Company (NYSE: DE) Board of Directors today declared a quarterly dividend of $1.47 per share payable February 8, 2024, to stockholders of record on December 29, 2023.
The new quarterly rate represents an additional 12 cents per share over the previous level of $1.35.
