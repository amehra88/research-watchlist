Item 8.01 Other Events.
On August 16, 2018, Welltower Inc. (the “Company”) issued $600,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 3.950%
Notes due 2023 (the “2023 Notes”), $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 4.950% Notes due 2048 (the “2048 Notes”) and $200,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 4.250% Notes due 2028
(the “2028 Notes” and, together with the 2023 Notes and the 2048 Notes, the “Notes”) pursuant to an automatic shelf registration statement of the Company on Form S-3 (File No. 333-225004) filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on May 17, 2018 (the “Registration Statement”). The 2028 Notes are a further issuance of, are fungible with, are consolidated and
form a single series with, the $550,000,000 aggregate principal amount of our 4.250% notes due 2028 that the Company issued on April 10, 2018. The Notes were sold pursuant to an Underwriting Agreement, dated as of August 9, 2018, between
the Company and Barclays Capital Inc., Citigroup Global Markets Inc. and MUFG Securities Americas Inc., as representatives of the several underwriters.
The Notes were issued under an Indenture between the Company and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as trustee (the
“Trustee”), dated as of March 15, 2010, a form of which was filed with the Registration Statement (the “Indenture”), as supplemented by Supplemental Indenture No. 13 between the Company and the Trustee, dated as of
April 10, 2018 (“Supplemental Indenture No. 13”), and as further supplemented by Supplemental Indenture No. 14 between the Company and the Trustee, dated as of August 16, 2018 (“Supplemental Indenture
No. 14”). The 2023 Notes bear interest at a rate of 3.950% per year, payable semiannually in arrears on March 1 and September 1 of each year, commencing March 1, 2019. The 2023 Notes mature on September 1, 2023. The
2048 Notes bear interest at a rate of 4.950% per year, payable semiannually in arrears on March 1 and September 1 of each year, commencing March 1, 2019. The 2048 Notes mature on September 1, 2048. The 2028 Notes bear interest at
a rate of 4.250% per year, payable semiannually in arrears on April 15 and October 15 of each year, commencing October 15, 2018. The 2028 Notes mature on April 15, 2028.
The foregoing description of the Indenture, Supplemental Indenture No. 13, Supplemental Indenture No. 14 and the Notes is qualified
in its entirety by reference to the Indenture, Supplemental Indenture No. 13, Supplemental Indenture No. 14, the form of global note due 2023, the form of global note due 2048 and the form of global note due 2028, filed herewith as
Exhibits 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5 and 4.6, respectively, and incorporated by reference herein.
