TSMC October 2024 Revenue Report
HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Nov. 8, 2024 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for October 2024: On a consolidated basis, revenue for October 2024 was approximately NT$314.24 billion, an increase of 24.8 percent from September 2024 and an increase of 29.2 percent from October 2023. Revenue for January through October 2024 totaled NT$2,340.09 billion, an increase of 31.5 percent compared to the same period in 2023.
