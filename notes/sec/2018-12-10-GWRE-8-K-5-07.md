Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
On December 6, 2018, the Company held its Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). The three proposals considered at the Annual Meeting are described in detail in the Company's 2018 definitive proxy statement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on November 5, 2018.
