Item 8.01. Other Events.
To allow additional time to engage in constructive dialogue with the Ministry of Commerce People’s Republic of China (MOFCOM) aimed at
securing regulatory approval of their proposed business combination, Applied Materials, Inc. and Tokyo Electron Limited have refiled their merger notification form with MOFCOM.
The closing of the transaction is subject to customary conditions, including regulatory approvals. The parties continue to expect to close the
transaction in the second half of 2014.
