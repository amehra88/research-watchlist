TSMC March 2024 Revenue Report
HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – April. 10, 2024 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for March 2024: On a consolidated basis, revenue for March 2024 was approximately NT$195.21 billion, an increase of 7.5 percent from February 2024 and an increase of 34.3 percent from March 2023. Revenue for January through March 2024 totaled NT$592.64 billion, an increase of 16.5 percent compared to the same period in 2023.
