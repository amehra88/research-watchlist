CHANGE
OF CUSTODIAN BANK
We hereby
announce that we were recently informed that JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., the depositary for our American depositary shares (the “ ADS(s) ”),
will change its custodian to HSBC Hong Kong with effect from August 12, 2024. As a result, from August 5, 2024 to August 12, 2024, we
will not be able to facilitate the conversion between our ADSs and our ordinary shares registered on our Cayman Islands share
registrar and the conversion between our ADSs and our ordinary shares registered on our Hong Kong share registrar, Computershare
Hong Kong Investor Services Limited.
Save for
the abovementioned impacts, the change of custodian will not affect the trading of our securities on The Nasdaq Global Market or on the
Hong Kong Stock Exchange during the migration period.
