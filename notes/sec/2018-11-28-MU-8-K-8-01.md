Item 8.01.
Other Events.
On November 28, 2018 during a webcast presentation, Sanjay Mehrotra, the President and CEO of Micron Technology, Inc. (the “Company”), noted that for the first quarter of fiscal 2019, the Company’s revenue will be near the low-end of its previously announced guidance range of $7.9 billion to $8.3 billion, and non-gaap earnings per share will be somewhat above the previously announced guidance midpoint of $2.95.
The information set forth in this Item 8.01 is intended to be furnished under Item 8.01 of Form 8-K. This information shall not be deemed "filed" for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the "Exchange Act"), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section. In addition, this information shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, regardless of any general incorporation language in such filing, unless it is specifically incorporated by reference in such filing.
