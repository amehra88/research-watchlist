Announcement of change to Director and CEO on behalf of TSMC Arizona Corporation, a major subsidiary of TSMC
TSMC Arizona Corporation adopted the following resolutions by written consent on September 25, 2025 (Taipei times):
• Special meeting of shareholders for election of Director. In light of the position adjustment, Y.L. Wang will step down as a Director of TSMC Arizona Corporation, effective October 1, 2025. Ray Chuang was elected as a Director of TSMC Arizona Corporation, effective on the same date.
• Board meeting for election of CEO. In light of the position adjustment, Ray Chuang was elected as CEO of TSMC Arizona Corporation, effective October 1, 2025.
