Item 5.07
Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
On December 9, 2020, Palo Alto Networks, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2020 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, proxies representing 77,259,767 shares of the Company’s common stock, or approximately 81.35% of the total shares entitled to vote, were present in person or by proxy. The matters before the Annual Meeting were described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement (the “Proxy Statement”) filed with the United States Securities and Exchange Commission on October 20, 2020.
