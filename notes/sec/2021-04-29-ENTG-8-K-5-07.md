Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
On April 29, 2021, Entegris, Inc. (the "Company") held its 2021 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the "Annual Meeting"). As of March 5, 2021, the record date for the Annual Meeting, there were 135,247,595 shares of the Company's Common Stock issued and outstanding and entitled to vote on the matters presented at the Annual Meeting. Holders of 127,046,687 shares of the Company's Common Stock, or 93.93% of the outstanding shares entitled to be cast at the Annual Meeting, which constituted a quorum, were represented at the Annual Meeting in person or by proxy. The following proposals, which were described in the Company's definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 17, 2021, were voted upon and approved at the Annual Meeting:
