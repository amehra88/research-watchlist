Item 8.01.
Other Events.
II-VI Incorporated (“II-VI”) was notified on August 25, 2021 that its pending merger with Coherent, Inc. (“Coherent”) received unconditional clearance by the Federal Cartel Office in Germany. The remaining competition clearances are in the People’s Republic of China and South Korea. As previously noted, the parties believe that the merger will close during the first calendar quarter of 2022.
