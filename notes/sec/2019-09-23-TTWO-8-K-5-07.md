Item 5.07
Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
On September 18, 2019,
Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (the “Company”) held its annual meeting of stockholders (the
“Annual Meeting”) in New York, New York. As of the record date for the Annual Meeting, the Company had
113,178,028 shares of its common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Common Stock”), issued and outstanding.

submitted to a vote of the stockholders at the Annual Meeting.
