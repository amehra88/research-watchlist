ITEM 8.01 OTHER EVENTS.
On November 29, 2019, Lumentum Holdings Inc. (the “Company”) made a voluntary pre-payment of $150 million in principal amount under the Company’s senior secured term loan facility (the “Term Loan Facility”). Pursuant to the Credit Agreement with Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch and certain other parties, which provides for the Term Loan Facility, there is no penalty upon the Company's pre-payment of the Term Loan Facility.
As of December 4, 2019, $196 million in principal amount remains outstanding under the Term Loan Facility.
