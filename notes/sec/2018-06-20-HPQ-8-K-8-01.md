Item 8.01.
Other Events.
On June 19, 2018, the Board of Directors (the "Board") of HP Inc. ("HP") authorized an additional $4.0 billion for future repurchases of its outstanding shares of common stock. HP intends to use the additional authorization to repurchase its shares from time to time to offset the dilution created by shares issued under employee stock plans and to repurchase shares opportunistically. As of April 30, 2018, HP had approximately $1.2 billion of repurchase authorization remaining under the share repurchase authorizations approved by the Board.
Repurchases by HP under the repurchase program may be effected from time to time through open market purchases, trading plans established in accordance with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's rules or other means, depending on satisfactory market conditions, applicable legal requirements and other factors. The repurchase program does not obligate HP to repurchase any particular amount of common stock, and it may be suspended at any time at HP's discretion.
