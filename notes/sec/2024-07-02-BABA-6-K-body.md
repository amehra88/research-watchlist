Share Repurchase Update
During the quarter ended June 30, 2024, Alibaba Group Holding Limited
(NYSE: BABA and HKEX: 9988 (HKD Counter) and 89988 (RMB Counter), “we”) repurchased a total of 613 million ordinary shares
(equivalent to 77 million ADSs) for a total of US$5.8 billion, including the repurchase of approximately 14.8 million ADSs at US$80.80
per ADS through privately negotiated transactions concurrently with the pricing of our convertible senior notes offering on May 23, 2024.
These purchases were made in both the U.S. and Hong Kong markets under our share repurchase program.
As
of June 30, 2024, we had 19,024 million ordinary shares (equivalent to 2,378 million ADSs) outstanding, a net decrease of 445 million
ordinary shares compared to March 31, 2024, or a 2.3% net reduction in our outstanding shares after accounting for shares issued under
our ESOP.
