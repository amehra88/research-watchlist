TSMC January 2024 Revenue Report
HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Feb. 7, 2024 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for January 2024: On a consolidated basis, revenue for January 2024 was approximately NT$215.79 billion, an increase of 22.4 percent from December 2023 and an increase of 7.9 percent from January 2023.
