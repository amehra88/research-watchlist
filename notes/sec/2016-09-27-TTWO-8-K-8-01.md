Item 8.01 Other Events
On September 27, 2016, Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (the “Company”) elected to settle its conversion obligations in connection with any 1.75% Convertible Senior Notes due 2016 (the “Notes”) submitted for conversion on or after September 28, 2016 or at maturity entirely in shares of its common stock. In accordance with the foregoing, the Company has notified The Bank of New York Mellon, the Trustee for the Notes, that the Settlement Method (as defined in the Indenture, dated as of November 16, 2011 (as the same may have been supplemented, the “Indenture”)) for all Notes submitted for conversion with a Conversion Date (as defined in the Indenture) on or after September 28, 2016 or at maturity shall be Physical Settlement (as defined in the Indenture).
