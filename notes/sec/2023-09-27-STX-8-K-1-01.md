Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
On September 27, 2023, Seagate Technology Holdings public limited company (the “Company”) and its subsidiary Seagate HDD Cayman (the “Borrower”) entered into the Tenth Amendment to the Credit Agreement (as defined below) with The Bank of Nova Scotia, as administrative agent (the “Agent”) and the requisite lenders under the Credit Agreement (the “Amendment”). The Amendment amended the Credit Agreement dated as of February 20, 2019, as amended from time to time, by and among, the Company, the Borrower, the Agent and the lenders from time to time party thereto (the “Credit Agreement”), to, among other things, (i) modify and/or remove certain financial covenants, (ii) permit certain internal restructurings of the Company and its subsidiaries, and (iii) modify certain other restrictive covenants. The other material terms of the Credit Agreement remain unchanged.
Certain of the lenders under the Credit Agreement and their affiliates have engaged in, and may in the future engage in, investment banking and other commercial dealings in the ordinary course of business with the Borrower or its affiliates. They have received, or may in the future receive, customary fees and commissions for these transactions.
