Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
On July 10, 2019, MongoDB, Inc. (the “Company”) held its Annual Meeting of Stockholders (“Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, the Company’s stockholders voted on the four proposals set forth below. A more detailed description of each proposal is set forth in the Company’s Proxy Statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on May 30, 2019.
Proposal 1 - Election of Directors
Each of Charles M. Hazard, Jr. and Tom Killalea was elected to serve as a director of the Company’s Board of Directors until the 2022 Annual Meeting of Stockholders and until his successor has been duly elected, or if sooner, until the director’s death, resignation or removal, by the following votes:
