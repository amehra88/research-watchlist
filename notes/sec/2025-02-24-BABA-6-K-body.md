Alibaba to Invest RMB380 billion in AI and Cloud Infrastructure
Over Next Three Years
Alibaba Group Holding Limited (NYSE: BABA and HKEX: 9988 (HKD Counter)
and 89988 (RMB Counter), “Alibaba” or “Alibaba Group”) announced plans to invest at least RMB380 billion (US$53
billion) over the next three years to advance its cloud computing and AI infrastructure, reinforcing its commitment to long-term technological
innovation and underscoring the company’s focus on AI-driven growth and its role as a leading global cloud provider.
