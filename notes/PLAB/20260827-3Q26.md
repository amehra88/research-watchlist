# PLAB — Q3'26 earnings read

_Prepared by earnings-reviewer, 2026-08-27. Tier: T2 (`tier_2_active_candidates`, added 2026-06-24; also duplicated in `tier_3_watchlist`)._

**Fiscal period note:** fiscal Q3 2026 **ended August 2, 2026** (per 8-K Ex-99.1, verbatim). PLAB has an October fiscal year end. FactSet month-end-normalizes this to `fiscalEndDate` 2026-07-31 — see §10.

## 1. Headline read

Photronics beat its own prior-quarter guidance on all three guided metrics and beat a Street bar that had been cut after last quarter's soft guide — but the beat is a recovery of deferred timing, **not an improvement in operating economics**: non-GAAP EPS of $0.50 is *down* from $0.51 in Q3'25 on +2.7% revenue, and operating margin compressed 180bp YoY. Against the 2Q26 note — which read the quarter as a guide-down driven by delayed IC design releases — this print **confirms the delay was timing, not demand destruction**, while introducing two new watch items: a decelerating FPD segment (+13% YoY last quarter → −2% YoY this quarter) and an R&D line down 28% YTD against unresolved EUV questioning.

Three things the surprise line hides:

1. **Non-GAAP net income was flat YoY — $29.428M vs $29.356M — despite +2.7% revenue.** Margin compression absorbed the entire top-line growth. A ~1.1% higher diluted share count (58,718K vs 58,068K) then turned flat dollars into a **$0.01 EPS decline** ($0.51 → $0.50). Demand was not the drag; margin and dilution were.
2. **GAAP EPS rose $0.39 → $0.49 on non-operating items that *more than fully* explain the improvement — operating income actually fell.** Operating income went from ~$48.2M (22.9% × ~$210.3M) to ~$45.6M (21.1% × $216.0M), **down ~$2.6M**. Meanwhile other income (expense), net swung from **−$9.428M** to **+$9.368M**, an ~$18.8M swing (FX/JV). GAAP net income rose ~$6.0M. So the entire GAAP improvement — and then some — is non-operating. Non-GAAP strips this out, which is why the non-GAAP line is flat while GAAP is up 26%; the non-GAAP adjustment itself was only ~$0.5M this quarter vs ~$6.5M in Q3'25.
3. **YTD operating margin is down 270bp** (21.9% vs 24.6%) and YTD gross margin down 220bp (33.2% vs 35.4%). The sequential GM recovery (31.3% → 33.2%) is a rebound off a weak Q2, not a return to prior-year levels.

## 3. Forward guidance

**FQ4 2026 guide (8-K Ex-99.1, verbatim):** revenue **$207–227 million**; operating margin **19–24%**; non-GAAP diluted EPS **$0.40–0.56**.

**Versus Street (FactSet `consensus_rolling`, relFiscal=1, as-of 2026-08-27, `fiscalEndDate` 2026-10-31 — correct for the Oct FYE):**

| Metric | Guide midpoint | Consensus (n=3) | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $217.0M | $215.69M | +0.6% |
| Non-GAAP EPS | $0.48 | $0.4433 | +8.3% |

**This is a directional reversal from last quarter.** In 2Q26 the guide midpoint sat 1.7% below Street on revenue and 15.5% below on EPS. This quarter the midpoint sits modestly *above* Street on both. FactSet already shows one upward EPS revision (`up: 1`) on the day after the print.

**Guidance philosophy changed, and management said why.** The revenue range widened to a $20M band (vs. $8M last quarter). Management attributes this to reduced visibility from high fab utilization, elevated memory costs, and geopolitical uncertainty, and specifically to the fact that **a single high-end mask set can materially swing quarterly revenue** [summary-derived paraphrase]. The EPS band widened correspondingly to $0.16 (vs. $0.06). Read this as management declining to narrow a range it cannot forecast, rather than as sandbagging — the midpoint is above Street, which is not the shape of a sandbag.

**Capex guidance cut, framed as timing.** FY2026 capex guidance revised to **$255–305M from $330M**. Management: "capex guidance reflects the timing of tool delivery receipt with a portion of capex moving from FQ4 2026 into fiscal 2027. U.S. and Korea expansion plans remain on track." [verbatim — 8-K Ex-99.2 slides]

**Watch item — the implied FQ4 capex does not reconcile.** YTD capex is $130M [summary-derived; internally consistent with the disclosed 37.0 + 45.8 + ~47.2 quarterly progression]. Against the revised $255–305M full-year range, FQ4 alone must absorb **$125–175M** — versus $37.0M in Q3 and roughly $43M average across Q1–Q3. Even the low end is ~3.4x the Q3 run rate.

The arithmetic is **directly at odds with management's own explanation**: they said capex is moving *out of* FQ4 into fiscal 2027, which should make FQ4 smaller, not 3–4x larger. That asymmetry points to two co-equal explanations, and the PM should not assume the second:

1. **A reporting error in the summary-derived inputs.** The $130M YTD figure and the $255–305M range both reach this note via the AI summary, which §10 documents as demonstrably unreliable on this very call (it softened a −2% FPD decline into "near all-time highs"). If either number is misstated, the gap closes. **This is the more likely explanation.**
2. **The deferral into FY27 is larger than the $25–75M headline cut implies** — i.e. the FY guide was effectively cut by more than stated.

Verify against the FQ4 print and the FY26 10-K cash-flow statement. If FQ4 capex lands materially below $125M and the $255–305M range was accurate, explanation 2 holds.
