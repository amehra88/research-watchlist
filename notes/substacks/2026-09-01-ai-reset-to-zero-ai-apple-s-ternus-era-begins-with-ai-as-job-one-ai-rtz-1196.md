# [AI: Apple’s Ternus Era Begins, with AI as Job One. AI-RTZ #1196](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=684161&post_id=213619843&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxMzYxOTg0MywiaWF0IjoxNzg4MjM5MjEwLCJleHAiOjE3OTA4MzEyMTAsImlzcyI6InB1Yi02ODQxNjEiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.K94U2O5LuBlyIC2ax2zG0IqiMqv_m-cLbV3ngTS5WiI)

### ...25 of Apple’s 50 years, AI as job one, Apple Silicon on up, Cook upstairs. Compounding the Jobs & Cook legacies.

Well, it’s official. Apple has rebooted.

John Ternus is Apple’s chief executive as of today, September 1 2026.

Tim Cook is now executive chairman after his 15 years at the helm following the iconic founder/CEO Steve Jobs.

The long-telegraphed changing of the guard at the world’s favorite tech company is done, right on schedule, the Apple way. I discussed the handoff on this week’s [ARD #152](https://substack.com/redirect/afdf95c6-9fb1-4920-9e4b-a9ea99346dd4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and covered the announcement when it landed in April in [AI-RTZ #1063](https://substack.com/redirect/6112920e-c7f5-4988-bdd4-69430a19829e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Now the fuller profiles are in. They sharpen the picture of what Apple looks like from here, mid [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/2e3bcacb-46e4-4d8e-b5b7-699f07c1de9b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Uber-Apple reporter Mark Gurman lays it out in Bloomberg Businessweek’s [“Apple’s Ternus Takes the Reins as CEO, With AI as Job No. 1”](https://substack.com/redirect/d4ea2eef-2540-4d8b-8da9-74ef95f41cf2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA):

The key takeaways from the piece are useful to note. The transition will be gradual, with Cook as executive chairman, keeping the Washington and Beijing briefs he knows best.

The deputies step up, with COO Sabih Khan, CFO Kevan Parekh (no relation), and services chief Eddy Cue all poised for bigger roles.

And the calendar does not wait. Ternus hosts the September 9 iPhone launch, foldable expected, with Cook not featured on that stage for the first time in 15 years.

The veteran. Ternus, 51, has been at Apple for 25 of its 50 years. Half the company’s life, and nearly half of his. He started as an engineer designing Macs. Then iPads, AirPods and iPhones, and eventually the entire hardware engineering operation. Cook began seriously considering him as successor two years ago, and put it simply to employees: “He’s an elevator guy.”

Clear vision from 30,000 feet, and the ability to go to the basement on the precise details. That is the profile Apple chose for its next CEO. With a similar time-span, he has until 2041 to do his thing. Leading with Siri AI driven iPhones and Apple Services.

I’d make five points that are important markers for the John Ternus Era.

- Leaning into AI

The full-scale deployment of the new Siri AI comes this month, rebuilt on Google’s Gemini models under the hood, a deal I covered in [AI-RTZ #1113](https://substack.com/redirect/381c5a7f-a5de-4712-a22a-0940c89ac70f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Early reviews are positive so far. In one of his last moves as hardware chief, Ternus stood up an AI platform to speed Apple’s own product development and testing. And the device pipeline is unusually ambitious: the foldable iPhone and a smart display that recognizes who is speaking this fall, with camera-equipped AirPods, smart glasses and new home devices further out. He is clear-eyed on the misses too. He never truly backed the Vision Pro as a mainstream product, per Bloomberg, and is open to retreating from head-worn devices if the glasses do not gain traction.

- Hardware, with Srouji.

The other half of the story is the stack under the products. Chief Hardware Officer Johny Srouji now runs Ternus’s old hardware engineering group, combined with his silicon operation. That gives Apple’s new hardware CEO a vertical stack from silicon on up, the thing rivals cannot buy off a shelf.

I have written about Apple thinking [‘out of the box’ on AI chips and M&A](https://substack.com/redirect/22efcc18-8781-491e-8057-f00384d011c9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and about Apple silicon’s unified-memory edge in [the ‘Need for Speed’ in AI inference](https://substack.com/redirect/f6ac5fca-ae99-4b93-8a67-b3c1ec63fc38?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The retention watch is real, though. Srouji, 61, considered leaving last year and stayed for the transition. He has truly put Apple on a unique silicon trajectory relative to peers. So he could leave in a year or two and Apple could continue without a hiccup.

Hundreds of former Apple hardware folks now work at OpenAI, whose poaching A[pple is suing over](https://substack.com/redirect/38299946-9c86-4290-b182-d1d52032ce89?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

And roughly half the senior leadership team could turn over in the next few years, per Gurman. Apple’s [‘People Problem’ signals](https://substack.com/redirect/176748da-be61-4a96-998b-7307517e046f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) were an early read on exactly this.

- The compounding franchise

In the meantime, Apple under Ternus continues to compound the Steve Jobs global product and brand franchise, and the truly under-appreciated Tim Cook global [supply chain legacy.](https://substack.com/redirect/7b26f7e2-b769-42af-92bd-8c3e680699c8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) Especially with years of [‘RAMageddon’](https://substack.com/redirect/c0382f38-610b-409a-8f54-b02e2013fe03?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) ahead.

Underline the Cook financial legacy with this: Warren Buffett ended up making [one of Berkshire’s most profitable investments ever](https://substack.com/redirect/e4d46385-9188-4a29-aba8-689d92cf8c1a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in Tim Cook’s Apple, a stake first bought in 2016 that grew into its largest holding by far. As Buffett put it at Berkshire’s 2025 annual meeting,

> “Tim Cook has made Berkshire a lot more than I have made Berkshire.”

The Verge underlines the operational half: Cook’s [relentless supply chain mastery, from the early Foxconn bet on, was its own kind of innovation](https://substack.com/redirect/6e742cb6-b242-40fe-9238-ab1cf87ca3f6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), delivering an era of predictable profitability. That is the unique Cook legacy: an unparalleled global supply chain, and an incredible financial record navigating Apple through global growth over his 15 years.

Ternus has pushed back on the idea that the iPhone is nearing the end of its relevance. He oversaw the three-year revamp now underway: the thinner Air last year, the foldable this year, and a redesigned model for the iPhone’s 20th birthday in 2027.

His argument to Wall Street analysts this summer: people want a do-it-all device with a screen. Add billions of active Apple devices worldwide, and the opportunity in front of a hardware CEO is immense as AI moves into the physical world, the subject of this weekend’s [Physical AI two-parter](https://substack.com/redirect/1d98eb97-ce9a-498d-aeb3-ea38386cab10?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). I have also made the longer case for Apple, alongside Nvidia, as [a global US open source AI champion](https://substack.com/redirect/41d74cf4-b2c0-4457-a92a-8f0ee106ab80?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Above Avalon’s Neil Cybart marks the day with [“Gadgets Won’t Define Tim Cook’s Legacy”](https://substack.com/redirect/65fb8386-afdc-4582-be89-69e87b39eac0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). His argument: judging Cook by product launches misses the point. Cook’s legacy is the culture he sustained and solidified after Jobs, small teams with unusual autonomy, buttressed by ideals of privacy, trust and environmental responsibility. It shows up in the numbers: the iPhone installed base grew 13x from 2011 to 2026, the iPad 12x.

Cybart rates Cook among the best all-around CEOs of the modern business era, and argues future Apple CEOs will be graded the same way, on the culture they sustain rather than the products they launch. He closes the loop on succession too: a strong culture is what builds the internal bench, and Ternus, who succeeded Dan Riccio and Bob Mansfield in the hardware chair, is himself the proof.

And I [continue to be of the view](https://substack.com/redirect/69770882-0661-4ee8-b34c-c6a5447b8b0d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)that with Siri AI, Apple Personal Intelligence build on foundational infrastructure from Google and Nvidia, the iPhones of today and tomorrow are likey the [mainstream AI devices](https://substack.com/redirect/20a5a429-f70d-4e0e-9dad-0d6a70a20331?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in the hundreds of millions we are a[ll waiting for.](https://substack.com/redirect/987aac53-1ff8-4275-9b2a-117793f7d984?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) Not the ones from [OpenAI/Jony Ive](https://substack.com/redirect/baa3ba4f-6f68-4acc-bf8d-f88ddf4467f1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), [Meta Smart Glasses](https://substack.com/redirect/f06013b2-c068-4c06-833e-f404f6be777c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), Google Gemini/Android Pixels, and others.

- Cook, upstairs

The executive chairman is not going far. Colleagues threw him a farewell dinner at Apple Park on August 23. His memo to employees on his last day, via 9to5Mac’s [“Read Tim Cook’s full memo to Apple employees on his last day as CEO”](https://substack.com/redirect/4c686cb9-0c5f-4fb4-a9c2-8428476fc32f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), reads as a man at peace with the decision, taking comfort in the handoff, and telling staffers the company remains his number one priority. He keeps the Trump and China relationships.

The closest model, per people around Apple, is Jeff Bezos handing Amazon to Andy Jassy while staying executive chairman. The cautionary one is Disney, where Bob Iger hovered and eventually took the company back. As Cook himself reminded employees, there is only one CEO at a time.

- The new and old guard evolve around him

The same day, Bloomberg reported that Phil Schiller, 66, is stepping down from running the App Store and Apple’s product events, in [“Apple’s Phil Schiller Steps Down from Running App Store, Product Events”](https://substack.com/redirect/26b53a87-746e-429e-9a28-9361ba638956?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The App Store, estimated at more than $30 billion a year, moves under Eddy Cue’s services division. Events go to Nola Weinstein. Schiller stays on as an Apple Fellow. It is the pattern flagged on the show yesterday: much of Apple’s long-tenured senior bench is expected to turn over in the next few years, right as the new CEO settles in.

My take: this is succession done the Apple way, and that is the point. A 50-year-old company promoted a veteran who has been there for 25 of those years, and it chose the hardware chair to do it. Cook’s numbers are the outgoing benchmark, with revenue roughly quadrupled to $416 billion and market value up 15-fold on his watch, per Bloomberg. The next act is different. It is about products again. Compounding Apple’s investments and legacies.

The opportunities in front of Ternus are immense, and they are leveraging Apple’s unique vertical tech stack. From chips to Applications.

Apple owns its silicon with Srouji, its devices, its operating systems, and the iPhone franchise billions of people carry every day. AI as job one, on top of that stack, is a different proposition than AI as a feature. And the succession keeps Cook, the operator who built Apple’s global supply chain machine, next door in the executive chairman’s office for the geopolitics.

As I outlined on [ARD 152,](https://substack.com/redirect/afdf95c6-9fb1-4920-9e4b-a9ea99346dd4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) Cook served 15 years. If Ternus serves a Cook-length tenure, the next handoff lands around 2041, on something like iPhone 34. Decisions made this September will echo for a very long time. It is about time, compounding in every sense.

Apple will [make its dent](https://substack.com/redirect/4c686cb9-0c5f-4fb4-a9c2-8428476fc32f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on the [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/2e3bcacb-46e4-4d8e-b5b7-699f07c1de9b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) that will be noticeable. Stay tuned.
