# [AI: Frontier Labs’ FDE Armies Battle for the Enterprise. AI-RTZ #1190](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=684161&post_id=212248363&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxMjI0ODM2MywiaWF0IjoxNzg3NzIwNzE2LCJleHAiOjE3OTAzMTI3MTYsImlzcyI6InB1Yi02ODQxNjEiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.t7EESGaSgVZpFXxrj8mqhfZ1rskAKIPc8TxAX_5FPFc)

### ...Anthropic’s $1.5B Ode with Blackstone & Goldman, OpenAI’s $4B TPG venture, & Karp’s Frontier Model warning.

The frontier labs are forward deploying platoons of engineers deep into enterprises. Helicoptering them in as fast as possible ahead of their mega-AI IPOs.

We spent Monday on Anthropic’s record IPO pitch and Tuesday on OpenAI’s pre-IPO re-grounding through their ongoing [senior people churn.](https://substack.com/redirect/2d480cc0-fd39-4222-822e-249c4b96e098?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) Today, the battlefield both roadshows are really about: the enterprise, and the armies of engineers the frontier labs are now deploying into enterprises via a range of partners. The fashionable acronym is ‘FDE’ of course, for ‘forward deployed engineers. It’s a wave to watch in this [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/9135bb06-c8bc-4038-9c16-6a13c79e3449?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

The WSJ lays it out in [“Private Equity Is Deploying an Army of AI Wonks to Embed in the Firms They Back”](https://substack.com/redirect/2df101b4-e759-43e4-a8f9-9344e9669a72?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and Anthropic have formed Ode, a standalone firm with a roughly 160-person senior engineering team. Part of a $1.5 billion joint venture whose backers also include Apollo, General Atlantic and my alma mater Goldman Sachs, with the three lead partners committing around $300 million each.

Ode acquired the startup Fractional AI in May to seed the bench, and plans to sell far beyond the private equity world; its CEO calls the customer pipeline immense. OpenAI has formed a rival consulting venture with a $4 billion investment led by TPG. The key items to build on: the embedded engineers, the money behind them, and how this an old playbook modified for this AI wave.

The case studies are decidedly unglamorous. At Chamberlain Group, Blackstone’s garage-door opener maker, Ode engineers sit in three-person pods helping build a ‘digital doorman’ business: package alerts, familiar-face recognition, routine-learning software, three million cameras going onto homes this year. Chamberlain now expects $500 million of annual revenue from that line by 2030, against the $160 million ceiling it projected before the venture, with its Prime Day ad campaign running ten times cheaper on AI tools.

Accounting firms Citrin Cooperman and Baker Tilly have Ode engineers mapping auditor workflows for AI agents. Meanwhile CEO anxiety is cooling: 20% of CEOs in a May EY-Parthenon survey expect AI to drive significant job cuts, down from 46% in January 2025; the pitch has shifted to new revenue, not headcount.

Regular readers will recognize the playbook, as it was shaped with an AI narrative by Palantir.

These are [Palantir’s Forward Deployed Engineers, the FDEs we covered as they went from engineers to entities](https://substack.com/redirect/220218c2-a962-4f7e-9ea8-83139ed0cc91?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), adopted wholesale by the frontier labs as they deepen enterprise sales. Anthropic is wholly committed to the strategy, [the premium enterprise machine at the heart of its IPO pitch](https://substack.com/redirect/2ab2c217-3291-4ece-8d7c-311d10b627c4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA); OpenAI is running the same play, with [enterprise now bigger than consumer](https://substack.com/redirect/93d3b72b-79a7-4a1d-83e3-cee59382abd7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) per its CFO. The goal is the long tie-in: enterprises wired into their frontier models at the top, their smaller faster models below, and the productivity layer of Claude Cowork, ChatGPT and the coming super apps on every desk, [the agents-into-the-enterprise arc we mapped early](https://substack.com/redirect/68a7fa7e-2a6e-4b3c-9a73-e8f1f5fc8e9e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Which flies directly into the counter-narrative we covered in [the ‘Us vs Them’ trillion-dollar enterprise AI fight](https://substack.com/redirect/4fccc3d7-c567-4b0f-9d84-9d0a4548fb72?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Palantir’s Alex Karp has been harshly warning CEOs that the frontier labs are hoovering up their data and IP, and will eventually compete with the very businesses they serve, positioning Palantir as the shield and the layer in between, with its own FDEs on site. Microsoft under Satya plays the same game with a leg in each tent, [having picked its side versus the frontier models](https://substack.com/redirect/750c6736-ac2e-4122-87cd-583817508071?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) while [leaning its own AI shifts into the enterprise](https://substack.com/redirect/c23d1fc8-5aa1-495b-ba9b-edea53c4a5e4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), just a bit softer than Karp about it.

That is why the data-retention split this week matters more than it looks.

Axios lays it out in [“OpenAI previews zero-retention safety system as Anthropic requires data logs”](https://substack.com/redirect/4c065a16-dd53-49bd-9d20-55e9c507f8a7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

OpenAI is previewing ‘Private Safety Processing’, a system meant to catch misuse patterns across related interactions while preserving zero data retention: narrow safety signals only, customer data on customer-controlled infrastructure or under customer-held encryption keys, white paper due in September.

Anthropic has gone the other way, requiring 30-day data retention for business customers on its most capable Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models, conceding in its own risk report that the decision will be unpopular and commercially risky, but calling it essential to catch sophisticated attacks that span multiple requests. The same week, OpenAI paused some new-model training work over safety concerns while Anthropic said it sees no current need. The poses keep swapping, [as we covered in Strike a Pose](https://substack.com/redirect/9b058ca4-1f3d-4716-9505-c2acc6cdc4ce?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Retention is exactly the surface Karp’s warning lives on: the fear that the model company sees your data. OpenAI is turning zero-retention into a sales weapon against Anthropic’s security-first logging; Anthropic is betting enterprises will accept a 30-day window from the safety-first vendor. Both are answering the shield argument without naming it.

Zoom all the way out and this is the AI makeover of traditional enterprise software and services, a multi-trillion dollar global market catered to for decades by IBM, Salesforce, SAP, and countless other software majors across prior tech waves.

[The entrenched software battle we framed](https://substack.com/redirect/a5101178-8cbe-41e0-a978-e83c5aa6bf1a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [watched shift at IBM and Microsoft](https://substack.com/redirect/8f599e73-313b-4a1b-b487-b1c55491eed7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Those incumbents deployed software services reps embedded long-term at enterprise customers; the AI companies have simply renamed them FDEs. An updated term for a similar consultant, sales and services role, wired in for the long haul.

My take: the FDE armies are the frontier labs building the enterprise relationship layer directly. Investing billions of dollars of joint ventures with the very PE and financial firms that control thousands of businesses, rather than waiting for CIOs to come to them. It’s a development that cuts across [AI Tech Stack](https://substack.com/redirect/9135bb06-c8bc-4038-9c16-6a13c79e3449?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). With Box no. 6, the AI Application and Services layer, being the holy grail.

The long-term battle is over who owns that layer: the labs directly through ventures like Ode and TPG’s, the shields like Palantir, or the platforms like Microsoft straddling both. Data trust just became the pricing card in that fight, and with both labs heading to the public markets, enterprise trust IS the roadshow.

Decades of enterprise software history say the embedded services rep always wins more than the product demo. And then there’s billions in the maintenance and services layers.

The frontier labs are re-defining that enterprise software history for the [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/9135bb06-c8bc-4038-9c16-6a13c79e3449?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), their way. And deploying them for duty as fast as possible. Stay tuned.
