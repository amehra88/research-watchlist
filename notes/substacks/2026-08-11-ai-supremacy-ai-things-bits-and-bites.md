### Amazon Robotaxi Zoox Enters AV Race

Amazon’s robotaxi Zoox has launched its first paid service in U.S. this August. Las Vegas is first. San Francisco and other markets should follow. It’s Sin City to the max, with more robotaxi competitors entering the fray even as [Uber is investing again](https://substack.com/redirect/65f74a05-0d9a-4b5d-afa4-75d4209f8819?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in autonomous vehicles. A lot of consumers [totally forgot about Zoox](https://substack.com/redirect/184e7aec-3de9-46e2-9d3a-a8bc2be2472c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Paid rides on Zoox became available for the first time ever, with services that launched in Las Vegas this Monday (Aug. 10). Tesla robotaxis that only use cameras have been a huge disappointment. While Waymo is the outright leader in the U.S. There’s no steering wheel here so more room. The seating feels like public transport with these weird carriage-style seating arrangements.

### Nvidia Organizes Financing Consortium with Wall Street for AI Infra

When you are financing Neo Clouds, why not go all in? Nvidia makes a rather outrageous claim that [AI factory compute is becoming an investable asset](https://substack.com/redirect/ca4d871d-9d7b-4211-b737-755f542538b6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). It’s a [$500 Billion third-party](https://substack.com/redirect/d4e9cda7-d9e2-4d5d-b71d-4ed5256f86af?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) financing gig. Nvidia has cash to spare and will backstop up to $125 ​billion, or 25% of the potential deals. Financial partners include at least Apollo Global Management, Blackstone, BlackRock (via Global Infrastructure Partners), Brookfield Asset Management, Goldman Sachs, and KKR. Nvidia has been guilty of some creative circular and vendor financing deals in what we can only assume is a U.S. National defense AI infra mandated project where they think they need all of this compute. We are entering larger and larger gigawatt scale AI clusters of datacenters, even as HBM memory chips and other bottlenecks make said compute way more expensive pushing hyperscaler Capex higher for multiple reasons. Now Datacenters are increasingly being financed on debt including [off-balance-sheet margin debt](https://substack.com/redirect/7535510e-5037-4326-87b7-6a1777f6eb4b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

### Meta Claims to be an Open-Source Good Guy

Mark Zuckerberg wrote an OpenAI or Anthropic-esque “essay” about the future of AI called [The Future is for Everyone](https://substack.com/redirect/4b24329b-32b8-4c59-9ab1-38951842450b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). While suddenly pretending that Meta isn’t a Metaverse company but a benevolent AI open-source lab. Meta released [Muse Glimmer](https://substack.com/redirect/ba9248a7-b22f-4fff-9610-97207f618d73?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), a neat little model that appears to be yet another pivot in[their rather baffling](https://substack.com/redirect/1c2f5feb-ee9b-4adf-b3de-d715cf6397c6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [constantly shifting](https://substack.com/redirect/8a835b5c-e0d5-494e-8ad9-8fa2231a7447?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) AI strategy.

Muse Glimmer competes favorable with Alibaba Qwen3.6-27B and has a favorable no strings attached Open-source permission. So at least they finally executed on a frontier model, in a manner of speaking (it’s been a while).
