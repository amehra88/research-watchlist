### Charting the Incredible China AI Boom

If you are reading this article, you have probably already witnessed[Unitree Robotics’ listing](https://substack.com/redirect/d31ed677-ae7d-4578-ad8e-d332d6e0a27c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on Shanghai’s STAR Market.

Or, more likely, you did not.

That is exactly the point.

This story is not really about one robotics company. It is about an entire market that most global investors rarely see: China’s AI-driven technology rally.

Over the past 18 months,[the Shanghai Composite Index](https://substack.com/redirect/394e87ae-5764-4bf9-b3ab-a6dceb90a92e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) has risen 17.3 per cent. That is a respectable return, but it has not beaten the S&P 500 or the Nasdaq. The real gains have happened elsewhere, among the technology companies listed on[ChiNext](https://substack.com/redirect/1f98b461-4a01-4719-aec8-f95f3ee3be31?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), Shanghai’s[STAR Market](https://substack.com/redirect/fcc00396-3efe-4c33-9907-d2d0a45d4ea7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and the[Hong Kong Stock Exchange](https://substack.com/redirect/829cf625-6a2a-416f-8084-ffc80679e111?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Once you move beyond China’s broad market indexes, the picture changes considerably. Some Chinese AI-related indexes have even outperformed the Nasdaq over the same period.

So why has the rally attracted so little attention?
