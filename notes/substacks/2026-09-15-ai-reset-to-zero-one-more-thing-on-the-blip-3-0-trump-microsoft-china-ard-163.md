ARD #163](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=684161&post_id=215865353&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxNTg2NTM1MywiaWF0IjoxNzg5NTAyNzQzLCJleHAiOjE3OTIwOTQ3NDMsImlzcyI6InB1Yi02ODQxNjEiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.5zCGTschsduTHKm4roqKs8LaSHdxkvj68OW7ZavMSxE)

### ...Anthropic AI ‘Pace Pause’ now a wrestling match: Trump’s hoax call to Jensen, Microsoft’s rules, Beijing’s rebuke. Siri AI ships.

It is Tuesday, and the AI ‘pace pause’ show continues.

Today’s theme is ‘One More Thing’, in honor of Apple, which had a big week and has more to discuss today. But one more thing really applies to a lot more things on what I have been calling ‘The Blip 3.0’, Anthropic’s call for the whole industry to pace the frontier.

Dario Amodei made that call on Saturday. His peers and arch rivals, Sam Altman, Elon Musk and Demis Hassabis, jumped in within hours. The exception was Nvidia’s Jensen Huang, who said this may be more of a show: watch what they are trying to sell, and how much of it.

I covered all of that on [Sunday’s Bigger Picture](https://substack.com/redirect/3e2615d7-c5e6-4313-92f9-586d9b9e514c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), [yesterday’s show](https://substack.com/redirect/4fb83e0e-dae8-4ec1-a99a-9416c0ebe59b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [yesterday’s post](https://substack.com/redirect/26747ebf-614b-44f9-b041-3a9a45b88a89?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on Jensen.

One day later, the drama intensified, in this [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/4019abaa-e2bc-4bbe-837b-93f02d85959f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Three things today, my takes on each, and an overall take.

One, how Washington is responding, starting with the president on Jensen Huang’s speakerphone. Two, how the rest of Big Tech is responding, starting with a 15,000-word code of conduct from Microsoft. Three, how China is responding, abroad and at home.

Then the Gadget AI. Apple shipped Siri AI to the world this week. Its own one more thing.

## (1) They All Talk and Pose. Trump, the Speaker, Sam and Jensen

First up. On Monday the president called Jensen Huang while Jensen was on stage at a tech conference in Los Angeles, co-hosted by Trump’s former AI czar, David Sacks. Jensen put the president on speaker in front of the audience.

The message, per the[New York Times](https://substack.com/redirect/64683388-cef3-494b-8b06-f76bd0393858?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): Dario Amodei is executing a hoax. There is no AI safety crisis. ‘The robots are not going to be taking over the world.’ I agree with most of that, by the way, an exception in this context.

A five-minute call, with a lot in it.

That morning Trump had already posted, per [Axios](https://substack.com/redirect/2df8bb5e-b844-4edb-9c40-46ce012a94a9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [Bloomberg](https://substack.com/redirect/c6927e4c-7969-4c95-ad16-32321ff40ed6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The only guardrails AI needs are ‘a strong and smart, high IQ, president’. The doom talk is a ‘sick conspiracy’. We are already leading China. He named Dario specifically.

There are also reports the administration is talking behind the scenes with the other companies. Trump [met privately](https://substack.com/redirect/22d0b5de-e104-49fd-babf-085850e5b9fd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) with Sam Altman at a Republican event last week, at Sam’s request. Everyone is talking to everyone.

And Jensen continues to hold the line. Whistle-blowing is fine, he said. The scientific prediction about the future is less fine, because it is not grounded in science. Pausing and pacing are voluntary things a company can do on its own if it feels out of control.

Then the Speaker. Mike Johnson said Monday, per [Bloomberg](https://substack.com/redirect/717f923b-3217-4d5d-a4d2-f884c583925c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), that Congress ‘potentially’ needs guardrails, carefully, preserving the edge over China. Fifty days before the midterms. On Sunday he had said Congress would not lead.

Sam Altman, meanwhile, made three moves in three days. Thursday, the private meeting with Trump. Sunday night, [a post](https://substack.com/redirect/df1fa817-ffc1-4931-aad1-300368bdd353?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) with 5 million views: pacing is not stopping, and no amount of competitive pressure justifies recklessness. Tuesday, per [Politico](https://substack.com/redirect/979932d3-3c91-4c90-a0b6-6203b8a312a8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), OpenAI backed a bipartisan House bill that would put outside evaluators inside the big labs by law.

MP Take: I agree with Jensen. I have written for years, well before ‘AI became AI’, that we are over-anthropomorphizing this technology. Including the AI researchers, who are humanizing the thing they build.

It is technology. Remarkable technology, probabilistic rather than deterministic, but still software and hardware computing technology.

When we look back in a few years, I dare say we will be scratching our heads at the ‘AI Anthropomorphizing’ tempest in a teapot.

MP Take: Right now everyone is using the dramatic aspects of AI for their own purposes, and getting attention doing it.

Millions of views for the president on Truth Social, for every party’s blog posts, podcasts and social posts. The drama continues, all sides have their lines and are in on the act.

It is [Strike a Pose](https://substack.com/redirect/f11d8198-8515-451f-a76e-506a92dba9aa?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), which I wrote a few weeks ago, with a bigger stage. And everyone is playing every side of the room, the core companies like OpenAI and Microsoft most of all.

## (2) Big Tech Reacts. Microsoft’s Code, a DeepMind Exit, and Three Labs Already Talking

Second. Microsoft published its own 15,000-word ‘[Humanist AI Code of Conduct](https://substack.com/redirect/691f2ddf-7047-42e7-9c3a-7f5a20d746f0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)’ on Monday. A draft, out for six weeks of public comment, to govern its own models from 2027.

I have a [whole shelf of AI leader essays](https://substack.com/redirect/3e2615d7-c5e6-4313-92f9-586d9b9e514c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) now, fourteen or fifteen of them, Bill Gates included.

And a reminder of why the fear is overdone, relative to other technologies advanced and commercialized at scale by mortals.

Now Microsoft’s ‘Humanist [AI Code of Conduct](https://substack.com/redirect/691f2ddf-7047-42e7-9c3a-7f5a20d746f0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)’ missive goes on the shelf.

Per [The Verge](https://substack.com/redirect/99f55eab-28f8-45e0-b54e-a45563e864c1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), the models are not conscious.

Underline that. And they should not imitate consciousness. Underline that too. No legal personhood, no rights, no model welfare. Models stay subordinate to humanity, and fail a task rather than break the code.

It is a direct swipe at the consciousness talk coming out of Anthropic, which Microsoft AI’s Mustafa Suleyman called ‘really, really dangerous’ back in June. Satya Nadella set it up on Sunday: the benefits cannot be controlled by a handful of entities.

The side drama continues too. Another AI researcher, this one at Google DeepMind, [resigned Monday](https://substack.com/redirect/0218666e-d49d-4ded-a546-c4d98f92f831?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) with an exit post saying he earnestly believes AI has the potential to kill us all. They keep walking out the door with a manifesto.

And the coordination is happening behind the scenes. OpenAI’s policy chief Chris Lehane said Tuesday, per [Bloomberg Law](https://substack.com/redirect/6498df00-e6b0-46e0-b11b-eeef626501dc?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), that OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind have been working together on safety for weeks. And that they do not need an antitrust waiver to do it.

That has been the important legal issue underneath all of this, because companies in any US industry are not supposed to coordinate on business matters.

Then today. Jensen, Dario and Sam are all at Marc Benioff’s Dreamforce in San Francisco, per [Axios](https://substack.com/redirect/20171b63-c876-4d6b-8875-b31321c1f131?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). That is Benioff’s stage, and there will be more drama from it to talk about tomorrow.

MP Take: I agree with Microsoft on this, and with how Satya is setting it up.

There are billions of models and agents ahead, not a singular model or set of models to ‘take control’ as[I’ve long argued.](https://substack.com/redirect/2d16eedc-f669-4d81-81b0-f60acecffa36?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

The whole notion of a single Skynet-type model is just highly entertaining science fiction.

Reality is billions of models and billions of agents, and we are going to be stunned at the number when all is said and done. Microsoft’s code is worth reading in full. On the shelf it sits closer to Jensen’s pragmatic end than to the doomers’.

MP Take: My key take is that the broader tech industry has now officially entered what I call[The Blip 3.0.](https://substack.com/redirect/4fb83e0e-dae8-4ec1-a99a-9416c0ebe59b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

The [Blip 1.0](https://substack.com/redirect/3b61025c-f85b-42ef-96cd-0e6e985e108b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) was Sam Altman fired and rehired over a weekend, three years ago.

[The Blip 2.0](https://substack.com/redirect/6429c2d6-4ff0-4b1d-98ef-0948568df1c9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) was the government stopping Anthropic from selling its Mythos models for three weeks. This one could run much longer.

And remember, all of it is happening while Anthropic is gunning, pedal to the metal, for its IPO. [Today’s post](https://substack.com/redirect/7f99965c-1fc9-46be-8181-90da5af4d0ce?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) has the revenue and margin numbers. They have not lost sight of the end goal while making all of these dramatic claims.

## (3) China’s Reaction. Fearmongering Abroad, a Threat to Party Rule at Home

Third. All of this is ahead of the big meeting between President Trump and President Xi at the White House on September 24, and everyone is posturing for that too. Beijing answered the essay two ways.

Externally, per [Bloomberg](https://substack.com/redirect/698aa744-cd99-4ac5-88fe-87e9a0f86d99?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), the Foreign Ministry said ‘fearmongering, confrontation and vicious competition’ will only disrupt global AI governance. We do not abide by the US safety positioning that frames China as the threat.

More interesting was a separate essay, internally directed. Chen Yixin, the head of the Ministry of State Security, China’s top spy, published his most detailed statement yet on AI, per the [New York Times](https://substack.com/redirect/79de1433-6216-42d0-be34-1eac1ccfa68f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), in the Party’s own cyberspace magazine.

The threat he describes is not external. It is internal. AI as a potential threat to the Party’s hold on power. Deepfakes of officials in propaganda wars, something our own regulators worry about too. Foreign models, he named Claude and OpenAI’s, as tools for espionage and attacks on infrastructure. His answer: stricter oversight and more Party control.

MP Take: A reminder from China’s security chief of their priority number one, the security of the Party. That is an independent variable from the drama in the US-China negotiations, and it does not move for a summit. I laid out [six steps](https://substack.com/redirect/a803eb47-8331-482f-aa69-d0fa23ef02b8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) for a White House meeting back in May, and none of them was a joint AI slowdown.

MP Take: But underneath that, China has to be worried about one more thing. AI runs on data. So far all of the models, the open-source ones from China included, run on the world’s internet data. Increasingly the Chinese market, the second largest AI market in the world, is going to want its own data harnessed as well.

And that data sits behind China’s famous internet firewall, with a lot missing from it. Models as capable as these are claimed to be will find all of it. They will find Tiananmen Square and they will be able to talk about it. And a whole lot more.

That is what the head of state security is talking about without talking about it, and it is beyond the drama. I wrote about [looking beyond the space and AI races](https://substack.com/redirect/f9dedfe4-1439-4801-9c6a-49f1a4a8f0fa?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in July; the two sides are still not describing the same problem.

## My Overall Take

All three events, when you come back to the drama of it, are more of the same. Just at higher intensity.

The president is louder. The Speaker is hedging: Sunday it was ‘we are not going to do anything’, Monday it was ‘we may want to consider some motions’. The labs are coordinating and competing in the same breath. Microsoft is in, with a very long essay.

And Beijing is saying, do not blame us if you want a good deal when we meet in nine days.

No one has paused anything that started this weekend. The Blip 3.0 is going to intensify.

MP Take: Three things I would hold onto. The one concrete thing surviving the noise is outside evaluators inside the labs, the thing Dario asked for.

The industry seems to be supporting it, and OpenAI just backed a House bill for it. That was one of the core things Dario wanted from all of this, and he may be on his way to getting it.

MP Take: The broader thing. I have been calling this kabuki theater, but when I step back it may be more akin to one of President Trump’s favorite sports: wrestling. This feels like a wrestling match.

Everyone is in the ring, everyone is pretending to be mad at each other, throwing punches and counterpunches, throwing one another against the ropes and the floor, with more lines yelled out for the cameras, the podcasts and the evening shows. A wrestling show with the signature drama.

MP Take: Trump calls the whole thing a hoax, live on Jensen Huang’s speakerphone in front of hundreds of investors. The Speaker talks guardrails. Sam Altman supports and does not support Dario. Sam and Elon agree and disagree behind the scenes, with antitrust waivers in the background. It is all drama, all posing, all wrestling.

MP Take: In the meantime, China is pragmatically executing, bottom up.

Its companies are not distracted from what they have to do: building the best AI they can, building the power plants and the utilities and then the data centers, on technology that may be a few months or a few years behind the US, but with a lot more of it. The race in that sense continues.

Do not lose sight of that when we focus on this one more thing, The Blip 3.0, in this [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/4019abaa-e2bc-4bbe-837b-93f02d85959f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

## Gadget AI: Apple Ships Siri AI in iOS 27

Apple just shipped. If you have any recent model of iPhone, Mac, iPad, Watch or AirPods, you have big free updates this week: the 27 series of operating systems, per [The Verge](https://substack.com/redirect/bd8270c9-32d6-4e73-89a2-d777f410156b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Check them out and download them all.

They bring useful features, stability and safety. But the core thing they bring is Siri AI, and Apple’s personal intelligence, with a higher level of trust and privacy than the alternatives. Siri AI is a beta in English today, with French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish in October.

Siri AI gets your personal data and your apps. It lives everywhere: the side button, the wake word, and a new home in Spotlight when you swipe down. Type or talk. A standalone Siri app keeps your conversations. Six Colors’ [review](https://substack.com/redirect/2ca2ac6d-2684-4fd7-8bf4-dd7869e2c1f2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) calls it a ‘Snow Leopard year’ of small fixes, plus one doozy.

MP Take: I continue to view Siri AI, with its focus on the local data in the applications on your phone, across a couple of billion devices around the world, as the AI gadget we have all been waiting for.

It is a whole different type of personal, local AI that we have not had, even with ChatGPT or Google Gemini. And of course Apple is partnering with Google Gemini for the model, [the deal](https://substack.com/redirect/d28158a9-b2da-4ea2-9791-2069cc4f4343?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) from June, and with Nvidia for the chips in its server clouds.

All of the companies are working together on it.

I said Siri AI was [ready for takeoff](https://substack.com/redirect/2c66fd7a-2efd-4645-82a8-cad322a121b2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in June. Takeoff was this week.

### Q&A

Q1: What is my favorite thing in iOS 27?

ANSWER: I have been using Siri AI and iOS 27 in the developer version for weeks now. No surprise, it is how Siri AI works with your local data: Messages, Photos, Notes, all of the first-party Apple apps. It miraculously finds things relevant to you from years ago when you just ask, typed or spoken, in a choice of voices.

That part works. It is more useful in many ways than the general ChatGPT moment we all had three-plus years ago. And I am glad it is now available to mainstream users at large.

Q2: What is the key thing to watch on Siri AI for the rest of this year?

ANSWER: Third-party application support. Developers tend to support Apple’s platforms before Android, even though Android is two to three times larger globally in units. Android is far more fragmented; Apple’s ecosystem is unified. Something like 60 to 80 percent of Apple devices upgrade to the latest operating system, against 5 to 15 percent on Android.

So third-party support for Siri AI, which Apple is rolling out aggressively, is the secret sauce. Data from your Spotify, your running apps, your exercise and food apps, integrated into Siri AI. OpenAI does not have that. Claude does not have it yet. Meta and Google are trying.

Apple has it, on the [App Intents](https://substack.com/redirect/a9d24eab-a7dd-42b7-9bf3-cdf8f392e00e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) plumbing it has been laying since last year. How fast that integration happens is the thing I am watching.

## Clips from today

### The Blip 3.0 Is A Wrestling Match

I have been calling the AI slowdown drama kabuki theater. Step back and it looks more like one of President Trump’s favorite sports: wrestling.

Everyone is in the ring, everyone is pretending to be mad at each other, throwing punches and counterpunches, bouncing each other off the ropes, with lines yelled out for the cameras, the podcasts and the evening shows.

MP Take: Trump calls the whole thing a hoax, live on Jensen Huang’s speakerphone in front of hundreds of investors. The Speaker talks guardrails. Sam Altman supports and does not support Dario. Sam and Elon agree and disagree behind the scenes. All drama, all posing, all wrestling. And while the US wrestles, China’s companies pragmatically execute.

### Siri AI Knows What’s On Your Phone

Apple shipped iOS 27 and Siri AI this week. I have been using the developer version for weeks, and my favorite thing is how Siri AI works with the local data on your phone: Messages, Photos, Notes and the rest of Apple’s first-party apps. It finds things relevant to you from years ago, whether you type or talk to it.

MP Take: That part works, and in many ways it is more useful than the original ChatGPT moment three years ago. Siri AI with local data across a couple of billion devices is the AI gadget we have been waiting for, a personal, local kind of AI that ChatGPT and Gemini in a browser tab cannot be.

Now it is available to mainstream users at large.

### Siri AI’s Secret Sauce: Third-Party Apps

The key thing to watch on Siri AI for the rest of this year is third-party app support.

Developers tend to support Apple’s platforms before Android, even though Android is two to three times larger globally in units, because Android is fragmented and Apple’s ecosystem is unified: 60 to 80 percent of Apple devices upgrade to the latest OS, against 5 to 15 percent on Android.

MP Take: Third-party data from your Spotify, your running apps, your exercise and food apps integrated into Siri AI is the secret sauce. OpenAI does not have it. Claude does not have it yet. Meta and Google are trying to get it. Apple has it. How fast that integration happens is the thing I am watching.

### China’s AI Data Dilemma, Behind The Firewall

China’s top spy chief just published his most detailed statement on AI, and the threat he describes is internal: AI as a risk to the Party’s hold on power. Underneath that is one more thing China has to worry about. AI runs on data, and so far the models, including China’s open-source ones, run on the world’s internet data.

MP Take: China is the second largest AI market in the world and will want its own data harnessed too. That data sits behind the Great Firewall, with a lot missing. Models as capable as these are claimed to be will find all of it, Tiananmen Square included.

That is what the security chief is talking about without talking about it, and it sits beyond the US-China drama.
