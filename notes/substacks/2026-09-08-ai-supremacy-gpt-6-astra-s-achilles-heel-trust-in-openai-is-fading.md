### GPT-5 Astra will be Less Trackable and Knowable

OpenAI’s new Astra model uses a reasoning technique called “recurrent depth” that allows it to operate outside of the sequential thinking that characterizes most reasoning models, [The Information had recently reported](https://substack.com/redirect/a978d460-dcd7-4f53-bd19-c9bd2aa4023f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Astra is essentially skipping steps meaning it’s more difficult to read and obviously align. For instance, as the model has grown more intelligent, it handles complex reasoning steps internally or with far fewer language tokens. Because it bypasses the need to write out every granular step, the traditional safety safety-net of reading a clear "thought process" becomes less reliable. [How is that a good thing](https://substack.com/redirect/16e93ced-4332-4174-8614-d841666c761c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) if you care about Trust and Safety, nevermind bigger issues of alignment?
