AI-RTZ #1207](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=684161&post_id=215252761&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxNTI1Mjc2MSwiaWF0IjoxNzg5MTg5NTQ5LCJleHAiOjE3OTE3ODE1NDksImlzcyI6InB1Yi02ODQxNjEiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.iFpBG1LTbJLBSZlZqTfdpXGoYJ3pvbVQIPXoIlis-J8)

### ...Round up for week ending Friday, September 11, 2026

- Nvidia leans into Open Source with Hugging Face: [Nvidia’s](https://substack.com/redirect/63f186d1-0dbd-4b13-9aee-8aa8d22c47a8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) surprising [$13B+ acquisition](https://substack.com/redirect/041a5bc3-3847-47fd-aa6d-d5506909f428?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) of [decade old Hugging Face](https://substack.com/redirect/30c772c4-ae9b-480c-a18e-2b1b9e645f61?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) highlights its growing global commitment to open source AI platforms. The price, $12,930,300,000, hides an emoji in hexadecimal, per the WSJ’s profile of the three ‘deeply nerdy’ French founders who came to New York in 2016 to build a chatbot less boring than Siri. What they built instead is the home shelf of open AI: 3 million models, 18 million developers, 200,000 enterprise customers, per Jensen Huang on [CNBC’s Squawk Box](https://substack.com/redirect/89b493fc-9eac-4df9-bb87-eba864f3cf3c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) the morning after. The catalyst, in CEO Clément Delangue’s telling, was July’s [agent breach](https://substack.com/redirect/7f1b73c8-8835-427b-9f29-1598c587f231?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) of Hugging Face’s own infrastructure, which the company could only dissect with an open-weight model from a Chinese lab; the proprietary APIs’ guardrails would not let it. Huang’s framing is the one to hold: use closed models as much as you can, and remember the other half of Nvidia’s business runs on open ones, so Nvidia grows in both directions. My take: the [‘Kingmaker’](https://substack.com/redirect/c6c54ab5-bc63-41e6-b68a-604268b2d69f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) just bought the town square. After funding, convening, lobbying and allying for open models all summer, Nvidia now owns where the world goes to get them, whichever lab built them, and the shelf’s biggest tenants are [Chinese](https://substack.com/redirect/89bf5f0a-d48d-4ba2-a535-77e2d77111b5?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Open and closed are not a contest with a winner; they are two halves of one market that grow together, as I argued in the case for [Nvidia and Apple as the US open source champions](https://substack.com/redirect/61537703-40a8-4f20-9ef1-727ee769b76e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The checks: neutrality has to be proven, roughly 80 times forward revenue is a price, and owning the shelf does not build the [rocket](https://substack.com/redirect/86e03256-e99c-4729-9dac-08d8156a66a4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). More [here.](https://substack.com/redirect/33b4c858-523b-4a11-958b-9467d57d2a02?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)[](https://substack.com/redirect/3d0dde17-b0a9-4757-93b4-c4fec019c60f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

- Anthropic & OpenAI’s AI Compute Totals ahead of mega-AI IPOs: Both Anthropic and OpenAI’s [AI Compute commitments](https://substack.com/redirect/dff5ddf9-a4da-4de9-b1ad-ad642db316d3?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) now total over a trillion dollars on latest reports. The Information tallied Anthropic at $517 billion of deals in eleven months, for at least 14.8 gigawatts of capacity, close to three times the $180 billion it told investors nine months ago. OpenAI’s own forecast [moved to about $750 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/64bdf85b-e9b4-4563-b6d6-d9c3f237998f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) through 2030, from $665 billion, with 30 gigawatts planned. Three numbers do not point the same way: OpenAI leads on dollars and gigawatts, Anthropic leads on revenue, over $65 billion annualized against more than $40 billion, on two different [revenue rulers](https://substack.com/redirect/afe73223-1e1d-4df8-956b-9776d6b0ddf1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Anthropic is now buying compute from nearly every rival it has: Amazon and Google for 11 gigawatts, Microsoft Azure for a gigawatt, SpaceX at $1.25 billion a month with a ninety-day exit, the neoclouds for $80 billion, and its own data centers with Fluidstack, TeraWulf and a twenty-year Riot lease. Nvidia sits on both sides, supplier and investor, with Huang calling the $100 billion OpenAI pledge [‘never a commitment’](https://substack.com/redirect/4668fa7a-6995-4665-b04d-ab5b1edf352f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). My take: the frontier race has stopped being about who ships the better model this quarter. It is about who holds the power contracts and who can pay for them. The inversion is the story, the smaller compute book under the larger revenue line, and it is only defensible if the capacity arrives; [compute constraints throttled growth](https://substack.com/redirect/95cd9158-1c2a-468c-8f10-d490eeab0fd4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in May. A ceiling and a contract are different instruments, and the audited prospectus is where we learn which parts of these ledgers survive. Hold onto the [gigawatt as your unit](https://substack.com/redirect/c61c22b5-f552-4dcc-a13f-8863d21ca1cd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), at $50 billion plus each. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/f937bd53-0aaf-4b00-abd3-9db646c10853?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). [](https://substack.com/redirect/7ca69bc0-b347-4ea5-9595-40dfc82d1cc1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

- OpenAI’s cautionary ‘Alien Mind’ AI Essay: OpenAI’s chief scientist, [Jakub Pachocki,](https://substack.com/redirect/23a8935e-b313-414c-beb1-ce9d12338a8e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) published an [essay on AI](https://substack.com/redirect/d2d9dd32-e283-41bb-8cc6-10b20a1eba2d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) that added to regulatory concerns over AI Cybersecurity risks. It is the most candid statement from a frontier lab this year: progress is driven by compute, nobody fully understands what scaling produces, doing the task is not the same as holding the values, and the lab’s main safety tool, reading the model’s chain of thought, is progressively diminishing. His ask is extreme caution, voluntary slowdowns, and safety bars made law. The same week an Anthropic researcher [quit the industry](https://substack.com/redirect/3a2c33ee-1bd2-4a25-9a4f-e298cad05de2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) over the fear the essay describes, and Anthropic’s alignment lead [put his own odds](https://substack.com/redirect/1a636306-770a-47a6-9743-0de44961510a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on AI killing every human at better than one in ten within a decade, weeks before Anthropic markets a mega-AI IPO. My take: I agree the black box got bigger; I have said the best scientists barely understand [why their models do what they do](https://substack.com/redirect/058dcfa6-42fe-4aab-a0b4-b7f9c70d21ad?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) since 2024. But the goal-versus-value distinction is Asimov’s Three Laws from 1942, the fear is the wrong one, and the compute the scenarios run on does not exist yet, at any price, on power not yet permitted. Most of all, a handful of labs cannot gate eight billion people: open weights ship months behind the frontier, distillation runs downhill, and the answer every prior wave found was ship, break, fix, defend, repeat. The [toothpaste is out of the tube](https://substack.com/redirect/46665487-bd69-4647-9b8b-689bf362a23d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Part two of the essay canon, and China’s different method, in tomorrow’s [Bigger Picture](https://substack.com/redirect/f62f0c10-e9da-4cc7-bb46-4448d521f951?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/c35061bb-a124-4e81-95f0-e8df64bfad5d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - AI Coding vs AI Coworking markets: AI Coding continues its white hot growth due to its [measurability in AI benchmarks](https://substack.com/redirect/076d276f-5863-4171-be32-08a0de0731b1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), while AI Coworking, the larger longer term market, remains less measurable. Code compiles or it does not; tests pass or fail; an AI coder gets a free verdict on every turn. A memo, a market analysis, a plan for the week get none, and what is left is a feeling. Even the coders’ own numbers do not say what coders think: [METR’s randomized trial](https://substack.com/redirect/ab1214d5-9a73-4cdd-b1ff-aeddbf20be1c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) had experienced developers taking 19% longer with AI tools while believing they were 20% faster, a forty-point gap in the one domain where measuring is easy. And on Claude, the most developer-weighted assistant, computer and math occupations are [about 30% of users](https://substack.com/redirect/8d2a1ddc-08ef-4237-840b-b118676d4568?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) against 4% of US employment, which means seven in ten users are doing work nothing can grade. My take: the industry’s entire public scoreboard is built on the part that can be measured, and sold to the far bigger part that cannot. When you cannot verify, you buy a [credence good](https://substack.com/redirect/fed0e9c3-18a3-4311-9fc5-9054d00d940d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and price becomes the quality signal; I do it myself across the top subscriptions, as I wrote in the [‘Race to Zero’ that really isn’t](https://substack.com/redirect/6a5f6f6e-ac88-4dd8-83d3-dc329b9bcd21?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). It also explains the chasm between the labs’ existential essays and the deployed reality: the models those fears describe improve themselves inside a lab, on the coding side, on compute nobody else has. Until AI Coworking gets its own compiler, price stands in for proof. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/79de6ad5-b227-4f78-95cd-3fbea74b7777?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). [](https://substack.com/redirect/3a33555f-7c24-403d-825d-d2f045f2f1a7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

- China’s CXMT vs Big 3 HBM Companies: No. 4 global HBM company [CXMT snagged](https://substack.com/redirect/ef4637a2-4b34-46a9-a917-c319ee808706?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) Xiaomi’s flagship phones for its next gen memory chips. At its first investor event as a public company, ChangXin Memory laid out the case: 10% of global DRAM in the second quarter, up from 4% a year earlier, per Counterpoint; first-half revenue up nearly ten-fold and a swing to profit; and, per a Goldman Sachs estimate in the WSJ, a path to half of China’s DRAM and as much as 40% of its HBM demand by 2028. In a market where memory prices have roughly quadrupled and [20% plus shortages](https://substack.com/redirect/9d235bd9-ce76-4f70-a6e4-aa285c3829cf?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) run through the decade, a supplier going from 4% to 10% in twelve months is the marginal supplier. Apple remains the open question: the WSJ reported in August that Apple has been [testing CXMT memory](https://substack.com/redirect/9c6d2a27-7d9c-4199-a4b4-be072298bab8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) across iPhones and MacBooks and seeking Washington’s blessing, with the iPhone 18 Pro’s bill of materials set to rise roughly 38% on memory alone. My take, three points. CXMT is the global number four and closing the gap on SK Hynix, Samsung and Micron regardless of the politics; the most concentrated critical layer in this AI Tech Wave needs its fourth supplier, and the one with captive demand, state backing and a half-trillion-dollar currency is CXMT. Apple likely gets a blessing eventually and in stages, China-sold devices first, with the September 24 Xi visit the likeliest venue. And the US [hamstrings itself](https://substack.com/redirect/1f8c42e1-615f-4259-864d-8e91773cae2a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) by fencing off CXMT while the incumbents run 75% margins and pass the bill to every American phone, PC and data center. It changes what your next phone costs, not what is in it this fall. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/65aae7f6-3a40-4b10-9047-aa7bd723b0ff?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).
