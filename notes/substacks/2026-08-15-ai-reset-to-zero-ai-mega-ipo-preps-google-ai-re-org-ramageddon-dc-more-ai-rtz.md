### ...Round up for week ending Friday, August 14, 2026

- Anthropic & OpenAI prep for their mega-AI IPOs: Anthropic and[OpenAI](https://substack.com/redirect/76922c93-9c87-4e34-b482-0d44b48e1a9a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) are[ramping up their preparations](https://substack.com/redirect/c383ea1a-7993-4336-87df-c266947781a2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) ahead of their planned mega-AI IPOs later this year and next respectively. Both have filed confidentially with the SEC. Anthropic, last privately valued around $965 billion, is rehearsing its roadshow story with institutions ahead of an anticipated October float, with early investors floating trial-balloon valuations of[$2 trillion or more](https://substack.com/redirect/f668c5a3-4c7b-456d-853d-2c4ecab6584e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): the math being 30x revenues, the ‘incredibly low end’ against Palantir’s 55x, on $100 to $120 billion in annualized revenues by year-end, implying a $3 trillion upper boundary. It is even shopping on the way to the roadshow,[in talks to buy Decart for about $6 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/df1b4078-b853-4eda-9e50-8725ffdbcb6a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). OpenAI, at a $40 billion-plus run rate that has roughly doubled since year-end, is rebuilding its revenue engine for the public markets: a[second new chief revenue officer in under a year](https://substack.com/redirect/9942d54e-fd33-45d6-b061-02a9eee15333?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in Wiz’s Dali Rajic, senior departures from Brad Lightcap to Fidji Simo, about half its business now enterprise, and price cuts plus Codex momentum to close the AI Coding gap with Anthropic. More[here](https://substack.com/redirect/e165673b-2005-4bb0-8278-0d7ab8e33c7e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and[here](https://substack.com/redirect/86397f8d-7320-4c74-bece-fdd801fd6445?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - Google puts AI Products ahead of AI Labs: Google is[moving its global AI management](https://substack.com/redirect/d7c10158-dd28-4875-9868-390bc2ad41dd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) from DeepMind London to California, post[recent senior management changes](https://substack.com/redirect/0fc908f8-4516-4f2e-b706-d5c2efeb721e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), with Koray Kavukcuoglu, ‘KK’, running models, the Gemini app and developer teams as one product group reporting to CEO Sundar Pichai. The consensus scores Google on the frontier model-of-the-month game; my view remains that Google is playing a different one: efficient models scaling to billions with Gemini, and Google Cloud growing 82% as the company’s big focus, selling to the AI who’s who. A product, not a lab, is the right job description for this race. More[here](https://substack.com/redirect/3ce9160c-22e5-4ca0-9816-cad33bd3b8f8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The receipts kept arriving this week: Google[added $100 to every Pixel 11](https://substack.com/redirect/3ce9160c-22e5-4ca0-9816-cad33bd3b8f8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) while trimming RAM,[Lenovo managed through](https://substack.com/redirect/eb84c777-e7c5-4eac-a62d-5e8f7535f823?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) by leaning into AI servers, and China’s state memory maker[CXMT passed Tencent](https://substack.com/redirect/a0b54152-e503-43ba-9285-c3385c3111b9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) as the country’s most valuable company,[through the roof](https://substack.com/redirect/9cac7721-e7f0-4426-ac8f-e99c057d1909?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on the memory boom. New memory supply is 2027 at the earliest, so near term the lobbying decides who waits, while consumers pay retail.
