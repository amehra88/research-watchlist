### Autonomous Teammate Systems

For me, Meta’s Muse and SpaceX’s Grok Bot make chatbots a bit less relevant in that these are about AI as a utility, helper and assistant in accomplishing tasks. They feature agentic capabilities that have:

- Isolated Cloud Machines

- Tools with asynchronous & background execution

- Human-in-the-loop safeguards

- Proactive goal orientated assistants

Grok Bot is aided by X user integration and so even if the [Grok chatbot](https://substack.com/redirect/57613997-bb4f-4749-9fde-a9649b8fc97a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) was a relative failure compared to ChatGPT, Gemini and [Claude](https://substack.com/redirect/f25ac27a-9118-4e52-a058-4b9f62ebf3d2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) - Grok Bot could accelerate SpaceX AI’s Enterprise adoption and Cursor’s real world utility. It remains to be seen what Anthropic and OpenAI come up with in this vertical. Grok Bot [was first introduced](https://substack.com/redirect/0a5e1bd6-7324-4c51-8e3c-62f8812ff8cc?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) about a month ago on August 11th that sounds a lot like Meta’s Muse, branded as an AI teammates you can give real work to.

Grok Bot also seems more like a primarily B2C product but with strong B2B support. We’re still seemingly a far way away from the kind of AI companion that is truly capable even four years after ChatGPT first launched which is astounding to me.
