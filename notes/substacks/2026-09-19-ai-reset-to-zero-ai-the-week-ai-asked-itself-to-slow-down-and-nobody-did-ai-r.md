# [AI: The Week AI Asked Itself to Slow Down, and Nobody Did. AI-RTZ #1214](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=684161&post_id=216291655&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxNjI5MTY1NSwiaWF0IjoxNzg5Nzk0MzgzLCJleHAiOjE3OTIzODYzODMsImlzcyI6InB1Yi02ODQxNjEiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.gb5p5mEsGb5fGq4QwFWcA_pft6pLUvqrnDibrkLUR_o)

### …Dario asks the industry to pace the frontier and his rivals agree, the President calls it a hoax, Wall Street rotates instead of selling, Anthropic floors its IPO, and China just keeps shipping. Busy week ending Friday September 18, 2026

Week of Friday September 18, 2026

1. US AI’s Angst, ‘The Blip 3.0’: It started with an essay. On Saturday the 12th Anthropic’s Dario Amodei published [‘We Must Pace the Frontier’](https://substack.com/redirect/e106f758-77cd-4f7d-84cb-e71562256d0f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), arguing the industry should slow the growth of frontier capability, and committing Anthropic unilaterally to the first of his steps: permanent, employee-level access for third-party evaluators, with the right to publish what they find. The rest of the plan asks democracies to coordinate, and asks for a safe harbour so that labs choosing to pace together are not exposed on antitrust grounds, since coordinating with your competitors is exactly what antitrust law is built to punish. The [Wall Street Journal called the response a rare display of unity](https://substack.com/redirect/85c6ba49-c75e-4375-abff-d80a4de959f2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and it made [Sunday’s New York Times front page](https://substack.com/redirect/95ea0dcb-dd8e-478d-bedc-775e458879e4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Sam Altman [agreed on X](https://substack.com/redirect/ec0c9123-fb9d-4566-9e6c-cbda14c8e9b4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), then [told Fortune](https://substack.com/redirect/8ba5a09f-60ba-4c63-b6eb-8dca96103b4e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) this was an ill-advised moment to go public. Elon Musk said Dario is right, Demis Hassabis said the direction is correct, and [Satya Nadella](https://substack.com/redirect/456add58-bc0a-4b1a-87ec-3ca5d1c9da56?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) welcomed deliberate pacing while warning the benefits cannot end up with a handful of entities. [Politico counted the endorsements](https://substack.com/redirect/1ff5584b-b7a8-4dc8-a143-563e3bb9c0f9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Jensen Huang went the other way, [telling Axios](https://substack.com/redirect/cbeacb0f-c360-4ef0-88d5-a80c4ad07bc7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) the cyber alarm is a way to create demand by creating a problem, and [explaining to TechCrunch](https://substack.com/redirect/ac1dc7f2-4d0e-4182-8bac-55a2d812a0f1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) why Nvidia grows another 70 percent next year: ‘One GPU now is not $399. It’s $8.5 million dollars.’ I sided with him in [AI-RTZ #1209](https://substack.com/redirect/d68269db-5f75-4a4a-9879-5614f7213424?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). My take, from [ARD #162](https://substack.com/redirect/fe750e13-4d45-4046-8f9b-b2bc79a9bfcb?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): this is ‘The Blip 3.0’ as I’ve been describing them. Blip 1.0 was the Altman board weekend in November 2023, which lasted 3 days. The Blip 2.0 was the US halt on Anthropic in June. Lasted almost 3 weeks. This one the industry did to itself, then asked governments for the gates. Be careful what you wish for. Who knows how long this one lasts. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/fe750e13-4d45-4046-8f9b-b2bc79a9bfcb?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [here](https://substack.com/redirect/d68269db-5f75-4a4a-9879-5614f7213424?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

2. US Government Responds: Washington handed the brake straight back. It was sheer theater, the way the President likes his Wrestling matches staged with drama and theater. On Monday the President [called Jensen Huang while Jensen was on stage for the ‘All In’ podcast conference in Los Angeles](https://substack.com/redirect/da0b3724-f4cb-4b84-a438-dfa6120f7a72?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and Jensen held the phone to the microphone: ‘the AI danger is a hoax, and the robots are not going to be taking over the world’ he proclaimed. At an event co-hosted by his former AI Czar, David Sacks. That morning Trump had already posted that the only guardrails AI needs are a ‘high-IQ president’, and that the doom talk is a sick conspiracy, [naming Dario directly](https://substack.com/redirect/bf33487f-dd1c-420c-a57b-3665a358e187?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). [Axios read it as the administration closing the door on a slowdown](https://substack.com/redirect/5039b780-e835-4713-9e39-e396f6152de3?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Speaker Mike Johnson, who had said on Sunday that Congress would not lead, allowed by Monday that guardrails might ‘potentially’ be needed, [at some risk of Trump’s ire](https://substack.com/redirect/9a6b6d8c-3ab3-42e4-a958-bab6c7f476b5?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), fifty days from the midterms. And Sam Altman made three moves in three days: a [private meeting with Trump](https://substack.com/redirect/37e0428f-6420-427a-a660-f49006bf3e52?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) he asked for, [a post](https://substack.com/redirect/d470e30c-8ea4-4056-9add-e346c0ac6fcd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) arguing pacing is not stopping, and then [OpenAI backing a bipartisan House plan](https://substack.com/redirect/ffd29fb9-696b-4054-bbb2-b719ea6cf048?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) to put third-party safety assessors inside the big labs by law, the first federal mandate OpenAI has ever backed. My take, from [ARD #163](https://substack.com/redirect/9e106b89-9382-4256-916a-0c70418900b7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): I upgraded my own frame from kabuki theater to a wrestling match. Everyone is in the ring, everyone throws punches for the cameras, and the global views and mainstream attention keep going up. The one concrete survivor of the noise is ‘outside evaluators’ inside the labs, which is the thing Dario actually asked for. Right now its getting gated approval by his peers, but a lot remains to be nailed down. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/9e106b89-9382-4256-916a-0c70418900b7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

3. Companies Respond: The commercial world got the message and mostly did not over-react to it. Investors did not sell the AI trade, they rotated inside it: [the Journal reported](https://substack.com/redirect/1a2b71d3-f9f8-4152-9162-eaf07bfbed08?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index falling almost 6 percent on Monday while Microsoft, Alphabet and Meta each rose 2 percent or more, money moving out of the chip makers and into the hyperscalers whose data centers buy them. Operators said the same thing with their hands: at the [Journal’s Technology Council Summit](https://substack.com/redirect/eea43e42-9f8e-40bd-92b0-20379efee39b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) about half the room supported slowing development at the frontier, and that slowdown did not apply to their own deployments, because the models companies actually run are a generation or two back and well understood. Then the labs, four of them, each proposing a different fix. OpenAI went to Congress. Elon went to his competitors, telling the All-In Summit that [rival labs should run test harnesses on each other’s models](https://substack.com/redirect/66f69808-adc1-4da4-81ac-a19a5b49d016?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) rather than grade their own homework. [Jensen went to the market](https://substack.com/redirect/86765198-2665-4d08-b615-433dfe15b021?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) at Dreamforce, saying no new laws are needed and safe-or-fast is a false choice. And [Zuckerberg went on X](https://substack.com/redirect/c764c68e-5fc7-49cc-9421-de620e19b0e0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) to say each lab should simply do the work unasked, as Meta did in delaying Muse for months. Underneath it all, a fifth move that is not about grading at all: [the Financial Times reported OpenAI in early talks](https://substack.com/redirect/ed22d49c-86da-4954-aa7a-7498e65c243a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on a private round near $1.2 trillion, against $852 billion in March. Meta, meanwhile, [put a price on its apps and its AI](https://substack.com/redirect/418c5405-f76d-4449-9854-e9fbe9cd3c4e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). My take, from [ARD #164](https://substack.com/redirect/610e56e9-d3af-4db6-ab26-0eb17fbe5676?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): FOMO beat Fear, again. And the durable thing is that all four proposals accept outside evaluation in some form. They differ only on whether anyone should be made to do it. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/610e56e9-d3af-4db6-ab26-0eb17fbe5676?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

4. China responds and carries on: Beijing answered twice. The Foreign Ministry [publicly rejected fearmongering, confrontation and vicious competition](https://substack.com/redirect/f79fffb8-deae-4657-ac16-ead10e2adf09?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Separately, and more revealingly, [the head of the Ministry of State Security published his most detailed AI statement yet](https://substack.com/redirect/b470c28e-eacf-4e4f-aab1-06e105ef69ed?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in the Party’s own cyberspace magazine, and the threat he describes is internal: AI against the Party’s hold on power, deepfakes of officials, with Claude and OpenAI’s models named as espionage and infrastructure-attack tools. His answer is more Party control. Then the Chinese tech/AI companies simply carried on. Huawei’s chairman said in an official transcript that the goal is [to become Nvidia](https://substack.com/redirect/ed94e983-a53c-4668-a2df-c4997f270831?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and the company [pulled its Ascend 960DT forward three quarters](https://substack.com/redirect/436f0a43-a626-4b1d-b9e9-2c99febcd372?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) to the first quarter of 2027 at twice the performance of its predecessor; Morgan Stanley puts China’s AI chip market near $67 billion by 2030 with over 80 percent of it domestic. In a different universe that number was flipped. [ByteDance’s revenue rose about 30 percent to $120 billion over six months](https://substack.com/redirect/f77a8944-924a-4e02-963d-9134a43f356d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), roughly matching Meta’s $117 billion at a private mark near $630 billion against Meta’s $1.7 trillion. And [Manus, which Beijing took back from Meta, is raising half a billion at $4 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/73f07e28-e4c3-4a05-8bfc-d88eb9e6ea8f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). My take, from [ARD #165](https://substack.com/redirect/e8d50cb2-f922-4358-9dfc-bf5ad99eab07?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): China is not a fast follower anymore. They are not copying your IP; if anything we should be inspired by theirs. Continue down the us-versus-them road and we balkanize AI globally, and we are all poorer for it. Watch the Trump-Xi meeting at the White House on the 24th. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/9e106b89-9382-4256-916a-0c70418900b7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [here](https://substack.com/redirect/e8d50cb2-f922-4358-9dfc-bf5ad99eab07?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

5. Anthropic IPO Prep Continues: Two days after asking the industry to pace itself, Anthropic showed what it is doing with its own feet. The Financial Times reported in [‘Anthropic tells investors it will be profitable for second straight quarter’](https://substack.com/redirect/0f62fc6b-37d9-4c5c-b632-f91952ae2688?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) that the prospectus expected that week went instead to a small group of shareholders, with adjusted operating income positive for a second consecutive quarter and gross margins above 80 percent before revenue shared with partners and the cost of training. It [chose Nasdaq](https://substack.com/redirect/c0b698e8-8d07-405d-8f03-a3608053e1c5?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) for a listing that could value it at $2 trillion or more. And it [struck a $13.7 billion, six-year compute deal with a Trump-linked neocloud](https://substack.com/redirect/6a041e19-927a-4913-baf8-a9ef0c1c265b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) that does not yet have the money to build the data center. The numbers underneath, which [Bloomberg surfaced in August](https://substack.com/redirect/800e8cec-cf93-4b3f-b906-89cf55fda6e8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): second-quarter revenue of $11.5 billion, up fourteen-fold in a year, an annualized run rate of $65 billion at the end of July against $9 billion at the end of last year, and a last private mark of $965 billion. A SemiAnalysis analyst told the FT investors now model $120 billion of annualized revenue by December and almost triple that by the end of 2027, both well above the guesstimates circulating a week earlier, and [Reuters had the 2028 forecast underpinning the valuation](https://substack.com/redirect/da10055f-c611-4ed6-a804-8c6b0236d20d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). My take, from [AI-RTZ #1210](https://substack.com/redirect/d819a28c-b60f-4e33-9035-9d6419f29949?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): Anthropic is playing the pace-pausing game and the pace-accelerating game at the same time, for its own IPO, ahead of arch-rival OpenAI. Both games at once, pedal to the metal. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/d819a28c-b60f-4e33-9035-9d6419f29949?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).
