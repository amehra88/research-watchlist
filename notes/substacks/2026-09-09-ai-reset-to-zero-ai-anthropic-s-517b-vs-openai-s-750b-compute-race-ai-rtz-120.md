# [AI: Anthropic’s $517B vs OpenAI’s $750B Compute Race. AI-RTZ #1204](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=684161&post_id=214775120&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxNDc3NTEyMCwiaWF0IjoxNzg4OTMwMzM3LCJleHAiOjE3OTE1MjIzMzcsImlzcyI6InB1Yi02ODQxNjEiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.ucD0s9tWvafOWI6ei5r94T1tHFrLtWSA0lfQqDKxh9E)

### ...the two compute ledgers ahead of both mega-AI IPOs, in dollars and in gigawatts. Nvidia sits on both sides.

AI Data Center builds are [digging in](https://substack.com/redirect/2ecea600-3904-4c82-a8a1-f18ea0fca364?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) across the country, led especially by two frontier lab companies OpenAI and Anthropic. They of course include gobs of AI Compute mostly from Nvidia, and gigawatts of Power, and more than a few drops of water.

Anthropic and OpenAI are both accelerating their financial and technical build plans as fast as possible. And the numbers are adding up to at least over a trillion and beyond.

Both before their highly anticipated mega-AI IPOs this year and next. And we have an update thus far in this [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/38ab07ee-bafb-47e5-b307-6e1c30ce846a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) on their cumulative numbers.

The Information put a number on Anthropic’s compute bill on Sunday, in 
“[How Anthropic Clinched $517 Billion in Compute Deals in 11 Months](https://substack.com/redirect/1001632a-c64a-4006-ab2b-a59972201774?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)”. Over eleven months, the company has signed agreements worth up to $517 billion.

> “Since October, Anthropic has entered into agreements for at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute capacity it could tap over the next several years. ([How Anthropic Clinched $517 Billion in Compute Deals in 11 Months](https://substack.com/redirect/1001632a-c64a-4006-ab2b-a59972201774?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), The Information, September 6, 2026)”

That is not a figure Anthropic published. It is a tally of announced deals, added up by reporters from public statements and prior reporting.

Nine months ago Anthropic told investors it would spend $180 billion renting servers through 2029. The new tally is close to three times that, over a longer window.

OpenAI’s number moved too. It now forecasts roughly $750 billion on compute through 2030, up from $665 billion earlier this year.

Two labs, two ledgers, both walking into mega-AI IPOs. This piece lines the ledgers up in dollars and in gigawatts, names the partners on each side, and marks where Nvidia sits.

The above is on the cost/investment side. On the revenue side, as a reminder, here is where things stand between the two.

Anthropic revenues generally grew from almost nothing from Claude Code/Cowork to likely over $100 billion this year, and over $200 billion next. Current rates of over $65 billion plus for Anthropic and over $40 billion plus for OpenAI, on different revenue recognition methodologies, [discussed here](https://substack.com/redirect/fdbb7e49-ad62-4578-bc02-1d809abf486d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Why all this matters for the [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/38ab07ee-bafb-47e5-b307-6e1c30ce846a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA): compute contracts are the closest thing we have to a public map of what each lab thinks it will need. To really meet the AI demand they’re anticipating, in a globally constrained environment for AI Infrastructure, Compute, Power and everything else. Likely for at least through the end of this decade.

As a result, they are placed years before the revenue that has to pay for them.

## The two ledgers, side by side

Three numbers matter here. They do not point the same way.

On dollars committed, OpenAI leads. Roughly $750 billion against $517 billion. But these are very fluid metrics.

On capacity, OpenAI leads by more. It told investors in April it plans to have 30 gigawatts by 2030. Anthropic has signed for at least 14.8 gigawatts since October, on top of the 1 to 2 gigawatts it already held.

On revenue as highlighted above, Anthropic leads. Annualized revenue has passed $65 billion. OpenAI reported more than $40 billion as of July.

So the smaller compute book sits under the larger revenue line. That inversion is the whole story of this race right now.

Hold onto the gigawatt as your unit. One gigawatt is enough to run a city the size of San Francisco.

> “Two years ago it was going up from 100,000 GPU data centers; then gigawatt-scale facilities became the new AI table stakes; then the power plays began to build gigawatt data centers in earnest. ([AI-RTZ #1192, Data Centers 101](https://substack.com/redirect/eb5808da-e5e7-4ce1-b428-94a973acf7e9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

Remember it’s generally $50 billion plus for a gigawatt of AI data center compute, built typically over a couple of years, mostly more.

Thirty gigawatts is thirty of those cities. That is the scale being contracted for, by two companies, in one industry. Globally. That’s how we get to numbers that surpass the GDPs of most countries.

I’d highlight the current state of play and points below.

## Anthropic’s $517 billion, deal by deal

1. The original backers still carry most of the bill. Amazon and Google account for 11 gigawatts and more than $300 billion over roughly ten years. Every other deal on the list is a fraction of that one line.

2. The rival’s cloud is now a supplier. In November, Anthropic committed at least $30 billion to rent about a gigawatt of Microsoft Azure servers running Nvidia chips. Microsoft is OpenAI’s oldest and largest backer.

3. Elon rents Anthropic the spare room. In May, Anthropic agreed to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion a month through May 2029. Up to $45 billion in total. Either side can cancel on ninety days’ notice.

I wrote about that one the day it landed, and about why it happened:

> “In the meantime, Anthropic is scrambling for AI Compute wherever it can find it. Yesterday, one source was rival SpaceX/xAI, yes the one led by Elon Musk and his xAI enterprise. It came in true Elon style. A deal of sheer convenience for both sides. ([AI-RTZ #1080, May 2026](https://substack.com/redirect/34eba682-4cbf-4dcf-9670-a2a99ee6a886?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

4. The neoclouds got an $80 billion order. Last month Anthropic signed cloud contracts with Lambda and the UK’s Nscale for at least 460 megawatts over six years. These are the GPU rental shops, buying Nvidia parts and renting them out by the hour.

The [neocloud tier](https://substack.com/redirect/dac6c788-53d0-4461-b6a7-ff679dd03ed1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) was a curiosity two years ago. It is now a line item large enough to move a frontier lab’s cost structure.

5. Anthropic is becoming its own landlord. Fifty billion dollars with Fluidstack to build its own data centers in Texas and New York. A 401 megawatt lease with former bitcoin miner TeraWulf in Kentucky. A twenty-year direct tenant lease with former crypto miner Riot in Texas.

That last one is worth pausing on. Twenty years is not a cloud contract. That is a utility’s time horizon, signed by a company that is eleven years old.

> “Some of the announced capacity may not come online in the planned time frame or indeed ever. ([How Anthropic Clinched $517 Billion in Compute Deals in 11 Months](https://substack.com/redirect/1001632a-c64a-4006-ab2b-a59972201774?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), The Information, September 6, 2026)”

## OpenAI’s number is bigger, and it keeps moving

OpenAI laid out its own arithmetic for investors, reported in “[OpenAI Boosts Revenue Forecasts, Predicts $111 Billion More Cash Burn Through 2030](https://substack.com/redirect/e09ba4e7-247b-45e0-a8ea-d70bce56dbcd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)”. Its compute forecast went from $665 billion to about $750 billion inside a single year. An $85 billion revision, on a number that was already the largest in the industry.

Underneath it, the cost lines are the ones to watch. OpenAI told investors that the cost of running its models quadrupled in 2025. Adjusted gross margin fell to 33% from 40%, against its own 46% target.

It expects to burn $25 billion in cash this year and $57 billion next year. It expects to turn cash flow positive in 2030.

Anthropic’s pitch to investors puts that crossover two years earlier.

> “Anthropic’s 2028 projection is management asking investors to underwrite exactly that expanding pie, and the margin story, training and inference getting cheaper as a share of revenue, is the same curve from the supply side. ([AI-RTZ #1181, Anthropic’s mega-IPO math](https://substack.com/redirect/a88081df-ca8a-4c8e-a052-f8df307b635c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

Two years of cash burn is the real distance between these two companies. Not the model benchmarks.

> “OpenAI has told investors the costs of running its AI models, a process known as inference, quadrupled in 2025. ([OpenAI Boosts Revenue Forecasts, Predicts $111 Billion More Cash Burn Through 2030](https://substack.com/redirect/e09ba4e7-247b-45e0-a8ea-d70bce56dbcd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), The Information, February 20, 2026)”

## Where Nvidia sits, on both sides of the table

Nvidia is a supplier to both labs. It is also an investor in both. The two positions are not the same size, and the difference is instructive.

On the Anthropic side, in November 2025, Nvidia agreed to invest up to $10 billion and Microsoft up to $5 billion. That came alongside Anthropic’s $30 billion Azure commitment and about a gigawatt of Nvidia systems. Money out, purchase order in, both disclosed together.

On the OpenAI side, in September 2025, Nvidia signed a letter of intent to invest up to $100 billion, tied to at least 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems. Ten times the Anthropic figure, and the largest single number in the entire compute race.

Then in February, in Taipei, Jensen Huang put that number in perspective.

> “They invited us to invest up to $100 billion... but we will invest one step at a time. ([Pledge to invest $100 billion in OpenAI was ‘never a commitment,’ says Nvidia’s Huang](https://substack.com/redirect/c995e5f6-da3e-4b78-a148-35eb2cb3b369?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), Fortune, February 2, 2026)”

He called the pledge “never a commitment,” and said the current round would not approach $100 billion.

That is the sentence to keep in mind every time one of these headline totals appears. Nvidia is also named among the investors in OpenAI’s current round, at a $730 billion pre-money valuation.

Longtime readers know where I land on the circularity argument:

> “Circular financing, in other words, becomes a pathway to recognized revenues. For OpenAI on its way to the mega-AI IPO, and for Nvidia across that trillion-dollar pipeline. Bookings become buildings become billings. ([AI-RTZ #1171, Nvidia the Kingmaker](https://substack.com/redirect/81d30e83-5887-4f51-b601-1c99055c24d6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

The point is not that the deals are fake. The point is that a ceiling and a contract are different instruments, and the press releases rarely distinguish them.

It’s a sensational term for views and clicks, with their negative connotations especially. ‘Vendor financing’, practiced in most industries, is the more prosaic term. And not as attractive for views and clicks.

## The revenue race is run on two different rulers

Again, both labs are competing on revenue as hard as they are competing on compute. They do not count it the same way.

> “The headline numbers: Anthropic’s latest update points to annualized revenue exceeding $65 billion this year. OpenAI says it is on track to exceed $40 billion. Both annualize in roughly the same way, a recent four-week run rate times thirteen. ([ARD #156, Lots More Apples and Oranges](https://substack.com/redirect/fdbb7e49-ad62-4578-bc02-1d809abf486d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

The annualizing method matches. What happens above it does not.

> “Anthropic views itself as the principal. OpenAI treats Microsoft as the principal for the Azure OpenAI service. Neither approach is wrong. Both follow GAAP. They just answer the same question differently. ([ARD #156](https://substack.com/redirect/fdbb7e49-ad62-4578-bc02-1d809abf486d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

So when you set $65 billion next to $40 billion, you are setting a gross number next to a partly net one. The gap is real. It is smaller than it looks.

And both revenue lines sit on top of the same cloud backlogs I have been tracking since the spring, where these two companies are most of the demand.

> “And the numbers are building up substantial backlogs at the big cloud providers Amazon AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud and others. Not to mention billions of additional backlog for Oracle, CoreWeave and a whole host of other companies worldwide. ([AI-RTZ #1079, May 2026](https://substack.com/redirect/d91745e0-c67a-4848-b788-74ad69da2dc2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA))”

## What to look for in Anthropic’s eventual prospectus

The IPO filing could go public in the coming weeks. Five things would start addressing most of the bigger open questions.

- How much of the $517 billion is take-or-pay. The SpaceX deal has a ninety-day exit. If a large share of the book is cancellable, the total is a plan, not an obligation.

- Utilization. Signing for 14.8 gigawatts is not the same as running 14.8 gigawatts. The gap between contracted and energized capacity is where the margin story lives.

- The duration mismatch. A twenty-year Riot lease and a three-year cloud contract do very different things to depreciation and to lease liabilities on the balance sheet.

- The bridge from $180 billion to $517 billion. Management explaining that jump is worth more than reporters reconstructing it.

- The principal question, stated plainly. How much of the $65 billion is booked gross, and on which lines.

## My take

The frontier AI race has stopped being about who ships the better model this quarter. It is about who holds the power contracts, and who can pay for them. Up and down the [AI Tech Stack](https://substack.com/redirect/38ab07ee-bafb-47e5-b307-6e1c30ce846a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

Anthropic is now buying compute from nearly every rival it has. Microsoft, Google, Amazon, SpaceX. That is not a strategy. That is what scarcity looks like from the inside. With accelerating demand around the corner, measured in years, not quarters.

The inversion is the interesting part. Anthropic has for now, the smaller compute book and the larger revenue line. On the numbers as reported, that is the more defensible position of the two.

It is only defensible if the capacity actually arrives. The failure mode is the one I flagged in May, when compute constraints were throttling growth. If OpenAI’s 30 gigawatts lands and Anthropic’s does not, the revenue lead goes away, because customers who get throttled go elsewhere.

For everyone reading this who does not spend their days in data center contracts: none of it means an AI bust is scheduled. With rapidly changing numbers, mostly in the upward direction for the foreseeable future.

These are two pre-IPO companies buying options on a scarce input, in the only way that input can be bought, which is years in advance. Options expire. Contracts get renegotiated. The headline totals will not be the spent totals, and nobody involved expects them to be.

What we will learn in the next few weeks is which parts of these two ledgers survive contact with an audited filing. That is the real update.

So the compute race now runs on two ledgers that do not measure the same things, and one of them is about to be filed and audited.

That is the part of this [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/38ab07ee-bafb-47e5-b307-6e1c30ce846a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) worth tracking week by week, because the contracts get signed years before the revenue that has to pay for them.

Welcome to the Revolution. Stay tuned.
