#### Listen to the Essay 🎧: 25 minutes, 37.

I’ll be honest I’m not the most persuasive storyteller, I’m just a blogger and a relic of an internet that no longer exists. A human led internet that may never exist again, the way things are going. But as I get a bit older and after writing and curating hundreds of articles about AI, I’m noticing a shift in the world with regards to Generative AI specifically. You[can read reports](https://substack.com/redirect/0b8a9c00-8ad1-4cc0-8824-78778197a5fe?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), but the truth is somewhere in the silent consensus beneath the hype. The costs of the over-investment in this AI cycle are beginning to gnaw at the social fabric, rule of law and the social order. The way things were has not caught up to the new reality. The internet is demoralized under the weight of all of the AI slop. But there’s nowhere to go, this labor market is punishing. And you have to begin to wonder if it’s all by careful design. There’s a financial fragility to the madness and a cost to real people. It’s disappointing to witness. AI is now synonymous with greed and a deterioration of the social contract we used to take for granted and believed would always exist. Let me try and explain in this essay: put plainly, AI hardware should not be put ahead of the well-being of people.

We are nearly four years since ChatGPT first went live. If you would have told me that AI would create a more fractured internet, with a [more hollow and disappointing experience](https://substack.com/redirect/ab40da73-7473-45f8-b66b-6dfaaaa85315?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), hurting human culture, literacy (we read less), a force for white collar deskilling, and detrimental to human well-being with questionable economic benefits, I probably wouldn’t have believed you. But there’s a weight in pretending and a nagging feeling that this isn't it. And people are upset, not only has AI not reached its promised potential - [it’s increasing inflation](https://substack.com/redirect/5652745b-5a66-49ce-b2ca-f7eb3032bd87?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and comes with a rising cost in economic, psychological and even geopolitical and sociological terms difficult to quantify. With all the [Billions of dollars in funding OpenAI](https://substack.com/redirect/aa59de20-b8f6-4155-bf59-de796e9e55fd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), Venture Capital and BigTech marketing, lobbying and PR, we have been led astray. It’s beginning to harm our collective future. Generative AI appears fraudulent and its impact appears to be harmful at scale.

Generative AI as a movement and investment, has been more divisive than unifying or empowering. It’s humiliating and deeply unsettling to live in a world of AI slop and work slop. It’s not just you, we all feel it. It’s not been a good technology. It’s increasing corruption faster than it’s siding with any sense of rule of law, fairness or even demonstrating real growth. The cascade of funding a failed promise could be devastating.

It’s not being adopted or diffusing into society as would a significant general purpose technology (GPT). It’s not building value that’s leading us as a civilization, as a species, to a more meaningful or better future. It’s important to say this aloud. I take no great pride in trying to frame this realistically. Generative AI isn’t what we were promised when ChatGPT suddenly appeared. As models became larger and more sophisticated and spending increased astronomically we were expecting tangible benefits and that the dangers to society would be demonstrably mitigated, carefully regulated and monopolies given some ground rules. But the detrimental impact of the tech are accelerating faster than the tech can show real value. By the late 2020s, it will be out of control. Americans are not good custodians of this degree of financial and artificial power. But they sure know how to build a technological ponzi scheme to inflate the corporate monopolies of their increasingly dystopian internet. Where’s the “opt-out” button? Where’s the reset switch? Generative AI is leading us astray.

#### Degrading Culture and Revamping Economic Opportunity

Generative AI however isn’t just harming human culture, demeaning our time or damaging our sense of purpose - it’s benefitting the privileged with key elements of stock manipulation, over the common person in society, degrading the middle class and it’s hurting our digital civilization and online experience with AI slop and greater untold debt (at a time when the U.S. National debt is mounting) and wealth inequality is being accelerated at a scale difficult to reconcile with the supposed benefits. If you hurt the wealth, opportunities, labor and career aspects of the general population, you better be delivering. And, Generative AI is not delivering value at scale or helping most citizens, consumers or businesses.

Even the corporate insistence and vague promises of higher productivity or a sustainable impact on GDP is highly questionable now in comparison to the cost of compute and the expense of building these huge larger language models, the hardware, Fabs and most of all, the circular financing. The size and scope of the super clusters of AI datacenters is getting alarming. How the technology benefits the most powerful countries while hurting the rest of us is alarming. The abuse of power and lack of ROIC has become more serious. After four years we need to take stock and look at the movement objectively and scrutinize the players and the technology involved. The companies at the forefront need to be held accountable. The countries manipulating our future need our collective feedback.

On the global stage, generative AI is significantly increasing the hegemony of the U.S. and China over other nations that is extremely toxic for the global marketplace, world order, rules of the marketplace and detrimental for middle-sized, smaller countries (and their economies) not to mention detrimental for the middle class and potentially many white collar workers. It’s clearly an economically exploitive technology that is a “divide and conquer” mechanism being used by the world’s financial elite and most powerful Nation States for their own agendas and self-interests. Generative AI has dystopian characteristics on more levels and layers than we can possibly name in an article. To say that it’s not good, is one of the great understatements of our times. And this stark realization will become more than just about protesting datacenters in the coming years and decades. This could lead to social upheaval, the crumbling of empires, the storm before a dark age. This isn’t like the industrial revolution, this isn’t a kind of automation that’s making us better. The energy, datacenter infrastructure and fabs required are not worth it. The price is too high. The bottlenecks not just economic. The consequences won’t just be that of corporate consolidation.

### The Missing Links

After four years of watching the evolution of AI developments, I have to conclude that not only is a return on investment (ROI) absent, the cost to people, communities and trust in capitalism and democracy is highly concerning and a rising sameness of the narrative vs. the obvious exploitation in the real world is rampant and deceptive. Why, why is this not being talked about in mainstream circles? Instead, we are constantly bombarded about how great AI is and how much funding it requires and how it’s a matter of national importance and national defense competition with national competitors while Venture Capitalist, Tech executives, AI researchers and political leaders and the financial elites of our systems pad their pockets. An internet and on platforms where even VC firms pretending they are an authoritative media companies. That’s not the internet I signed up for in the late 1990s. This isn’t going to be the revolution that these companies had in mind. It’s doubtful now that Generative AI is the next leap in artificial intelligence that the makers of ChatGPT and their peers said it was. It’s technological fraud at a grandiose scale.

When the technological on a fundamental levels isn’t benefiting most people, most communities or making the marketplace and opportunities better. AI isn’t improving our quality of life, on the contrary in most cases it’s diminishing it. AI has hurt the and tightened the labor force, creating massive uncertainty, economic strain and a post ChatGPT world is now the worst time to start a career, go to college, or plan a career path, in decades. It’s a generational abyss. I have written in previous articles about how AI is hurting both our youngest and older more established workers at both ends of the spectrum. As a writer [I’m literally judged by how](https://substack.com/redirect/ac7044d5-b948-4440-880c-b78a1d766a28?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and how much I employ AI in my work. But something doesn’t feel right, is off and is deeply unsatisfying and is unscrupulous in this so-called - “AI revolution.” The narrative the mainstream media is telling is obviously not the entire truth.

After covering the AI space and developments for 5 years I can tell you, it isn’t what BigTech or powerful Venture Capital firms or the Government or the elite controlled Media, or all the lobbying, is claiming it to be. The investment in compute, in models and in a technology with detrimental impacts of Generative AI and its outputs is not positive for society or a great help to [how companies are organized](https://substack.com/redirect/95f5986e-0262-4c1d-a34e-f656746d7030?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). It’s not what we were hoping it would become almost four years ago. And I don’t have much hope that it will suddenly become that. The [2017 paper](https://substack.com/redirect/0525fff8-de89-403c-9808-40c37f6256fe?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) introducing the world to Transformers didn’t deliver us a tool that would build a better internet or improve the world. Vibe coding, AI coding or token prediction didn’t justify the cost or bring us any salvation - if anything it’s entrenched us in a system and in an internet reaching a level of joyless decadence, addiction and technological loneliness - that is making people miserable, broke, disconnected from each other, and uncertain about what comes next. Businesses are also confused at the lacklustre results of their investment in token spend.

### Circular Tactics

However it is growing and significant Capex, debt and circular plus vendor financing by the likes of Nvidia, OpenAI and AMD that have really made me lose trust in the ecosystem where capacity is paid for in-advance. The hardware, model training, inference, HBM chip costs are startling to unravel vs. the utility of the technology. It’s not just disappointing, it’s becoming deeply irrational. [Moody’s recently warned us](https://substack.com/redirect/c2c77384-43bf-4a9a-87f1-ecfaeee69b55?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in a note that ‘unprecedented’ AI spending threatens credit quality. What could go wrong?

> “Software costs little to replicate, yielding fat profit margins and fortress balance sheets. Generative AI, by contrast, demands a vast physical footprint: warehouses crammed with expensive and energy-hungry servers and chips.”

OpenAI is in discussions with Nvidia about a backstop of up to $250 billion that would help fund its plans to lease a new [AI data center in Ohio](https://substack.com/redirect/d4b4eaf9-9938-4c5b-a824-a34228b57c8f?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) that seems to be very close to Government intervention and a bailout for OpenAI over Oracle’s weak credit rating and execution risk that endangers the entire ecosystem.

The vagueness, lack of transparency about the details and obvious implications are incredibly concerning whereby according to Bloomberg:

- The guarantees from Nvidia would help OpenAI lease a 10GW project that SoftBank is developing in Ohio.

- In total, the project could cost more than $500 billion, the largest data center project ever announced.
