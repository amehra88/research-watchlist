#### A Starting definition of a World-Model

> “A world model is an internal mental simulation constructed by an AI system that represents the physics, spatial relationships, geometry, and dynamics of the real world.”

As we build the physical infrastructure to power the compute for some much debated and uncertain AI-future, all these robots and IoT systems needs a better understanding of their environments.

Given the limitations of LLMs, it’s not clear how long or how useful these physics based and simulation friendly world models might take to be able to fast-track the training of our suddenly humanoid looking robotics projects.

While China has established itself a formidable open-weight model builder, that western companies are[increasingly building upon](https://substack.com/redirect/13821327-b509-440c-94b1-dc365633f2c3?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) (like Harvey building a model on a Moonshot AI base), the new race to world models is heating up.

As a Canadian it was exciting to hear that Khosla Ventures and Radical Ventures are leading the seed funding of what it turns out to be one of the largest seed financings ever raised by a Canadian startup. It’s called Veeda and is by three former Nvidia employees with ties to the University of Toronto.

### Helping Robots Learn and Train Faster

Nvidia has been democratizing Physical AI in a major way pushing open-source in the field. [It just dropped](https://substack.com/redirect/c499623b-07e7-4ba3-9b45-4e1118c55735?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)Cosmos3-DROID that is 707 GB of real-world robot training data.

[Read Paper](https://substack.com/redirect/eefa5ca6-d70f-41d2-8293-af6847df739a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

World models enable robots and robot fleets to learn in simulated reality. Sanja continued:

> “For Physical AI, the central challenge is making this kind of interactive learning possible at scale. The real world is simply not a practical training ground for robots to learn through trial and error. It is unsafe, expensive, and time-consuming. To scale interactive learning, robots will need to learn in simulated reality.”

Veeda Innovation is really doubling down to build simulated reality for Physical AI. Their conviction is that this will become the critical infrastructure layer for all areas of robotics. In some technological sense, this team are like missionaries of Nvidia’s [mission for physical AI](https://substack.com/redirect/71c1297f-f4ad-46e1-9000-6e1f82c24fd4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

World models essentially enable robots to understand the physical world better and become proficient at tasks in it. Veeva will be creating “infinitely scalable” simulated environments that can be used to train embodied artificial intelligence agents through repeated interactions.

### Build the Matrix and they will Learn our Reality

We’re not there yet but there’s an industry consensus that World Models are the foundational technology that can make this possible. Make humanoids are other kinds of robotics truly take the next step in autonomous work. As training gets better with improved world models, perhaps robots will become more useful in the years to come. The challenge for Veeda is developing world models as a kind of “Matrix” for physical AI.

Toronto is also an interesting location since there are many great Universities in the area. The Toronto-headquartered startup is now staffing up, recruiting scientists and machine learning engineers to help build technology that works across language, image and video, with applications in robotics and other forms of so-called embodied intelligence.

Why so expensive? World models are among the most compute intensive paradigms in Modern AI. That’s why World Model startups need so much money so early in their development like [World Labs](https://substack.com/redirect/0afc3782-1ba1-43b6-ab92-8a1071673e08?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), [AMI Labs](https://substack.com/redirect/32f70d10-b24b-43b3-8de2-62f6f580b8ff?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), Waabi and Decart have shown - Israeli Decart who are indeed [likely being acquired](https://substack.com/redirect/a2c806f9-bf44-4965-acf8-cebd7019a752?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) by Anthropic. So Veeva’s $90 million just gets them on their feet.

Sanja is co-founding the startup with her incredible longtime collaborators [Zan Gojcic](https://substack.com/redirect/50eb5cc9-d8ec-40da-b881-af83d0ee2a46?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [Huan Ling](https://substack.com/redirect/6fff8c0b-b0ac-415f-a314-dadb82517a7a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Fidler's major research interests include 2D and 3D object detection, object segmentation and image labeling, and 3D scene understanding. Clearly joining University of Toronto and then Nvidia has evolved her interests quite naturally. She was at Nvidia for a little over 8 years.

### We are Early on Capable Robots, Caveman Physical AI today

China is also prioritizing this spatial and embodied AI involving world models very highly as they are the global leader in robotics so far. World Models for the West you could say, is a matter of National security. Veeva intends to push the frontier of this technology and build the foundational infrastructure for interactive robotics learning and evaluation at scale. Veeva Innovation immediately becomes one of the most important AI startups in Canada.

Sanja at Nvidia was tasked in May 2018 to head up a new Toronto unit, later named the [Spatial Intelligence Lab](https://substack.com/redirect/1e51649c-e413-41b8-a83e-6aeb94bf5510?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)(SIL). Fast forward nearly ten years and Physical AI is becoming more trendy as more robotics startups IPO, like Unitree. Unitree founder [Wang Xingxing recently famously said](https://substack.com/redirect/72729696-fefd-447d-bf24-51be1dc5a425?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)it could take up to 10 years for humanoid robots to have their “ChatGPT” moment type breakthrough.

We know what once robots and AI systems are capable of self-improving themselves the world could change very quickly but without highly scalable world models we are nowhere near that “singularity”. It will take so much compute, highly speculative orbital compute startups are [getting some serious funding](https://substack.com/redirect/1ebeba0a-10a2-4c03-8faf-9021441d7211?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

### World Models are Extremely Compute Intensive

To give you an idea, [world models are considered](https://substack.com/redirect/4566ce78-da47-4f96-84d9-80c803e3f9bb?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in the order of 10x to 100x more compute intensive than regular LLMs. At the IPO of Unitree the stock’s value raised over 600% and finally stabilized at in the over 400% higher range. Meanwhile at the conference its Founder said robots still struggle to generalize across unfamiliar tasks and execute precise movements. That a “ChatGPT” moment for Physical AI is [nowhere on the horizon](https://substack.com/redirect/91d9b97a-c649-4959-a37e-ce78456f3d55?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)quite yet.

One thing is sure, we are going to need a lot more World Model startups in the years ahead to help train all of these robots that we will be building. Veeda Innovation was founded technically in early June, 2026. It did not take them long to get a serious Seed series funding round in place.

> “Robots should not go to school in the real world. It's expensive, time consuming and, ultimately, ineffective.” - Radical Ventures

Sanja, Huan and Zan will be building deeply reactive simulation environments at Veeda where autonomous systems can learn, reason, and adapt before deployment. It’s one of the most exciting Canadian AI startups I’ve ever covered.

Veeda AI has raised $90 million in seed funding where Radical partner [Tomi Poutanen](https://substack.com/redirect/d8d5c9a8-83d7-469a-81c3-afdaec21b6d7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)and Khosla partner[Sven Strohband](https://substack.com/redirect/57c9d33a-9eb8-4940-8b7f-c7b482495862?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) joined the company’s board of directors. While the HQ is in Canada this is a high calibre global startup. Veeva said it will use the capital to fund high-caliber research talent acquisition across its hubs in Toronto, Mountain View, Singapore, and Zurich, where it will procure dense high-performance graphics processor compute clusters, and scale its data pipeline operations to train its foundational systems.

The age of world models has officially begun in the mid 2020s. Since agentic AI, world models and physical AI are so compute intensive the demand for compute is going to be surging for years to come I suspect.
