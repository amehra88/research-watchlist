### ...AI weekly roundup for the week ending Friday August 7, 2026

- Big Changes at Google AI: Google saw big changes in [senior AI personnel](https://substack.com/redirect/b213ec85-b478-4786-a82e-3eb8d1db0946?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [geographical](https://substack.com/redirect/a1848ec2-66f6-4711-b836-f970019ccef2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) shifts this week. Demis Hassabis stepped up to chairman and Alphabet chief scientist, with [Koray Kavukcuoglu taking over all AI model development](https://substack.com/redirect/2ffd9a0b-81fd-4a7f-b0fd-b1d3fe2d5e92?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) from Mountain View, reporting to CEO Sundar Pichai. And [four of Google’s most important builders](https://substack.com/redirect/9ecf86fe-bc38-4358-8ce8-dfa1982c65b1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), led by Jeff Dean, are striking out to found Discovery Loop, a recursive self-improvement startup with Google itself investing. The AI center of gravity crosses from London to Silicon Valley, a real loss for the UK and Europe. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/96fcfc91-c01e-4af0-a5d3-0f064d7d2cb8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [here](https://substack.com/redirect/d0efcc6d-96e3-437e-b4e7-c436a42548b6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - Nvidia now ‘Uber Kingmaker’ in AI: Nvidia is shaping up to be a bigger AI ‘Kingmaker’ than ever, with [over $750 billion in resources](https://substack.com/redirect/49c8ef02-127c-4f10-9ec0-b2099dd0c010?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and growing, including talks to [backstop up to $250 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/13a73787-3455-4887-b4d5-9b96545b7619?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) so OpenAI can lease its way to more compute. Circular financing concerns aside, these deals are a pathway to recognized revenues across Nvidia’s trillion-plus dollar backlog. The Kingmaker is now also something like a lender of last resort. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/7668b829-9f4c-476d-b08c-1c54f40f720e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - Global AI Pricing NOT ‘going to Zero’: New [reports on AI pricing](https://substack.com/redirect/81491ee6-44e8-4802-a410-02cf30f75bc2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) ‘going to zero’ are overdone. The pricing spectrum is widening instead, down at the commodity end and up at the frontier end. Exhibit A this week: [DeepSeek told developers](https://substack.com/redirect/4c2ef23e-4fd6-4654-b76b-6af784dfd80d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) it plans significant price increases, while [resuming an $8 billion round](https://substack.com/redirect/07971560-71db-4977-9210-f18bbb653cd8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) at a roughly $70 billion valuation. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/8869040c-5a51-48f2-ac47-8f97bb3ebe92?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [here](https://substack.com/redirect/d0efcc6d-96e3-437e-b4e7-c436a42548b6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).[](https://substack.com/redirect/c2959b0a-dfe1-43e0-8bfa-6a50c155c96d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)
