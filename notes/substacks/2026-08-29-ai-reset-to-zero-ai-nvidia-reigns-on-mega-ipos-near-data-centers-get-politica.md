### ...round up for week ending Friday, August 28, 2026

- Nvidia Q2 2027 ‘Reigns On’: [Nvidia](https://substack.com/redirect/2f67c6c0-376e-40ee-94eb-055a06b67b4e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) had a [blow out Q2](https://substack.com/redirect/525ea782-194d-4e83-8ac5-39a0f948d4e0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) again, and the market recognized it as such. The numbers: $96.2 billion in revenue, up 106%, with [data center alone at $89 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/d07c5b98-29cd-43ea-877c-fd0732f887a2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), up 117%. Guidance went UP to $108 billion, with zero China revenue assumed. Margins held at 75% despite the memory-price crunch. The stock rose, snapping seven straight down days of AI Trade worries. Management took the gloom checklist head on, circular financing included: CFO Colette Kress said some will call it circular, ‘We see it differently.’ The counterweight remains what the WSJ calls the [$1.5 trillion question](https://substack.com/redirect/05194cf1-9e45-4b84-8a46-6a0c4e4e743e?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), the still-widening gap between AI investment and AI revenue. And hours after the print came the chess move: Nvidia agreed to [buy Hugging Face](https://substack.com/redirect/776c6713-256f-4301-a880-fc278dbaf338?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), the home shelf of the world’s open AI models, for $12.9 billion. My take: this is a meet-and-beat reign that has now run quarters upon quarters, from the industry’s [‘Kingmaker’, now its uber investor and banker](https://substack.com/redirect/dbbb079a-8f95-458b-95ff-ba4c3e1b65fd?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The Hugging Face buy keeps Nvidia captain of the open-source ‘Avengers’ alliance. The prints keep outrunning the worry wall, [quarter after quarter](https://substack.com/redirect/fbc3e798-2ccd-47ce-820f-220662f1f2f2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/bef1fd8d-fd29-43e7-bd8c-1c8ed07106b4?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - Anthropic closer to mega-AI IPO filing: Trial balloons are going up for a [$2+ trillion valuation](https://substack.com/redirect/caaa2f01-d686-4223-8802-b314696f3409?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) for Anthropic’s mega-AI IPO. The fundamentals behind the balloons: [revenue surging more than 14-fold](https://substack.com/redirect/7e542fef-27d8-4cd7-9566-c6c6dfb82d4d?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) year over year to a $65+ billion run rate, led by its class-leading AI Coding and enterprise products, with the company [shoring up investor confidence](https://substack.com/redirect/68a44688-8caf-483e-9429-1f18d4b592bb?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [warming up institutions](https://substack.com/redirect/650535c2-0394-4323-b57d-62f1b62a98ea?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) ahead of an expected fall debut. The talk track has stretched to a [$30 trillion TAM](https://substack.com/redirect/d564dd32-3590-422b-bfb3-56b9f1742172?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), ambitions beyond their wildest dreams. And this week brought an unexpected assist: a federal judge [blocked the Pentagon’s blacklisting of Anthropic](https://substack.com/redirect/873b5644-deee-4f71-a98e-174e1c369bc7?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) as ‘illegal and baseless’, lifting a designation the company said could cost it billions, weeks before the float. My take: the fall calendar is shaping up as the [largest IPO story in market history](https://substack.com/redirect/db33235a-3f50-4d02-9ab8-4dcc3d4d6ab0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), and this week even the courts cleared a lane. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/5d546efd-f66b-4aff-bc7a-bb51d7807567?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - OpenAI not far behind: OpenAI is [consolidating its senior management](https://substack.com/redirect/ea91d432-d3fe-457d-862e-07806319cd67?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) efforts behind co-founder Greg Brockman ahead of its mega-AI IPO. The consolidation caps a year of revenue-engine rebuilding: a [new chief revenue officer](https://substack.com/redirect/5e7eb80e-d7d4-4ca1-b8cc-8df5b17ef368?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in under a year, aggressive AI Coding price and product moves to close the gap with Anthropic, and CFO Sarah Friar telling investors that [enterprise is now a bigger business than consumer](https://substack.com/redirect/28fbea52-c027-4960-b5f1-1b8003faeac1?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) for the company best known for ChatGPT’s billion-plus users. The [run rate is past $40 billion](https://substack.com/redirect/ca72caaa-a6ad-498b-bdf1-fa4ebfcf6591?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), roughly doubled since year-end, with [$500 billion of Ohio data center fine print](https://substack.com/redirect/dcf76a34-9a71-45f8-b2af-7142c618cd82?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) underneath. My take: two mega-AI IPOs [pre-marketing in parallel](https://substack.com/redirect/ed8a250d-0a43-4846-b625-1c7a9adba58a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) is itself the story. The public markets are about to get their first direct read on frontier AI economics, and Brockman at the operating center is the stabilizer OpenAI wants on the roadshow. More [here](https://substack.com/redirect/87f1470a-75d3-4ec7-9274-8ac82a6e26d8?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - AI Data Centers get political: [AI Data Centers](https://substack.com/redirect/295e542c-03b7-465e-a11f-60bb268f3932?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) with their burgeoning resource demands, are becoming a [political headwind issue](https://substack.com/redirect/88c82c06-4370-4434-96a0-b3a770166c09?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) ahead of the midterms for both parties. Politicians who once championed them are now bashing them, over power bills, water and grid strain. And this week added a second front: Politico reports the White House is weighing [sweeping semiconductor tariffs](https://substack.com/redirect/19dbb333-e9e6-4d4d-9d28-0e94b2795d1c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) that could reach past chips to the very servers that fill these data centers, a tax on the buildout’s own inputs. My take: we wrote the two primers this week for exactly this moment, the political football and the megawatts-to-gigawatts basics. The [four-part supply-and-demand series](https://substack.com/redirect/60b119f8-b274-449f-a8ba-48af222c56ca?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) said it plainly: compute is in short supply for the next five years on this [AI Tech Wave](https://substack.com/redirect/091f07f9-1e8c-4251-b734-5f6ca7a3e1c6?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). Political and policy speed bumps only stretch that math, [unforced errors in a race the US says it is running](https://substack.com/redirect/09364162-2726-4963-a380-2469d7dac760?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). - Frontier Labs deploy FDEs: Both Anthropic and OpenAI are expanding their efforts to deploy [forward deployed engineers (FDEs)](https://substack.com/redirect/65451af6-a69c-4e80-afd7-320d32864c70?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) into enterprises of all shapes and sizes. Private equity is running the same play, embedding armies of AI wonks in the firms they back. The logic is straightforward: the models are capable, but turning capability into deployed, paying work inside real enterprises takes humans in the loop, on site. It is the services layer of the AI buildout, arriving just as [long-running AI agents with metered pricing](https://substack.com/redirect/a1ce20ff-4208-4d3c-bf9f-70c0fb1d3328?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) go mainstream. My take: this is how the frontier labs convert model leadership into the enterprise revenue their mega-IPO valuations now depend on, the same enterprise mix Friar highlighted above. And it fits the frame I keep coming back to: [not one ‘Skynet’, but billions of AI agents](https://substack.com/redirect/1b0cc607-d0a4-414b-9947-f9ef1dd38f6b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) from many makers, deployed into the world one enterprise at a time, by people.
