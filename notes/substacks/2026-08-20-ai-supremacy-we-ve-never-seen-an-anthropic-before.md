# [We’ve never seen an Anthropic before](https://substack.com/app-link/post?publication_id=396235&post_id=211849192&utm_source=post-email-title&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=8ne7oq&token=eyJ1c2VyX2lkIjo1MjMwMjM3MjIsInBvc3RfaWQiOjIxMTg0OTE5MiwiaWF0IjoxNzg3NTI2NzAyLCJleHAiOjE3OTAxMTg3MDIsImlzcyI6InB1Yi0zOTYyMzUiLCJzdWIiOiJwb3N0LXJlYWN0aW9uIn0.r_0ID0GPyQQUz_Frr2GeaNI37qh1AJsS-JQtyqza62w)

### Anthropic’s IPO is going to leave OpenAI in the past.

As Anthropic’s IPO approaches likely in October, likely just 8 to 12 weeks from now - it’s beginning to attract a different kind of attention. SpaceX, Anthropic and OpenAI are likely to be the biggest trio of IPOs the U.S. public markets have ever seen. While OpenAI’s revenue growth continues to slow, Anthropic’s ARR continues to accelerate even at a very high scale.

- Anthropic told investors over the weekend that its annualized revenue run rate hit $65 billion at the end of July, 2025.

- While OpenAI said its ARR has hit $40 billion.

Since Anthropic includes marketplace partner commissions (gross basis) in its reported ARR, whereas OpenAI reports metrics net of Microsoft’s cut, Anthropic’s relative ARR might be slightly overstated compared to OpenAI. But even while ChatGPT rushes to Ad markets, [OpenAI’s slowdown](https://substack.com/redirect/cd055056-174d-4855-a773-e0e63241c47b?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) is sort of a big deal pre-IPO. This at a time when routing and code wrappers favor Chinese open-weight models over OpenAI model usage due to cheaper token costs. OpenAI reduced API prices for the GPT-5.6 family on July 30, 2026, even going so far as to cut the cost of GPT-5.6 Lune by 80%. What an act of desperation. The revenue tells the real story.

### Open-Source is disrupting OpenAI’s Revenue Growth

So ironically OpenAI that makes around half of its revenue from B2B vs. B2C, risks being disrupted by Open-source models out of China, even as Anthropic doesn’t feel the same bite. Anthropic has a premium advantage as the global leading frontier lab for coding LLMs.

While Anthropic even had its [best Mythos model blocked](https://substack.com/redirect/c249eab4-8c9e-441f-b92e-15ab05ebaadb?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), its revenue doesn’t seem to have been impacted yet: and, like I had predicted months ago, Anthropic has blown by OpenAI and might have 2x the revenue OpenAI does by the time OpenAI finally goes IPO. It’s hard to believe, and it’s one for the history books.

If SpaceX’s IPO valuation is anything to go by, it (Anthropic) should be targeting [a $2 Trillion valuation](https://substack.com/redirect/ddc7ce1f-36c3-49e4-989f-3f53c5a21978?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). I believe Anthropic could have close to a valuation of $3 Trillion by the end of 2026, because we’ve never seen growth like it has shown.

#### Historic Anthropic Revenue Growth in 2026

Anthropic's Enterprise AI strategy experienced an inflection point in 2026.

On a quarterly basis, Anthropic already nearly has double OpenAI’s revenue in Q2, and OpenAI has been accelerating fast the last few years. We’ve essentially never seen a company or an AI startup like Anthropic, in history. We know that OpenAI has higher compute costs, has worse financials and executes much more poorly on AI product. As the market becomes more competitive, it will continue to be squeezed. We simply won’t consider it a frontier lab for very much longer.

> Anthropic's revenue for the second quarter surged to $11.5 billion, up from $787 million a year ago. It's been growing at an incredibly fast pace.

It doesn’t matter that ChatGPT has 800 million “weekly” active users, ever since its marketshare[dropped below 50%](https://substack.com/redirect/1806e521-1bff-435a-a011-0af56d4f67fe?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and it’s market share continues to rapidly decline. Anthropic’s success as the dominant Generative AI API, is accelerating the entire Cloud computing industry by itself.

### So how much has Anthropic’s Revenue increased in the last two years?

It’s hard to understand how growth could continue even at this scale (of the tens of billions) ? It’s meaningfully changed the trajectory of Google Cloud and AWS growth who both have significant equity (and partnerships) in the AI startup.

> Anthropic's ARR has increased between 2,900% (from year-end 2024) and 64,900% (from start-of-year 2024).

Anthropic is thus the single primary tailwind for BigTech Earnings in 2026. Nobody grows 14x as this scale, nobody. For all the marketing gimmicks of OpenAI and insane confidence in SpaceX (including sadly some Pension funds), its three companies carrying the entire AI boom: TSMC, Nvidia and Anthropic. These companies are the backbone of what we call Generative AI of the last three years.

If Nvidia wants to subsidize compute as the “[Bank of AI](https://substack.com/redirect/8382a34b-e3c0-4b3a-9ace-a8f7cb958636?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)” (so prolific is it in circular and vendor financing), it should enable Anthropic to have compute parity with OpenAI. Nvidia is subsidizing Neo Clouds and the costs of compute at radical levels artificially inflating the value of the cost per token.

Anthropic is increasing ARR around $9 Billion per quarter in mid 2026. Anthropic's Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) has been growing roughly 3x to 4x faster than OpenAI's over recent quarters, driven largely by enterprise adoption and tooling like Claude Code. By the time OpenAI goes IPO, Anthropic won’t be a comparable company. It won’t have a peer competitor, do you understand what that means? Not Google, not OpenAI, not DeepSeek or Alibaba, not even close. By 2030, Anthropic will have achieved [AI Supremacy](https://substack.com/redirect/6e318fb8-e46a-4da8-a5d5-0e3b9974dec2?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

### OpenAI really is Slowing

OpenAI grew revenue from $5.7 Bn in Q1 to just $6.7 Bn in Q2. It’s slowing down at the worst possible time, just months before a supposed IPO.

At a time when Meta might lose a huge court case for its [addictive products hurting young people](https://substack.com/redirect/8c3bb032-f2b0-4c26-aaa3-35ca787efd3c?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), the ChatGPT maker launches a [ChatGPT product for Teens](https://substack.com/redirect/c3060f0c-b19b-435b-8933-540863e9bdd0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). The optics are just incredibly bad and show a level of desperation we haven’t seen before from them. I don’t believe Sam Altman is on the right side of history on this one.

OpenAI’s slowdown in ARR in stunning, especially when compared to Anthropic’s pre IPO pace. Quarter over Quarter growth in 2026 tells the real data story.

> “OpenAI grew just +18% q/q in Q2, while its main rival, Anthropic, grew +142% q/q - ending the quarter at $11.6B in revenue.”

Anthropic’s incredible 2026 pushed Google Cloud for instance, to accelerate growth by 63% YOY in Q1. It’s almost [impossible for Anthropic to grow as fast](https://substack.com/redirect/4a4fc8d6-3247-421c-a23c-bbedd2960bb3?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) in 2027. The reaction to token maxing that was all the rage in the first half of 2026 has pushed more Enterprise spend to Open-source models.

Boris Cherny has described AI coding tools as comparable to Gutenberg's printing press, and in some ways he’s not wrong. Anthropic was founded just five years ago in 2021 from AI researchers who didn’t agree with the direction Sam Altman was taking OpenAI. Anthropic’s seven core co-founders don’t even have much equity in Anthropic. Anthropic release reports that are even detrimental to their sales, yet don’t seem to suffer by doing so.

Anthropic is the original public benefit corporation (PBC) where profit motives are balanced with more ideological safe AI development and alignment motives. Even going up against the Pentagon, to do what is right. That’s a Chaotic Good company if I ever saw one. An AI startup that’s governed by a long-term benefit trust that apparently oversees safety protocol compliance.

It’s nothing like OpenAI or the way Google is structured, and maybe that’s the point. Anthropic is believed to hold around 42%–54% of the high-margin enterprise AI coding market (according to a report by Menlo Ventures), with premium pricing power at the upper end of dev focused productivity tools and starkly high recurring token consumption patterns.

While OpenAI keeps releasing features as products, and exaggerating the efficacy of Ads in ChatGPT, Anthropic doesn’t have to play those games.

Anthropic was founded by 7 core cofounders, all of whom left OpenAI together in 2021. It’s hard to believe it’s been just five years now:

- Jared Kaplan (Chief Science Officer)

Even with a severe compute Crunch, Anthropic has had the best year in technology and software history.

Anthropic and OpenAI are a “BigAI” duopoly that combined grew very well in 2026.

But once they IPO their respective futures diverges greatly. Anthropic will likely have a market cap twice as big just months from now. While OpenAI faces the same high level of executive churn it always has that only has a viable future if bailed out by Nvidia financing and dangerous margin debt from the Oracle and Softbank sides.

Anthropic has come a long ways in just two years time. Think about it, back in August 2024, Anthropic's annualized recurring revenue (ARR) stood at approximately $700 million. It’s grown revenue 93x since then.

Anthropic also is the one firm with the biggest equity exposure to BigTech itself. Though I don’t know the exact figures these are approximate estimates:

- Alphabet has an equity stake of about 14%.

- Amazon has an equity stake of about 7.8%

- Nvidia has an equity stake of about 2.6%

- Microsoft has an equity stake of about 1.4%.

Anthropic is the anchor company of the Generative AI movement of the 2020s. It has the highest concentration of AI researcher talent and has the most leverage in how AI coding models (and to some extent Agentic AI) keeps evolving.

By the time Anthropic goes public it will be comfortably profitable. In this Q2: Anthropic reported preliminary second-quarter revenue of $11.5 billion and posted a projected adjusted operating profit of $559 million. Relative to SpaceX and OpenAI grifting, Anthropic is at the other end of the spectrum. To the extent that [Elon Musk is giving them compute](https://substack.com/redirect/9a8c4e4d-bf18-4d3e-ad11-b4fe04db98cf?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), rather than even trying to compete against them. It’s quite telling really. Cursor’s entire success is only because of Anthropic’s API. Any router or wrapper growing revenue at incredible rates essentially owes its success to Anthropic’s AI coding supremacy. It’s the origin point of the AI boom, if Nvidia GPUs and TSMC fabs are its foundations.

You can be a skeptic about the AI boom and bubble cycle, but Anthropic’s revenue growth is like Nvidia’s revenue growth we saw earlier in the cycle, or what the HBM bottleneck has done for Micron, SK Hynix and Samsung. Anthropic doesn’t have the bizarre moonshots of a SpaceX. SpaceX spent $18.37 billion on capital projects in the second quarter. It booked just $7.81 billion of revenue. For every dollar of that, about $2.35 went out for data centers, chips, satellites, and rockets. When Anthropic goes IPO, it won’t have that kind of unsustainable revenue to capex ratio.

SpaceX has an end of year target of $100 Bn. in revenue, but so does Anthropic (actually a range of $100 Bn. to 120 Bn.). Anthropic is 19 years younger than SpaceX as a company and 6 years younger than OpenAI. OpenAI has spent an enormous sum on marketing since 2022 with a degraded first-mover advantage, while SpaceX might be the most overvalued company in the world. The real question is if Anthropic wasn’t compute constrained or constrained by the bizarre (regulatory) breaks in the Trump Administration, what would have it become in 2026 that is now pushed to 20267? And what will it be in a decade? This in a near term future where OpenAI and SpaceX are overwhelmed with competition.

Up until now xAI now called SpaceXAI has failed to compete in either Enterprise or consumer AI in a meaningful way. Much of its growth comes from Starlink that has an expiration date on its first mover advantage. I predict Anthropic wins AI supremacy status in 2027 and will have increasing potential to radically diversify its business model and take on increasingly hard problems. Whatever you think of [Dario Amodei](https://substack.com/redirect/5399088b-89dc-49f0-823b-4151486882e5?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), Daniela is running the company well. Anthropic is the AI startup where top talent goes to work with other top talent at a time when the exodus of talent and executives at [both Google](https://substack.com/redirect/95b35adb-8a8a-4285-b0d4-ba8c554ad3a3?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) and [OpenAI is conceding](https://substack.com/redirect/18f2a018-f381-455a-8d2d-e655475159c0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) a kind of “game over” passing it onwards to the next era of AI.

While Alphabet can keep up on [creative ways to squeeze capital](https://substack.com/redirect/cbaf31f9-aa64-4efc-8e41-6fb626538ca0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) before a rate hike, few others now will be able to follow what Anthropic becomes.

Anthropic is the most likely company where recursive self-improvement (RSI) emerges and we are still technically [many years from any kind of a fundamental](https://substack.com/redirect/490a2681-dfd5-4b1e-83c6-7690ee7561ca?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) technological singularity. Whatever this becomes, we are witnessing history.
