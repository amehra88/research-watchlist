### Igor Babuschkin's new startup profiled. Issue #1

Good Morning,

Visions of AI is a new feature format I’m experimenting with that will amount to a short profile on an AI or emerging tech startup. The cadence of this style of article is unknown as of yet, but there are a lot of fascinating startups I want to share about.

This is designed to be light evening reading to go out at a time-slot of 8 pm EST.

Not that many AI researchers have worked at Google DeepMind, OpenAI, xAI and started their own companies. Igor Babuschkin in 2026 is the founder of [River AI](https://substack.com/redirect/c195ca8c-e03a-4774-9ba8-05d136abbb45?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). River is building a stack for personal AI that is both open-source and adapts to you. This is a bit like the holy grail of personalization combined with all the recent advances in AI.

I actually find myself attracted to the philosophy and goal of the startup:

> We imagine a future where your AI works entirely for you and deeply aligns with your values. Compared to the corporate chatbots of today, it will feel completely different: responsible, curious, and truly yours. Most importantly, you'll own everything: the hardware it runs on, the data it learns from, and the intelligence itself.

This has legit open-source vibes to me and the interview with the lead investor [General Catalyst felt](https://substack.com/redirect/c9250db1-4266-4493-8f88-c20f855267a0?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)authentic:

## A 2-Month old Startup

River AI is among a group of AI labs that are taking on hard problems with quick capital not seen previously in the history of Venture Capital. It’s also very clearly a bet on Open-Source American AI.

River AI has just secured $1.1 billion in funding in a seed/Series A round led by General Catalyst and AMP PBC, with participation from Nvidia, AMD Ventures, Y Combinator, and Temasek.

> “We need the frontier labs to push the boundary of humanity, but we need open weights to empower humanity, putting intelligence into everyone's hands. Open models are the counter-architecture to concentration: they keep intelligence from collapsing into one provider and hand control to whoever wants to build” - [Hemant Taneja](https://substack.com/redirect/b358b022-9373-47cc-a2e0-98c1c49abd48?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA).

So just to be clear AMP PBC is actually an AI-focused investment firm founded in 2026 by former Andreessen Horowitz general partner [Anjney Midha](https://substack.com/redirect/ae695822-1681-446f-8741-c13471ab6146?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), backer of companies at a16z like Black Forest Labs, Mistral AI, LMArena and OpenRouter.) But River AI isn’t just a kind of Mistral for the United States, it’s more like Thinky (Thinking Machines Lab) that is helping to optimize, humanize and customize what LLMs can do for developers, new ideas and people.

They are leaning in hard to principles of Sovereign AI where you give businesses and countries ownership of their data and intelligence. That is what a healthy American AI ecosystem, really sovereignty anywhere, requires they argue. While China leads in open-weight models the U.S. are funding alternatives with a more American stamp.

River essentially came out of stealth in June, 2026. But at the time it was difficult to pin down exactly what they hoped to do. Now with this huge Seed round, there’s a more tangible direction.

The startup directly wants to helps enterprises customize open-source artificial intelligence models and make it as simple as possible to use them. They want to the rebuild the AI stack end to end: training, models, the product layer, and new hardware that lets personal AI live close to you.

[Introducing River AI (Blog)](https://substack.com/redirect/0347dd49-32af-4a03-80f7-9314afdc8eae?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA)

See the [River API](https://substack.com/redirect/1b2d8d82-41a6-4b1d-a1a1-e5888a9779c9?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA). It allows anyone to build custom agents and LLMs based on open weight models. [Babuschkin](https://substack.com/redirect/fbf92bb6-cec3-4073-a2c0-e74ccc4b82f5?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) actually comes from a Physics background and was among the most successful of them that transition from Physics into Machine Learning about 10 years ago. He completed his education at the Technical University of Dortmund. He began his career in high-energy physics, conducting research at CERN on the LHCb (Large Hadron Collider beauty) experiment, where he focused on particle collisions and high-energy physics data analysis. Who knew that decision to change fields from Physical into AI would be among the best decisions of his life.

Their product is practical though, as it allows companies run reinforcement learning and LoRA fine-tuning on open-weight models without managing the underlying infrastructure. Their first customers are likely to be Enterprise customers where revenue generation shouldn’t be a problem. It’s important to note he’s also a Fund manager and VC now - after departing xAI in August 2025, Babuschkin launched [Babuschkin Ventures](https://substack.com/redirect/042cbc6f-e677-405a-bda2-6eb2fe8188be?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA), an investment firm targeting AI startups and safety research.

The API allows developers to use both reinforcement learning (RL) and low-rank adaptation (LoRA) fine-tuning on the models and is billed per 1 million tokens. The API allows you to work with the world’s best open-weight models personalizing it to your needs. It’s a question of convenience and accessibility for Enterprise companies where River said enterprises can complete reinforcement learning runs in 15 to 20 minutes, while its platform handles weight transfers, sampling-training consistency and elastic compute.

By bringing together open models, LoRA fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, infrastructure, and instant deployment through a simple API, River AI is working to make accessible AI more convenient for businesses and possibly even entrepreneurs, developers, and so forth.

### Sovereign AI and Open-Source is the Ideological Theme of late 2026 in AI

There are just a handful of startups on the planet that have managed to raise $1 Billion in a Seed round bridge to Series A. The select list includes Safe Super Intelligence (SSI), World Labs and Advanced Machine Intelligence (AMI), but most resembles for sure in philosophy and mission Thinking Machines Lab. So you have an original Google DeepMind Alum competing with (and complementing) an OpenAI Alum.

As Quartz rightful explained we can think of River’s thesis as essentially a future where the enterprise market will migrate from off-the-shelf offerings by major AI labs toward privately owned, open-weight models tailored to each organization's needs in that now all too familiar Sovereign AI pitch.

Igor Babuschkin wasn’t at the top of my watchlist to bring this home either, but it’s opportunistic of him at a time when even Brad Lightcap of OpenAI is leaving OpenAI to start a new venture. The secret theme here in AI is perhaps that best years of your career should be on something you own. Hopefully Babuschkin can execute at River better than he did as a co-founder of xAI.

So far, River’s API supports LLMs with 35 billion to 1 trillion parameters. In an era of routers and operating systems the potential of LLMs are only beginning to truly impact Enterprise industries. Western Open-weight models are months to years behind China and open-source principles unites the superficial divides of geopolitics. AI is a thing that all of civilization is moving forwards.

How will River further personalize AI and personal intelligence remains to be seen. It’s also not clear what the valuation is if you raise $1.1 Billion after just a few weeks or how this could be viewed anything but a Seed round.

[LoRA](https://substack.com/redirect/f12b82bf-7f19-47f6-9335-af0761b92c9a?j=eyJ1IjoiOG5lN29xIn0.bqmJ9TjykdtTXCviJ3jD2X5vxhprRGd5tpCaC89FQIA) (Low-Rank Adaptation) enables fine tuning massive LLMs with far less hardware, VRM and storage costs. There’s a radical memory efficiency in this technology with microscopic file sizes. LoRA was actually introduced by researchers at Microsoft in June, 2021. It was open-source that enabled its rapid adoption in the first place. River AI is in a sense coming full circle in an industry where closed-source models are being flooded with capital where two companies are responsible for the majority of Cloud computing’s acceleration and the spike in BigTech Earnings.
