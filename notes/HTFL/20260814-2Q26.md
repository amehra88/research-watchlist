# HTFL — 2Q26 earnings read

_Prepared by earnings-reviewer, 2026-08-14. Tier: T2._

## 1. Headline read

Q2'26 is a clean acceleration on top of an already-strong 1Q26 — revenue growth stepped up from +41% to +48% YoY (fastest in eight quarters), gross margin expanded another 280bps sequentially to 83.3%, and management raised FY26 revenue guidance for the second consecutive quarter, this time by ~$18M at the midpoint. Where the 1Q26 note read Confirm-with-conservatism, this print resolves the open question from that quarter in the bull's favor: Plaque revenue more than doubled sequentially ($3.2M → $7.8M) and the clinical-awareness friction flagged last quarter did not resurface. The one genuinely new element is legal — an ongoing CID and patent litigation appeared in Q&A for the first time in this corpus.

## 3. Forward guidance

Second consecutive raise, and a larger one than Q1's:

- **FY26 revenue: $246M–$250M** (40–42% YoY), raised from $228M–$232M — roughly +$18M at the midpoint.
- **FY26 Plaque revenue: $29M–$31M**, raised from $19M–$21M — a ~50% increase to the Plaque-specific guide.
- **FY26 non-GAAP gross margin: ~82%**, raised from ~81%; +500bps YoY. Midterm **85%** target reaffirmed.
- **Plaque activated accounts: ~1,250 by YE26**, raised from 1,200.
- **Cash flow profitability by mid-2028**, described as consistent with prior guidance.

Guidance philosophy is unchanged and explicitly conservative — Verghese described "a conservative guidance approach, allowing room for outperformance in the second half." This is now the second straight quarter management has used near-identical framing after a double-digit beat, which converts it from a one-off into a pattern. The arithmetic makes the cushion explicit: H1'26 actual revenue is $116.67M ($52.59M + $64.08M), so the $248M FY guide midpoint implies **H2'26 of $131.3M, or ~$65.7M per quarter against Q2's $64.08M** — essentially zero implied sequential growth for two straight quarters, against a business that just grew 22% sequentially. Treat the FY26 guide as a floor, not a forecast.

The 2027 setup articulated on the call is more concrete than Q1's: Plaque Staging (launched July 2026) driving H2'26 and 2027 adoption, Plaque Tracker launching 2027, autonomous processing broader rollout 2027, PCI Navigator broader rollout 2027, FFRCT ASP declines expected to moderate starting 2027, and Plaque contractual price step-ups in early 2027. Pricing inflects positively in 2027 on both products simultaneously — that is the single most important forward item on this call.

## 6. Competitive advantage signal

- **Innovation rate** (current: [SCHEMA GAP]; prior note recommended 4+)
  - Evidence: Three product events inside ~4 quarters — PCI Navigator (April 2026), Plaque Staging (July 2026, validated in 23,000+ patients), with Plaque Tracker and autonomous processing both slated for 2027. 625+ peer-reviewed publications. Three RCTs into high-risk asymptomatic populations beginning over the next three quarters, requiring **no FDA clearance** because they leverage already-cleared technology — a materially faster path to TAM expansion than a de novo regulatory route.
  - Recommendation: **initialize to 4+** (hold).
- **Distribution** (current: [SCHEMA GAP]; prior note recommended 4-)
  - Evidence: Activated-account target raised to ~1,250 by YE26, with management stating the Plaque activation pace is **exceeding the pace previously achieved with FFRCT** — a clean internal benchmark. Record levels of new and repeat physician orders. US revenue +51% YoY. Plaque is applicable to ~60% of CCTAs vs FFRCT's ~33%, so the same channel carries roughly double the attach opportunity. The asymptomatic RCTs will run through existing sales channels. Notably, the clinical-awareness gap that Piper raised in Q1 — physicians unaware Plaque reimbursement existed — did **not** recur this call; the Piper question this quarter was about revenue allocation instead.
  - Recommendation: **revise to 4** (up from the prior note's proposed 4-).
- **Overall** (current: [SCHEMA GAP]; prior note recommended 4)
  - Recommendation: **initialize to 4+**.
  - Reasoning: Not a mechanical average of 4+ and 4. The interaction changed this quarter in a specific way: last quarter innovation was leading and distribution was the lagging constraint. Plaque Staging collapses that gap, because it is a product launch that raises *utilization inside the already-activated account base* — innovation converting into same-account revenue without proportional new distribution spend. That is the highest-leverage form the two dimensions can take for a per-case-billed diagnostic. Farquhar's answer to Jacob Dodd (Morgan Stanley) is the direct evidence: Plaque utilization is "ramping well, though not yet at maximum levels," alongside record levels of both new *and repeat* orders — i.e. headroom remains inside accounts already sold. The overall therefore sits above the distribution component rather than between the two. Held below 5 because the model still depends on a single vertical and on payer behavior HTFL does not control.

## 8. Management Q&A flags

- **William Plovanic (Canaccord) — CID and patent litigation:** The clearest flag on the call and the only new risk disclosure. Farquhar expressed confidence in HTFL's legal position and said the matters are not distracting from operations, but gave no specifics on the CID's scope or subject. On an otherwise unusually transparent, data-dense call, this was the one answer that was reassurance rather than detail. First appearance of legal matters in this corpus — establish a baseline and track for escalation.
- **Matthew O'Brien (Piper) — guidance conservatism, second consecutive quarter:** Verghese explicitly described a conservative guide leaving room for 2H outperformance, echoing near-identical Q1 framing. Two quarters of the same language after two double-digit beats makes this a management style rather than a situational choice. Read as durable conservatism; the practical implication is that the FY26 guide should be treated as a floor.
- **Robert Marcus (JPM) — CMS MedTech-as-a-Service proposal:** New policy variable not present in Q1. Farquhar framed it positively and aligned with CMS goals, with stable economics for both FFRCT and Plaque. Management volunteered more enthusiasm here than the question required — worth watching whether the proposal is genuinely accretive or merely non-threatening.
- **Gene Mannheimer (Freedom) — PCI Navigator not monetized:** Farquhar confirmed **no current plans to charge** for PCI Navigator despite strong interventional-cardiologist feedback. A deliberate give-away-to-lock-the-platform strategy. Defensible, but it means a shipped product carries zero direct revenue — a monetization option held in reserve, and a question to revisit if platform competition intensifies.
- **Rick Wise (Stifel) — 2027 pricing inflection:** FFRCT YoY ASP declines expected to moderate starting 2027 while Plaque contractual step-ups arrive in early 2027. Both pricing vectors turn favorable in the same year, on top of volume growth — the most underweighted item on this call relative to the attention it received.

- **`ai_native_vertical` and `vertical_ai_applications`** — both used in the prior 1Q26 note but absent from the watchlist entry. These are existing vocabulary, and this call supports both. Recommend the operator formally assign them.

- **"MedTech-as-a-Service"** — CMS reimbursement construct, new this quarter. Policy vocabulary likely to recur across any healthcare-tech names the PM adds.
- **"Competition is the standard of care"** — management's framing that its competitor set is traditional non-invasive testing rather than named vendors. A useful lens for category-creation names generally.
- **"Proprietary CTA image database"** — proprietary AI training asset in a regulated vertical (carried forward from Q1; still unmatched by a watchlist theme).
