## 1. Headline read

The substance of the 2026-09-04 read stands: revenue landed on the company's own guide midpoint ($108.125M vs. $108.0M) and +0.34% vs. consensus, non-GAAP gross margin landed below the guide midpoint (59.3% vs. 59.75%), and the non-GAAP EPS beat (+5.3%) came from operating leverage below the gross-margin line. The 1Q27 drift item — inventory build and FCF burn — persists but does not break: the cash leg normalized (Q2 FCF ≈ −$7.1M vs. −$29.6M in Q1), the inventory leg did not (days 145 → 157), and the memory risk migrated from AMBA's balance sheet to customers' bill of materials with Q4 explicitly hedged. Against the five open thesis assumptions, the call confirms two (AI SoC installed base >50M; Hanwha LTA with no amendment or termination on file), confirms the automotive record was not a single peak, and is silent on the two whose conditions are dated to 3Q27 or to the watchlist itself.

Where this read departs from the 2026-09-04 note, it is on facts the 10-Q added rather than on judgment: (1) the 11.0% "Acuto" in the vendor summary is **Hakuto Co., Ltd.**, AMBA's Japanese distributor — a second channel intermediary, not an end customer; (2) the $9.0M credit is the unrefunded remainder of a **$13.5M customer development deposit** on a project terminated **2026-05-12** — sixteen days before the 1Q27 call, on which it was not surfaced; (3) the H1 inventory build was **WIP-led** ($35.5M → $52.8M) rather than finished-goods-led ($16.7M → $24.1M), which reads as wafer pre-buy rather than unsold product; (4) the 10-Q's supply-chain risk language was **modified this quarter** to name memory-component tightness at customers as a demand risk to AMBA — the mechanism the CEO described on the call is now in the filing.

## 3. Forward guidance

**Q3 FY27 (ending 2026-10-31), per 8-K Ex-99.1, verbatim:** "Revenue is expected to be between $115.0 million and $124.0 million. Gross margin on a non-GAAP basis is expected to be between 59.0% and 60.0%. Non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be between $56.5 million and $59.5 million."

- Revenue midpoint **$119.5M**, +10.5% QoQ at the mid, an acceleration from Q2's +7.7%. **The midpoint sits on the Street, not above it:** pre-call FactSet consensus (2026-09-02/03, `fiscalEndDate 2026-10-31`) was mean $119.28M / median $119.53M, n=11. Post-call (2026-09-04) mean moved to $119.75M with 2 up / 2 down and the dispersion collapsed (std dev 2.17 → 1.11; low 115.6 → 118.4). Revenue revisions were flat; the guide anchored the Street rather than lifting it.
- **The range widened**: $9.0M wide (±3.8% of mid) vs. $6.0M (±2.8%) for the Q2 guide. Read alongside the Q4 memory hedge, that is management pricing in more dispersion, not less.
- Non-GAAP GM **59.0–60.0%**, mid 59.5% — re-centered **25bp below** the Q2 guide mid (59.75%) and with the top end cut 50bp (60.5% → 60.0%). Floor held.
- Non-GAAP opex $56.5–59.5M vs. $57.354M delivered in Q2 (derived in the prior note from the 8-K reconciliation tables): flat to modestly up, so the Q3 guide does not assume Q2's opex leverage repeats.

**Q4 hedge (unchanged from the 2026-09-04 read):** memory supply constraints "could impact fourth-quarter performance"; management expects "regular seasonality for Q4 barring any significant disruptions" (transcript summary paraphrase). ## 8. Management Q&A flags

_Paraphrase, not verbatim quotation, except where a figure is attributed to the 8-K or 10-Q — see §10. InsiderScore reports `challenging_analyze_exchanges = 0`._

1. **Does the 3Q27 guide fit the recorded guidance pattern? Yes on the range, no longer on the skew.** Store B track record (2020-04 to 2026-04): SALES guide hit rate 0.96 over 25 guided periods, **in-range 1.00**, sandbag index −0.031, average beat vs. guide mid +1.9%, consensus beat rate 1.00. 2Q27 fits the in-range pattern (108.125 inside 105–111) but the beat-vs-mid has decayed across five consecutive prints: **+6.1% (2Q26), +4.3% (3Q26), +0.9% (4Q26), +0.4% (1Q27), +0.1% (2Q27)**. The guide-vs-consensus premium decayed the same way: guide mid over consensus-at-guide was +14.1% (3Q26), +6.0% (4Q26), +3.3% (1Q27), +0.9% (2Q27), and for 3Q27 the $119.5M mid is **+0.2% over the pre-call mean** ($119.28M). The honest read of the 3Q27 guide is therefore a genuine central estimate inside a range management has never missed — not a sandbagged floor. The wider range ($9M vs. $6M) is the only new element and is consistent with the Q4 memory hedge. Expect a landing inside 115–124; do not underwrite the historical +1.9% cushion.
2. **Gross margin guide: coin-flip on the mid, but the Store B hit rate is contaminated by a basis mismatch.** INC_GROSS shows guide hit rate 0.48, in-range 0.92, sandbag index −0.045. However, Store B's INC_GROSS actuals are **GAAP-basis gross profit** while the guide mids are **non-GAAP** (1Q27: actual 58.589 ÷ revenue 100.357 = 58.4%, which is GAAP; guide mid 59.7725 = 100.0 × 59.75% non-GAAP; same pattern on 4Q26 and 2Q27 where 64.5525 = 108.0 × 59.77%). The ~160bp GAAP/non-GAAP gap (~2.7% of GP) accounts for the persistent "below mid" readings. On a like-for-like non-GAAP basis: 1Q27 59.9% vs. 59.75% mid (+15bp), 2Q27 59.3% vs. 59.75% (−45bp) — a real coin-flip. For 3Q27 management re-centered the mid to 59.5% after a below-mid landing, which is the pattern; with the 10-Q citing advanced-node cost as the driver, lean to a landing at or below 59.5% but inside the range. **Operator action:** correct the Store B INC_GROSS actual basis before the credibility scores are used again.
3. **The $9.0M credit — the 10-Q supplies what the call did not (Bolton, Needham).** Bolton probed the item; CFO Young said it was unrelated to the semi-custom opportunity and involved an automotive customer. The 10-Q (accrued liabilities note) states: a $13.5M development deposit liability at 2026-01-31 "represents a cash advance from a customer for funding a development project that is subject to certain refund conditions. On May 12, 2026, the Company terminated the development project with the customer. Pursuant to the terms of the termination agreement, the Company refunded $4.5 million of the deposit to the customer. The remaining $9.0 million of the deposit was recognized as a reduction of" R&D expense. Two flags: the termination pre-dated the 1Q27 call (2026-05-28) by sixteen days and, per the 1Q27 note, was not surfaced on it; and an automotive customer walked away from a funded development project while forfeiting $9.0M — the customer, the product, and the reason remain undisclosed. The InsiderScore summary still calls it a "charge"; it is a credit.
4. **Memory cost decoupled from AMBA's margin, coupled to customer demand (Pharr, BofA; Moore, Morgan Stanley).** Wang's position is that memory prices do not touch AMBA's gross margin (AMBA does not buy or resell memory) but could affect customer order volumes; he expressed confidence in managing Q3 and Q4 but conceded Q4 visibility is lower. The 10-Q now encodes the same mechanism in modified risk language (customers "may delay orders of our products or hold inventory of our products for longer periods"). This is the assumption `inventory_drift_not_break`'s adjudication mechanism, and it is now on the record in a filing, which makes a 3Q27 cancellation or push-out disclosure harder to omit.
5. **Wang deflated his own partnership announcement (Moore, Morgan Stanley; Yang, Oppenheimer).** Asked about revenue from the ecosystem partners, Wang put meaningful revenue 2–3 years out with first design wins next year; Minhazuddin distinguished Capgemini (value play) from Macnica (volume play). Announcing ~$1B of seven-year potential and immediately damping the near-term is a credibility-positive tell, and — per flag 1 — consistent with a management team whose guide-vs-Street premium has compressed to zero rather than one still building cushion.
